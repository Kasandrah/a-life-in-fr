Alertness You get a +2 bonus on all Listen checks and Spot checks.

Armor Proficiency (Light) When you wear a type of armor with which you are proficient, the armor check penalty for that armor applies only to Balance, Climb, Escape Artist, Hide, Jump, Move Silently, Pick Pocket, and Tumble checks.
Armor Proficiency (Medium) When you wear a type of armor with which you are proficient, the armor check penalty for that armor applies only to Balance, Climb, Escape Artist, Hide, Jump, Move Silently, Pick Pocket, and Tumble checks.
Blind-Fight In melee, every time you miss because of concealment, you can reroll your miss chance percentile roll one time to see if you actually hit.
Divine Vengeance See Text, You can spend one of your turn undead attempts to add 2d6 points of sacred energy damage to all your successful melee attacks against undead until the end of your next action.
Extra Turning (2x) You can use your ability to turn or rebuke creatures four more times per day than normal.
Martial Weapon Proficiency (Shortbow) Choose a type of martial weapon. You understand how to use that type of martial weapon in combat.
Point Blank Shot You get a +1 bonus on attack and damage rolls with ranged weapons at ranges of up to 30 feet.
Precise Shot You can shoot or throw ranged weapons at an opponent engaged in melee without taking the standard -4 penalty on your attack roll.
Shield Proficiency You can use a shield and take only the standard penalties.
Simple Weapon Proficiency You make attack rolls with simple weapons normally.
Weapon Focus (Shortbow) You gain a +1 bonus on all attack rolls you make using the selected weapon.
