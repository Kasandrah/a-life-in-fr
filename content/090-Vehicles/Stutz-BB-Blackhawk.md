# 1928 Stutz Model BB Blackhawk Boattail Speedster

![[cc_stutz_bb_blackhawk.jpg]]
**1928 Stutz Model BB Blackhawk Boattail Speedster**

The car that was the essence of glamour and the epitome of speed – **the** car to be seen in. More so than any other automobile built during the 1920s, it made a statement – a statement of style, sophistication, and, most of all, speed.

The fastest production car of its era. Winner of every race in which it was entered in its maiden year. A car so superb in handling, it even won the respect of competing manufacturers.

The famed “boattail”, the fender aprons that protected the body from debris, the convertible top, the fabled Stutz hood ornament – the sun god Ra – and the sunburst of spokes forming the wheels all made the car unique and exciting.
