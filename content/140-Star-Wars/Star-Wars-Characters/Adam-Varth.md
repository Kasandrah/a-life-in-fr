# Wing Commander Adam Varth

Varth was under Chan's control for offensive operations in Atrivis Sector, and is still directly answerable to Alliance Sector Command for protecting the ORCC and to Alliance STARCOM for combined operations with other sectors' star fighters.

![[sw_varth_sean_connery.jpg]]

Varth came from a merchant shipping line (cruise ships).  2000 civilians were killed when a ship of the line was destroyed by an Imperial cruiser (the slaughter was then covered up by the Imperials).  Varth found out later that his wife and young son were on board.  They were coming to surprise him for a family reunion.

Varth and General Kryll met Elkior Toiyabe when they travelled with him and Mon Mothma to Generis Base to organise the Treaty of Generis.  Varth stayed on to organise the starfighter wing and pilot training.  General Kryll stayed on to set up the ORCC.

![[sw_varth.jpg]]

**Wing Commander Adam Varth by Tim Howe**

Once General Kryll became aware of Jess' relationship to Toiyabe, he contacted General Vernan, Chief of Alliance Intelligence.  Kryll was denied permission to provide any more information than what he and Varth had already given Jess.  Vernan's reason was that information concerning a member of the Alliance Cabinet was not given out to anyone, but especially not to someone who wasn't a member of the Alliance.

As well as being the Minister for Supply, Elkior is a member of the Committee on Starfighter Acquisition and Maintenance.  Varth is on the same committee and had no wish to find his starfighters reassigned to some other base because he had upset the Minister of Supply by referring to a personal situation in which they were all involved, i.e. Jess.  Varth avoided discussing this with Jess as he was bound by General Vernan's orders.

Varth had some potent feelings for Jess but did not want to re-experience the pain of losing someone he loved.  His wife and son were murdered by Imperials and he felt that getting too close to Jess would invite disaster.  Varth was attempting to cut off his feelings for Jess, partly by trying to ignore her and partly by dwelling on her faults.

He had taken exception to Jess' constant harping about "I'm not in the Rebellion, I'm an honest trader".  That implied that Jess considered the Alliance dishonest.  Varth had chosen sides in this conflict and had taken the Alliance Oath.

He was disappointed at Jess' lack of commitment and believed she would eventually leave to go trading.  This was particularly brought home to him by Jess' behaviour on Fest when the group was trying to load the ship.  Her display of temper would certainly not have been tolerated in any of his personnel, especially when dealing with an Imperial officer and Imperial stormtroopers.  His reservations over her attitude were reinforced during Princess Leia's visit.  Varth had discussed none of this with Jess; instead, he was waiting to see whether she would eventually change her attitude and stop being so wilfully disruptive.

Varth's recent reunion with his wife Seleene and son Adam, rescued from Kuras III by the crew of the _Star Wind_, has been made more difficult by Selene's new partner, a fellow survivor of the _Solar Wind_ with whom she wishes to stay.  Varth is attempting to rebuild a relationship with his son, now a boy of twelve.
