## Saxons in Anglia

Word has reached me that Anglia has been taken by more Saxons.  Earl Robert has chosen me to investigate with some of the other Lords' men to find out if these Saxons are going to link up with the other Saxons.  Arcavius for Praetor meets me and Hywel in Sarum, and we go to Levcomagus and with Cynon to Silchester to Staines to London.

Learn of sword in stone - "whomsoever pulleth out the sword is the rightful king of England".  Has been there for 5 years.  Test it.  Get Arcavius to confirm what it says.

To Hertford to Royston.  Visit sister.  Go to Cambridge.  It has a number of Iceni who have fled from Anglia.  Cynon advises getting a guide.  I orate and _[[010-The-Seasons#^bd8287|Clwyd]]_ steps forward and will take us if we do justice on those Saxons who burnt his village.  I agree.  Our plan is to go to village and eliminate opposition.  Kill em, but take a chief or leader-type prisoner to get information needed.  On trip there, try and avoid fighting.

Leave at dawn.  Head east a couple of days.  Nearing Clwyd's village.  The village has been burnt down.  Order Hywel to look for tracks and Arcavius to look for anything.  _[[010-The-Seasons#^3bc69d|Madog]]_ (Hywel's new squire) has found something.  Hywel goes over to him and he's chortling, trying not to laugh.  I look around and find a dead body to the side.  It has its arm and side eaten away.  Go to speak with Hywel.  Madog says he found a dragon's tracks, but Hywel is pointing out it's a combination of four tracks.  I bring Hywel to weird corpse.  We discuss its like the goblin's blood, but the arm being eaten away meant it was a giant - a Fomori.  I find tracks.

We go tracking, late in afternoon, Hywel leading his horse.  Back tracks and thinks trail went one way while Cynon thinks trail went the other way.  Party splits up, plan = for 1/2 hour and then we come back to that point.  Hywel and me with squires and Clwyd go one way, the others the other.  Ten minutes after splitting, a big roar and a four-armed, armoured Fomori swings giant club and knocks Hywel over.  I inspired to save my good and noble friend and vassal, charge and fight the Fomori.

Hit him critically once, knocking him down, as he's getting up and squatting he grapples my horse, I swing and critical him again.  I make horse roll and leap off horse.  My sword has been eaten away and is still being eaten away.  Three Saxons are fighting Hywel and the two squires and three Saxons are facing me.  I throw stub of sword at Saxons and fight defensively with shield.  Cynon and Arcavius come charging on horseback.  They fight Saxons who are facing me.  The two knights take out the Saxons.  The others had only one Saxon (who was berserking) and they were letting him rave himself out, but Cynon steps in and takes him out.

Camp at Saxon's camp.  On Arcavius' watch, he yells at something in forest, we wake and Clwyd calls out to forest.  _[[010-The-Seasons#^f40473|Pedrog]]_ - stay = escaped from an encampment - lost in forest for days - he was originally taken before winter!  Three other prisoners.  He escaped when the Saxons were distracted by two powerful Saxons who came a visiting.  Us knights chat, Clwyd climb tree in morning and we go in morning.  Clwyd and _[[010-The-Seasons#^05e962|Budd]]_ climbed tree, haze was to south (some place on fire), there was smaller fires (camps) to north nearer Fens.  Speaking with Pedrog and asking him whether he remembered Fens, he said he remembered the smell.

We head north.  As we travel, notice that we cross Pedrog's wandering trail north.  As we travel, Arcavius sees a shine of metal ahead.  We tried to wait but I couldn't see enough cover to ambush them, so we rode straight at them.  There are six Saxons (two of which are big ones).  Me and Cynon go at them.  Cynon down in first blow, I knock mine down (it's a chieftain).  I inspired to protect Cynon, as Chief getting up, I strike at one of two fighting Hywel.  Then next round I fight Chief, two more blows and he goes down.  Hywel and squire against one now, berserker who took Cynon down and one other are fighting Arcavius.  I attack berserker - one blow normal, 2nd blow cleft him in half.  Battle finishes.  Arcavius and then Hywel first aid Cynon, I first aid Saxon chief.  We head back to Cambridge and will head back to Royston.

## Iceni Refugees

Travelling southeast to Cambridge.  Hear sounds ahead.  I take Hywel ahead and meet with two coming from southeast to northeast, also heading to Cambridge.  They are Iceni - women "warriors".  We talk.  They are led by _[[010-The-Seasons#^67ac49|Coulomb Not-A-Lady]]_.  I notice we're being outflanked.  We agree to join our forces.  I spout how well we've done.  One of the Iceni chirurges Cynon, but they all dislike the Saxon and Arcavius (Roman).

Getting to Cambridge.  Find out the Iceni are going to Warwick as they have kin there.  We (party) are talking about overcrowding.  Travelling to Royston taking hundreds of Iceni.  Arcavius suggests we could visit the Lady who is known for rescuing Cymric people.  I decide all party including Saxon and … Coulomb.  I persuade her to come and find out about this "place of safety".  We ride out of Cambridge and once out of sight gather all horses and people in a clump and when Arcavius does his stuff and we all seem to fly alone and then we appear by tree which Arcavius fell out of.  We travel to cutting and in there tell Arcavius to go ahead and warn of our coming.

Speaking in feast hall.  We decide we need to gather all Iceni refugees to Cambridge, for forwarding to here, and also we need to capture a Fomori.  We all, except Cynon and Owain, go to hall next morn and ask to speak to the Lady.  The horses are ready and await outside.  The Lady is giving folk-dancing lessons to the young ones.  When there's an easing in the dancing (it just doesn't stop), we inform the Lady that its time for us to leave and create the cairn and we request her aid in allowing Cynon to remain in her gracious care.  She asks who's going back (to where and when) and I say just us, so she sends us back ... without the horses.  In the blink of an eye, we're on a road track.  I set the squires to gathering rocks.  An old couple and their livestock are travelling south on the track and stop and gawk.  Arcavius asks where the road leads to and finds out we're 1 1/2 miles north of Cambridge.

Head south (walking in armour).  After a mile, there's a noticeable tree (large, gnarly, hangs over track and has Latin graffiti etched into it).  After looking around get squires to build a cairn behind the tree.  Arcavius places the refugee stone amidst the pile.  We then walk to Cambridge, in spite of Coulomb's urgings to go back and get the horses.  When we get to Cambridge, she goes to gather her supporters and the rest of us rest.  She comes back and informs us that she has spoken to her group and has convinced another group of the same size.  We head back to the cairn and she says show us how to go.  I step forward to the cairn, and after a while think of going to the place of safety.  I find myself at the bottom of stairs leading up to gateway, with gargoyles lined up.  Speak to gardener and then direct some of the Celts up the stairs.

Bath and then dinner.

## The Saxon / Fomori Alliance

Next morn, we are transported back to 10 minutes after we left.  Say goodbye to Coulomb and head east.  That night we camp and during my watch, a faun visits Anwyn.  In morn, Anwyn says we need to go south and I find out what the faun told him.  5 miles south, Anwyn gets off his horse, strings bow and tells us to be quiet.  Arcavius off horse and Cynon off horse.  Anwyn goes forward and shoots twice followed by two cries.  Fomori comes charging out and Cynon faces him.

The rest of us face up to the four Saxons.  Me onto two of them.  first round I major one of them and knock him down and Cynon major wounds the Fomori.  Next round, nothing much happens except Arcavius is knocked down.  Then I kill one, Hywel kills his and Arcavius knocked down again.  Then Hywel kills the one standing over Arcavius, I kill my remaining one and Cynon brings down the Fomori.  Arcavius first aids the Fomori back to positives and then the Saxon.  Anwyn goes to get the Lady.  Hywel first aids Arcavius.  The Saxons are separated from their goods.

The Lady and Anwyn arrive.  We move the Fomori so it's resting on its back against a tree.  She talks to him.  Gets Cynon to threaten him but it doesn't work.  She growls (like a dragon) at the Fomori and the Fomori blabs.  The Fomori King has offered the Angle King a great treasure for two brides for the Fomori.  Bye-bye prisoners.

We talk around what we want to do.  Eventually, after talking about demons (not Gorbodok), I suggest we go ask the fish.  Vision shown by fish is of a church, small with an angel hand on sword hilt and other holding a spear.  Arcavius asks how do we stop the Saxon / Fomori alliance.  Anwyn says he doesn't think the Wise One will answer just as the Salmon shows a very beautiful queen (?) / Saxon.  Arcavius and I remember about rumours of the Jutish-Saxon king's wife being extremely beautiful.

As we are ready to depart, Hywel throws an acorn and asks to see Margawse.  See a twisted, bitter woman who turns around and sees us and scares the fish.  She must have been taught by the Witch of the North (as mentioned in 496 AD).

We travelled back to the castle and discussed our plans some more.  Our decision is to send Arcavius to Bishop Bawdwin to get him to strengthen the protection at Glastonbury while Cynon and I go to Silchester to tell Duke Ulfius of the new Saxon situation (they are not allied / connected to the currently resident Saxons, but they are allied to the Fomori) and to convince him to make common cause with Earl Robert and Praetor Jonathel of Dorset.  From there, we would journey to Salisbury to do the same with Earl Robert.  Each of us offers to provide our service for the Lady when she requires it of us.

Arcavius and Gessius are sent a week prior to the fight with the Fomori, and arrive ½ mile from the Bishop's residence.  The day after the fight with the Fomori, we reappear in the Amphitheatre which was just off to the north of Silchester.  We go straight to Duke Ulfius' court.  Politicking allows me to pick out all the factions of this court.  Just like Salisbury, really.

After arranging a private meeting, we tell our story as: "We are returning from the important scouting mission about the latest Saxon meeting."  Cynon, the Steward of Levcomagus, reports to his vassal lord.  The new Saxons are not allied to the established Saxon tribes.  However, they are allied to the Fomori, like the one that we killed in the caverns below the city.  As proof, we show some of the Fomori equipment we retrieved.  Cynon also talks of the great treasure offered by the Saxons to the Fomori, which is being held at Glastonbury Tor. Arcavius had been sent to request reinforcements for the Tor from Bishop Bawdwin.

Cynon passes on my suggestion of a more formal alliance between Silchester and Salisbury.  I expand on it to include as many Cymric and Roman lords as we can for the defence of all.  I suggest the reforming of the Council of Lords / Regents (whatever it was before) which would provide leadership for such an alliance.  The conversation wavers over to the High Kingship and the sword in the stone and a resulting possible ruler and I throw the comment that maybe that would provide someone with no affiliation to just one land and as a result the strong-willed lords would be more willing to follow such a one.

But returning to the point, Ulfius raises the question of how to break such an alliance.  Feeling compelled to find some answer to the puzzling vision shown to us by the Wise One (very fishy), I tell of how we were shown a vision of a very beautiful Saxon princess / queen, whereon Cynon mentions we thought it might be the Jute queen.  Ulfius questions how we got this vision, and I gave him the truth which he wouldn't have believed if we hadn't shown him the Fomori were involved.

In discussing what to do, I suggested that we conduct raids on the Saxons in Essex, killing all we fought and leaving behind broken bits of the Fomori stuff we had found so that we might cause dissension between the new and the old Saxons.  Ulfius and Cynon suggested we concentrate on the Jutes in this, but any raids on the Kent - Silchester border would pass suspicion onto us and we wouldn't survive long by boat on the coast where we wouldn't be able to penetrate / infiltrate that far.  I told Ulfius that for too long the Saxons had profited from our divisiveness and now was the time to repay what we had learnt.

Other places that we would like to sound out for this alliance were Londinium, which was ruled by a council and had about 70 knights and 1000 soldiers, Malahaut, a kingdom ruled by the King of 100 knights who had about 300 knights and 2000 soldiers, Idres King of Cornwall and maybe Mark of Totnes, Duke Corneus of Lindsey who commanded about 300 knights and 1000 soldiers.

## Angering Oberon

We stay overnight, then travel one day to Levcomagus, then travel one day to Sarum where we bring Earl Robert into the fold.   Then we travel on to Vagon castle.  We choose to travel so we camp away from the castle.  On my watch, Puck appears with an invitation (?) for me to see _[[Oberon|Oberon]]_.  I wake up the others and Cynon suggests he doesn't want to go and I review that the Fae can delay our return and I think he probably wants us to go get the Cauldron now, but our mission is urgent.  So only Budd and I go with Puck.  We are supposed to meet up with the others at Glastonbury Tor.

Speak with Oberon and I piss him off mightily.  I say no … and maybe one or two other things.  One of the offers I recall is the use of the sword Gorias for the duration of my life.  Can't remember now why I refused … was it a choice of loyalty between Arthur and Oberon?  Not sure.

## Prince Ailil and Morgaine

We go head south to Dorset.  I stop off home while the rest continue south.  Speak to Jeanne about what has happened during this season, and especially about pissing off Oberon.  She is sorry but she cannot help with some of my enemies.  I pass on the invitation from the Lady.  Jeanne brings up the topic of Morgaine.  She had seemed moody when I had returned, but I had thought it was something of her age (teens?!), but she has apparently had a lover (!) which no-one had seen.

This raised thought that she was of an age for matching.  I tried to think of possible matches ( I don't know .. the name Gondarins seems to spring to mind) and hoped she would be as lucky as I had been with my match.  But Jeanne tops my idea by suggesting Sir Cynon is a very good match, both as a known companion for Morgaine, but also good as a joining of Sarum and Silchester.  Once again my love shows her astute wisdom.

I go to speak to Morgaine and as I'm about to knock on her door, I overhear from within her questioning of "Did my lord like my playing?"  I am shocked to recognize the voice of Unseelie Prince replying.  Pushing open the door barely seemed to surprise them for they turned to look at me and then they just disappeared.  Quickly, I run to Jeanne, shouting that Ailil's taken Morgaine.  She lets me rant till I can talk normally, then she finds out what I'm talking about.  She races to Morgaine's room and stops at the doorway.  Closing her eyes for a while, she then moves into the room and then jumps out the window and changes in a shimmer into the form of the Raven which then flies off.

Striding down to the hall, I order servants to prepare my horse and gather supplies for a week.  As Sir Gilbert, who is acting as my Seneschal enters, he asks when do we ride.  Truly a good friend, but I instruct him to protect Broad Chalke while I'm gone.  I return to Morgaine's room to await my love's return with what she has learnt.  But as night falls, the weight of the rest of the world returns and I must take care of my family.  For the next four days of waiting, I find I cling to Arthur and my children.

Arcavius and Cynon return from their embassy to Dorset, bringing word that the Praetor agrees to this alliance if Earl Robert can provide 10 knights to support him.  But that is almost forgotten as I bring them up to speed on what happened.  We travel to Sarum and pass word of the embassy to Dorset, and also of my current urgent mission.

Travel to Sauvernake Forest and speak to Puck.  He comes when I call!  Although I am in great disfavour with Oberon, I tell Puck to pass a message to Oberon of what has happened to Morgaine.  He wants to know what I'm going to do, and I reply I'm going to talk to Ailil and his quick reply is "I suggest not like last time."  He offers to pass the word on to Oberon if I promise to reward him as my honour suggests.

We go to Avebury circle.  Puck is very unhappy and the horses are spooked.  Blood sacrifice.  Red Cap comes (like the anti-Puck).  We butt heads verbally.  I make the point that I ask politely here where I could have visited Ailil's circle as before.  Red Cap goes into the stone.  After a while he comes back and says Ailil welcomes the foster-father of his new companion.  Then the Red Cap bows us through the stone.  Arcavius tries first and his faith bounces him back thrice, and then Cynon, me and Hywel.

## Unseelie to the Bone

Tunnel like a postern gate into a cavern.  Carved statues of mortal realm which eventually pass to non-mundane statues.  I get the feeling they are not carved statues.  After five times of passing a flying raven statue, I call the Red Cap to take us to Ailil, but he says he wanted me to recognise the artwork and how good it was.  I do a double take on the Raven statue and its Jeanne (crit. Recognise - into shock-cum-rage).

The Red Cap takes us past a guard named _[[030-Arthurian-Britain#^f5e6ab|Otho the Troll]]_.  Looking into his eyes is a confusing experience - the absence of any intelligence gives me vertigo.  We approach the sounds of a party.  The Red-Cap taunts me with words that the wedding has started.  When he asks for our invitations, we gate-crash.  What we see is ten-times wilder than Beltane behaviour.

Ailil is on a raised dais, with a female in raven gear and mask.  There is also a capering big-eared fool, which is strangely monkey-like.  The Red-Cap makes a parody of a bow and announces me but forgets my name.  I announce myself.  Ailil welcomes me and we talk.  I ask for the Red-Cap to bring my wife in and Ailil acts innocently (? hah!).

Then the bird-lady rises and puts her arm in Ailil's and whispers to him.  Ailil smiles <SHUDDER> and says he is not the one I should be asking; but at the same time, he also looked surprised and a little concerned.  Then it is my turn to be surprised … it was Morgaine who petrified my love.  She says that Jeanne tried to take her away - away from being a queen, from getting something she deserved and she wasn't being handed everything [?]  She had also been saying that Jeanne was jealous of her.  I say I came to the wedding.

Smiling mischievously, she asks if I will drink to her at her wedding.  I can only respond with agreement if I can share it with my wife (this I would do for her, no one else), while my more sensible companions vigorously refuse.  She once again asks will I not even drink to her, again I can only refuse.  She finally asks won't I even dance with her.  Her eyes are bright with unshed tears, but she still smiles her smile.  "For what I felt for you, I acquiesce."

We begin the dance and then the music joined us.  The rest of the court joined in the dancing, but it was shortly thereafter that the music changed.  We were caught in the music, which skirled around and around more and more frenetically.  Hywel had joined in the dancing, but Cynon managed to resist the lure.  Cynon knew we were caught and had to hit me on the back of my head twice to bring me out of it.  It had drawn me from the dance by appealing to one of my most basic instincts (fighting).

As we are bundled out (carefully as I had drawn my sword because I had been under attack), I am surprised to find we have been joined by a couple costumed as a wolf and a bear.  It is our enigmatic, occasional companions, _[[Lillane-of-Clan-Bahari|Lady Leda]]_ and _[[Tyler|Tyler]]_.  She is holding a 4' dude garbed as a misshapen sparrow.

Leda says we must leave now.  We go to Jeanne's statue.  With my sword still drawn and after Leda's calm, yet measuredly terrifying, polite inquiries of the _[[030-Arthurian-Britain#^abd763|Unseelie Sparrow]]_, the Sparrow thinks it is best to transform Jeanne back to her natural state.  Unfortunately, in his eagerness to do so as quickly as possible, he failed on his first few tries.  The Raven statue transmutes from stone to flesh and feathers, and she collapses to the ground.  Dropping my sword, I too fall .. to my knees and cradle her.  "Oh, Jeanne!"

But there is no time to tarry, for a loud scream comes from the far side of the gardens as something huge and dark and Fae this way cometh.  Uncertain of whether we could reach the exit we had entered this realm through, Arcavius says he knows of a way to go to another realm.  He gets us all to hold hands, and suddenly we find ourselves outside of the Lady's castle.  When we enter and are directed to the Lady, the Lady and Leda do the silent stare (which can mean anything, but always means trouble).

## With Lady Voklaynn

I beg of the Lady to please help my love.  She does.  I answer all her questions truthfully and without hesitation, distractedly even.  Answering about why Morgaine did what she did depresses me in true knightly style, and I am at an all-time low where someone could have put a knife in my hand and it would have done the rest by instinct.  But then Jeanne caws, and once again my knees are punished as I throw myself to her side on a stool by the fire.  She shimmers once again and returns to me as I know her best.  Huge, humongous hug.  As we are embraced for a while, I answer a few more questions until food and drinks arrive.  As everyone begins to eat, I begin (and finish just as fast) to pass out.  By the way, I don't recall anything after the start of the dance.

Late the next morning, I'm given something really foul-smelling and foul-tasting (it must be really good for me) and I feel a lot better.  I share a walk with Jeanne and I console her after she has had her first setback.  She wants to go home, directly.  Go looking for my fellow knights and ask them what happened in the time that I can't remember.  I'm surprised again about the arrivals of Leda and Tyler and again by their mysterious absence now.  I tell them we're going home.  I then go to find my gracious hostess.  After introductions are made between the Lady and my wife, and a strangely nice and quiet chat, she kindly sends Hywel, Jeanne and I back home the day after we arrived at Avebury.

Our fellow two knights are returned at the same time to Avebury with their squires.  Time passes.  Jeanne improves, but she always has the loss of her invincibility and the fact that Morgaine (her successor, her adopted daughter) had betrayed her.  Word is passed to Sarum of the situation and our prior mission to take the messages of the proposed alliance to the other big lords is passed to other respected messengers.
