**08/05/497** - Feast of Beltane

**15/05/497** - Hywel and I leave Raven's Gate for Upavon

**16/05/497** - Upavon to Mildenhall

**17/05/497** - Mildenhall to Donnington Castle - meet up with rest of party

## Silchester

**18/05/497** - That night try to find out about Silchester and Levcomagus.  Find out the Duke of Silchester is hard but fair.  Silchester is a Roman town fallen on harder times - about 20 knights and 200 men-at-arms.  Steward of Levcomagus has knights / men on roads who aren't letting allies of Salisbury through.

**19/05/497** - Travel to Silchester.  Walled city, yet pretty open.  The sentries are told we're mercenary knights and they send us to their Captain-of-the-Guard, _[[030-Arthurian-Britain#^e5cfac|Bellator]]_.  He assigns us a mission - peasants report a monster in a river back along the road 1/3 day and then north, so investigate and take care of it.  That night we go carousing to find out about Levcomagus and monster.  At least we got drunk.

## The Water Leaper

**20/05/497** - Northwest up road, then north alongside river.  Find three peasants, they say monster is half way back towards the road.  We try to find tracks (practically non-existent skill for me but I succeed hah, hah) and we find blood and squashed flat area alongside river.  Cynon spots the monster - its a dragon, a "Water-Leaper" with large fins on the sides.  Lands on bank and heads directly at me.  I swing at it and lop its head off in one blow.  Three peasants see this - one offers me everything they have - he doesn't have anything, so I think quickly and get him to cut the teeth out of the monster (one will be to show Bellator, the rest are for Jeanne).

Return to Silchester, report (badly) to Bellator, then go carousing.  I tell everybody at inn (successfully).  Brion gets to talking to someone who I notice has bite marks on his neck.  I hang around but eventually Brion and guy leave.  Worried for Brion, I trail after them, lose them and then when I look for barracks, I find them at gates.  They go through (let by the guards) and I say I'm with them.  See them go up a rise and over.  Follow and see someone with Brion in a natural amphitheatre.  He disappears in a flash.

## Saxons

**21/05/497** - Next mission.  Band of Saxons to west in Purdue Forest.  They had been effective and killed four knights and seven men-at-arms in the last month.  We get supplies and follow river (downstream Thames) find some burnt / sacked villages.  At freshly sacked one, we find tracks and follow them finding more dead villagers and then we reach river again and boat marks back into river.  I suggest we go downriver again.  That night we camp 100' away from river.  On Hywel's watch, wake up and hear Saxons nearby.  Wake up others, don armour and walk towards them quietly.

**22/05/497** - Very early morning (still dark).  Two Saxons for each of us.  In my fight, big Saxon lets smaller one fight me mono-a-mono (what does that mean?!)  I hit him, then he does a good hit on me and I fall, big Saxon holds little one back until I get up, then I cut haft of small ones axe, I let him pull his spear out, I hit him and knock him down, let him up again, I hit him again.  Cynon falls unconscious now, I kill mine, Brion calls on the Morrigan, fight big Saxon, he hits me twice and I fall unconscious.  Even without me, we barely win.

**23/05/497** - Morning.  Brion, _[[030-Arthurian-Britain#^fbe0d7|Nai]]_ and _[[010-The-Seasons#^05e962|Budd]]_ take horses back to Silchester.  Rest try to boat back.  Boat gets stuck for a while.

**24/05/497** - Arrive back at Silchester.  Brion arrives first and sends help to get rest of us.  End of day a Moor soldier / chirurgeon arrives to help us.  Cynon dislikes him (very suspicious).

**25/05/497** - We was chirurged.

**26/05/497** - We heals.  I become conscious.

**01/06/497** - Guard found missing baker's daughter dead.  Throat slashed and another body (decayed beyond recognition).  I send Nai to request the Moor to come earlier.  I ask him to help me and examine the body.  After discussion amongst party, we decide to aid if its a supernatural death.  I am already convinced and am vindicated.  We will do it.  The truth is out there.

**02/06/497** - We heal.  We try and get info out of Brion about his "friend".  We plan to go to amphitheatre but Brion gets the Moor to drug me to prevent me going.  They go and get info.

**03/06/497** - Duke's party (who had been attacked in a northern county (?)) arrives in Silchester.  Cynon learns of non-support provided by Levcomagus and Lord of St Albans.

## Goblins, Ghouls and Fomori

**04/06/497** - I awake.  I am briefed of situation.  I send Brion, Hywel and Cynon back to Brion's friend to find out more info on the "powerful interloper".  I go to talk to the Moor and get info of what can kill the creatures.  He also tells of an entrance in Captain's office in Basilica.  In afternoon, go through and find Mithras temple.  Through altar, down circular stairway - tunnel, junction, big cavern.  Goblin things throw rocks.  Brion rushes out and chops one down and its blood burns through sword.  Junction come across ghoul - fight.  Further to go but we call it a day.

**05/06/497** - First thing go to Moor and order some naphtha (can provide some in four to five days).  Then get appointment to see Duke to ask for aid vs. creatures.  Get armour.

**06/06/497** - Get naphtha from Moor and head down tunnels again.  Just go on left tunnel.  Seven goblins get in our way and we chase them.  We kill a few, but then let survivors run past.  About 15 ghouls in cavern.  Burn a few with naphtha, chase survivors to end of cavern to mouth of another tunnel.  Two ghouls are in tunnel are acting intelligently (huh?).  They burn.  Further on, a fissure breaks tunnel in two, we go through small gap and little further on tunnel collapses behind us.  Another pair of flesh-eaters back-pedal as fast as we advance at them with torches.  Turn-off to left, breeze coming from it, so we head down passage.  Most torches blown out.  Path alongside underground river.  Fomori giant we find, after conversation I think he is flesh-eater's master.  We begin to fight.  Long tussle between Brion, Cynon and me v Fomori Giant.  Major wounds result to all but Cynon.  Giant dead.  Four men-at-arms and one knight sent by Duke casualties.

**07/06/497** - Duke has offered some gifts of land to us.  We talk in coming week about what to do.

Time passes healing.

**21/06/497** - Summer Solstice

## A Black Bull, Four Lions and a White Hart

**24/06/497** - Go hunting.  Separate into groups.  Bellator leads myself and two other knights and our squires.  We find nothing and camp overnight.  A black bull visits camp at night.  I try and find out its a Mithras sign, but no response.  I scare it away from camp with a brand (torch, you idiots).

**25/06/497** - Hunt back towards Silchester.  I hear growling and lead rest of party and find four lions guarding a White Hart.  After seeing Hart, I let them head off.  We go back to town and I'm told that the Hart was the forest's guardian, a good omen.

Time passes healing.

## Steward of Levcomagus

**04/08/497** - Head off to Levcomagus.  Hard ride.  Half way we see smoke to the south, a village sacked by Saxons.  Survivors stolen by boat.  We continue to Levcomagus.  Tell guards we're here to see Steward and we're from Duke (to know reason why Steward didn't give aid to Duke).  Speak to Steward.  Brion speaks with him and Steward says messengers didn't arrive.  Then Brion tells of Giant's death.  Isolde and her older sister are there.

**05/08/497** - Go out hunting.  Head south / southeast and find a broach by the river.  Later we remember seeing something similar on Isolde.  She tells us she has a twin sister who is missing.

**06/08/497** - Breakfast.  Hywel found out about a screaming lunatic in dungeon.  Isolde wants to go with us but Hywel tells her she's not going with us to rescue her twin sister.  We then gather supplies and saddle up and ride away.  We tried to head back to where we found the broach, lose track and enter woods and then river further south.  Follow river and head into a Y-junction.  We head back to where we saw a ford across but on way through forest we hear Saxon voices.

## More Saxons

Us four knights charge in and attack a Saxon knight with three followers arguing with a Saxon knight with four followers.  Cynon reaches them first and we knock them down.  Hywel attacks three followers and Brion attacks four followers and he takes a blow and Hywel gets knocked down unconscious.  I make a quick review of battle, Brion's horse has bolted and his three surviving opponents are following him (one was scared off) and three Saxon foot-soldiers stand over Hywel.  My knight is down (and getting up in great pain).  I go rescue Hywel.  I'm knocked down by one of them .... BATTLE.

After, search for Brion find where he left his horse and then a chopped up Saxon and then we get lost.  After a couple of hours searching, we get back.  After seeing dead Saxon, I've got no worries about Brion taking care of himself, and he'll get back to the battle-site soon.  Owain has gone through Saxons and has found some goods and a ring (similar to the brooch).  Cynon can't find their tracks.  We camp.

That night, Owain's first watch.  _[[030-Arthurian-Britain#^9d81c7|The Captain]]_ comes and asks what we're doing, and I say we're looking for a kidnapped child.  He offers a deal.  He'll help for a fair fight with Cynon.  These words strike me to my heart and I agree.  Two other Fae take Hywel, saying he will be returned at dawn.  Captain explains Steward is a friend of Saxon King _[[010-The-Seasons#^9a36d4|Sissa's]]_ son, and that the Steward had given the son a bride (a dead Celtic knight's daughter).

**07/08/497** - By dawn, Brion hasn't returned.  I speak to Captain and he says Brion has been taken by his goddess.  After much regret, we continue on to rescue the damsel.  We ride south.  Ask for aid for Cynon and Captain mentions that it was done at the request of a lady Fae who came last night.  I leave it at that and say that my words were honourably earned and will be honourably borne.

We meet with two knights (what a coincidence) named _[[020-The-Antediluvians#Gwyn|Sir Gwyn]]_ and _[[010-Knights-of-Arthurian-Britain#Sir Arcavius|Sir Arcavius]]_.  We travel onwards and come across one of Sissa's villages after half day.  We go around open area to get behind village.  The PLAN - squires go and fire long boats while Captain throws me over back gate and other knights help Gwyn over.  The REALITY - I FLY over wall and into a thatched hovel further on.  We fight (me afoot and rest ahorse).  We win while Saxon women and kids flee.

In one hovel, I search and find a brush of Celtic origin with some red hair (same colour as Isolde).  Cynon sets fire to village (Vandal!).  Gwyn and Arcavius first aid some Saxons.  We question a broken Cymric Saxon (? huh ?) and he says they went southeast.  To avoid retaliation, we head northeast and camp in dark.

**08/08/497** - Head southeast.  Cross river and travel more in forest.  Find tracks of a large party.  We follow.  Head onto plain and see a two Saxon rear-guard.  Attack and kill them.  Further on we see main enemy group camping.  We head out of sight and eventually three Saxons are sent back and we waylay them.  Cynon is at deaths door and I first-aid Arcavius (and fumble).  Deal done with Captain and they are taken away.

Night falls.  We head directly south as more Saxons expected from the southeast.  We then go around them to their camp and we see one Saxon guarding girl and two others arguing with Sissa.  I get Captain to go get girl and kill the one Saxon.  Hywel, Gwyn, and me charge at others.  I charge at Sissa and two mighty blows bring him low.  We then kill the rest and throw Sissa over a horse and we ride off.

We're chased and being overtaken.  Girl had been on Gwyn's horse and he'd slowed.  I take girl but we're still slowing so cuts rope to the now dead deadweight Sissa.  We seem to lose them but they catch up as they are inspired to avenge their leader.  I make deal with Captain to protect girl till we get her back.  In fight, Gwyn knocked down.  We heal up and then find Captain and girl are gone.  Camp.

**09/08/497** - Camp.

## A Fae Banquet

**10/08/497** - Camp.  That night, Puck comes and asks why aren't we going to the banquet.  I get Puck to take all of us to banquet, Captain and our companions.  I get squires to break camp and we "ride".  A flash and we're in a tunnel with a huge black mastiff (horse-sized).  Puck is now elf-sized and he pats the "dog" (Cerberus, I realize) and says we can pass.  I ride towards tunnel while dog growls and stands in way.  Very frightening but I keep riding and don't stop.  Just before I ride into him, he steps aside and he lets the rest past but gives a huge bark at Budd who is scared off his horse.

Then flash and we're in a castle hallway, our horses gone and Gwyn is gone as well.  Puck shows us to a room with _Arcavius_ and Cynon.  We chat and catch up what's happened.  At Cynon's request, Puck opens windows and shows horses in stables.  I ask about learning Fae music.  Hywel is led off and Puck leads Cynon and _Arcavius_ to stables and a page leads me to the hall.  Music at far-end.  Banquet being prepared.  A few Fae greet me and one lady offers me a drink, and thinking I will not be able to leave till after partaking of the banquet and she says "to your health" so I drink.  Get to harpist and he's playing (Jeanne mentioned three types of Fae music - Lament, Laughter and Slumber) a long non-ending story.

STORY - Milesians robbed the Fae of four great treasures (reference - _[[Oberon|Oberon]]_ lost his hand).  Refers to original Fae home in Land-In-The-West.  Story continues of all heroes who failed to retrieve the treasures.  There are four houses with the great treasures, Falaius (Stone of Destiny), Barius (Sword), Feneus (Spear), Marius (Cauldron).  Races in the land have been Fomorians, Parthelon (plague), band of Nimed (mysterious death), Semion (ancestors of Firbolgs), Fae, Milesians (humans).

We're seated to left of _[[Titania|Titania]]_ and the Captain at the banquet.  Hywel introduces us to _[[030-Arthurian-Britain#^d47b60|Lady Etleyenne]]_ when she and I ask for introductions.  Arcavius wants us to go.  I go to Oberon and thank him for the banquet, but say we must leave and do tasks and he requests we search for the great treasures.  I say if we find them on our travels we will return them.

## Report to Earl Robert

**08/08/497** - We leave and arrive in a forest.  I work out we're near Amesbury.  Ride in and ask what's the date and monk replies it's 8 Aug 497.  We ride to Sarum and arrive 1½ hours after dusk.  Wake the Earl and inform him of evidence of Sissa / Levcomagus deal and Sissa's death and tell him he will die before dawn and I notice that Earl thinks I'm crazy when I explain the Fae aided us.

**09/08/497** - Arcavius arrives (as Gwyn stays at Amesbury).  I send message to Jeanne.  Explain well (made my roll) full story to Earl Robert making sure servants don't hear.  As Earl makes plans to attack, I suggest we don't have to fight, as many of Steward's men wouldn't support a traitor.  Cynon says that the Duke might deal with his vassal without resorting to war and giving opportunity to Saxons.

**10/08/497** - We leave for Silchester via Mildenhall.  I will head for Silchester via Longcott, while rest go direct.  Reach Upavon.

**11/08/497** - Mildenhall / Marlborough.  Talk with (stir-crazy) Edar and Gilbert.  Learn not to be polite with Aneurin.

**12/08/497** - Edar and I ride to Longcott.  Met in yard by Lady Anne.  News passed to her and she asks us to leave.  We do.

**13/08/497** - Reach Donnington.

**14/08/497** - Reach Silchester day after others.  Find out what plan is and I suggest a few things.  The revised plan is we go to Levcomagus, not bringing Isolde but bringing someone high in Duke's graces (Capt. Bellator), denounce the Steward and he must go to Silchester to answer charges, or that truth / right will be decided by combat if necessary.  We will bring a writ from the Duke.  In audience with Duke, he agrees to the above and grants us 15 more knights and 40 men-at-arms.

**15/08/497** - We head down road to Levcomagus.  We find tracks of a party of six Saxons have crossed the road (raiding party).  That night a "mist" creeps into camp.  I'm awakened by Edar and I feel its wrong.  I order everyone to be woken and all those who had been asleep woke chilled and with frozen joints.  I get torches from our fire organized around us and after talking, decide to get everyone heading back northeast along road.  We eventually pass out of mist and camp again.

## A New Steward of Levcomagus

**16/08/497** - Levcomagus.  On way find small party of dead Saxons at their camp ... the mist!  Reach Levcomagus and are stopped on road by Steward's knights.  They question us and after I strenuously tell them we are here to bring the Steward back to Silchester to answer charges of treason, they tag onto end of train (except couple who race back to Levcomagus).  Reach town and gates are shut.  Shouting match.  I maintain his failure to go with us is admitting guilt.  Eventually he arrives at gates and agrees to come.  He delays as long as possible and brings his 2IC and small party.

We camp on way back to Silchester.  I set a bonfire in middle of it and a few unlit ones around camp.  Again mist comes and everyone is woken and we ride on calmly and camp past mist again.  Before dawn, Merffyn and wolf come.  Welcome him and find out he is cause of mist use against Saxons and he tells us Brion is definitely dead.  We drink to his memory.

**17/08/497** - Before camp breaks, lots of questioning and discussions (amongst which the 2IC is told we rescued Isolde).  2IC attacks Steward, I step with drawn sword between duelling pair as I have an obligation to deliver the Steward to the Duke and I don't know if fight is for honourable reasons.  2IC is raging against Steward, so I fend him away but Steward swings at my back so Cynon steps in to stop him and we step out of the fight.  The two continue until 2IC kills Steward.  2IC is Isolde's closest remaining male family.

We take Steward's armour, etc to sell to provide for Isolde and sisters.  We reach Silchester and pass word to Duke.  He states he needs a temporary Steward of Levcomagus until the next year, looking at me and Cynon.  I wait a little while and Cynon accepts.  I cannot stay away from my home, my wife and my progeny for the whole winter.  Also there are matters to consider.

**18/08/497** - As Cynon heads with his party to Levcomagus, I go with Edar to Donnington.

**19/08/497** - head to Longcott to tell Lady Anne.  Merffyn brings word he is definitely dead, but she still wants / needs the body.  She also advises that I am no longer welcome.  Mildenhall.  Aneurin starts to complain to me I'm late, but Edar becomes forceful (?!?) and shuts her up.

**20/08/497** - Upavon

**21/08/497** - Sarum and give debrief to Earl Robert and then home to Raven's Gate.
