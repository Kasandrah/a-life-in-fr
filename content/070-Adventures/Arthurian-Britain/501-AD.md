# Rhodri's Tales - 501 AD

**Written by [[Ben-G|Ben G.]] (Rhodri)**

501 - Arthur is 9, Caradoc is 5, Nia is 2 and the twins (Gwen and Gwyn) are 1.  My sister, Astrigis, has gone missing.  She had married a Cymric merchant of Hereford in Gloucester.  She was lost in the Dean's Forest, the Forest which surrounds Hereford all the way into the Cambrian mountains.

This season's campaign began but a few days after our celebration of Beltane.  This year was strangely less frenzied, but more moving.  Then again, maybe the effects of last year were still being felt.

## A Promise to Oberon

Late at night, closer to dawn than to sunset, coming out of a dream.  Only recall some harp music.  Bright moonlight comes in through the window.  A shimmer and _[[Oberon|Oberon]]_ is there.  He is very agitated.  I fly and convince him to talk with me elsewhere but he wants to talk now and Jeanne will not wake up.  The fact that she is under his control upsets me, almost as much as when he starts talking about how would I feel if my daughter went missing?  Is he threatening my family?  He is upset at my not speaking to him about Morgaine's abduction.  He also continues about how the issue of Morgaine and Ailil will upset the balance between the Seelie and the Unseelie.

Foolishly, I buckle and promise to try and get the Stone of Destiny from the Fomori to restore the balance before the feast of Samhain.  We go downstairs to drink on this.  A guard sees me and Oberon and races off faster than he's ever moved before to get the wine I requested.  I try small talk for a very small time, before the fact that I want him gone seems to get through to him and he leaves.

First thing the next morn, I explain to Jeanne what happened during the night.  I say it's my problem, but Jeanne (bless her heart) refuses to accept that and states its our problem.  She tries to help me come up with a plan but it only points out how little I know about the Saxons and Fomori.  She does suggest that it is possible that the Fomori have someone who is linked to them like I am linked to the Fae.  Finding out from him as much as we can, a small dedicated, brave party may enter the Fomori Halls / Caves and achieve the impossible.

## Tamlyn's Village

Travel to Dorsetshire for Arcavius.  He is eager to leave his new family (17 yr old wife Margot, 20 yr old lady-in-waiting named Clarisse, and 2 young babies) although he shows misgivings about our adventure.  Go to Levcomagus.  Try to convince a doubting Sir Cynon to join our adventure, but as I'm trying (and not looking too convincing), a blond-haired man named _[[010-The-Seasons#^b842ab|Tamlyn]]_ enters, carrying a dead boy.  His village has been attacked by a band of 12 Saxons with 2 giants (Fomori!)  Cynon talks with him and says he will help.  He turns to me and agrees to aid me in finding information about the Fomori in return for my aid against the Saxon incursion.

The village we seek is one of the few that had been refusing to join the Saxons, and had held their own since they were first questioned.  That is, until the Fomori had come to change things.  We travel for a couple of days and are a fair distance into Saxon territory when we finally reach Tamlyn's village.  It is palisaded, and appears to be being fortified further.  Arcavius and Cynon note that there is one who is garbed in very strange, silvery armour (breastplate over chain mail (! What is this?)  The helmet has a horse head hanging over the front and a tail-like plume coming out the back.  We get Tamlyn to go ahead and announce that we're coming in.

>**GM's Note:** This is the point in time at which I brought Niani and Brennos [[The-Summer-Country#Pnessamo Boklaro Niani|across]] from [[100-Anfar-Refinli|Anfar Refinli]]. Niani's player joined after I had run this campaign for six in-game seasons (years). All the other characters **already had young children**. An important point I failed to consider. A point which came back to bite me so hard that it [[Out-of-Place#Campaign Issues|ran my campaign into the ground]].    

Inside, we meet a 5' 8" woman with strangely slanted emerald eyes, armoured in silvery, ornate armour which is almost like mine.  She is an extremely attractive redhead (but not the one from the warning dream of Hywel) and has an orange crystal battle-axe (?!)  She is named _[[010-Knights-of-Arthurian-Britain#Lady Niani|Lady Niani]]_ and she is accompanied by the knight in silvery armour. He is named _[[Sir-Brennos|Sir Brennos]]_. ^9d5d88

We've come to help (it is announced), but it is apparent Sir Cynon wishes to claim overlordship of this village in return for his protection.  We need to find out when they were last attacked, and find out not since the original attack.  When we question have they found out where the Saxons are located in the forest, we find out the villagers have not left their village for they could barely defend it, let alone go out to meet the Saxons out in the open.

We arrange for Sir Hywel, his squire Madog, and Tamlyn to scout out the Saxons.  In the meantime, Niani shows us around the defences they had been preparing.  For some reason, I find I can't focus on what she is saying or showing us, I find my thoughts going back to Oberon, my family and what I have promised to do.  Cries are heard from beyond the palisade; it is our scouts!  They are running back to the village as fast as they can, for the entire Saxon force is chasing them.  The Terrific Trio scatter to our battle mounts for the coming battle, meanwhile Brennos and Niani are running straight towards the Saxons on foot (I thought they had horses?)

## Battling Saxons and Fomori

They arrive just as we charge into the battle.  Cynon and I have put ourselves against the two Fomori, and Arcavius is focused on the Saxon chieftain, the rest are to be handled by the others.  Arcavius has knocked his opponent down, and has got off his horse to fight him on the ground.  Cocky pup!  Blows are traded back and forth throughout the melee with no end until Niani has taken a heavy hit and is knocked down.  Then Sir Brennos goes verily berserk and strides lustily towards Niani.  With every stride he swings his big sword and slays a Saxon, by the time he has reached his lady, the rest of the Saxons are either dead or fled.  He lifts Niani, and then strides lustily to the village, with nary a break of ... stride.

Arcavius slays the chieftain, and goes to help Cynon, as he has already been hit by the giant.  As he is distracting the giant for Cynon to kill it, I am hit by my opponent.  Cynon slays his giant by finally (!) hitting it, and then moves to help me.  Arcavius races to help Niani at the village.  Cynon returns back to missing the giants, while I continue to make small hits, which are destroying my swords (yes, plural).  Finally, I slay it before Cynon can manage more than one successful strike against these creatures in the entire melee.  My third sword has become but a charred stump inches from the hilt.  Damn these things!

We talk to the headman and work out how everyone got here.  Niani and Brennos arrived when the village priest of Raven prayed really hard for help, while they learnt why we were here.  Cynon lays it on the line and says he came to give his protection in return for their allegiance.  Niani objects and then Cynon overtly threatens the headman.  Niani stood for the village to stay independent and the village headman was happy to do so until he found out that Niani was already making plans to leave.

Arguments broke up between the headman and the priest.  The priest then tries emotional blackmail with Niani but she is adamant she has a mission to fulfil, a mission to create an alliance between her race and another race.  And then she starts on the fact that her agreement with the Raven was based on the premise she would be returned to where she was with no time passing (and she agreed.)

Meanwhile the headman apologises to Cynon and asks whether Cynon would ignore what has been said.  Cynon then asks for his fealty and then a party strikes.  After a while, the priest returns with a raven on his shoulder and he looks smug (just like Jeanne has looked when she has achieved a great "connect-call" with the Morrigan).  He is dressed in a cloak with raven feathers around the shoulders (just like Jeanne's).  He is the only male priest of the Morrigan I have seen.

He goes to Niani and Brennos and tells them the danger to the village has not passed; it will not pass until the Fomori are driven from the land.  Niani tries to make some points. That was not what she agreed to, but he responds the Morrigan says that it was.  Probably realising where the power lies within this agreement, Niani agrees to think on it.  The village headman broaches with Cynon the village's defence, and he is told what he wants to hear ... forces will be sent to reside at the village to protect it.

Off to the side, I notice Niani is talking to Brennos when he does something strange.  He pulls out his sword and "tunes out" for several minutes.  I believe he is communing with his goddess, and just found out that the Fomori eat horses, don't they?  For his attitude of fighting the Fomori is markedly different from how it was (i.e. he's for it now).

That night, we knights hold watches in case of another attack.  During Cynon's watch, he sees 2 pearly swans flying.  After dawn, he is telling me and Hywel about it when Hywel points out that swans don't fly at night.  It is a sign of the Fae's presence and the watchful gaze of Oberon.  Cynon is interested in investigating what is around here, when I suggest we trail the Saxon's path back to their camp to see if there are any more there.  We find out the village has an iron pipe they use to warn of attacks, and then we're led away by Tamlyn and Hywel.

Eventually, we smell the camp ahead of us.  Lots of dead horses.  The ashes of the campfire are still warm.  The camp had been occupied until recently.  Arcavius, Hywel, and Tamlyn find the trail of where they've gone.  Arcavius leads as he is finding the trail; I'm next then Cynon then the others.  On the trail, Arcavius senses something and ducks just as a log suspended on ropes swings in.

Unfortunately, he has ducked into it, but his strength and horsemanship are enough to stop most of the damage.  As I am chopping the log down, the Fomori shout out and charge.  The accompanying Saxons reach us first.  Arcavius and I are at the front, on this trail, and the others go further off the trail to fight.  Cynon tries to hold off the Fomori, but is hit nigh unto death and flung away in a single blow.  The rest of us are slaying the Saxons (I'm hit off my horse by a crafty Saxon blow) and then there seems a gathering of static, as a crackling thunder blinds us.  Smoking holes have appeared in the chest of the Fomori... it's Merffyn!  Just wandering around and happened to be there... so he says.

Arcavius has first-aided Cynon, but he doesn't look much better.  Merffyn tries to help him, but he says it's beyond him … and then suggests someone else's help is needed.  Heading to Arcavius, I guiltily ask him for a boon for our friend Cynon.  Whereupon, he also feels guilty as we remember that we were supposed to visit the Lady and Anwyn with our families in a time of peace and rest, not war and need.  I could use the excuse of concussion, but that doesn't cut it these days, does it?!

Now that the most urgent matter is in hand, it is time to introduce Merffyn to our new companions and vice versa.  Brennos and Niani seem unperturbed at meeting the great mage, while Merffyn seems a bit bemused, as well as amused.  As we prepare to travel to the Lady's realm, Merffyn makes a pointed suggestion for us to check the bodies of the fallen.  How morbid!  But when Arcavius does (almost mercenary, really), he finds one of the Fomori bodies had an elaborate necklace.  A large orange gemstone on a lengthy gold chain.

While most of us are concerning ourselves with the necklace and it's implications, Merffyn has taken himself over to Niani's horse and seems to be talking to it. (?!)  What amazed me further is when Niani went over to the pair and made introductions (?!)  Other than seeing them all talk, no one hears what is said.

## Rest and Healing

We all gather in a group and touch each other, as Arcavius activates the Lady's medallion.  And so we are transported once again to the Lady's realm.  But instead of appearing in front of the lower entrance to the castle, we appear beside the pond of the Fish ... The Wise One.  Since we are here, we might as well as the questions that are uppermost on our minds.  Arcavius wisely instructs his squire, Gessius, to go to the castle to alert them of our imminent arrival.  Meanwhile Arcavius throws the Fish ... The Wise One a gratis acorn.

Niani had not appeared surprised at what was taking place.  In fact, it was she who asked the first question.  She asked if the Fish ... The Wise One was the same one that she knows.  The vision that is shown is of this clearing with the pond in the middle.  Hmmm, very illuminating.

Next, I ask about where I can get a sword that won't melt when I hit a Fomori, as I was mightily sick of having to bring many replacements when I went adventuring.  As I ask the question, Arcavius is nodding like his neck had broken.  The vision that is shown is that of a smith at a forge.  Sir Brennos said what I already knew, that the smith was _[[010-The-Seasons#^08815a|Goibniu]]_.

Next, Niani has a question of how she is to get back home, and she is shown a vision of the Lady's castle (She lives with the Lady?)

Next, Arcavius prompts Sir Brennos to ask about a question he had raised earlier in the day - what happens to his sword when he hits one of the giants?  The resultant vision is of him striking Fomori and damaging them, and his sword does not melt away like a normal sword, but it is not as spectacularly effective as Cynon's is.  Next, Arcavius then asks how we get to Goibniu, and we are shown the procedure that Cynon went through, something he hadn't told us about, at the White Horse.

Arcavius mind is bubbling away with more and more questions, but he manages to restrain himself enough to suggest we move on to the castle to take care of our badly wounded friend.

We get to the castle lower entrance, which Niani claps her hands delightedly as she recognises the entrance.  When we encounter Anwyn in the halls of the castle, after being allowed in by the servants, he and Niani show that they know each other.  He appears slightly bemused until it is pointed out that Merffyn is amongst us.  From that point, he assumes a graceful, cool mien.  We request that Cynon be seen to first, and then we can talk in peace.  Since this obviously followed his beliefs of how a host should act, we soon got to meet the Lady and the young _[[Karwin|Karwin]]_.  Again, Niani is recognised, and recognises them.

I confess what we're doing here (a lot about the promise which started this year's adventure) and Merffyn points out some things which make me realise I've been conned.  He points out that Oberon could have obtained the Stone himself, if he really wanted it.  Merffyn also confirms our suspicions that the Fomori medallion that was found on the last dead Fomori was a signifier of rank.  He then says that the Fomori king will not be happy.  Having been in a similar situation before, I make the connection that we (well, Merffyn really) killed one of the Fomori King's sons.

Niani is naming the Lady, Voklaynn.  She is asking about how she can be returned which bemuses the Lady no end (for she does not know how to explain it that she exists in a number of different realms at the same time).  Niani also shows her great annoyance with the Morrigan and keeps saying She has reneged on the deal.

We then try and establish what to do next.  For I cannot continue to attempt to retrieve the Stone of Destiny from the Fomori.  If I did so, the resultant imbalance between the Fae and the Fomori would conclude with the Fae warring with the greatly weakened Fomori and destroying the mortal realm, as we know it.  We need to find out about the Saxons.  We need to get someone who can speak with the Saxons.  We talk around to the fact that we would need a female Saxon slave, who (folklore says) would be grateful to be away from beer-swilling, boating, Saxon beasts.  We also talk of whether the Fomori know that the Saxons don't have the Great Item that they had promised the Fomori.

As Cynon is recovering (but still bed-bound), I (only badly wounded) chat with him and we talk of how I need to have a conflicting promise with my liege to give me an out with Oberon if we could get something on / over / for / about him.  Arcavius and Hywel go back to the Fish ... The Wise One.  Arcavius asks what does Goibniu require for such a sword as we require.  They are shown visions of a blood sacrifice, offering our own blood, or the offering of whatever we value most highly.  Hywel asked something, and they see a vision of the red-haired woman we have been trying to keep him from.

Upon seeing this, Arcavius tells him we saw her in a vision when Arcavius asked what we each needed to avoid to get to our greatest desire (not the truth).  Then Arcavius asks some questions concerning me, like what would happen if I avoided the promise honourably and then if not honourably and then who would be hurt for both the previous differences.  I don't find out the answers until later.

Meanwhile, back at the castle, Niani starts a game with various occupants of the castle involving some ball.  How undignified.  Arcavius speaks to Merffyn about the visions (I think).  Arcavius then breaks the news to me of the visions.  If I chose not to comply with the promise honourably, I am most likely to kill myself.  If I chose not to comply with the promise dishonourably, I am most likely to be killed.  The resultant people who would be hurt are a vast panoply of people, ranging from Jeanne, Arthur, my family, my friends, etc, etc, in both situations.  So my options appear to be to kill myself, to fail in my mission and die, pretend to have died and hide myself away from the Fae, succeed in the mission and get the Stone for Oberon and also steal Gorias away from Oberon without him knowing it was me, or apologise.

We spend 4½ weeks waiting for Cynon to heal.  I needed only 2½ weeks to heal, and so began to fret.  By the third week of our relaxing stay, a celebration arose for the Red Dragon of Cymru (Ooh I wonder who that was?)  The next day after the celebration, I felt I needed to visit the Fish ... The Wise One, and Arcavius and Hywel accompany me.  I asked if there was something I could give to Oberon which would avoid all likely ill-will and was shown an image of Niani, who was very beautiful, as I have said before.

Of course.  Oberon has shown a great penchant for very beautiful women (as Morgaine and Aneurin can attest to).  It was unthinkable to betray a companion.  Briefly in mind I thought of the red-haired Jute Queen, but I refuse to sink to the depths of being a procurer for the Fae!  Thankfully, Arcavius and Hywel had given me some privacy to ask my questions.  I ask how will Oberon react if I apologise now, and I'm shown a vision of him very angry - a dark area where his face would be, and ram horns coming from the forehead of his blue skin.  I then ask how he would react if I apologised at Samhain, and I'm shown a vision to wide lands still burning, or already turned to ash and the shell of Raven's Gate centred in the middle of the flames.

I then ask about my sister, Astrigis, who went missing during the autumn of last year.  I'm shown a vision of her being fascinated by a young Fae.  When I question as to where she is now whether with the Seelie or the Unseelie, I am shown the young Fae is now courting her in an idyllic scene.  Has this been something Oberon has arranged?!

Arcavius now asks about his missing grandmother from 2 years before, and he is shown an attack by brigands.  We return from the Fish ... The Wise One and tell the others of how Oberon will react if I apologise.  There is no choice - I must apologise as soon as possible.  Cynon makes a point that I need to apologise for failing Oberon in not being there for Morgaine when she needed it and failing in my duties as a foster father.  We need to talk on how do we solve the central issue of Morgaine being with Ailil, not of how do I achieve impossible side issues.  And we must keep it simple and not promise anything.

Speaking to Merffyn, he makes a suggestion that the whole party go with me and at our full strength.  So we must visit Goibniu before we face Oberon.  Merffyn also advises we take our two new companions.  I beg a boon of the Lady that she might pass on the news of my demise to my dear wife if I fail to return.  With a great sigh she says she would take care of my family if such a fate befell me.  In return, if I should survive then I would aid in questing for the Cauldron for the Lady.

## Goibniu's Forge

Niani and Brennos accompany Arcavius and I to Goibniu's Forge.  Before us is his forge and in the centre lies a huge bowl.  As we stare around wondering how to attract Goibniu here, Brennos sets his sword against the bowl and he prays.  Someone appears ... a red, short, stocky, hairy, muscly Goibniu! (a la Bob Hoskins).  He is gruff and blunt with us and wants plain speaking.  After a little bit of amazement of such a being has made me tongue-tied, I request that we have swords Sir Cynon was given.

As Arcavius and Hywel found out from the Fish … The Wise One, we bleed severely, and are very light-headed when we have filled the bowl.  Goibniu goes to his forge and starts banging away.  He returns with two swords that are like Brennos' sword, in that they will not be eaten away by the blood of the Fomori, and, again like Brennos' sword, will not damage the Fomori beyond what the blow is worth. We return to the Lady's realm.

After we rest some more, and by the time Cynon is ready, so are we.  We cannot travel directly to the Fae realm from here, so Cynon says he wishes to appear at Levcomagus as there things he needs to do.  We do so appear at Levcomagus.  Cynon sends 2 knights and a number of men-at-arms to protect his new village.  He then sends a messenger to Duke Ulfius telling him of what happened with the village.

Meanwhile, I get Arcavius to write a message (in Latin, of course) for Jeanne as he is also sending a messenger (a mercenary knight who he promises employment to) to Dorsetshire.  I request the aid of Brennos and Niani, while making sure I warn them of Oberon's interest in beautiful women (Brennos is offended).  They go off to talk to each other, but they return and agree to come.  They plan to hang together "like limpets"!

## An Apology

We go to the closest forest.  I walk away from the others while calling out to Puck.  Very shortly after, he arrives in his firefly-like form.  I break the news to him of my intentions without saying the actual words.  As Puck acquiesces, I arrange for us to leave the squires behind with the horses.  Puck leads us 5 knights and Niani through the woods into Fae Woods.  We see the day change from midday to dusk to night in minutes.

Varied Fae garbed in white and silver attire greet us.  Haunting harp music can be heard in the background.  As we approach a larger clearing than we have seen here, we enter into a crowded gathering, which is the Seelie Court of Oberon.  Oberon is talking with the Captain, but when he sees us he apparently has his total focus on Niani.  Niani's reaction is odd as she seems to be looking around behind her for something.  Maybe it was the fact that Oberon's hand is now resting firmly around the hilt of Gorias.

I start off well by getting to my knees before Oberon.  Room is made around us and I begin my apology.  I apologise for failing Oberon in not protecting his daughter (!???? What have I said?  I had been thinking to myself over and over ... just don't say daughter, say Morgaine, don't say daughter, don't say daughter) - everything goes very, very quiet.  I believe this is what they mean when they say deathly quiet.  After a little while of stunned silence, during which everyone, including the Captain has drawn back just further than the distance of a sword blade if it was in Oberon's hand.

Titania, who had been nearby but off to the side, now approached, to be by Oberon's side.  I come up with "I have failed to protect your foster daughter Morgaine", and suddenly there is sound again - like lots of sighs, murmurs and breathing (especially mine).  I continue with my apology for being incapable of fulfilling my promise to him as I am a mere mortal.  When Oberon raises the point that I retrieved Arthur and his family from Tintagel, Arcavius raises the point that the Fomori King was not sitting on Arthur.  I state that we could not fight our way through the Fomori.  Oberon retorts that we would not try that way, and my only reply is that we are not noted for our subtlety.  This is a response that draws some muffled snickers from the crowd.  I draw attention back to the basic, original problem.

Oberon commands the ball to continue and, without saying it, to ignore us on pain of something very Fae.  We fail to come up with a suggestion to separate Morgaine from Ailil without causing war between the Fae courts.  But then Cynon asks whether Ailil has a daughter.  Oberon gets a gleam in his eye and says yes.  Cynon, Hywel and I see this as a fair means of resolving the issue; that is, once Oberon points out that we don't understand the Fae, that the Fae can transfer between the courts and change their paradigm.

Arcavius, Brennos and Niani agree to help, but only if she agrees to come with us.  He tells us she resides in Londinium, and we would recognise her ... for we were surely intelligent enough for that.  After the discussion, I tell Oberon we all agree and he says he will not be petty.  He will fulfil his side of the bargain, even if I have reneged.  Feeling that this bemusement is as good as it is going to get, we take our leave of Oberon in this gala, and get going.

## King Arthur

Puck returns us to roughly where we had left our horses and squires.  But they are not here.  The place is very overgrown as if much time has passed.  Oberon has fulfilled his promise of crowning Arthur by sending us forward to when Arthur is King.

We travel north to the road between Sarum and Levcomagus, as Cynon contemplates the fact that he has probably been replaced by someone else as the Steward of Levcomagus.  When we reach the road, we are in time to see a group of four riders approach us.  It is a scene that is etched in my memory of a vision given to me by the Fish ... The Wise One.  It is an 18-year-old Arthur, a 15-year-old Caradoc, a hooded figure and someone I cannot recognise.  We're all shocked ... well, mostly me.

Red-haired Arthur is so self-assured and ... regal.  Calling me Uncle and telling me it was a momentous time.  The Saxons were on the move, and he was to be married to the daughter of Leodogrance (the red-haired King of Camelaird which is in the northeast part of Wales/Cumbria), someone who I had met once before. Oh bugger, it's Guinevere!  I had hoped to arrange a marriage with someone else, but Oberon stole that from me.  Well, Rhodri doesn't know what's going to happen with her.

But then the cloaked figure unhooded herself.  In seconds I threw myself at Jeanne, and the rest of the world disappeared.

>**GM's Note:** I advanced the external campaign nine years while the characters were in the Fae realm. This was so I could jump to Arthur becoming king without having to run another nine in-game seasons.

>What I didn't consider was the impact of this shortcut on Niani and Brennos. They didn't have any time for children, fostered or otherwise, before this time jump. For them, there was no next generation aging up outside the Fae realm for the subsequent phase of this campaign.
