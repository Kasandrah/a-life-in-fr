Winter.  Daughter born.  Sickly at first with the same illness that Morgaine had (although Morgaine suffered it worse).  Jeanne healed them, thankfully.  Arthur is 7, energetic and strong.  Takes after his father.  Shows all the chivalry traits in spades.

Spring.  Head to Cirencester - five days - through Sarum (finding no missions for us).  Pass through Campacorentin Forest to Tor Barrow Hill.  At Marlborough, Edar is ready to go.  His new son was born at the end of last summer, a tad early?  Edar remembers some story of this grassy mound, Tor Barrow Hill.  Several years ago, grave robbers dug into the hill and found a Guardian who beat them off.  We travel on to Cirencester.

I tried to orate and bribe for some shovellers to come with us back to the mound, but they all think I'm crazy.  Edar remembers from somewhere that we might camp and sleep on the faerie hill overnight and something might happen.  Boy, did he call that one!  The squires and horses camped off the hill, while all of us knights camped on the hill.

## Under Tor Barrow Hill

The next morn sees us rising and being shocked, for our squires camp is no longer there.  The road we travelled on is also gone.  The air is clear, and there are no tracks.  I try calling for Puck, but there is no response.  Hesitantly, I try calling to the Guardian, but that also draws no response.

![[da_karwin2.jpg]]
**Karwin**

However, along comes a small boy who I recognise from a few years back.  It's _[[Karwin|Karwin]]_.  He is of a very unique, Fae-like (in some ways) family.  The small diamond on his forehead was the key reminder.

He's there alone with a ball.  But he won't answer questions, but that he wants to play with us.  For some unknown reason, this seemed reasonable so we started half-heartedly playing soccer, still in armour.

Very, very shortly, we had to take off our armour to continue to play.  Unfortunately, we placed our armour on the mound.  After but a little while, when we next look, our armour has disappeared.

We get Karwin to stop and ask him where his father, Anwyn, and he leads us to him.  After a little chat, we leave Anwyn behind as we investigate the mound much, much more closely.  We find an opening at the base and enter.  We speak to the spirit who is the _[[030-Arthurian-Britain#^4f99d3|Guardian]]_.  Inside it was a bowl on the ground filled with some liquid in the earthen chamber.  We make a deal (I think) to get _[[Oberon|Oberon]]_ to bring Marius to Tor Barrow annually in return for providing him with something that will lead to another of the Great Fae Treasures.

The Guardian provides us all with new armour, but it is of different sorts of armour of various ages.  I receive Silvery Fae armour, just like the Captain's.  Arcavius receives a Roman Centurion's armour (with a great, red plume!).  Hywel received some odd red/gold armour of some strange material, and some strange workings under the arm joints / openings.  Edar received contemporary armour, much like mine had been, actually.  The Guardian also offers to return us to the Mortal Realm upon completion of this task concerning Marius.

As we are thanking the Guardian and are leaving, Edar trips and knocks the bowl over.  Bad mojo!  We go back to where Anwyn had been, but he's gone again.  As we stand around trying to work out where Anwyn has gone, when a big bear comes ambling by.  Thinking anything is worth a try, I call out to the bear which attracts its attention, and ask where our friend Anwyn might be.  We're all under whelmed when the bear directs us to where he is.  We meet up with Anwyn and ask his help to get us to see Oberon, so Anwyn takes us on some "pathways."

On one of the pathways, we encounter a bedraggled knight.  Hywel is getting a bit uptight as he recognizes this is the figure of the _[[030-Arthurian-Britain#^cae78d|Knight of the Red Feather]]_, who is doomed to never return to the Mortal Realm.  We have a mission to attend to so we must leave, but we promise to see what can be done for him.

## The Serpent of Many Colours

Our next encounter is with a large snake that is lying across the path ahead.  Anwyn says that it is the Serpent of Many Colours.  It will move away once it is given a good fight.  This is good enough for us, as we are happy to fight.  The fact that he says it also has a great hoard does not dissuade us.  Arcavius and I charge at it.  Arcavius gets there first and is struck a tremendous blow that flings him unconscious a long way away.  I reach it just as Hywel and Edar start running towards us.  I get knocked down as the others arrive.  Then Hywel and Edar are knocked down as I get up.  I go berserk, damage it critically.  After more rounds where it is us knights trading ineffectual blows with the Serpent, I am knocked down and then Hywel runs!!!!!!!!!!!!!  Finally, standing alone, Edar soon finds he is no longer standing.

## In the Hall of the Dragon Queen

We wake up in a cottage of sorts.  I feel better than I think we ought to be.  The white-haired lady-healer who had healed Aneurin is here.  She is feeding us and says we've already had something to drink.  She also questions why we would be so half-witted as to fight against the Unseelie Prince _[[030-Arthurian-Britain#^11871f|Ailil]]_.  As she leaves us, she makes the point that she is not Fae.

As more days pass, we heal much faster than we should be.  The young boy, Karwin, occasionally pops in and talks and plays until his mother calls him away.  When we can wander, we find that this cottage has many rooms of more splendour than the (apparently) simplistic, comfortable room we had been in.

One day, we find it is Karwin's birthday.  There are lots and lots of Fae children running around.  But there is no cake eating until the "Guests" arrive.  Lots of the female Fae are asking a mightily embarrassed Edar to sing, with little success.  But eventually, the special guest, Oberon, Titania and their court arrives (! What are they doing visiting this place?!  Who are our hosts?)  Then the cake is handed around.  Afterwards, games of hide and seek and treasure hunts are begun for the kids.

Oberon and Titania, with their Captain, do the rounds until they reach us mortals.  In the following discussion, we raise the issue of the "Guardian's" request / bargain for Marius.  Oberon reads the scroll that the Guardian had given us, and agrees to the deal.  Then he tells us to ask Angus ourselves.  Before we go, we request that Oberon provide us with a way back to Mortal Realm in our own due time.  Titania also tells us of the Unseelie Court being opposed to the recovery of the Great Fae Treasures.

Go to Angus where his playing is enchanting everyone.  When he is pausing in between some of his songs, I ask if we can talk to him and his lady.  The harp instantly changes into a woman.  We usher them to a more private locale before putting forward a request for the annual celebration of the return of Marius, which is to be held at Tor Barrow Hill.  When we put forward the honour associated with being specially requested as the crux of the bargain, Angus agrees.  Angus also says he owes Rhodri a favour for the return of his beloved.  Something like levelling a city, destroy a dragon, influence a King, etc.

Eventually the Court leaves, and things become more relaxed.  Arcavius and Edar retire.  Soon after, Hywel and I see the return of the kids, including Karwin the unicorn gallivanting around.  The Lady brings us drinks and requests for me to join her.  We go upstairs to a "sitting-room", which has a roaring hearth-fire and 4 chairs (with backrests!) gathered together.  Anwyn and Merffyn are already there, so when the Lady sits down, so do I.  We talk about what has happened to us and I am offered free answers to any questions I might have.

Question about the Knight of the Red Feather and what might free him, and am told to ask the Guardian to amend our travel request so it would apply to him.  Question about whether the Unseelie Court will bother us, and am told yes, in the Fae and Mortal Realms.  Question about the Balrogs, but am told there is no breakouts at the moment.  They have no leads for the Great Fae treasures.  Things peter out to a casual conversation, during which I find l am relaxing more and more until I must leave to sleep (or collapse).

The next morning, Edar awakens first as he can smell food.  So we are shortly left with only three of us here.  Arcavius then decides he wants to go for a walk in the forest, while Hywel and I decide to head to the kitchen at a much more leisurely pace.  Karwin arrives there just as we arrive.  He wants some more of his birthday cake, so I join him to keep him company.  Afterwards, I head back to the rooms to get the scroll and my sword, and I fail to find Arcavius there.  There is a servant tidying the room up and ask her whether our companion had returned while we were away, but she says he is still in the forest.

Being concerned, I get in my armour and garbed with my sword, I go out to follow Arcavius' trail.  I find from the tracks that a "small horse" had encountered Arcavius and something had happened, as there was much milling around.  I follow the trail quicker, as I find the "horse" tracks follow over Arcavius'.

## The Wise One

Find Arcavius standing by a pool underneath an oak tree and lots of acorns lying on the ground.  It looks like I've interrupted something.  He says he was directed here.  He turns back to the pool, reaches down to grab an acorn and throws it into the pool.  A big fish comes out from under a rocky overhang and eats the acorn and then settles down in the middle of the pool.  Arcavius asks it "show us the Stone of Destiny" and the pool shimmers to reveal the following vision: a block of stone is placed under a throne.  It's underground.  Seated on the throne is one of the Fomorians, but one even bigger than any we've seen.  It must be their king.  It seems to be startled by something, then it looks directly at us and the vision shimmers away and the _[[030-Arthurian-Britain#^fc6c1c|Salmon]]_ darts under its rock and doesn't come back for a long while.

Arcavius tells me that he has already asked some questions of the Salmon.  For instance, the one thing that Rhodri must prevent to achieve his ultimate desire (which I know can only be to have an effective, long-lived king Arthur) and he was shown a vision of the night before a battle in a camp on a hill.  The standard before a large tent showed 3 gold crowns on a blue background (Arthur's?!).  Some knights are guarding the tent when a woman speaks to one of them and that knight uses a knife to cut a hole in the back of the tent to let her into the ten - **MORDRED'S CONCEPTION**.

The key to Cynon's desire is a vision of a big horse.  This horse is as different from Cynon's current horse, as is Cynon's current horse is from a Celtic pony.  It is a bit threatening, but there has never been finer horse-flesh.

The key to Arcavius' desire is a vision of an elder Arthur, maybe in his late 20s, leading a battle against the Saxons.

The key to Edar's desire is a vision of wedded bliss and many children.

The key to Hywel's desire was a little more complex.  His true match would be a red-haired woman, but such a match would be very bad, but his best match appears to be someone we know = Lady Etleyenne.

## The Red Dragon of Cymry

The Salmon has not made another appearance, so we head back to the castle, but we get lost.  Arcavius climbs a tree to see what is around.  He sees a castle set into a mountain, and a dragon on the castle.  He falls out of the tree, I try and catch him.  I do and we fall down.  We head towards the castle.  While we are taking a breather, Anwyn and the Lady Voklaynn find us.  They lead us directly to the castle, but we enter a cutting, which goes deeper and deeper into the ground, and the walls rise and rise until they become the ceiling of an underground tunnel.

We are shown the statues and they are pointed out as stone guardians.  We reach stairs leading up to a rounded entry room with a largish dragon-shaped fountain in the centre.  Then to where the rest of the party was.  We're in the biggest castle I'd ever seen and it was built into a mountain = magnificent!  After such an aggravating day of walking in armour, Arcavius and I are tired again.  Before going to bed, we ask about the scroll and showing it to Merffyn.  He (?) doesn't believe we should show it, it wouldn't be honourable and he convinces me.  Go to bed.  When wake up, take the sword to Merffyn.

Speak to Merffyn and annoy him.  He tells me some of the Fae past, points of mortals have survived with the balance the way it is.  I leave sword for him to check.  Go to get scroll.  Talk to Arcavius and he lets me take the scroll, but he holds different views.  Take scroll to Merffyn.  He tells me my father's sword is only a very fine sword.  Show scroll.  He takes it to an empty room, lays it down while weighing down its ends.

Does something and a scene / window opens and see a fast moving trip to the north.  Flying to a mountain and into the mountain - **BEN NEVIS**.  Merffyn says that it is like Tor Barrow Hill in being in both realms.  He says that Oberon will send someone for the Cauldron.  They should be stopped or the Cauldron would be moved.  I say that I shouldn't know that and Merffyn says that he won't tell me.  I return the scroll and get breakfast.

Time passes.  Next morn, kids and young at heart can treasure hunt.  Karwin and the young kids are loud and racing around.  It looks like lots of fun, like how Arthur, Caradoc and Morgaine are.  I watch, seeing Anwyn hovering near Karwin.  I'm in the garden, playing my harp and see a slip of paper under a rock.  I notice that no-one is even coming near to it, so still playing, I wander over to it and stop playing and pick it up.  Somehow, all the kids are around me jostling me and most importantly the harp.  I throw the slip of paper clear in an effort to be freed.  The kids rush to it and the ones who grab it, tear it up between themselves and the small crowd quietens.  Arcavius and I put it back together again, and Arcavius reads the Latin script (which the note had suddenly changed into) and it says "behind Red Eye".  The kids keep asking where that is.  Arcavius suggests the dragon in the fountain.

They go there and the teen in charge.  We follow.  Arcavius suggests this isn't it.  Kids suggest the gargoyles and the older kid thinks of the tapestry in the study.  I feel generous and offer to mind the kids so the teen can look in the study (I think it would be really bad if all the kids tried to go in the study).  We ask the kids what else they know of.  A 6 or 7 year old girl says there's a big red dragon up the tower.  She's hushed and a younger kid suggests there's a statue in the great hall.  It has a Ruby eye.  Behind it was a chain with Cymric gold symbols on a medallion.  Take it to Lady Voklaynn and she congratulates all of us.  We are all ready to go home, for we're healed and have to see to our families and further tasks.

## Back in Briton

We arrive back on the hill, the following morning from when we left.  Arcavius sleeps on the Tor to return the scroll.  Pass on the Guardian's promise for our travel to the Knight of The Red Feather.

I tell story that day.  We travel back to Wandborough, but get as far as the ford and as we're crossing, I notice a white cloth is entangled in my horses legs.  It's a noble ladies shawl.  We split into 2 parties on either side of the river and head upstream.  Hywel finds tracks and Arcavius and Edar cross back to our side.  The tracks have come to the river, they stopped and went west along the river.  Hywel leads with me 2nd.  As we go on, I see something at my arm's level, with me on horseback.  It's a woman's undergarment piece tied to a bush.  She's a prisoner!  We go faster.  Pass a "road" marker with Ogham Runes (Druidic stuff).  Eventually we can hear a smithy at work, and we see ahead a clearing with a hill fort / village.

## Rescuing Margot

We ride to the gate.  A big man followed by about 10 men stop us from entering.  We can see that mistletoe grows on the side of the gate, one more sign of the old Cymric ways.  I tell him to return the woman who was brought here in the morning.  He replies that no-one came.

A young man is paying close attention to our armour.  He goes back through the gates.  Go in get the village people to split into men and women.  Arcavius investigates a hut.  Imp throws kettle and chair at him - red eyes - he's out.  I'm in shield first which stops kettle before it even touches it.  I'm about to torch the place when an old witch comes.  She knows of the woman and the druids.

Prince Ailil - she wants ears for him, the Unseelie Prince.

We are directed to some nearby standing stones.  At these stones, the smell of roses surrounds them.  Evil from one stone when I touch it, and fear and terror from another.  Wait till midnight and a druid pops up.  We trade statements about destiny.  He pops back into the stone.  I pray at Lady's stone, hear her voice and then try shield on the stone.  Big flash, etc.  Pale, pointy-eared, black-clothed and staff with a big ruby on it pops up.  He is of the mound.  "I don't think you should have done that."

He says he's bothered by the incessant chanting of the druids.  He pops out the lady.  She looks up and then faints.  He then pops the druid and the brigands out.  We fight brigands.  I strike my brigand, and then he strikes me unconscious.

Told later that Arcavius killed his and then ran after the druid who'd run.  He called for his horse and then chased down the druid and knocked him unconscious.  Arcavius gives some orders and then passes out.  We go back to ford and then to Cirencester.  She is extremely talkative (like all the time).

Get to see _[[030-Arthurian-Britain#^0b1942|Duke Eustace of Clarence]]_.  She gives a gift of square embroidery with Clarence arms.  Need to find a druid to advise Duke of how to deal with this.  Hywel goes with Tathyl.  Hywel warns us that _[[030-Arthurian-Britain#^2b21c9|Margot]]_ has been making moves on Tathyl.

That evening, we go to dinner.  Duchess speaks to me about Arcavius being such a nice young man, and Margot would be a good match.  I raise the point that Arcavius is the son of Marshal Dekmar of Dorchester, which pleases her so she will send messages to the Marshal, but she wants me to broach the matter with Arcavius.  I also mention Sir Cynon, Steward of Levcomagus.

Some talk around the table of besmirching of Margot's honour by her abduction - i.e. "what must have been done to her".

I speak to Arcavius.  He's dutiful of being the first son and doing what's best for his family and for Dorchester.
