# Rhodri's Tales - 511 AD

**Written by [[Ben-G|Ben G.]] (Rhodri)**

Uncle dies, Arthur is 18, Caradoc is 14, Nia is 9 and the twins (Gwen and Gwyn) are 10.  32 knights in Marlborough and 33 knights in Raven's Gate (not including Hywel, Edar, Tathyl, Nai and Gilbert) and 200 soldiers.

During winter, Arcavius and my families spend a fair amount of time visiting Voklaynn and Anwyn.

## Cynon Weds Arianwen

In December, Cynon is busy wedding his intended and planning to recover the Rydychan holdings.  Arthur came with Caradoc and Merffyn, but the rest of the court remained at Silchester.  It is a quiet wedding.  I tell Arthur of the vision of his greatest danger that was shown to us by the Wise One.  As Arthur is using his army to repair the walls of St Albans, it is also helping directing the building of many castles across Logres.  Sir Hervis de Revel is growing impatient without action and incites a band of hotheads to go raid the Saxon lands.  One that he failed to convince was Cynon.

## Battle of Carohaise

The court is planned to move to Carohaise in the lands of Camelaird and the word is that King Lot is coming with his wife, Queen Margawse, to court.  As we are moving with the court, I have brought 30 knights with me.  A muddy messenger meets up with us just past Cirencester.  He is from Leodogrance and asks for Arthur's aid.  King Ryons of Nogales and the Perilous Forests has attacked again, and is currently laying siege to Carohaise.  Arthur commands that all knights with us are to ride to repel the attacker, while the court returns to Cirencester.

The Battle of Carohaise lasts for four hours.  Merffyn aids us.  Although I am unhorsed in the initial lance charge, I continue to fight against horsed opponents and still manage to capture two of the superlative knights opposing us.  King Ryons forces are routed and Arthur leads most of the army in pursuit.  As I am unhorsed, I take charge of the battlefield.  While the sounds of isolated battle are scattered throughout the surrounding forest, the residents of Leodogrance's besieged castle come out and cheer us.

Our successful army comes back piecemeal, but Arthur doesn't come back.  I organize a number of knights to go looking for him, but it is I who find him, swaying over the body of King Ryons.  Both of them are badly wounded.  As Cynon is better at first-aid, I get him to help Arthur while I try with Ryons.  I fail to save Ryons, while Arthur is about to pass out but still alive.  I lift him bodily before me and climb on a horse and flog the horse to speed the Carohaise.

The castle's healer, Mathilde, is brought quickly when I arrive.  She's a very ancient woman - a nun-like pagan.  She leads me as I bear Arthur to a room.  In the process of which Arthur half wakes and but he's in great pain and out of his mind, but I get him to listen to my voice and calm him down where he passes out again.  While Mathilde is treating Arthur, Guinevere arrives at the door.  I let her in but tell her harshly to be quiet.  Of course she doesn't and tries to bother Arthur even while he is unconscious.  Before I can turn on her, Mathilde slaps her.  But then Mathilde drives us out of the room when she has finished her immediate treatment of Arthur.

## A Scabbard for Excalibur

Mathilde has healed Arthur quickly.  About a week after, Morgaine arrives with a timely gift for Arthur - a scabbard which will protect Arthur.  In return she wants Arthur's protection (!)  Ailil doesn't suffer betrayal gladly.  I don't know how she has gotten such a scabbard which matches the exact design of Excalibur?

In court, Morgaine is cutting a swathe through the married Christian knights and their wives have nagged Jeanne, who then passed the message on to me, who then passed the message on to Arthur, who springs the idea of introducing some rules of conduct for knights, but he is still thinking about what they should be and doesn't want to say what they are yet.  I get the feeling that he will be talking to some of the others about this, but he feels that he knows what I would say.

Morgaine and Merffyn don't get on - she wants his knowledge, and he wants her to leave.

Sir Hervis de Revel returns from his raid having lost very few knights and having harassed the Saxons tremendously.  Although the court is loud in its praise of him, I think that he has been extremely foolish in rousing the Saxons before we attacked them with our full forces.

## Caradoc and Guinevere

King Leodogrance has great gardens (it may not be a knightly virtue, but he enjoys gardening).  Jeanne and I go for a walk amongst the paths and arbours when we overhear Caradoc and a girl.  Jeanne becomes instantly still and quiet - Caradoc is with Guinevere!  I don't want to get involved until Caradoc picks a rose and hands it to her.  She pricks her finger and he grabs her hand and puts the finger in his mouth.  They're about to kiss when I make a loud noise but they don't hear, so I barge in and ask Guinevere to take a walk with Jeanne while I have a little talk with Caradoc.  Although Jeanne has quite vanished, Guinevere nods and goes for a walk.

Caradoc is in love with her - implacably - he won't take no for an answer.  He asks would I have let any thing get in my way to Jeanne (ouch).  He walks off after I told him I hoped that he would respect his foster-brother's betrothal.

## Battle of Rydychan

As there is no plans for battle, I can now assist and begin to fulfil my promise to aid Cynon in recovering the county of Rydychan.  The current situation is that the three main holdings of the county are being held by three brothers, each of whom is independent and has no overlord at the moment.  They must be captured in the order in which they can be reached; for if one is bypassed, then its army can come out to raid the supply train of the attackers to the north or south.

My long term plan of attack is to start with the large town of Wallingford, including its Motte and bailey castle (which has about 10 knights and 25 soldiers); then siege the town of Sherborn which also has a special Motte and bailey castle with a stone tower and a 2nd bailey (which has about 20 knights and 50 soldiers).  The conclusion will be an attack on the walled city of Oxford with a very strong castle (which has bout 30 knights and 75 soldiers).

The funding of this campaign is coming from the ransom of an Earl that Cynon captured at the Battle of Carohaise, but the funding will only give us reasonable time against the town of Wallingford.  The troops we use are Cynon's from Levcomagus, my forces from Marlborough and Raven's Gate and mercenaries he has bought.  Cynon has asked that I command the battles, while he will do the talking.  We travel to Wallingford to begin the campaign.

Cynon gives the enemy leader, Sir Bege, a minute to surrender and as he's refusing, his own troops open the gates and they troop out to surrender.  So instead of a fight here, Cynon, the mercenaries' captain and I agree that we can also attack Sherborn, which is held by Sir Bege's brother, Sir Bellus.  We travel there and present the same demands and again, like his brother at Wallingford, he refuses.  But this time, there is no betrayal.  We assault the outer wall and my plans create a breach and Sir Bellus fails to save any of his men on those walls.  He lost 20kv, while we lost only 4kv.  We camp that night.

The Fae Captain in human form visits Niani.  He mentions he has a charge to fulfil but this is just a courtesy visit.  Next day, we attack the main fortifications, but there are no great gains.  That second night, the Captain returns and hassles Niani about Guinevere and Arthur.  Niani visits me and tells me that the Fae have a problem with the betrothal.  Perhaps a new match of their suggestion might be acceptable.  If it would help, he had said, Arthur has already fathered a child - although not said.  Obviously Arthur's relations with Lysanor had not been just fraternal.

On the third day we achieved the same as on the previous day, but the walls are greatly reduced.  Arcavius manages to get on the walls with two followers atop a ladder while I get atop the wall alone.  I can't make any headway and am repelled.  Arcavius manages to stay on the wall a little longer, but the result was the same with myself.

That night the Captain returns, but this time he takes Niani for a walk where she meets up with Lysanor.  She takes her to me.  Lysanor wants the child, Uther, to be brought up by Arthur, but I can't help her with that.  I will support her to have a normal life here in Logres, but the unification of Logres can't afford to have the troubles that recognition would bring - both in a dynastic sense, and in a Fae sense.  Lysanor left disgruntled.

On the fourth (and final) day, we successfully assault the walls and take the town.  The season for combat is over.  I've lost ten knights, but a couple of the mercenary ones have requested to join me.  We will have to leave Oxford and the eldest brother Sir Basle until another time.

## Marlborough Castle

After settling dispositions of the new acquisitions, we return to Carohaise via Mildenhall with Arcavius, Niani and Brennos as Niani was interested in building a new castle.  I teasingly suggested that perhaps she might like Lucienne to build her a castle, so she wanted to see an example and Edar seemed to have a close connection with Lucienne.  Niani liked the looks of Marlborough while we were inspecting it, so Edar just stepped out of the room and after a few moments stepped back in with Lucienne.  After I introduced them to each other, they verbally danced around where they were from until they decided to get back to the castle business.  As I had watched and listened, I became involved as I realized I could now afford to upgrade Broad and Chalke and others elsewhere, as Arthur gave me leave.

## Uther, Son of Arthur

While we were talking, a page came in and spoke to Edar who then quietly went out, unnoticed by Niani and Lucienne.  Edar returned telling me that the Fae Captain had returned with company.  When we went out to look, it was to see the Captain, Lysanor, and a small boy who looked just like Arthur as a 2 year old, who had to be Uther.  The apparent age didn't match, but then he had been living in the Fae realm.  Lysanor said that the situation had changed and that Uther was in greater danger.  I managed to understand, even though nothing was said about it except that Lysanor had earned the enmity of Titania, that she was pregnant.  She said that she trusted me with her child … damn, how can they tell I will try not to let any harm come to children.

I had no choice but to accept fosterage of Uther, unless we could come up with a safer alternative.  I had initially been proposing having Uther stay here under the care of Edar and Aneurin (unbeknownst to either that they were cousins, but I didn't need to bring that up) but I couldn't leave him here when I found out about Titania (as it would be too dangerous for Edar and Aneurin, and Uther.  Lysanor said her goodbyes perfunctorily to all, including Uther, and walked away.  This obviously surprised the Captain, for he started, said a general (and quick) goodbye and raced after her.

I ask Aneurin if I can borrow a nurse for Uther.  Niani and Brennos will be staying here with Lucienne while Arcavius, Uther and nurse, and me are escorted by my troops to the court at Carohaise.

Spoke with Arthur and talked of Lysanor.  I misspeak and convince Arthur that Lysanor needs to be rescued when I say she's in a situation and she has changed.  He wants to go now, but I refuse to take him straight away.  Arthur is suspicious of me for not going straightaway.

Next morning, Cynon arrives with a gift from Epona for Arthur - as he has visited his holy place.  He also convinces Arthur that there is no need for him to go.
