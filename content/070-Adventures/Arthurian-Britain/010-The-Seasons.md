# The Seasons

## [[495-AD|495 AD]]

This campaign begins in 495 AD.  Three young squires, _[[010-Knights-of-Arthurian-Britain#Lord Rhodri|Rhodri]]_, _[[010-Knights-of-Arthurian-Britain#Sir Brion|Brion]]_ and _[[030-Arthurian-Britain#^0711d0|Edar]]_, are sent by their liege, Earl Robert of Salisbury, to investigate tales of a man-eating bear in the western acres.  They meet and dispatch the bear, and make first acquaintance with those who will become significant in their lives.

These include:

- Arthur, a boy child and future King of the Britons
- Merffyn, the legendary sorcerer and Arthur's guardian
- Countess Katherine, wife of Earl Robert
- Sir Gondarins, eldest son and heir of Earl Robert
- Sir Jaradan, Castellan of Vagon Castle and the Marshal of Salisbury
- Hywel, the older squire who captures women's hearts
- Tathyl, Brion's squire
- Aneurin, Edar's squire and holder of a secret
- Praetor Jonathel of Dorset, staunch ally of Earl Robert
- Lady Jeanne of Broadchalke, Priestess of Raven, soon to be Rhodri's wife
- Lady Anne of Longcott, soon to be Brion's wife
- Lady Gaille of Winton, hostess extraordinaire
- Anwyn the Forest Guardian and his son _[[Karwin|Karwin]]_, both unicorns
- _[[Oberon|King Oberon]]_ and Puck of the Seelie Court of the Fae
- Saxons infiltrating the forests east of Dorchester
- Sir Marchlew, in league with the Saxon enemy.

All three squires were knighted on the field of battle - the **Battle of Wareham Woods**.

## [[496-AD|496 AD]]

Rescue of the Boy King Arthur, his mother Ygraine and his half-sisters Margawse and Morgaine from Tintagel Castle, under the nose of the ambitious King Mark of Lyonesse.  _[[010-Knights-of-Arthurian-Britain#Lord Cynon|Sir Cynon]]_, one of Ygraine's household knights, joins the party.  After escorting Ygraine to Amesbury Abbey in Salisbury, marrying Margawse off to King Lot of the Orkneys and fostering Morgaine, Rhodri leads the party in exploring the cellars of Marlborough Castle, with Lucienne's assistance.  The season ends with the Festival of the Spring Equinox, presided over by Lady Jeanne in her guise as a Priestess of Raven.

## [[497-AD|497 AD]]

A journey to Silchester to see this old walled Roman town.  Odd jobs around Silchester include dealing with a water leaper, battling Saxons, finding evidence of vampires, and going underground to face goblins, ghouls and a Fomori giant.

The party moves on to Levcomagus to find out why the Steward isn't letting allies of Salisbury pass through.  During a search to find a missing girl, the party battles more Saxons, and Sir Brion is taken by his goddess, the Morrigan.  The party grieves (briefly) and then accepts _[[010-Knights-of-Arthurian-Britain#Sir Arcavius|Sir Arcavius]]_ and _[[020-The-Antediluvians#Gwyn|Sir Gwyn]]_ as replacements.

After battling with the Saxon leader _[[010-The-Seasons#^9a36d4|Sissa]]_, the party rescues the missing girl.  They are invited to a Fae banquet where they learn about the four great treasures of the Fae; the Stone of Destiny, the Sword, the Spear and the Cauldron.  Only the Sword remains with the Fae (in _[[Oberon|Oberon's]]_ very tight grip).  Oberon asks the party to search for the other treasures and return them to the Fae.

The party reports to Earl Robert on their return to Salisbury, then returns to Levcomagus to replacing the double-dealing Steward with someone loyal to the Duke of Silchester, Sir Cynon.

First appearance of:

- Bellator, Captain of the Guard, Silchester
- a skilled Moorish surgeon and maker of naphtha, Silchester

- a Fomori, enemy of the Fae (deceased)

- Lady Etleyenne, of the Seelie Fae

## [[498-AD|498 AD]]

Rhodri, Arcavius and Hywel travel to Medbourne to smite the infamous Gorbodok, half-human, half-demon.  After Gorbodok defeats Rhodri in battle, he takes Rhodri to the Other Realm and demands Rhodri train his army of demons.  Rhodri locates the magical Fae harp of Angus the Bard and plays music to lay Gorbodok low, then escapes back to Medbourne with Lucienne's aid.  Under extreme duress, Rhodri returns the harp to Angus the Bard.

First appearance of:

- Father Bawdwin, no relation to Bishop Bawdwin of Briton
- Baron of Lambor, Lambor Castle
- Marcus, woodsman and guide, Lambor Castle

- Bishop Bawdwin of Briton, no relation to Father Bawdwin
- Angus, Bard of the Seelie Fae, and his Harp.

## [[499-AD|499 AD]]

The party sleep on Tor Barrow Hill and wake up in the realm of the Red Dragon of Cymru.  They find the legendary Guardian, who asks Rhodri to ask _[[Oberon|Oberon]]_ to bring Marius to Tor Barrow Hill each year.  The Guardian believes that Oberon will do this in exchange for information on the whereabouts of one of the Great Fae Treasures.  The Guardian gives Rhodri a scroll to pass to Oberon - a scroll that none of the party can read.

On the way to delivering the scroll, the party meet a talking bear, the Knight of the Red Feather, and the Serpent of Many Colours.  Led on by a troublemaking unicorn, the party challenges the Serpent only to find they are fighting Ailil, the Unseelie Prince.  Ailil apparently often appears in this disguise.  Ouch!

The party heal up under the care of Lady Voklaynn, and are invited to _[[Karwin|Karwin's]]_ birthday party.  Merffyn, Oberon, Titania, the Captain and Angus the Bard are special guests.  Oberon reads the scroll and agrees to the Guardian's request, as does Angus the Bard.  Angus owes Rhodri for the return of his Harp.

The party meet the Wise One, a giant salmon who lives in a lake and gives prophecies in return for acorns from an oak tree on the shore.  The Wise One provides answers to various questions about the future, giving the party clues how to avoid upcoming difficulties and disasters.  Then Arcavius spots the Red Dragon from an insufficient distance.

The party hurry back to Karwin's birthday, now being held in the biggest, most magnificent castle anyone has ever seen.  Rhodri refers the Guardian's scroll to Merffyn, who reads it.  The Cauldron, one of the Great Fae Treasures, is at Ben Nevis.  Merffyn warns Rhodri that Oberon shouldn't obtain the Cauldron, as this would create a power imbalance between the Fae and the Fomori that wouldn't be good for the Britons.

On returning to their own realm, the party rescues a young woman from her kidnappers.  She is Margot, daughter of the Duke of Clarence.  Arcavius marries Margot to prevent any malicious gossip about her and her kidnappers.

First appearance of:

## [[500-AD|500 AD]]

Anglia is taken by new Saxon arrivals.  Rhodri and party are sent to find out whether these new Saxons will link up with those already established in the east.  On the way the party visits the sword in the stone - "whomsoever pulleth out the sword is the rightful king of England".

After fighting various Saxons and Fomori, the party captures a Saxon chieftain and keep him prisoner.  They hope to obtain valuable information from him on the state of the Saxon / Fomori alliance.

Travelling southeast towards Cambridge, the party encounter hundreds of Iceni refugees from the Saxons.  These refugees are escorted by Iceni women warriors led by Coulomb Not-a-Lady.  They take Coulomb and some of her warriors to Avalon, Lady Voklaynn's realm, and arrange a place of safety for as many Iceni as want to stay in Avalon.

After battling more Saxons the party capture a Fomori alive and, with a dragon's none too gentle assistance, interrogate him.  They learn that the Fomori King has offered the Saxon King a great treasure, in exchange for two Saxon brides.  The great treasure may be Finias, the magic spear that controls the elements.  The party learns from the Wise One that Finias is located in Glastonbury Church and protected by the Archangel Michael.  They also learn of a way to break the Saxon / Fomori alliance - a beautiful Jutish queen.

An alliance is formed between Salisbury and Silchester, and messengers are sent to other lords to request they join in against the new and old Saxons, and against the Fomori.  On the way to Glastonbury Tor, Rhodri angers _[[Oberon|Oberon]]_ by refusing to find and bring him Murias, the Cauldron of the Dagda.  Unfortunately, Oberon has a long memory and Rhodri is a mere mortal.

On the way to Dorset, Rhodri stops off at home - Broad Castle.  Here he is embroiled in the machinations of the Unseelie, as Prince Ailil makes off with an all-to-willing Morgaine from under Rhodri's very own roof.  Jeanne pursues the couple, but after four days none have returned.  Rhodri instructs Puck to give Oberon a message about Morgaine's fate, then persuades the rest of the party to accompany him through the Avebury circle and into the realm of the Unseelie Prince.  Rhodri barely avoids falling to the lure of the Unseelie, and with help from _[[Lillane-of-Clan-Bahari|Lady Leda]]_ and _[[Tyler|Tyler]]_, escapes with Jeanne.

The party returned home after recovering with Lady Voklaynn.

## [[501-AD|501 AD]]

Feeling guilty over Morgaine, Rhodri promises her father _[[Oberon|Oberon]]_ that he will obtain the Stone of Destiny for him.  Jeanne suggests Rhodri get information from a Fomori sympathiser and then steal the Stone, rather than openly challenge the Fomori King for it.  Rhodri gathers a reluctant party for a tough mission.

At Levcomagus the party are side tracked by _[[010-The-Seasons#^b842ab|Tamlyn]]_, whose village has been attacked by Saxons and two Fomori.  On arrival at the village, the party meet and are joined by _[[010-Knights-of-Arthurian-Britain#Lady Niani|Lady Niani]]_ and _[[Sir-Brennos|Sir Brennos]]_, recently arrived to protect the village on Raven's behalf.  Sir Cynon claims overlordship of the village in return for providing additional standing knights and strengthening the village's defences.

The party, with the addition of Niani and Brennos, battled the Saxons and the two Fomori, killing all.  They follow the trail back to the Saxon campsite, where they battle more Saxons and Fomori.  Merffyn assists by killing one of the Fomori, the King's son.  The party heals up at Lady Voklaynn's and ask the Wise One many questions.

After consulting with Arcavius and Cynon, Rhodri apologises to Oberon for not being there for Morgaine when she needed him, for failing in his duties as a foster father, and for not attempting to retrieve the Stone of Destiny from the Fomori.  To do so would upset the delicate balance between the Fae and the Fomori and incite the Fae to war on the Fomori, to the great cost of the mortal realms.

Before they leave Avalon, the party arms up at Goibniu's forge, making offerings of their own blood to obtain swords that won't be eaten away by Fomori blood.

Rhodri summons Puck to take them to Oberon's realm.  Here Rhodri makes his apology to Oberon, angering Oberon yet again by referring to Morgaine as his (illegitimate) daughter, in front of Titania.  Rhodri also apologises for not attempting to obtain Falias, stating that he's a mere mortal and could never take on the Fomori King.  In a more private moment Oberon "suggests" that Rhodri make up for his failings by bringing Ailil's daughter to him.  That will even the score between Oberon and Ailil.  Rhodri agrees.

Oberon returns the party to the mortal realms, but they quickly realise that nine years have passed since they left.  Arthur is now a grown man of 18 years, ready to become King of the Britons.

First appearance of:

- Tamlyn, from a village attacked by Saxons ^b842ab
- a male priest of the Morrigan
- Goibniu the Smith. ^08815a

## [[510-AD|510 AD]]

Arthur woos Lysanor, step-daughter of Earl Sanam of Bedegraine Castle on the Trent River, north-western Logres.  Lysanor is the Chatelaine of Karadigan Castle in her own right.  What is not well known is that she is _[[030-Arthurian-Britain#^11871f|Ailil's]]_ daughter.  Arthur fathers an unacknowledged bastard son on Lysanor, Uther.

## [[511-AD|511 AD]]

Winter with Lady Voklaynn.  Gwen (Rhodri's daughter) falls in love with _[[Karwin|Karwin]]_.  Anwyn, on Rhodri's request, makes her forget.  PCs return to Camelaird.

Leodogrance is besieged by Lord Ryons, who is defeated by the players at the Battle of Carohaise.  Arthur is severely wounded and nearly bleeds to death, but is healed by Mathilde, the old crone.

Morgaine arrives at court seeking protection and gives Arthur the scabbard for Excalibur.  Now Arthur won't bleed when injured.

Other knights raiding Saxons.

### Sir Emils

_[[010-Knights-of-Arthurian-Britain#Sir Emils|Sir Emils]]_ arrives from France.  He has been sent to Britain at the request of King Clovis of the Franks (who dies in 511 AD).  King Clovis was the founder of the French monarchy - a Druid-like King who had much in common with Merlin.  Emils is still fanatically loyal to Clotilde, Clovis' Queen.

In 511 AD the Merovingian domain was divided into four separate kingdoms; Theuderic in Austrasia (Metz), Chlodomir in the Loire Valley (Orleans), Childebert in Neustria (Paris) and Lothar in the Scheldt to Somme region (Soissons).  Clovis' death is the beginning of the end for the Merovingians.

Emils is carrying a sacred text for "the Merlin" - a genealogy of descent from Jesus and Mary Magdalene to both Clovis and Arthur, with a warning from Clovis - beware the Catholic Church of Rome.  Emils' sacred duty is to protect, nurture and support Arthur, as Arthur is a direct descendent of Jesus.

We have failed in France.  Let the Bloodline of Jesus succeed in Britain. - Clovis, 511 AD.

## [[512-AD|512 AD]]

### March

Lysanor gives birth to a boy child, fathered by _[[Oberon|Oberon]]_.  This incurs the wrath of Titania, who has yet to produce an heir for Oberon.  Oberon, worried for the boy's safety, instructs Rhodri to take Lysanor and the boy in and protect them.  Rhodri passes responsibility for Oberon's son on to Voklaynn and Anwyn, who accept in exchange for Oberon's assistance in recovering the Cauldron from Ben Nevis.

### April

Amid much pomp and ceremony (managed by Jeanne) and despite Arthur's reluctance, Lysanor marries Sir Cynrain, Jeanne's protector while Rhodri was away in the Fae realms.  Ailil (father of the bride) visits the wedding in the guise of a young musician, but doesn't cause any trouble.  The newly married couple settle in Karadigan Castle with Uther.  Rhodri sends his trusted allies (ex-household knights) to protect them.

### May

The Northern Witch's Pictish knights ambush the players just out of Silchester.  The Northern Kings are losing the battle with Arthur, and so their Witch is attempting to murder Arthur and his best knights.  A vicious fight ensues and the players narrowly escape with their lives.

### June

The great tournament held outside Silchester on St Alban's Day, June 22nd.  This tournament is sponsored by Duke Ulfius, and attended by 100 knights.  Brennos and Arcavius agree to act as Marshals of the Lists (judges, arbiters and overseers of events).  All knights are expected to participate in three events. the joust, the grand melee and the challenges.

The "away" team is led by Rhodri, the "home" team by Duke Ulfius.  Each participant presents themselves to Duke Ulfius, and then registers with one of the Marshals of the Lists.  Participants are qualified by "helm show" (inspection without wearing their helmets), "making of windows" (show of household devices or coat of arms) and proving noble blood.  Qualified participants sign up for specific events, choose a side for the Grand Melee, and obtain a lady's favour for inspiration, guidance and good luck.  Favours are tied around the upper arm or to the tip of a lance.

#### The Joust

The joust is run on horseback, with lances.  Competitors have three lances each, then must dismount and fight on foot with swords.  Wins and losses are marked in the joust scoring box.  Five elimination rounds and then the final match-up to decide who wins.

#### The Grand Melee

The grand melee is the main event.  Knights use blunted weapons in mock battle between two sides.  One side is the victor, and the Marshals of the Lists select the single most outstanding fighter as Champion of the Melee.  The Champion of the Melee wins the grand prize.

Knights may fight over several square miles and up to half a day, using sword and lance.  Knights may be assisted by their squires, servants and footmen.  Each side has an enclosed area that is considered a refuge from the other side.  Knights resting in their refuge will not be attacked by knights from the other side.

At the end of the Grand Melee, _[[030-Arthurian-Britain#^9bb92d|Lancelot]]_ (18 years old) of the exiled (from France) de Ganis clan is revealed as the "away" team's mystery knight.  Sir Lancelot's power comes from his chastity.  Guinevere (15 years old), Rhodri's daughter Nai (14 years old) and Morgaine all set their sights on Lancelot, despite Guinevere's previous interest in Sir Rhodri's son Caradoc (16 years old), and Morgaine's relationship with and child by Ailil of the Unseelie Fae.

_[[Merlin|Merffyn]]_, returned from parts unknown to watch the tournament, informs Emils that Sir Lancelot is not the most Christian of knights, and is therefore not the champion Emils seeks.

During the feast that night, Titania, disguised as Niani, misleads and abducts Brennos from under the noses of the players.  Titania later informs Brennos that she requires him "to perform a service", in exchange for Niani's life.  Brennos reluctantly agrees, then fervently hopes Titania's reputation as a barren queen is based in fact.  This eventually proves not to be the case, and Titania has her revenge on Oberon and Lysanor.  Brennos is the father of the Seelie Queen's unborn son.

#### The Challenges

Challenges are given and accepted to prove skill and strength, to resolve disputes, for glory and for love of the fight.  Challenges are spiced with feasts, amorous quests and socialising and may continue over several days.

During this time Arthur courts Maeve (17 years old), a guest from Voklaynn's realm who can see through Faerie Glamour to the truth beneath.  Rhodri and Niani encourage Arthur, as this ability of Maeve's is a highly desirable quality in a future Queen and mother to Arthur's heirs.

Jeanne has spoken to the Crone (an aspect of Morrigan), and tells Niani there is a small window of opportunity during which Niani and Brennos might return to Anfar Refinli.  All Niani has to do is find Brennos, missing since the previous night's feast.

Titania's handmaiden gives the players news of Brennos.  He is alive and well and will return to them soon.  This contact is made at Brennos' insistence, who knows full well the effect his absence must be having on Niani.  The handmaiden is accompanied by a huge Fae knight in silver armour, her protector in the lands beyond Titania's realm.

#### The Northern Wars

The players leave Silchester in mid-June.  Arthur has decided to bring the rebellious Northern Kings into line, and invades Malahaut.  He splits his army in two.  The larger group hides south of the smaller group while the smaller group draws out the forces of King Malahaut.  After a resounding victory, Arthur installs Rhodri's son Caradoc as his vassal in Malahaut.

After this battle, the player knights and Arthur travel north via Voklaynn's realm to Ben Nevis, to attack the Black Dragon.
