>**GM's Note:** Suitably armed and supported by additional marines, Snow and Co. investigate the second Nazi base deep in the Amazon jungle.  They rappel down to the jungle floor and discover a tunnel into a ruined temple.
>
>This time they hit pay dirt.  The team fights a running battle through the temple ruins against modern-day Nazis and primitive tribal people under their control.  Snow resorts to missiles and grenades to overcome the odds.  The Nazis used mortars and flamethrowers.  Heavy losses are incurred on both sides.
>
>In an enormous cavern breached by the team, they find a gigantic monster standing guard.  It attacks, and the team returns fire, assisted by one of Angela’s fireballs.  Beyond is a larger cavern in which many cultists are trying to escape in boats via an underground river, an escape route quickly blocked by one of Snow’s missiles.
>
>In the furthest corner of the cavern three women are summoning a demon, one of which is Rosa Montoyez / Martin Bormann.  Antonio advances on them, but they try to escape while the demon distracts him.  Snow assists Antonio by firing a missile in the demon’s direction, and then finishes Bormann off.
>
>Antonio then went to try his luck converting the cultists, to no avail.  Angela was planning her own solution to the cultist problem, but held off delivering it while she warned Snow to get the team out.  Snow ordered the team to evacuate the cavern and the temple ruins.
>
>Goldfarb spotted the two vampires in an alcove off the side of the cavern.  In this alcove was the gate that connected to the other gate in Antarctica, but no sword, the aim of the vampires’ search.  Goldfarb directed Antonio to deal with the vampires, while Angela threw Phoenix Fire on the fleeing cultists.
>
>The team fled from the heat and flames generated by Angela, and weren’t surprised when Antonio and Angela met them back at the tunnel entrance.  The team returned to Brasilia in high spirits.

## Written by [[Jenny-H|Jenny H.]] (Michal Herzeanu)

Armed with 20 marines, a troop transport and other paraphernalia, they headed to near the suspected base.  As an ex-Vietnam vet, Colonel Snow had to be talked out of napalming the forest to create an area for the helicopter to land.  They decided to rappel down instead.

The group found a steep entrance tunnel heading down into a ruined temple area.  Ropes were attached to an old stone bridge to safely descend past old stone spiked pits and boulders poised to roll onto unsuspecting trespassers.

The marines found a pulley system to cross a river.  On the other side, a couple of marines were dropped by poisoned darts.  Shooting at the trees dropped several brown-skinned pygmies.  The party blew up the entranceway to the trees to prevent more ideas from that direction.  Another entranceway led to an open area with more traps and some weathered and battered crates with Nazi symbols on them.

There was an obvious crawl-way made of wood and stone, but they took a less obvious route down a pit and another crawl-way with a river running through it.  Overhead, they heard jackboots and had a lovely time shooting Nazis and trading grenades.

A gateway took them to a little valley with more pygmies and the sound of a flamethrower being primed.  The Colonel went berserk and fired a missile launcher through the gateway for impressive results.  The marines spread out to inspect the damage and check for other hostiles.  Mortar shells took out some more marines, and everyone else took cover.  A well-aimed grenade by Goldfarb took out another flamethrower being primed.  Other marines mopped up the survivors.  Grenades mopped up the next lot of Nazis discovered.

Another room contained a 20-foot monster in a circle.  Snow launched a missile at it, which missed, but a few incendiary grenades, M16s, and a big fireball from Angela took it out.  Beyond this was a huge temple room with a lot of confused cultists trying to escape in boats on an underground river.  Snow launched another missile, cutting off their escape route.

In one corner of the room was another circle with 3 dead women and something foaming in it.  Antonio, as the resident demon expert, had a firefight with the summoners, who decided that retreat was the better part of valour at this point.  Snow launched a... you guessed it... a missile.

Snow shot the woman in the group - assumed to be the Bormann-inhabited one.  After dealing with the demon, Antonio went over to threaten the remaining cultists.  They also found an alcove containing a gate and the two vampires, who were trying to destroy it.

Goldfarb directed Antonio and Angela to deal with their vampire friends, as Snow ordered everyone else to evacuate the temple.  As they left, flames and heat followed them up.  Antonio and Angela met them back at the original entrance (minus the vampires) and accompanied the jubilant group back to Brasilia.
