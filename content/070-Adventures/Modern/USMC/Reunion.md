>**GM's Note:** Wallace and his family are recalled to the USA and a replacement Ambassador is on the way to Brasilia.  The party later learns that Wallace and his wife have separated.
>
>Michal identifies a man in a photo shown to her by Dean as her fiancé, _[[Jenny-H#^59824f|David Abrahamson]]_.  Dean and Michal return to Washington where she is reunited with David.  Michal informs David of events in Brazil, including what happened at the bus station.
>
>David suspects that Bormann may have mind-transferred into another body, something he saw Bormann do at Buchenwald.  After passing this information on to Dean, Michal and David may return to Brasilia to pursue Martin Bormann, now possibly either Rosa Montoyez or Major Ilsenburg.
>
>Dean contacts Snow and informs him that, on the strength of what has already been decoded from David’s stolen papers, Bormann must be taken alive at all costs and as soon as possible.  After clearing Ilsenburg, Snow and Goldfarb focus on Rosa Montoyez’s whereabouts.
>
>They find out that Rosa is a wealthy socialite who was running away to join her lover, a man deemed unsuitable by her influential family.  After the incident at the bus station, Rosa was admitted to hospital, where she discharged herself before her family arrived to take her home.
>
>Snow, Goldfarb and Ilsenburg found no further trace of Rosa once she had left the hospital.  All they could find was a missing persons notice fitting her description from Hector Lobos of De Tillo Investigations.
>
>After David and Michal return from Washington, the party contacts Lobos.  He tells them that Rosa had not been seen recently by her family, her lover or her friends.  The party also contacts Inspector Jorge Amado, Antonio’s friend in the Brazilian Federal Police.
>
>Amado tells them that there have been two recent murders in Sao Paulo.  The victims were young women; one was murdered in the Metro and one outside a lesbian bar called the Corintho.  Both women were murdered by an assailant using a garrote and had strange symbols carved into their bodies.  Michal and David’s worst suspicions are confirmed.
>
>Amado is instructed to look for a possible serial killer using a garrote (Bormann’s preferred weapon) and any neo-Nazis connected to Bormann’s accomplices (imprisoned when Bormann was shot at the bus station).  Dean is given the names of two prominent industrialists whom Milton Andrada da Silva considers neo-Nazis.
>
>Further investigation by the party links the symbol carved into the murdered women’s bodies to a recurring symbol in Bormann’s annotated “Mein Kamph”.  The symbol is also connected to a new industrial park in Sao Paulo.  The two industrialists named by da Silva Snr. are on the park’s Board of Directors, nearly all German.  David tells the party this symbol is that of the Ultima Thule Society, a secret society that supported the Nazis during the war.
>
>Snow sends marines with a photo of Rosa to watch the industrial park.  The park is the site of research into mining, marine exploration, rainforest drugs and civil engineering, as well as one of the largest satellite communication facilities in Brazil.  Major Ilsenburg has all satellite transmissions monitored.
>
>At a public information lecture, the party learns that a consortium operating out of the park is looking for research funds for the development of countermeasures to the unique airborne viruses found in the rainforest.

## Written by [[Jenny-H|Jenny H.]] (Michal)

Michal discovered that Dean's mystery man was indeed her David.  Despite her fears, he recognised her immediately.  It turned out that he had no memory of his time in the 1920s (when he had had no memory of his past or of Michal at all), but only had memories of the 1970s prior to landing in Germany in the 1940s.  He'd been caught and sent to Buchenwald before finding another gate to escape through, carrying with him coded information.

As part of their catching up on the past 9 years (effectively), Michal told David about the recent events in Brazil.  David asked her if she was sure Bormann had actually died.

"What do you mean?"

"Are you sure he didn't swap bodies before he died?"  He went on to explain mind transfer and that he knew Bormann was a sorcerer.  Michal was shocked.  She had come across such things before, but not in that form.

She discussed the possibility with Dean, who sent them back to Brasilia to investigate.  Michal contacted da Silva for backup before arriving at the Embassy.

As one of the two possibilities for the transfer, Dean had sent orders that Goldfarb (Snow being away on holidays) arrange for Ilsenburg to be brought in for questioning.  He had specific instructions that it be done without warning and that no one be within 5 feet of him.  Goldfarb carried out his orders with a great deal of trepidation and confusion.  Ilsenburg wasn't happy with the strange treatment, but Goldfarb begged him to come along peacefully.  Ilsenburg wasn't someone he really wanted to tangle with.

Da Silva helped clear Ilsenburg of any suspicion.  He knew too many personal details that only the major would know.  The marines were not happy with the news, but brightened at the sight of Michal back-pedalling furiously.  She was extremely conscious of the fact that the last time David remembered seeing her; she was a sweet young thing and was worried that after all this time of hunting for him, he wouldn't be interested in her anymore.  Trying to tone down her usual style was becoming a strain.

Major Ilsenburg and Doctor Goldfarb gathered with Michal, David and Antonio da Silva to discuss tactics.  The only other suspect was the young woman whom Bormann had taken hostage on the bus.  She would have been the easiest target anyway; as for most of the time he'd had his arm around her neck.

Following up on the woman, they discovered she was a wealthy socialite who had only been on the bus because she was running away to join a lover who was deemed unsuitable by her family.  She had discharged herself from the hospital after the incident; shortly before her family was due to collect her.

Goldfarb used some initiative and checked the personal ads for some trace of her trying to contact her lover.  What he found was an ad from a private investigator, Hector Lobos, looking for her.  He rang the number, pretending to be from the Wiesenthal Institute following up a possible Nazi connection with the woman, Rosa.  Unfortunately, Snow and the others picked the same investigator to hire to find out about Rosa.  They both gave the return phone number of a nearby hotel.

When they finally met with the investigator, Michal enjoyed playing the part of Goldfarb's superior, apologising for the unauthorised activities of her underling.  They still kept him to follow up Rosa, although he clarified that he had found no trace of her either with her boyfriend or with any other of her known associates.  Which made it even more likely that she had become Bormann.

Their friendly local policeman, Jorge Amado, was asked to keep an eye out for any garrotting or other unusual activities.  He could not make the surviving neo-Nazis disclose any useful information.  And da Silva's industrialist father came up with 2 possible neo-Nazi sympathisers that were worth checking out.

Snow came back from holidays to find Ilsenburg, David and Michal in conference.  Goldfarb had been suddenly recalled to Washington to discuss the medical reports he had been sending.

Snow wanted to know more about where David had been and what he knew about the situation.  David informed him he had met a man called Kraft on the train to Buchenwald.  He was the astrologer who had predicted the bomb attempt on Hitler.  He had done a reading for Bormann that predicted that he would have success in a new time and would attempt to set up the 3rd Reich later on.  He had been shipped off to the camp along with other astrologers when they failed to predict Hesse's defection.

In the camp, David had become secretary to the Commandant and observed Mythos experiments, which were even more terrible than the medical experiments.  They made new creatures out of people.  (Snow; "So we've got dwarf ghouls!)  There had also been plans to move a 20,000-man base forward in time.  David had observed Gate experiments and escaped through one of them, minutes after Bormann had used it to also escape, though they had ended up in different times.

Snow received an urgent message from Dean, stating that they had managed to partially decode the information David had brought with him. Bormann was to be taken alive and as soon as possible.

Amado reported that there had been two garrottings in San Paulo, one on the Metro and one outside a lesbian bar.  Both were women.  Bormann had liked to kill women.  They piled into a Blackhawk with a couple of marines, including _[[USMC-Fireteam-SG-21#Gunnery Sergeant Doug Anderson|Gunnery Sergeant Doug Anderson]]_.  They landed in da Silva's backyard, which was certainly big enough to accommodate them.

Snow went to a police contact he had been given by Amado and showed them a picture of Rosa, claiming they were investigating the possibility of her involvement.  The autopsy had shown a person of her size was likely to have committed the murders.  He was also shown a picture of a symbol carved into the stomach of one victim and the back of the other.

Back at base, they filled in the gunnery sergeant with the current state of events, and he suggested the murderer had been trying to attract attention.  It was possible that the gate-jump and then the shootout had cut off Bormann's contacts and he was trying to attract others.  The mind transfer might also have destroyed some of his memories of contacts.

This seemed likely, as David revealed the symbol as an alchemical one belonging to the Ultima Thule Society.  Ilsenburg dug out Bormann's copy of Mien Kamph and noted that it and other symbols were scribbled in the margins.  He dug through local newspapers hoping Bormann had used these symbols in an ad.  What he found was an article about the opening of an industrial park, which had one of the alchemical symbols as its trademark.  One of the suspect industrialists was on the board of directors.

A brief investigation revealed that a change of logo was registered for the park development, even though the organization dated back to the 1920s.  This seemed to show that it was a signal in itself.  The next day Anderson and a marine were sent to watch the industrial park, with little success.

Goldfarb arrived back to Brasilia to confusing information about murders, Nazis and suspect industrialists and instructions to meet the rest of the team in San Paulo where they were all off in different directions, trying to piece together the puzzle.  Colonel Snow and Major Ilsenburg investigated the murder scenes and the Industrial Park.  This comprised a mainly German board of management and did research on a wide variety of areas, including components, mining, marine exploration, rainforest drugs and civil engineering.  They had a remarkably large satellite dish, which Ilsenburg had monitored.

The following day, Antonio had a visit from Caravello, an old native Indian who span them wild stories about how the World Bank was deliberately destroying Brazil by funding projects that destroyed the rainforest.  From Angela's reaction, this was a common theme from him.

The Colonel and the doctor visited a public lecture at the Industrial Park, which was seeking sponsors for new research.  The rainforest apparently had the potential to produce airborne viruses, and they wanted to develop them in order to find counter-measures.
