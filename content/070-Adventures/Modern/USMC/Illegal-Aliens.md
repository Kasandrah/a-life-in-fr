>**GM's Note:** Michal Herzeanu arrives through a gate from an isolated mountain location in Brazil in 1992, in pursuit of Martin Bormann.  Bormann has already run off when Michal lands near a high stone fence.  However, she is not alone.  Several ghouls are closing on her with obvious intent, summoned from the local sewers by Bormann, so she climbs over the fence to escape.  On the other side of the high fence she meets the USMC guard for the US Embassy in Brasilia.  They are not happy about her intrusion.
>
>After sorting things out, the party pursue Bormann via a couple of garrotted bodies and some pawned diamonds to a bus station, where he is shot dead before he can escape.  A young woman hostage, Rosa Montoyez, is subsequently taken to hospital after the drama, but recovers enough to check herself out later.

## Written by _[[Jenny-H|Jenny H.]]_ (Michal)

Michal Herzeanu had stopped being the hunter and instead was being hunted by creatures that her mind refused to accept were real.  She was still a little disoriented by the gate she'd followed Bormann through and to suddenly be confronted out of the dark by things that looked like an unclean cross between a wolf and a man was startling to say the least.  Especially as they didn't seem to be worried by bullets.  She'd confronted many things in her time, but this was something new and frightening.  They chased after her with a rolling lope that was awfully effective.

At the end of the park were houses and then a wall.  Its solid construction promised shelter until she could get her bearings.  She thought she was still in South America by the climate and the style of buildings, but couldn't be sure.  When was another problem.

Michal jumped at the wall, which offered less purchase than she'd expected.  Her knee crunched against the solid object and she winced at the sound.  There was razor wire at the top.  So much for her coat.  One of the things leapt up at her and she jumped, half fell down the other side.  There was a sharp pain through her ankle and she realized that it was probably broken.  Panic started to set in and she fought against it.  If it was her time to die, there'd still be a fight.  She tried to roll over and stifled the scream of pain.  She registered the running sounds and looked up.  They were carrying weapons, but at least they were human.  One planted a knee in her back and roughly restrained her.  Not that she was going anywhere.  Her weapons went next.

There was the sound of gunfire and some inhuman sounds as the things retreated.  Apparently sustained gunfire hurt them.  Good.  She was in either an Army camp or an embassy.  American, if the accents and the uniforms were accurate.  Great.  How the hell was she going to explain herself, especially as she was dressed in black with boot polish on her face.

Their leader, a colonel by the insignia, came to question her.  The soldiers holding her tried to bring her to her feet and the scream was released at the sharp pain.

"Who are you?"

Michal spat out, "Herzeanu. Lieutenant," and her serial number.  That would keep them guessing.  "What were those things?"

"What are you doing in here?"

"Trying to escape those things.  Believe me, if I'd known I was heading into a… is this an embassy?  I would have tried somewhere else."

"Why were they chasing you?"

"I don't know.  Possibly the man I was following contacted them to waylay me.  Or maybe I was just unlucky."

"Who were you hunting?"

"A criminal.  That's all you need to know."  More they wouldn't believe yet.

They weren't forthcoming on the information, but did call for a doctor and moved her to the infirmary under armed guard, which gave her a chance to think strategies.  She couldn't afford to tell them the truth, no one would believe her.  They had to creep up on it slowly.  In the meantime, Bormann was getting away.

Colonel Snow considered the information that had been gathered over the last few days on his 'guest'.  The spooks downstairs had informed him that Lieutenant Michal Herzeanu was the name of a Shin Bet operative who had vanished into thin air in 1984, twelve years ago.  If still alive, she would be 36 years old, while his guest looked younger than that.  They had the same distinguishing scars, down to the artificial right kneecap, which Goldfarb informed him was of the correct vintage.

Herzeanu had tried to hide her ignorance, but it was clear she had no clear idea of the current date, or even the year.  Her story of how she had entered the country was a complete fabrication.  He couldn't check her passport for details because she was carrying no identification whatsoever.  Even the labels from her clothing had been removed.  Major Ilsenburg had informed him that this was standard field procedure for an intelligence agent.  What wasn't standard were the gold Star of David and an engagement ring, which she wore on a chain around her neck, a packet containing some uncut diamonds and what he recognised as an Elder Sign.  Goldfarb had some doubts about her Jewish ness, but he was rather conservative in such matters, while she clearly was not.  She at least spoke fluent Hebrew along with weirdly accented English.

Most puzzling was the only other distinguishing item in her possession was the colour photograph of a man the spooks told him was Martin Bormann.  A Bormann as he had looked in 1944 when he disappeared.  Herzeanu claimed she had been searching for the man to find out who he was.  Her theory was that he was a child or even a clone of the real Bormann, but Snow could tell there was more to it than that.  She was too insistent that Snow should use any contacts to find him and a little vague on where she had been following him from.  Not surprising, as she wasn't sure what country she was in.  And she was also looking for another man who records showed had died in an explosion in 1979.

Backtracking her steps had not been easy.  His men had followed her trail to a point in the main park where she had apparently leapt from transport.  Except that there were no tracks from a vehicle and they would have noticed a helicopter flying around.  The ghouls that had chased her had come from the local sewers, intersecting her tracks and those of another person, which they had lost at the main bridge.

Inspector Jorge Amado, his contact in the Brazilian Federal Police, could shed no light on the events, apart from informing him that there had been a couple of murders by garrotting in the general area that night.  Time for this Herzeanu to tell him the truth.  He contacted Antonio Andrada da Silva for help.  He was the colonel's main help in unusual situations.

Michael considered the colonel and the new man.  Had he had time to find out enough about the holes in her story?  The presence of a new person suggested something had changed.  She sighed.  Too bad if he hadn't.  Goldfarb considered her more than a little mad already.  Especially as she'd asked him if he knew of David.  Quite justifiably too.  Looking for someone who could be anywhere, any when, using either of 2 names, or maybe another, possibly with amnesia, through any number of gates, had taken its toll.  Sometimes she thought she was mad herself.  Oh well, here went nothing.

"The photograph you have was taken in 1992.  As best as I can determine, it really is Martin Bormann.  He disappeared in 1945.  I believe he time-travelled to the 1990s."

"Time-travel?  Is that possible?"

Snow's question was directed to da Silva, but Michal answered it anyway.  "I've recently spent 2 years in the 1920s.  I assure you it is perfectly possible."

"How did you do it?"

"I was sent by people from the future to correct an anomaly in time.  I was abandoned there.  Eventually I discovered how to work the gates myself."

"You can travel anywhere in time?"

"Not exactly, the spell I learnt was incomplete.  I can target situations, but not exactly."

Da Silva was incredulous.  "You use an incomplete spell?  Do you know how dangerous that is?"

Michal shrugged.  "Necessity must."

"You found Bormann through a gate?"

"Not quite.  I tracked him down by conventional means and when I nearly had him, he used a gate to escape me.  I followed him through it.  The rest you know."

It fitted the facts better than any other explanation.  With recent events, Snow was prepared to accept any story that did, however implausible.  And da Silva informed him that while a gate through time was unusual, gates from location to location were certainly possible.

"What now?"

"We have to find him.  Whatever he's planning must be stopped."

Easier said than done.  Amado had turned up nothing.  Apart from a report that a man fitting Bormann's description had attempted to sell some precious stones.  The jewellers had become suspicious and called the police.  Bormann had otherwise disappeared.  They tried watching known Nazi sympathisers, in case he had contacted someone, without success.

After much consideration, Michal suggested that he might return to the hideout he had been using in the mountains.  They should have whatever means of transport was available watched.

Time passed.  Michal slowly became more mobile, but kept a low profile as much as she could.  Having come this far, she didn't want to be left out of any action.

Word eventually came through that a man matching Bormann's description had been seen boarding a bus to the nearest town to the mountain retreat.  The marines went into action, requisitioning a helicopter, the only way they could overtake the bus.  On the way, Snow baited Michal with the information that Israel had made peace with the Palestinians and had given them their homeland back.  Michal, ever right wing, replied, astounded, "What the hell did they do that for?"

Amado arranged for the bus station to be covered, specifically the bay where the bus was due in.  The marines were there as observers and unofficial backup if required.  There were a couple of men loitering around the bay who looked suspicious. They were kept under observation and the other people were quietly moved away, substituting undercover police to avoid it looking suspiciously empty.

The bus came in and passengers began to alight and move away.  Colonel Snow was watching one of the loiterers in the bathroom when it turned ugly and he found himself in a close-range shoot-out.  The gunfire alerted the other loiterers and Bormann on the bus.  In the ensuing fight, the bus driver was shot and Bormann took a young woman hostage.

Major Ilsenburg crept onto the bus to try and release the woman, but was noticed and pinned down a couple of rows of seats too soon.  Eventually, there was a major shootout, during which Michal fired the fatal shot at Bormann.  Miraculously, the female passenger escaped unharmed.  And there was much rejoicing.

Ilsenburg appropriated Bormann's copy of Mien Kamph, which was an annotated edition.  Then they checked out the mountain retreat, just in case and found it empty.  Michal was able to dig up and retrieve some personal possessions, including a photo of David and several now out-of-date passports in various names and nationalities.  Michal was invited to convalesce at the da Silva house and the marines breathed a sigh of relief as she disappeared into the sunset.
