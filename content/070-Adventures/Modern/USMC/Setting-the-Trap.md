>**GM's Note:** David and Michal contact an art dealer, posing as a Jewish couple who have discovered Bormann’s copy of “Mein Kamph” in their recently deceased grandfather’s possessions.  They rented a house on the outskirts of Sao Paulo and settle into wait for a response.  They are contacted by Hans Gustaf and arrange a time for him to come and view the book.
>
>Just before Gustaf arrives, they have an unexpected visit from Antonio, who meets Gustaf just as Gustaf is leaving the house after having viewed the book.  Shortly after Gustaf has driven away, Angela arrives and chews Antonio out for making a target of himself.  Angela is interrupted by a sniper who hits Antonio, but to no apparent effect.  They run inside where Antonio hands a bemused Michal a hollow-point, high-calibre sniper’s bullet.  Michal pretends not to notice the hole in the front of his shirt.
>
>All hell breaks loose as those in the house are attacked by a number of heavily armed assailants using high-powered weapons and grenade launchers.  Antonio summons a wind to put out the fire and dispose of some of the attackers.  The party returned fire, survived the attack, and took one of their assailants in for questioning.  Antonio was delegated to pay for the damages to the rented property.
>
>The party decides to head for Iguape, a seaside town south of Sao Paulo where Antonio has a beach house.  Here they questioned the surviving Nazi attacker, aided by Ilsenberg’s chemical interrogation assistants.  All they learned was the whereabouts of their attackers’ original meeting place where the Nazis were supposed to bring “the magician”, presumably Antonio.
>
>On paying a visit to the meeting place, the party discovered a training area inside a couple of old warehouses.  After a fire fight within one of the warehouses, the party returned to the beach house with another prisoner.  Still no more information.  The decision was made to raid Gustaf’s house.
>
>Here Gustaf attacked the party in his guise as a human fireball with a flaming sword, completely unexpected and very damaging to Ilsenburg, who received 3rd degree burns during the battle.  Colonel Snow finally dispatched Gustaf in a hail of bullets.  With the house on fire the party recovered what papers and books they could, then ran.  They also took the sword, no longer flaming as Gustaf was dead.
>
>Ilsenburg was hospitalised in Sao Paulo, then transferred to Brasilia for safety once he was able to be moved.  The party reviewed the books, papers and building plans recovered from Gustaf’s house, but most were in German and required a long and concentrated application of David’s translation skills.

## Written by _[[Jenny-H|Jenny H.]]_ (Michal)

David and Michal contacted an art dealer, posing as a Jewish couple that had discovered Bormann's copy of Mien Kamph in their recently deceased grandfather's possessions.  It seemed the easiest way to flush out the opposition.  They rented a house on the outskirts of town and started setting up the trap.

A Hans Gustaf, who had the same surname as one of the suspect industrialists, contacted them for an appointment to view the book.  Unexpectedly, Antonio arrived at the house and stayed hidden during the viewing, but came out to allow the man to see him just before he left.  Shortly afterwards, Angela arrived and took Antonio out the back of the house to have a piece of him for making himself a target.  Quite literally, as they were shot at by a sniper.

This appeared to have the intention of making the inhabitants flee outside the front, which they stubbornly refused to do.  The attackers then attempted to make them change their mind by firing a couple of grenade launchers at the house and setting it on fire.  The defenders avoided the burning sections and returned fire.  Antonio revealed himself to be more than what he seemed by first failing to be shot, although it appeared to be a dead on shot, and then by summoning a wind to put the fire out and to dispose of one of the attackers.  The marines and hangers-on finished off the rest, apart from someone to be questioned.  Antonio was delegated to pay for the damage to the house.

After the confusion of the fire fight died down, the party bugged out and headed (after some discussion) to Iguape, a seaside area south of San Paolo.  In a rented holiday home (Antonio is so useful!), they questioned the surviving Nazi attacker.  Michal tried vainly to suggest David was tired and should rest, so she could participate effectively in the questioning, but he stubbornly declined, so it was just as well that Ilsenburg had more standard chemical persuasions.  The prisoner turned out to be a soldier who was just following orders and who couldn't give any useful information.  He did supply them with the meeting point for after the raid, where they were supposed to bring 'the magician' - presumably Antonio.

The Marines and Michal headed out to the industrial area the prisoner described and found a series of warehouses that were set up for practice for the Nazis.  There were targets set up in one building and they acquired a second prisoner after a fire fight in another.  Unfortunately, the enemy was using sensible tactics and was telling its grunts nothing useful to the enemy.

This left them only Hans Gustav as a lead and they decided to raid his house.  Halfway up the internal stairs, Gustav appeared at the top, doing an impression of the Human Torch and attacking them with bouts of fire and a flaming sword, hurting Ilsenburg badly.  Machine gun fire appeared to do little damage, though the Colonel eventually fired enough bullets at him to get through whatever protection he had to eventually kill him.  Unfortunately, by then the house was firmly on fire.  They looted what papers looked interesting in his study, but failed to open the safe before it became too dangerous to stay.

Ilsenburg was hospitalised with 3rd degree burns in San Paolo.  They stabilised him enough to permit evacuation to Brasilia.

The papers were mostly propaganda books, newsletters and other paraphernalia, but did include building plans.  Most were in German, so needed to wait for translation.  The 'swat team' then tried to figure out what to do next.
