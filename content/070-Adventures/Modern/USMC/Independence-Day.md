## Highlights

Ghouls under the control of a white-haired child witch attack the Ambassador’s Independence Day dinner party, then hide out in the sewers under the Plaza.  The Ambassador’s USMC guards protect the guests, including Antonio and Angela, and drive off the ghouls.

Some days later the USMC hunt the ghouls through the sewers, meeting up with Angela and some street kids on their own ghoul-burning campaign.  Angela cements an elder sign into a passage doorway while Colonel Snow and his men try to defeat the witch.

After the fight, Colonel Snow sends medical details, autopsy reports and photos of the dead ghouls to his immediate superior in the USMC.
