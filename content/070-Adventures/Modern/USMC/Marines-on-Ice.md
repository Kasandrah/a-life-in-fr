>**GM's Note:** The group takes a military transport plane from Washington to Port Stanley in the Falkland Islands.  Then they travel by military helicopter to a Los Angeles-class nuclear submarine, the USS Miami, off the Falkland Islands.  Next is a submarine journey to Antarctica, with 12 Arctic Seals.  The only thing to break the monotony is an attack by “some large unidentifiable marine creature”, repelled by the use of torpedoes.
>
>Best US intelligence had located a site for the base on the Ronne Ice Shelf, close to Berkner Island.  The submarine surfaces in large ice caverns beneath the surface of the ice shelf.  The team, plus Arctic Seals, investigates tunnels leading from the farthest wall of the ice caverns off into the rock of the island.
>
>Ruins of a base are discovered.  Resident Elder Creatures had attacked the Nazis and driven them off, at least twice by the different uniforms on the frozen remains of Nazi soldiers.  One survivor is taken prisoner, but is insane from the trauma of his experiences.  The base would most likely remain inoperative for as long as the creatures are there.
>
>Avoiding the creatures as best they can, the team finds a functional gate in one tunnel.  Snow throws a transmitter through the gate prior to destroying it.  Antonio is called on to dispatch a hurricane-generating monster in a deeper cavern, which he does by bringing the roof down on top of it.
>
>The team retreats to the submarine and awaits discovery of the transmitter by Dean’s personnel.  The transmitter has gone through the gate to another Nazi base in the Amazon jungle.  Back to Brazil.

## Written by _[[Jenny-H|Jenny H.]]_ (Michal)

Along with a contingent of Arctic-trained Seals, the party boarded military flights to the Falkland Islands and rendezvoused with a submarine off the coast, then headed towards the Ronne Ice Shelf in a Los Angeles class submarine, the USS Miami.  Ilsenberg spent as much time close to the exit hatches as possible.  He really wasn't happy about being in a submarine.  Neither were Antonio and Angela. David and Michal played chess and bridge with them to take their minds off it.  It appealed to Antonio's competitive streak.

Closer to Antarctica, they encountered a large squid-like creature.  The crew called on Michal as the nominated best mythos expert.  She wasn't sure what it was, but suggested they persuade the creature, which was as large as the sub, that they weren't food.  A torpedo persuaded the creature to back off, but no one was sure they killed it.

When they reached the ice shelf, the equipment detected inconsistencies in heat sources on the edge of Berknen Island.  Further exploration determined there were natural fissures in the ice, with added pockets and formations that may or may not be natural.  They surfaced in one of the pockets, took the duckies and headed to a tunnel formation.

Along the tunnel, they found pieces of shredded bodies frozen in ice.  Closer examination revealed them to be Brown Shirts.  The wounds conformed to the creature that attacked them next - a hideous barrelled creature with 6 long 'arms' ending in long-fingered talons.

There were further branches in tunnels with no indication of the best way to proceed.  Various Seals were left at points to guard the rear.  All tunnels had piping sounds from them, more creatures and more shredded bodies - now with a mixture of Brown and Black Shirts.  Later, near a gate and some wrecked equipment, they found some bodies that had been flayed.

From behind, they heard footsteps and captured one of the Nazi soldiers.  He was beyond fear, at least of them.  He confirmed that the Nazis had been trying to set up the base when the same creatures that were attacking them attacked the Nazis.

From further on, the group heard a wind noise that was picking up in strength.  They sent Antonio, as the resident wind expert, to investigate, and took shelter from what was becoming a hurricane.  They heard a jarring crack and the wind died down.  Antonio had brought the roof down on the thing that was creating the wind, but had also trapped himself behind a 'rock' fall of ice.  Investigation of another branch found another way to the area he was in.

It was clear that the base wasn't in existence.  There had been an attempt to set it up, possibly at 2 different times, but the creatures objected and wiped out the attempts.  They blew up this side of the gate to discourage further attempts and fought a retreat to the sub.

Colonel Snow had thrown a transmitter through the gate before its destruction and back on the sub, the group used it to pinpoint the origin of the gate - the Amazon jungle.  Michal looked smug - that had been her guess.  Goldfarb reminded her that they hadn't known exactly where the Amazon base had been.  Michal reminded them of Caravalo's project.

He had told them that elements of the World Bank were funding projects to destroy the Brazilian rainforest.  He reasoned this from the debt the government had with the Bank - so high that they had no hope of repaying it.  The government was therefore forced to promote short-term projects (logging, mining, research into rainforest exploitation, etc.) over long-term ones.  At the time, the USMC had dismissed him as a kook.

They made their way back via the Falklands to Brasilia, nervously looking out for monsters of the deep.  They were met with satellite pictures of the suspected area.
