## Highlights

![[cc_ayrton_senna_2.jpeg]]

**Antonio Andrada da Silva**

Rebecca Wallace and her friend Anna Dunbain are threatened while walking home from school.  Suspicion eventually falls on the General, a director of a company that lost the bid for a new dam project to one of Ambassador Wallace’s business associates.

The dam site is in the mountains southwest of Brasilia, in an area so significant to the local Indians that they use any means necessary to protect it from intruders.

Major James Campbell is sent from USMC HQ in Washington to investigate Colonel Snow’s report of ghouls.  He accompanies the USMC to the interior to investigate the dam site.

Antonio Andrada da Silva’s crashed helicopter is found at the site, marked with traces of blood.  An undamaged and unmarked military troop-carrying helicopter is nearby, but the landing field is deserted.

Some bodies of paramilitary troopers are found on the road to the dam site, victims of the Indians' quick-acting poison darts.  Investigations into a cavern network near the dam site reveal the General and the rest of his men stealing crystals, a wounded Angela being magically protected by her husband, Antonio, and a huge wormlike creature that can travel through rock.  The rock worm proves very dangerous to all parties before the USMC blows up the cavern.  The General escapes into the tunnels, but the USMC seals the exits.

Major Campbell takes the unusual crystals found at the site to Washington for analysis, where the resulting explosion destroys a laboratory and kills three scientists.
