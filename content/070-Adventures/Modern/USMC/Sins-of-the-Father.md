>**GM's Note:** Ambassador Wallace’s mistress Xusa, who is unaware that she has poison under her new acrylic nails, accidentally poisons him.  Xusa rings the Embassy in a panic, and Michael McLane delegates the marines to recover Wallace discreetly from her apartment.  They find Wallace still unconscious, but don’t find Xusa.
>
>This latest scandal prompts the US State Department to send Mr Jefferson Dean to investigate goings-on at the Embassy, including oddities previously reported by Colonel Snow.  After several days of questioning, Dean asks Colonel Snow, Major Ilsenburg and Captain Goldfarb if they would like to come and work for him soon.
>
>Also, after investigating Xusa, her beautician and two of her beautician’s unsavoury associates, the party determines that Xusa has been murdered and that the whole situation was probably engineered by the General in retaliation for the incident at the dam.

## Written by _[[Jenny-H|Jenny H.]]_ (Michal)

Scandal continued to dog the US Embassy in Brazil.  The Marines were sent to retrieve Ambassador Wallace from the house of his mistress, Xusa, an actress.  He was suffering from poisoning, apparently entered into the bloodstream by nail marks on his back.  It was concluded after some investigation that Xusa had gouged the marks during lovemaking, been frightened by his collapse and rung the embassy.  She had then fled the scene.

This coincided with a visit by Mr. Jefferson Dean from the US State Department, who was investigating the competence of Wallace's reign.  While Wallace was hospitalized, Colonel Snow, Major Ilsenburg and Captain Goldfarb all gave him fairly damning reports.  They were also informed that Dean's visit was to investigate the strange events that had been happening, and they were all asked if they would be interested in transferring to work for Dean directly.  He apparently heads an X-Files style unit.

He also called in da Silva and Michal regarding their part in the Bormann events.  Michal asked him offhand if he had come across David and was surprised to for once get a reaction to her description.  Dean refused to say anything more than that he would make inquiries.  Michal haunted the Embassy corridors in hope of finding someone who knew more, but all she could manage was to 'accidentally' trip up someone who was carrying letters.  All she could see before the material was swept up again was her and David's names.

Dean eventually came back with an offer.  He believed that David was being held at a military installation in the USA.  Apparently, a US Navy corvette had found him half-dead, floating in the sea in the Gulf of Mexico.  He was carrying sensitive military documents but was refusing to answer any questions.  If Michal would help with the current investigation of Wallace's poisoning, she would be given a passport and taken to where David was being held.  She read between the lines that they wanted her to also persuade David to explain about the military documents, but that could wait.

Some inquiries elicited the name of Xusa's beautician, and Michal rang the premises, pretending to be a fan of Xusa's.  She was informed that she could not have her nails done the same way, as Xusa used an exclusive colour for her nails.  They were happy to make an appointment for her, but could not guarantee when, as the beautician was currently sick.  Some pleading got Michal the information that Xusa sometimes worked from home and her phone number.  They quickly discovered that the woman was not home and didn't look like she was coming back.

Michal announced that she was unable to continue the investigation because of a lack of local knowledge and demanded that she be taken to David.  Luckily Snow agreed, and they thankfully waved her off into the sunset again.

Eventually, Snow's team discovered that the beautician and her 2 male accomplices had set up Xusa and later murdered her to avoid detection.  Her body was found some days afterwards.

Snow suspected that they had been hired by The General, a long-term enemy of Wallace and by extension, the Marines (who had been doing all the real work!)  Wallace didn't survive the resulting scandal (though it was hushed up to a great extent) and was recalled to Washington.
