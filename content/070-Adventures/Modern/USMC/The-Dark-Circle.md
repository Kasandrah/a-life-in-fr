## Highlights

>**GM's Note:** I wasn't the GM for what follows. I'd informed my players I'd be interstate. When I returned, I was told one of my players had called the other players together and run the game in my absence.
>
>Had this stand-in GM asked me beforehand, I would have said hell no. They'd expressed a prior wish to get their overpowered vampire character's teeth into the necks of the mortal characters. Something I would never have allowed.
>
>Perhaps they thought **"[it being less difficult to obtain forgiveness for it after it was done, than permission for doing it](https://quoteinvestigator.com/2018/06/19/forgive/)"** meant they didn't have to ask me. I think they knew I'd say no, for obvious reasons, and jumped on the opportunity my absence presented.
>
>I have two choices regarding this content. Toss it out as "never happened when I was GM", or include it with annotations. As this was a learning experience for me, and not a good one, I've chosen to include it and the impact it had on my campaign.    

Colonel Snow receives news of changes in personnel at the Industrial Park, an outcome of the toll the marines had taken on the Neo-Nazis.  Johann Rattenhuber had become the new director of the Park.  David recognises Rattenhuber as a survivor of Hitler’s Bunker and one of Bormann’s aides in the camp in which David was held during WWII.

The team already knows, thanks to files previously obtained from David by Dean, that the Nazis had experimented with creating vampires and prolonging life using vampire blood, probably Gwyn’s.  The new Park director could be the same Rattenhuber from WWII, aged but not significantly so thanks to Gwyn’s blood.

On receiving this report from Ilsenburg, Dean instructs Colonel Snow to confirm whether Gwyn’s blood could prolong human life and to find ways of neutralizing her and Owain if they become a threat.  Colonel Snow talks to Antonio to find out whether a vampire’s blood has this property and how to kill Gwyn.  Antonio suggests prayer, beings with true faith, nuclear weapons, and flame-throwers. When pressed further, Antonio tells Colonel Snow that Gwyn’s personal weaknesses are unicorns, lovers, her Sire, and the Holy Grail.

Angela suggests that Colonel Snow and his team find Excalibur for Gwyn and then let her clan deal with her.  Gwyn apparently lost Excalibur to the Nazis when they experimented on her during WWII.  The team reports this newly gained information back to Dean and awaits further instructions.

A few weeks later, and in an apparently unrelated incident, Major Ilsenburg identifies a series of reports regarding recent events in and near a small village in the Amazon jungle, located some miles from the destroyed Nazi base.  There are reports of people suddenly going mad and alleged sightings of local monsters.  After deciding that investigating these incidents is within the scope of the team’s mission, the team, **excluding Colonel Snow who is on leave in the States**, prepares to travel to the village in question.

>**GM's Note:**  Myself, Snow's player, and Anderson's player were all absent. Only Goldfarb's and Ilsenburg's players were present. Another reason I could have denied this scenario as canon for my campaign. 
>
>The team, in whole or in part, wouldn't have been deployed without hard evidence that the "alleged sightings of local monsters" were directly linked to the team's current priority, and only after orders from Jefferson Dean, Colonel Snow's new superior.  My Jefferson Dean would have re-directed these reports to his Brazilian Intelligence Agency (BIA) contacts for initial confirmation of a link and action.

Before the team sets out, Michal and David are kidnapped in a white van.  The two appear to resist, but eventually go with their captors relatively peacefully.  Ilsenburg and Goldfarb proceed to give chase but receive a call from Dean instructing them to “let the Israelis take care of their own”.  Best guess based on analysis of the evidence and additional information from Dean is that Michal and David’s Shin Bet connections have caught up with them.

>**GM's Note:**  Given what Michal and David knew about prior events and "things supernatural", my Jefferson Dean would have pulled out all stops to recover them ASAP, regardless of who took them and why.  If he couldn't negotiate with Shin Bet for their immediate return, he would have informed BIA that Shin Bet agents were conducting unauthorised operations in Brazil, closed down any flow of US intelligence to Israel, and deported any known Shin Bet agents from the US.  As a last resort, he would have had Michal and David eliminated before Shin Bet could learn anything from them.

Ilsenburg and Goldfarb press on, requesting some additional support from Dean to replace their missing comrades.  While waiting for the new arrivals, they travel to the village to make some initial enquiries, posing as a pair of investigative newspaper journalists.

>**GM's Note:**  Something Dean's BIA contacts could have and should have done.  Acting undercover without prior BIA knowledge and agreement left the team open to charges of spying in a foreign country.

Here they find a priest who has been sent by his church to find out what has been going on.  The priest tells them that the situation is far too dangerous and they should leave it to the professionals.  Ilsenburg and Goldfarb also find out that a couple of hikers matching the description of Gwyn and Owain have been making their own inquiries in the area.

>**GM's Note:**  Given the threat posed by Gwyn and Owain, my Jefferson Dean gave Colonel Snow standing orders to avoid any contact with them and to notify Antonio immediately so he could deal with them.  This would have come out in game, **had either myself or Colonel Snow's player been in attendance**.

On returning to Brasilia, Ilsenburg and Goldfarb meet the additional support sent by Dean: _[[Dresdina-Belle-Cooper|Dresdina Cooper]]_, _[[The-White-Magicians#Kellion O’Shea|Kellion O’Shea]]_ and _[[Jenny-H#^14ff80|Mairi McPherson]]_.  Dean has placed Dresdina in charge of the new team.  Dresdina, Kellion, and Mairi give a guarded summary of their backgrounds and ask for up-to-date details on the situation at the village.

>**GM's Note:** The stand-in GM didn't conclude their scenario before I returned from interstate. They asked to complete it. I agreed and played my NPCs as PCs. Snow's and Anderson's players were still absent.
>
>Dresdina, Killion, and Mairi were civilians, raising the question of why they were in a USMC campaign. Because I'd already blurred the lines by including Angela, Antonio, Michal, David, Gwyn, and Owain. Ilsenburg and Goldfarb needed backup, so why not? More on this later. 

After a relatively uneventful boat trip up the Amazon and a few days' travel inland by four-wheel drive, the team arrived at the village.  Ilsenburg and Goldfarb question the priest again, this time mentioning a recent interview with Antonio Andrada da Silva that impresses the priest no end.

The priest becomes quite helpful, but is most insistent that “the women not be told about the situation”.  It becomes clear to Ilsenburg and Goldfarb that the priest isn’t as knowledgeable as he likes to think, but can however narrow down the location of the strange events and give them a few more leads.

The team follow-up reports of a werewolf and are the first to find a mutilated human corpse lying amongst evidence of a fight that includes large claw marks.  The team camped out and observed a flying devil.  Shooting at it has no effect.  They track down a vampire victim who subsequently died.  Keeping a vigil on the grave, they kill the emerging new vampire.

Deciding that things were getting serious, they contacted Antonio for advice.  After Ilsenburg describes the events of the last few days, Antonio concludes that this is not a result of real werewolves, devils, or vampires because “they just don’t work that way”.  Something else is going on.  His curiosity piqued by Ilsenberg’s description, Antonio decides that he and Angela should join up with the team as soon as possible.  Having his own helicopter, Antonio makes this sooner rather than later.

>**GM's Note:**  Not knowing where the stand-in GM was taking my game, having two powerful "PCs" up my sleeve was quite comforting initially, until I realised the GM could place limits on them where none had previously existed.  It was about this time in the game I concluded the whole thing was a terrible idea.
>
>In hindsight,  I don't recommend playing immensely powerful, deliberately unlimited NPCs as PCs in a game run by someone else.  The risk of "they can do that" meeting "no they can't" from a stand-in GM unaware of the NPC's true capabilities is too high.  You either compromise the NPCs, setting a nasty precedent for future games under your control, or you make things too difficult for the stand-in GM.

Once the da Silvas arrive at the village, the expanded team follows the trail of clues to an area near the old Nazi base.  Dresdina and Kellion discover the entrance by falling down a deep hole.  Owain and Gwyn catch them midway, stopping their fall, and carry them to the bottom of the shaft.  Here leaves and animal bones crunch underfoot with every step.

Torches reveal a man-made tunnel that leads to a gate.  Gwyn and Owain walk through this gate, and return with news of another gate beyond the first that is impassable to them.  Antonio and Angela walk through the first gate and return after not being able to pass through the second gate either.  Kellion and Mairi attempt to walk through the first gate, but cannot.

This suggests to the team that only supernatural beings can pass through the first gate.  How to get the human members of the party through?  With no personal experience of becoming supernatural, the human members of the team are at a loss how to achieve this.  Gwyn suggests, with a perfectly straight face, that she can turn the human team members into ghouls “just for a little while”.

>**GM's Note:** The raison d'être for the stand-in GM's entire scenario. The one thing I'd not allowed since Gwyn had been present in my USMC campaign. From all the way back to [[Fire-and-Air|Fire and Air]]. I'd been played.

There is complete silence in the tunnel.  After a few minutes, Kellion replies, “you must be joking”.  Antonio, looking coldly at Gwyn, informs Kellion she isn’t joking and that, given what may be at stake, her suggestion should be considered seriously.  While Antonio can’t turn humans into supernatural beings, he can certainly purge any taint of ghoulishness from them afterwards.  “It’ll just hurt a bit."

Kellion and Ilsenburg each volunteer to taste a drop of blood provided by Gwyn.  Each walks through the gate and up to the second gate with Gwyn and Antonio.  Angela and Owain wait with Goldfarb, Dresdina and Mairi on the other side of the first gate.  Passage through the second gate is possible for Kellion and Ilsenburg after several attempts and nearly an hour’s delay.  Neither Gwyn nor Antonio can follow.

On the floor of a large vaulted chamber at the end of a short passage, Kellion and Ilsenburg find a circular pattern of eight smaller circles and one in the middle.  Arcane silver symbols are marked out on each of the smaller circles.  Beyond the circle was another gate.  After returning through the second gate, Kellion and Ilsenburg explain what they have found to Gwyn and Antonio.

Gwyn identifies the circle as an inverted mirror of her Sire’s Circle.  While Lillane uses her Circle to communicate with the forces of Order in their many and varied realms, the Nazis would use this Dark Circle to communicate with the forces of Chaos.  All that was required to activate the Dark Circle was the power of a magical item like Excalibur.  That was the reason the Nazis had captured Gwyn and taken the sword.  This is the culmination of a grand plan begun during WWII.

Antonio tells the others that long ago he assisted Lillane in the reconstruction of her Circle, destroyed when a force of Chaos had been summoned into it.  If the reverse holds true, summoning a force of Order from within the vaulted chamber should destroy the Dark Circle.  He tells them that humans of strong faith can summon the Archangel Michael in times of extreme danger.  Ilsenburg volunteers to attempt to summon Michael.

Antonio and Gwyn return to Owain, Dresdina, Mairi, and Goldfarb and inform them of what lies beyond and how the team is going to deal with it.  Mairi volunteers to assist Ilsenburg summon Michael.  With the aid of a drop of blood from Gwyn (and assurances from Antonio), Dresdina, Mairi, and Goldfarb walk through the first gate with Owain, Antonio, and Gwyn.

In the glowing light of the second gate, Antonio instructs Mairi and Ilsenburg in the fine art of summoning an Archangel.  When that is fully understood and the effects of Gwyn’s blood have worn off, the once again human team members walk through the second gate.

Here, a sorcerer carrying Excalibur disturbs them.  They fight him and eventually defeat him, but not before Mairi is injured.  She and Ilsenburg summon Michael, and the team flees back through the second gate before the destruction starts.  The resulting blast destroys the second gate and collapses the tunnel in the immediate vicinity.  Antonio and Gwyn heal the injuries incurred by the team.

After more drops of blood from Gwyn and assurances from Antonio, the team walks back through the first gate, climbs (or flies) up the shaft, and heads back to the waiting four-wheel drives.

On the journey back to the village, they are met on the rough road by a black stretch limousine, a vehicle that couldn’t possibly have travelled anywhere on this track.  The uniformed driver, a tall, thin man, opens the door, and a darkly beautiful woman of immense charisma and very expensive dress sense steps from the car.

Gwyn kneels before her Sire, Lillane, and offers her the sword.  Lillane demands an explanation for Switzerland.  Gwyn simply states that she made a mistake.  Owain explains the children had been slain by fearful cultists after Gwyn had attacked the cultists.  Lillane accepts the explanation with the comment that she won’t slay Gwyn just yet.  Lillane returns Excalibur into Gwyn’s keeping and leaves in the limousine.

Gwyn and Owain made their own way to wherever they were going.  The rest of the team made their way back to Brasilia in the vain hope that this would now be the end.

>**GM's Note:** After "The Dark Circle", all my players but the stand-in GM complained they were playing the other player's game, not mine.  This problem had been building since [[Fire-and-Air|Fire and Air]], but after being locked into the stand-in GM's story as bit players, they didn't want to continue.
>
>My intention with the United States Marine Corps (USMC) characters was to explore the outcome of matching heavy weaponry against various Cthulhu and non-Cthulhu threats. By catering to the wishes of only one player, the stand-in GM, I'd allowed my game to drift off mission and, finally, out of my hands.  My naivety and inability to say no when necessary almost cost me another campaign. 
>
>To address the glaring imbalance in player agency, I asked the stand-in GM to create a new USMC character comparable with the others.  Hence, _[[USMC-Fireteam-SG-21#Major Kris Morrissey|Major Kris Morrissey]]_ replaced _[[Jenny-H#^27cdd0|Michal Hertzeanu]]_ and _[[Jenny-H#^59824f|David Abrahamson]]_.  I also ensured _[[020-The-Antediluvians#Gwyn|Agnetha]] and [[020-The-Antediluvians#Owain and Juanita|Wim]] Wenders_ never appeared in this campaign again.
