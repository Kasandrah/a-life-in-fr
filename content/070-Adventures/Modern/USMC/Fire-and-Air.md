>**GM's Note:** Wallace is replaced by Ambassador Lisa Bennett, a highly competent career diplomat in her mid-forties with considerable clout in Washington circles.  Ambassador Bennett proves her mettle by delaying for over a week Dean’s order for the party and the da Silvas to join him in Washington immediately.
>
>During the delay Sgt. Anderson reports strange goings-on at the industrial park.  David and Michal have a late response to their bait (Bormann’s “Mein Kamph”) and arrange to meet the interested party for dinner at an exclusive Brasilia restaurant.  Antonio and Angela, now in Brasilia in preparation for the flight to Washington, are sent in first to establish a forward observation post.  Michal and David follow and wait at a separate table across the room from the da Silvas.
>
>_[[020-The-Antediluvians#Gwyn|Agneta]]_ and _[[020-The-Antediluvians#Owain and Juanita|Wim Wenders]]_ arrive, Agneta appearing somewhat distressed for no apparent reason.  Conversation over dinner is stilted; their desire for the book borders on aggressive.  Agneta seems to recognise Antonio and vice versa.  The evening deteriorates into a farce of tragic and epic proportions.
>
>Angela, angry at being left out in the cold by Antonio and goaded by Agneta, leaves the hotel in the small hours of the morning.  The next thing Antonio knows is that she had an accident driving back to Sao Paulo and has been seriously injured.  On arrival at the hospital, he is informed that she has died under somewhat unusual circumstances.  The party is shocked and tries to comfort Antonio.
>
>A fire starts in the morgue and rapidly grows into an out-of-control inferno.  The hospital staff marshals all reserves to evacuate the patients, while Antonio heads for the morgue.  A miraculous wind turns the fire on itself and puts it out.  Antonio and Goldfarb search the ruins of the morgue for Angela.  Goldfarb doesn’t know why he expects to find Angela, but find her he does, alive and untouched in one of the few morgue drawers remaining intact.  At Angela’s request, he closes the drawer and calls Antonio over.
>
>Antonio gathers Angela, still in a body bag, up in his arms and carries her out of the morgue, through seemingly oblivious firemen and rescue workers to a secluded lane at the back of the hospital.  Here Goldfarb returns with a car to take them back to their hotel.
>
>The following morning explanations are sought.  Abdiel makes his first appearance, rattling the party and seriously impairing the da Silvas relationship with the USMC and David and Michal.  An uneasy truce is established with the Wenders, who are really, according to Antonio, a powerful vampire named Gwyn and her ghoul Owain with urgent business in Antarctica.  Something about a missing sword.  This and the suspected future summoning of an Outer God serve as common ground for Antonio and Agneta (or Gwyn), despite a long history as antagonists.
>
>The still human members of the party can only watch and wonder.  Ilsenburg builds a detailed profile of Antonio/Abdiel and, via the Embassy’s intelligence staff, sets the resources of the National Security Council on a search for others of Antonio’s kind.
>
>After all the excitement of the previous night, the flight to Washington is uneventful.  These days military transportation is only occasionally dangerous, and not usually between Brasilia and Washington.

## Written by _[[Jenny-H|Jenny H.]]_ (Michal)

The team headed back to San Paolo.  The only leads left were the Industrial Park and the books from Gustav's house.  On the way back, Anderson reported a break-in at the Park.  He and the other marine watching the Park had seen a couple running from one building.  It was dark, but they appeared to be moving incredibly fast.  A person had standing jumped onto the top of one building and then helped the other up, then they had moved out of sight.

Snow contacted the police, but as yet there was no report made.  Before going to investigate what his marines were on, Snow dropped Michal off at Antonio's house, where she asked David to translate the papers and books they had taken.  They were the usual Nazi propaganda and more information on secret societies, but little of immediate use.

Back at Antonio's house, he explained about ley lines.  The Nazi base in the mountains and the one in Antarctica were prime areas of power.  The shape of the base showed it concentrated power and could summon something big and nasty.

Snow reported this to Dean and was immediately advised that he and the team (including Michal, David, Antonio and Angela) would be recalled to Washington, as there was additional information that needed to be discussed face to face.

The new Ambassador impressed the marines by successfully mounting delaying tactics, showing she had considerable clout.  Dean was fairly high in the scheme of things, so thwarting him would take some effort.

While this was going on, they found an ad in the national newspaper obliquely referring to the copy of Mien Kamph that they had offered for sale earlier.  With delays, it was decided this was worth following up.  They did the usual trick of renting a room in a swish hotel and then made a call, arranging for David, Michal and Goldfarb to meet them for dinner.

Angela and Antonio were sent early to act as lookouts and backup.  A couple arrived, introducing themselves as Wim and Agneta Wenders.  They looked at the book and were keen to buy it.  David and Michal noticed Agneta seemed quite nervous, not eating and fidgeting with the cutlery.  Wim did most of the talking.  They then realised that she and Antonio had recognised each other.  Angela and Wim were unaware of this and confused by their respective behaviour.

>**GM's Note:** I allowed one player to use their powerful supernatural characters, _[[020-The-Antediluvians#Gwyn|Agneta]]_ and _[[020-The-Antediluvians#Owain and Juanita|Wim Wenders]]_ from _[[040-Dark-Ages|Dark Ages]]_, while everyone else played mortal humans. My blunder created an unsatisfactory in-game experience for my other players, who all voiced serious concerns about the power imbalance and its detrimental impact on my game.
>
>They felt their characters were bit players for a couple who could have dealt with the bad guys all on their own. My other players also objected to one player running four characters: Agneta, Wim, _[[Jenny-H#^27cdd0|Michal Hertzeanu]]_, and _[[Jenny-H#^59824f|David Abrahamson]]_. My blatant favouritism had soured the game for my other players and turned out to be the beginning of _[[The-Dark-Circle#^714712|the end]]_ for my USMC campaign.

David asked the couple to come back the next day.  He had been disturbed by Wim's detailed knowledge of the war period.  The couple was not happy with this, insistent that they were interested in buying the book straight away, but eventually left.

Back in the room, Antonio hedged around how he knew the woman, but eventually explained that she was a vampire (with references to turning into bats and graveyards) and that the man was her ghoul (confusing the PCs, who had only heard of CoC ghouls).

Agneta had been listening in to the conversation (from elsewhere in the building) and was most offended by the terms she was described in.  She phoned Antonio, who decided to speak in Spanish to her so that Angela wouldn't be able to listen in.  Agneta, replying in Portuguese so that Angela had some chance of overhearing her end of the conversation - or perhaps just to annoy Antonio, reminded him that he had his own secrets that he wouldn't like to be discussed openly, especially in front of Angela.  He decided to be civilized and invited her to join them.

When they arrived, they looked somewhat different, having discarded their disguises.  Antonio and Agneta retreated to opposite corners (literally) and traded barbed comments.  David realised that he had seen the woman before - apparently dead in Buchenwald.  Bormann had brought the body in and intended to perform experiments on it.

Agneta finally confessed she was looking for a sword.  Antonio was amazed that she had lost it.  Agneta insisted she hadn't lost it, it had been taken from her.  The group deduced that she was talking about Excalibur, that she had been entrusted with the safety of this sword and that it would be definitely of interest to the neo-Nazis.  Bormann had it last, but he had not taken it through either gate, so it would have to have been given to someone else.

Agneta believed the sword was in Antarctica - the main base.  To this point she had been unaware of the Nazis' intention to summon an Outer God and was amazed that anyone would be stupid enough to want to.

They arranged to meet up in Washington.  Antonio gave Agneta his mobile phone number to contact them.  "You have figured out how to use a phone, haven't you?"  Agneta sailed out of the room, commenting to Angela, "It was nice to meet you again." To Angela's recollection, they had never met.

Everyone retired to his or her respective residences or hotel rooms for the night.  At 3.00 am, Antonio rang Michal and David, informing them that he and Angela had argued and that she had run off.  After various ineffectual attempts to find her, Antonio received a call that she had crashed her car on the way home to Sao Paolo and was in hospital with serious, but stable burns.

Michal and David drove Antonio to the hospital, where they found out that her burns were worse than expected and that she had in fact died.  Michal finally called the marines to let them know what had happened.

Goldfarb arrived at the hospital while the doctors were trying to talk Antonio out of seeing Angela's body because of the severe burns and got a look at the medical notes.  Angela's burns had not been severe enough to cause death and the doctors were grasping at an explanation of spontaneous human combustion.  Soon afterwards, there was a fire alarm and a report of a major incendiary device going off (with no accompanying explosion) in the morgue.  Ilsenburg, co-incidentally in the same burns unit as Angela had been taken to, decided at this point to check himself out of the hospital.

Antonio, followed by Goldfarb, headed into the morgue, using his wind powers to put out the flames.  Goldfarb found a very much alive and well Angela hiding in one of the gurneys.  Everyone exited stage left to the hotel where Angela ate a huge breakfast and explained that it had been time for her to wake up.  She was the Phoenix (there is only one) and Antonio is an Angel.

The original reports of Angela's death were explained to the media as a case of mistaken identity.  Otto's spooks in the basement had followed up Wim and Agneta Wenders and discovered that they had originally been identified as among the dead from the Order of the Solar Temple at a farmhouse in Cheiry in 1994.  As they had later turned up elsewhere, it was determined that the original identification was incorrect.  The major decided that the rumour of a special sword might be the reason the pair had linked up with the cult.

Agneta rang to welcome Angela to her new status and to request use of the book en route to Washington.  Antonio agreed, and Wim arrived to collect it after Otto had turned up with the final travel arrangements for Antonio and Angela to travel to Washington.  Wim asked to speak to Antonio privately, who revealed afterwards that he had asked him to intercede with Lillane, Agneta's sire.  She had apparently broken one of the fundamental tenets of her clan and killed children.

Otto called in Colonel Snow for a briefing and asked Antonio lots of questions about the vampires, Angela and himself.  Antonio was in an expansive mood and answered them fully, though he was less than complimentary about the vampires.  He even showed himself in his true form.  Most on-lookers saw this as intimidation, especially putting up the backs of the religious in the room.

He was then taken aback when Snow flatly refused to work with the vampires and also refused to work with Antonio if he did.  "If you're an angel and she's a vampire, why did you let her go?"  Antonio tried to explain it in terms of allies with little conviction.  Snow asked for a transfer to somewhere safe - like Iran.

"Well, dear, that did a lot for building trust, didn't it?" commented Angela sarcastically to Antonio.  They left for Washington on a military transport that day and were then escorted to the Pentagon.  Goldfarb had already left for the capital and done some research on angels, deducing that Antonio was probably Abdiel.
