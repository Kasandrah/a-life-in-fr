## Highlights

A “Colour out of Space” has hitched a ride to Earth on the space shuttle Atlantis.  The shuttle crashes in Kansas where the Colour begins eating the citizens of Gove and increasing its strength as it attempts to protect its young.  While NASA covers up the fate of the mission with false newscasts and press releases, Jefferson Dean sends in the Marines, under the command of Colonel Snow, to deal with the menace.

The Marines finally immobilised the Colour by trapping it in a magnetic field generated by three cranes from Buddy’s Wrex.  The military and scientific community is left with the puzzle of how to contain it.  In the weeks following the crash, the military brings in heavy-duty magnetic equipment, purchases the land and sets up a containment centre from which the Colour can never escape - theoretically.
