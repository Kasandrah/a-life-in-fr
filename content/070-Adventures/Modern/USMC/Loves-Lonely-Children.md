## Highlights

A man and his wife, under the control of a Cthulhu monster, murder their daughter (a prostitute) and dump her body in the Plaza.  A USMC private falls under suspicion, as he was the last person seen with the prostitute before her body was found.

The USMC track down the killers and confront the man, his wife, and the monster they summon in the couple’s seedy house in a run-down part of Brasilia.  The monster returns to its own realm when the man and his wife are killed in the fight.
