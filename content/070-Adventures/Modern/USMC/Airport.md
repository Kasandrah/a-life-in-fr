## Highlights

A Cthulhu magic item has been stolen from a major crime scene (the sabotage of a London power plant) being covered by the Metropolitan Police.  Angela suspects that cultists have infiltrated New Scotland Yard (again).  The magic item is now in transit to New York, with three cultists, all members of the Metropolitan Police.  Angela informs Dean and provides descriptions of the package, a “lightning rod”, and the cultists.

Dean sets Dresdina and some others to watch the airport in New York.  The package in question is rerouted to another aircraft - destination Brasilia International Airport.  The pilots of the flight from London reported some problems with flight avionics, so aircraft sidelined for investigation.  Suspect electronic interference from illegal cargo item (absorbing, not emitting) - to strong to be passenger laptop, mobile phone, etc.  Mechanics found nothing wrong with the aircraft.

Dean informs Major Ilsenberg.  Wait and see if the last link in the chain can be caught.  Gives the Major the arrival date, time and flight details and a description of the package.  Must keep this very low key - no obvious USMC action on Brazilian soil.  Brazil has just established the civilian Brazilian Intelligence Agency (BIA) and we don’t want to come to their notice.

Meet the aircraft at the airport (at night) - aircraft delayed.  Pilot has reported intermittent problems with the avionics but continuing on to Brasilia.  Meet Antonio and Angela on business trip to Brasilia (they remain in the coffee shop in case they are needed) - party to notice a BIA agent is following the couple, probably due to their unusual activities of late.  Antonio has already “made” the agent.

Aircraft arrives, drifts off the runway on landing and crashes into the boarding lounge between the runway viewing area and the main airport entrance/exit areas.  As per photos of recent crash.  Many injuries, some very serious.  Power drain commences from immediate area.  Lights go out - all dark and still onboard the aircraft.  Communications out from nearby boarding lounges.

Immediate response - three cultists in the boarding lounge awaiting arrival of the plane climb through the smashed aircraft windows, past the dead pilots and into the main passenger cabin.  All three collapse due to the effects of the lightening rod.  Airport staff help passengers waiting in the boarding lounge and boarding lounge staff injured in the initial crash.  Boarding lounge manager (slightly injured) asking for calm and who needs help.  Uninjured directed away towards main airport area.

Antonio and Angela rush to help - Antonio recognised by a small girl who asks him to help her mother - he’s reluctant but does help.  Angela says that children know him for what he is - not what he appears to be.

Emergency crews, ambulance crews, airport police, then Federal Police.  Key problem is something on the aircraft is absorbing all energy, including wireless transmissions, etc.  Can’t get power operated rescue tools to work near the aircraft.  People feeling faint, tired then collapse if they don’t move away - will die from heart/respiration failure if not dragged away.  Rescue workers now among the casualties.

Circle of effect grows exponentially over time - will engulf all of Brasilia in a couple of hours.  Players must solve the problem before the “lightning rod” gains enough energy to ignite and summon some big gnarly monster, not to mention the collateral damage.  Players should also remove Antonio and Angela from the initial area of effect, before the lightening rod starts pulling energy from them.  Angela could provide enough power to ignite the item all on her own.
