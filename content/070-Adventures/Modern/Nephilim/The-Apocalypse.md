## Highlights

The group meets Gabby’s new boyfriend, Arkie Whitmore, at their favourite occult bookshop.  Here they also meet _[[Nephilim-PCs#Robert Howard)|Robert Howard]]_, a nascent Earth Nephilim.

After identifying an exhibit at the Museum of Fine Art as being real people turned to stone, the group traces the source through a gallery to one Rashida Sulaiman (28), a Gorgon who claims to be Nick’s aunt.  They negotiate an understanding with a Gorgon that, in exchange for her not turning more people to stone, the group must recover her stasis item, a Mycenaean vase, from the museum.  Rashida lives in Cleveland, but she has “family” in Washington: Tahirah (37), Asesimba (24) and Dalia (10).

This they agree to do at a later date, although one of the museum tour guides is Magdalene, a member of the Prieure de Sion.  Through Magdalene, Remy is introduced to Jeanne Saint-Clair de Chambord, Commandeur of the Prieure de Sion, East North America, who lives and works in Washington, D.C.

The group must also contend with an apocalypse that will be brought about by the three ancient Selenim freed from under the Serpent Mound at the end of the previous initiation.  The Selenim are going to summon the “end times” Wendigo which will destroy Cleveland and surrounds.  The group enlisted the aid of Attica, a Fire Ka Dragon who is an enforcer for the Emperor Arcanum.

The group has also befriended Michael van der Kren III, a nascent Fire Ka Nephilim and the son of a rich industrialist.  Michael’s father is a member of the very exclusive Yacht Club, on whose grounds the ancient Selenim are preparing to summon the end times Wendigo.

After a battle in caves on the Yacht Club’s island, the group has destroyed at least two of the three Selenim.  Some question remains as to the fate of the sole female Selenim, but Attica believes she died when he flamed the tunnel to clear away her writhing, entrapping tree roots.

Initiation closes with Remy and Bethany and _[[Nephilim-NPCs#Mark|Mark]]_ and Jeanne enjoying a cruise to Hawaii on a luxury liner, at Remy’s expense.
