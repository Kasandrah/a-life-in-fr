# Nova Scotia

>**Player's Note:** I don't remember playing through this, but it was dated 2001 or earlier.

**Written by [[Jenny-H|Jenny H.]]**

_[[Dresdina-Belle-Cooper|Dresdina]]_ was dreaming, but it was a dream quite unlike any she had ever had before. An army of people, dressed in elaborate, archaic armour, marched across a desert area. Their faces were oddly distorted, almost inhuman. They fought opponents who were even less human - tall, thin, evil-looking. Clouds of swirling colours leapt from their finger-tips.

A voice semi-sang, semi-chanted a song:
"Three brothers in a windy land,
Far beyond death's cold hand,
Serving their sister tall and grand -
Who waits high on the hill.
When shall she vacate her nest?
Only when one brother is laid to rest."

Dresdina felt the evil reach out for her. She woke.

She was late to work, her mind concerned with the night's dream and its meaning. Most of the team were already out, working on their respective assignments. Mostly they chased dead ends - a strange symbol graffitied on a wall that turned out to be a drug code, churches with weird names and weirder parishioners, but a normal New York weirdness, rather than occultism. She had been wondering why she'd left the FBI. Wherever evil was, it wasn't here.

Jocelyn (or Shadow, depending on what Dresdina prefers) handed her a typed sheet. "Can you swim?" At her nod (I haven't got your character sheet, I'll assume a yes here.) Jocelyn added. "You're leaving for Nova Scotia on the 2.00pm plane. Let me know anything you need. Algren will organize the tickets.

Dresdina read the sheet she had been given. It was a series of reports from a small community on the west coast of Nova Scotia:
 - A weird-shaped, 6 legged octopus washed upon shore and subsequently lost.
 - A shower of small fish.
 - The phones suddenly going dead for exactly one hour for no mechanical reason.
 - And more.

In isolation, none were particularly noteworthy and could be easily explained away, but together there was a pattern. It could be nothing, but that was what they were paid to check.

Before she left for the airport, Jocelyn rang. "Algren will be coming with you."
 
"Why?"

"He can sail. He'll explain what's happened."
 
At the airport, Algren handed her an update. The day before, a team of scientists had set out to follow a pod of whales. They hadn't come back as planned and couldn't be raised on the radio. The weather wasn't bad and the sea was glassy smooth. A search had found no trace, but a purple cloud had been seen on the horizon. Early that morning. It was being discounted as a freak meteorological effect.
