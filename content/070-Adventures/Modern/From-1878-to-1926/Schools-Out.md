## Involvement

Alex arranged a temporary position as a teacher of visual appreciation at the school.

## Highlights

This girls school was attended by one vampire (Carmilla), one teacher who turned into a Jungle Jane type, part Deep Ones and murderous psychopaths.

There was one large monster in the attic which was worshipped by a coven of teachers and students. The coven was disbanded and elder signs placed at the doorway to the monster’s enclosure.
