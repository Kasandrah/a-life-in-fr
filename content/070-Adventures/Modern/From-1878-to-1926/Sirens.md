## Involvement

Alex’s mother’s sister married and emigrated to Napier, New Zealand 45 years ago. Her daughter, Julie, asked Alex for help when Julie’s husband disappeared.

## Highlights

Investigations into the disappearance of Julie’s husband, John McAlister, while he and Julie were staying at Robert Sheldon’s farm. Results were the discovery of a siren monster, Sheldon’s “wife”, in a cave network on the farm. Siren was destroyed.

Julie was subsequently kidnapped by a cult group and rescued by the party from a cavern network under Napier. An earthquake occurred as a result of the priest casting an unsuccessful summoning spell and allowing an uncontrolled monster to appear. The monster left after the earthquake.
