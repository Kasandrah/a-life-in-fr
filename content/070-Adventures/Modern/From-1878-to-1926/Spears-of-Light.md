## Involvement

Aletta was brought in by _[[Neil-F#^901589|Stanley Jenks]]_.

## Highlights

Cultists were operating under the guise of Merlin Lodge and Observatory curators on Taunton Peak, Glossop. They planned to destroy the entire town and all inhabitants as part of a bargain with the Cthulhu creature (Yog Shothoth) in exchange for Merlin’s Staff of Power.

## Injuries Suffered

Seriously hurt when blown through a solid wooden door by an exploding window in a wizard’s loft. Fell down the spiral staircase to the next floor. Glass shards in her back, serious bruising and battering, unconscious because of the force of impact with the door.

## Possessions Acquired

£15,000 black diamond

£12,000 red diamond

£10,000 rectangular-cut ruby

Magic dragon eye stone, fist-sized, very heavy, adds 1d4 to power when carried on person.
