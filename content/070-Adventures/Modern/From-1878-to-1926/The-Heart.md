## Involvement

Aletta was asked by _[[Neil-F#^901589|Stanley Jenks]]_ to read the writing (coded spell) on an Arabic plate given to him by Michal. Drawn in as a friend of Stanley’s.

## Highlights

Clues led to the discovery of haunted manor ruins and an underground temple/dungeon complex on the island of Rammos (Scilly Islands group off Cornwall).

Sacred and powerful “Heart” recovered from haunted manor after time-warping via spell back to 1100s (now in the Bank of England vault).
