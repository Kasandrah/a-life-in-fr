## Involvement

In England photographing stately homes of the wealthy and titled aristocracy for the American magazines. Chalotholme is on her list.

## Highlights

Mary Hiskins was being haunted by a huge, invisible, ghost-like horse intent on killing her. Horse turned out to be a squartch summoned by one of the other house guests (a rejected suitor) in order to kill her before her wedding. Squartch was dismissed after a spell book was found.

## Injuries Suffered

Nearly killed when attacked by the squartch in the basement of the house. Severe bruising, broken ribs, and wounds on the lower chest and back. Scars remain. (Squartch tried to bite her in half.)
