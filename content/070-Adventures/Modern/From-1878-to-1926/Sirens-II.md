## Involvement

Returned to New Zealand to check up on her cousin, Julie McAlister and Julie’s mother Rosalie, Alex’s aunt.

## Highlights

Korikea’s tribe were being framed by Maori cultists for the disappearance of a local white family. This was in revenge for Korikea’s involvement in the destruction of Napier cultists some two years earlier.

The cultist tribe had desecrated Korikea’s burial grounds by placing ghouls in the caves and had stolen precious magic items from Korikea’s tribe (Tarangi included). The group helped destroy the ghouls, recover the missing white family and recover the missing magic items.
