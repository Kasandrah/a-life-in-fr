## Highlights

Lord Vincent Wild hired the group to find a party of archaeologists missing from a dig site near Kigoma on the eastern shore of Lake Tanganyika. Following the murder of a London professor associated with the project and the theft of parcels destined for the professor from the London docks, the party became actively involved.

As retribution for the party's interference, Maggie was kidnapped and taken to East Africa. The party followed and rescued her from cat cultists at the Kigoma dig site, but not before she had momentarily become a big cat.
