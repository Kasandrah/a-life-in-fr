## Involvement

Mary Rafe is Diana’s cousin. Diana was visiting her at the time of these incidents.

## Highlights

Sir Giles inherited Boker Hall after a death in the family. Subsequent investigations revealed the hall to be populated by terrible monsters, the hall tower a living entity supported by the Phineas who acted as its eyes and ears, caverns underneath occupied by a group of homicidal vagrants, and Chileskas the corrupt policeman covering Phineas’ activities.

## Injuries Suffered

Spear wound to the outside of her left ankle (scarred badly on healing).

The following is from the diary of Lady Marion Ravenwood. Btess.

29th May 1922

We had dinner at Rafe's house tonight.  It was quite pleasant; Tom, Robin, Diana, a relative of Mrs Rafe, Rafe, his wife, his sister Louise, John (who are all staying there), N. L., Tommi and I.

While we were there Rafe asked John, who asked N. L. if he would look at a house he had inherited.  Rafe has broken his leg and can't go himself, and John has to go to Brisbane on urgent business tomorrow.  I became interested in the situation when I heard the story.  The house is called Boker Hall and is in St Andrews, east of the city.  I thought at once of that last cryptic entry in Dafa's diary, "Shunnig Gungerran.  What language?  East Ad."  Boker Hall is on the River Torrens, about a mile east of the Hackney Bridge, where Dafa's body was found.  Was it there that Dafa had spent those missing days?

Rafe had inherited the house when the last Boker baronet died, leaving only illegitimate children.  He had only recently taken possession of the house, as the direct descendants had contested the will.  Rafe sent three servants to look over the house a week ago, but they haven't returned.  The police (or rather Detective-Inspector Mark Chileskas - why does that name keep cropping up!) said they had gone to the house but there had been no signs of entry.  He concluded they must have run away, which is ludicrous for servants of up to 20 years' service.

I insisted on going with N.L. and taking a few solid males with us.  We settled the group fairly quickly; N. L. and myself, Robin (who is after all a doctor), Tom, Diana (who insisted on coming - I think she is lonely and wanted the company), and Arnie, someone Tom knows, who apparently helped Dafa out on the dig.  By all reports, he's huge, strong, and also intelligent.  With that lot, we should be able to cope with most things.

30th May 1922

We all met outside Boker Hall and began by looking around the outside of the grounds.  The place looked terribly neglected.  The garden area was badly overgrown.  There were signs of entry on the gates.  So much for Chileskas' story!  We did a circuit of the walls and discovered Zadok Boker camped out the back.  I verified who it was from Dafa's diary.  He offered us tea and I talked to him for a while; he was every bit as enigmatic as Dafa had written.  Quite mad by any sane or logical standard, but what we are dealing with isn't remotely sane or logical.  Perhaps he's saner than any of us.

It was frustrating in many ways, as it was hard to get straight answers to any questions and there was so much I wanted to know.  He tended to give literal answers to our questions.  E.g. to Robin's question, "How did you get here?" he answered, "With my feet".  He did tell us that he had been raised in Boker Hall but had been thrown out.
