## Highlights

Adventure begins at a New Year’s Eve party at the Vincenzo household, a new palazzo-style villa in the Audubon Terrace district of New York. Guests are served by an English butler.

Early the following morning an “Iris Green” is discovered locked in Don Vincenzo’s safe, which is hidden behind a Tintorello painting in the study. She is actually _[[Jenny-H#^502c32|Abish Pratt]]_, a Mormon, and she is being pursued by Silas Blacke, Sorcerer, and his henchman Elder Henry Broadmountain, both from Salt Lake City, Utah, and both belonging to the Mormon Sect “Sons of Dann”.

Abish has stumbled across their plan to assassinate the President and the vice-president of the USA, plunging the country into civil war, then to take control for themselves. Blacke and Broadmountain have access to cult items and books, giving them great power.

In order to help Abish and defeat the cultists, the party travels to the Dreamlands, where Abish is the ruler of a city. Here they seek the advice of a sage, then return to the waking world. They travel to Buffalo and Dunkirk in New York State, fight a shoggoth summoned by Jabez Silk, Right Hand impostor, and return to New York to fight it out with Blacke and his henchmen at his warehouse. The party had the support of the FBI and Hoover.
