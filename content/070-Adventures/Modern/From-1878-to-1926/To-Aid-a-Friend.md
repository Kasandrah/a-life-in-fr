## Involvement

Heather invited Alex to a Celtic dig site near Carter Fell.  Alex accepted as she was passing by on her way back to London after visiting MacDonald relatives (of Clan Ranald) in the north of Scotland and in the Outer Hebrides.

## Highlights

The spirit of a monster possessed Heather as she investigated items dug from the site. The key to her possession was a bracelet she picked up from the site and placed on her wrist.

Alex released Heather by destroying the bracelet and the remains of the monster.
