## Involvement

Caught in the witch’s invoked storm while returning from Chalotholme to London.

## Highlights

While returning to London from Chalotholme by car a fierce storm broke. The group sought refuge in a little cottage which turned out to be haunted by a red witch. The witch controlled a giant bloodsucking weasel who had killed previous occupants of the cottage.

After searching the house the wizened remains of the witch were discovered in a secret room in the attic and were burned and buried.
