## Involvement

This episode begins when Sarah’s grandmother, the Cornish witch _Grania Heale_, has a premonition.  Grania talks it over with _Matthew Patterson_, a close friend of the family and Sarah’s “uncle”.  In Grania’s premonition, Sarah and Charles are killed.  Grania begs Matthew to intercede and send them somewhere safe beyond danger.

He agrees, but at the cost of Grania never seeing Sarah or Charles again.  Grania tells Sarah that Charles is in mortal danger and that if she follows Uncle Matthew’s instructions to the letter, she will have Charles by her side for many years to come.  Suitably motivated, Sarah arranges things accordingly.

First of Matthew's instructions is for Sarah and Charles to attend a three-day occult conference being held at Herstmonceux Castle, near Bexhill-on-Sea.  Matthew is speaking at the conference and claims he wants some supporters when dealing with other occult factions, such as the Hermetic Order of the Golden Dawn and the Fraternity of the Inner Light.

On finding out that _[[The-White-Magicians#Dr Alexander Notting|Dr Alexander Notting]]_ and _[[The-White-Magicians#Professor Edwin Occultree|Professor Edwin Occultree]]_ are also attending the conference, Charles agrees to go.

Sarah and Charles return from their recent adventure in Egypt via the Orient Express to Boulogne, where the ferry is waiting to connect with the English part of the train at Folkestone.  They pick up their car in Folkestone and drive to Bexhill-on-Sea for the conference.

## On the Nature of the Bermuda Triangle

The Bahamas archipelago comprises the mountaintops and higher areas of the lost continent of Atlantis, sunk beneath these waters millennia ago.  The islands of North and South Bimini are a remnant of Atlantis, as first claimed by trance medium Edgar Cayce in 1932.

The great Atlantean fire-crystals that once powered Atlantis are still emitting strong energy beams into the universe from unknown sites on the ocean floor.  Unfortunately, these fire-crystals were damaged as a consequence of the sinking of Atlantis, and now only project energy at random.  From time to time these random energy projections strike passing ships or planes, causing them to disappear to who knows where or when.

In addition to the damaged fire-crystals, the great Timekeeper Crystal still stands guard within the ruined Atlantean Temple of Healing.  Those who understand how to use the Timekeeper Crystal will be able to travel through time into the future.

Persistent reports of persons experiencing sudden deviations in compass readings, either on ships or in planes, are the result of magnetic field anomalies caused by briefly appearing micro-wormholes.  There is clear evidence of such compass deviations at Fish Hotel, Lyford Cay, White Hole near Nassau, and Dogleg Reef near Marathon in South Florida.  Micro-wormholes may be transit tunnels between different dimensions of reality, between different times and spaces.

The [Atlantic Undersea Test and Evaluation Centre (AUTEC)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Atlantic_Undersea_Test_and_Evaluation_Center), an American naval base on the Bahamian island of Andros, possesses an underwater range for testing and research on acoustic equipment that comprises 1,670 square miles of the surrounding Caribbean.  In the waters off Andros Island, strange craft have been seen from time to time which not only resemble UFOs, but which display the same unbelievable swiftness of motion and execute the same incredibly sharp turns.
