## Involvement

Aletta was on the same plane as a Gorgon on a mission.

## Highlights

The Vickers Vimy plane on which Aletta was travelling disappeared on route to London from Paris, reappearing 22 days later over the English Channel. The disappearance was caused by a mysterious woman whom the party followed to a Wiltshire village near Stonehenge.

Here she attacked a group of cultists and destroyed their magic items, revealing herself as one of the Gorgons of myth.

## Possessions Acquired

Gorgon’s own obsidian knife with a beautiful, elaborately carved handle (Aletta picked it up from where the Gorgon left it).
