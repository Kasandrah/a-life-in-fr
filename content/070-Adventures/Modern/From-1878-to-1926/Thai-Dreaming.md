## Involvement

Aletta stole an invitation to the exhibition.

## Highlights

While attending an exhibition at Royal Albert Hall, a power stone was stolen by a large birdlike creature. The Curator employs the investigators to recover the stone, and their involvement leads them to be transported by the villain to the Thai Dreamlands with no way of returning.

Sought advice from a hermit, had to kill a demon to regain dreaming shells which would return the party to the waking world. Hermit also advised on the great danger from cultists in Siam (real world).

On returning to the real world, the party travelled to the North of Siam via Suez, Singapore and Bangkok. Cultists were hiding under the guise of monks in a temple and cave complex in the north of Siam.

## Injuries

Shot in the back while escaping the temple complex. A very serious injury requiring 8 weeks recovery in Siam via native medicine and healing. Also superficial knife fight injuries on forearms.
