## Involvement

Invited to a party at the Knickerbocker Hotel, New York City, by _[[Zoran-B#^91f12c|Runyard Ellis]]_, who had met Jonathon Wilde.

## Highlights

An alien creature is using advanced technology to defeat the 1920s police and set himself up as a dictator. His plans include setting off a thermonuclear explosion to generate enough heat and power to summon Cthulhu.

Mi-go from outer space intervene and are overpowered by the alien (MIB) who takes the Mi-go and the party to his home planet in another solar system.

The party eventually destroys the alien MIB with help from other aliens on the planet, and is eventually returned home to Earth and the time of their choice. Angela, Harry, and Brian return to 1984 at 6:00 p.m. on the same evening that they left all that time ago. Michal remains in the 1920s.

## Injuries

Took a thumping in hand to hand combat with the MIB (temporarily defeated him).

## Possessions

£350,000 of antiques, valuables, first editions, a mint condition _[[Stutz-BB-Blackhawk|1928 Stutz Model BB Blackhawk Boattail Speedster]]_, paintings by 1920s painters, stamps, coins, jewellery, some designer 1920s original clothes and gowns, lots of gold.
