## Involvement

Alex was working on a photo essay of the “Wild West” with Frank Saunders for a magazine. They picked up a hitch-hiking Runyard Ellis on the way to the ghost town of Kenneton Wells.

## Highlights

At Kenneton Wells, Dr Gentry was investigating graves on Boot Hill. He mysteriously disappeared after talking with a threatening stranger. Clues led to “old” Josh Eberhardt in the nearby town of Winston. He directed the group to the Diaz house after he recognised Diaz from their description of the threatening stranger.

At the house, Diaz sent the group back to December 1878, where he attempted to kill them. The group became involved with the people of old Kenneton Wells, who were in fear of gunslinger Diaz. Eventually the group went to the Diaz house and destroyed Diaz with the help of Marshal Josh Eberhardt.

When Diaz was killed, the group returned to 1925. Alex leapt on Josh at the point of transfer, so he came forward in time with the rest of the group.
