# Final Destination

Seventeen-year-old Deak Freehold stages his own death via a skyhopper "accident" while racing in the Yindu Wastelands on Ondos.  Only his parents, Senator Montross Freehold and his wife, know Deak is alive and on his way to the Anoid System to join the Rebel Alliance.

Captain Tor Skylow of the Imperial Navy Customs Frigate _Empress_ and trusted members of her crew defect to the Rebel Alliance.  After modifying the _Empress_ for use as a pirate vessel, they head for Minos Sector to prey on Imperial shipping.
