# Jess and Jayme

**Written by [[Jenny-H|Jenny H.]] (Jess)**

Straight after the campfire history lesson from Karan, Jayme waited until everyone else had left _The Maker_ and made a pass at Jess.  It was as subtle as grabbing her and starting to kiss her.  It took Jess completely by surprise.

"Well, you wanted to get laid, what are you complaining about?"

Jess pushed him away.  "Nice though that thought is, this is not sensible."

"Are you saying you don't find me attractive?"

"That's not the point."

"Ah, so you do find me attractive."

"Jayme, we have to work together."

"So?"

"So, I'm not willing to risk a working relationship that is important to me for the sake of getting laid.  So, thank you for feeling sorry for me..."

"Whoa there.  I am not feeling sorry for you, lady.  I've wanted you since the first day I met you."

"What?  You never said a word."

"You thought we'd stolen your ship, then you were Hadra.  Wouldn't have been right."

"I'm not sure it's right now.  I don't think I could stand to have to find yet another crew."

"Wroona's rings, they have shaken your confidence."  He swore softly and took her hand.  "I promise it wouldn't change my opinion of you or our working relationship, but you need some space to think about it.  Just remember, you need to get laid and the offer's there."

Jayme was at the door before she asked.  "Am I going to regret this?"

He covered the distance back in two steps. "Not if I can help it."

They won't be exactly hiding the relationship, but they won't be telegraphing it either.  Which means that Sari will be the first to figure it out and Leith will know by the next afternoon.

___

Sari sat on the end of Leith's desk and buffed her nails.  It was part of their daily ritual.  Leith pretended that he was completing paperwork and wasn't listening to Sari's stream of gossip, observations and suggestions and she pretended not to notice.  In actual fact, despite a polished blonde - or more accurately brunette - bimbo act, Sari could be relied on to give an accurate picture of base and individual personnel and Leith often acted on her suggestions.

"…Dren Nord, Jess' medic, the one who thinks he's the Force's gift to women, is getting stir crazy.  Keeps making noises about how he didn't sign on to be a mere base medic.  He's always going on about how over-qualified he is to be stuck here and much as that gets up Captain Bargat's nose, he's undoubtedly right.  Of course, the fact that Lieutenant Fresia hasn't succumbed to his many charms isn't helping the situation.  I think it's a toss-up whether he or Jess asks for a transfer first.  My money's on Jess, but you never know."

"Of course, she'll have a hard time prising Morgan out of Nadoc's lab.  She's in paradise working on whatever that thing is he's putting together.  Mind you, I know diddlysquat about building ships, but no one around the base has any idea what he's doing.  They keep trying to get a better look at it.  Some of them are trying to get a better look at Morgan too, but I don't think she's realised that yet."

"Of course, when she gets her head out from the hydro spanners, she's more interested in Jenik, the comms officer that transferred from the Outer Rim last month.  I think he needs a bit more time to recover from his last mission, but there's something there.  Which reminds me, you should keep Sergeant Lighe busier.  He's starting to get broody again and if he tries to pick another fight with…"

She was interrupted mid-stream by Leith's aide.  "General, Captain Storm would like a word with you, if it's convenient."

Sari hopped off the desk.  "If she's requesting a re-assignment, you owe me dinner.  I'll pop down to the mess and see what they can organise."  She smiled warmly at the aide, who not for the first time wished he was the base commander.

Jess came straight to the point.  "General, I'd like _The Maker's_ crew back on the active list."

"I thought you wanted 6 months off to train."

"I won't last that long.  I need walls and a job."

"Running away again, Captain?"

Jess smiled.  "Actually, quite the reverse.  When I agreed to take Hadra's identity, I told her crew that it couldn't be a temporary thing.  They've been kind enough to modify the arrangement so I can be Jess again.  They've been patient with me while I trained, but it's time I started honouring my commitments again.  By the way, it's Commander.  General Madine soon knew I wasn't Hadra and it was his condition that I personally commit to the Alliance, which I was happy to do.  He also confirmed the status level as mine, not just me as Hadra."

Considering some of their previous conversations, that came as a surprise to him.  It was also going to come as a surprise to more than a few people on the base.  He wondered idly if Sari knew.  He must remember to drop that bit of information during dinner.  "What about the Jedi?"

"What about them?  Whatever they're planning is likely to be long term.  The Empire is a clear and present danger.  What's the point of saving the universe if there's no one left worth saving?  Besides, they need twelve descendants.  They have fourteen.  If they got to vote for the favourite twelve, I know where I'd be."

"Quite frankly, I'm tired of being the spare.  I'd rather be out there doing something useful than as an understudy to the real actors.  As Hadra, I had status, purpose and admiration.  And contrary to rumour, I earned some of that.  I want it back again and I'm not going to get it on that planet.  Please understand, this is a choice, not an avoidance."

"What about Jayme?"

"He wants some space to decide on a future.  And if you can use _The Maker,_ I'm not necessarily suggesting a transfer.  If there's nothing you can use a Special Ops group for around here, we can go elsewhere.  Although, if the Alliance wants us to continue to supply ships, I'll need a new slicer.  Taking Morgan away from Nadoc's project would be cruel and unusual punishment."

"I'll look into it and get back to you."

"I know I'm not technically under your command, but I thought you should have first refusal."

Which, Leith thought, is a not very subtle way of telling me she's quite capable of going elsewhere.  There was a sharper edge to his tone when he told Jess again that he'd look into it.  Her training hadn't helped the diplomacy skills.

After she had gone, Leith called his aide in.  "Find out where Kezz is."  Before he agreed to anything, he needed to find out whether leaving their training at the moment was a bad idea for either Jess or Jayme.  She might think she wasn't needed, but hers wasn't the only opinion that mattered.
