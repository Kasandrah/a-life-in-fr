# The Corporate Sector

Riddick and his friend Kira were sent to find out information on Baron Tagge, as he had been shown on newscasts as having arrived back on the planet.  There had also been the hint of new technology that was nearly ready to be tested.

Just after he left, Darsie Vundt turned up, introducing herself as a paralegal there to explain legal penalties.  In fact, she was a plant by MMN.  They suggested that they all go outside to discuss the matter, a debatable move when they heard explosions and running and saw various people being chased by riot police.  The group was eventually persuaded by Jess to get back to the hotel which was being expertly barricaded.

Two Lafrarians started knocking urgently at the door.  The staff refused to let them in until Roger bribed the staff into accepting that they were part of the group.  The Lafrarians were descended from birds, but were now flightless, so they were totally awed by Wazza.  They kept pushing him and saying, 'fly'.  They were clearly not very bright but were highly attuned to the Force.

With Wazza questioning them, the Lafrarians admitted that they were terrorists, trying to free their planet.  They showed him the thermal detonator they were carrying.  It became clear that they were effectively slaves.  Roger called Nichelle to discuss the possibilities.  Wazza persuaded them that they could be free if they left the planet.  They agreed, provided they could fly.  Nichelle enlisted the help of the Circle to move the entire race elsewhere.  They disappeared, the two in the room leaving behind their thermal detonator.

Riddick arrived back with Kira and was given the explosives.  After more planning, they decided to spit the group.  Riddick was sent with the crew of _The Maker_ to get the containment field.  Plan to get slicer as well.

News reports showed that the riot on the dock by workers was because of the nationalization of companies. There were accusations of the abuse of workers.

There was a brief glimpse of espo troops (who looked familiar) raiding the warehouse for equipment.

The group arranged a meeting with Carflo through the legal firm they had hired.  The legal firm had the wind up them.  Their paralegal had been kidnapped and a ransom call received.  The group later warned Riddick about the R&K experts who had been hired to find her.

The group hired an impressive limo for their meeting with Carflo's lawyers, scouts, surveyors and a host of other interested people.  The negotiations were long and not altogether positive.
