# Mad, Bad, and Dangerous to Know

Commander Deak Freehold and Lieutenant Mara Jade are rescued from a deep undercover mission on Junction, having retrieved the Death Star plans from a murdered Bothan contact.  After returning to a Rebel base on Dantooine, they become romantically involved.  Three months later Mara Jade is missing, presumed dead after an Imperial attack on Dantooine Base.
