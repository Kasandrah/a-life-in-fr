# Storm Front

>**GM's Note:**  After having heard nothing from her father in over six months, despite having sent him messages through secure Rebel channels, Jess Storm decides that something is wrong.  The _Red Storm Rising_ crew goes looking for him.
>
>They follow an obvious trail to the fortress of Yerkys ne Dago, the Twi'lek crime lord who wants revenge on Jess for the loss of his precious cargo of nerve toxins.  Here the _Red Storm Rising_ crew rescues Taran Storm and his navigator, Tyler Jensen, and wreak havoc on the fortress and its inhabitants.

## Sixth Message

**Written by [[Jenny-H|Jenny H.]] (Jess)**

Transmission from _Red Storm Rising_ to _Storm Clouds_.

Dear Dad,

I feel a bit silly sitting here and recording a message I know I'll never send to you, but I can't stand the waiting for something to happen and need to talk to someone.  The trouble is, since Flis died, I haven't really had anyone I could really talk to.  None of the crew is anywhere near a confidant.  Most of the time I don't need one, but at times like this it would be nice.  Things seem to be emotionally harrowing at the moment.  And all my problems stem from men.  You have a lot to answer for.

First, there's Varth.  I mean, its all very well having a convenient man when you're in port, but its not much fun when the general attitude is "oh, are you back?  I didn't realise you were gone."  It was better last time I went back, but its a real strain.  Tor says I'm starting to get under his skin, but you'd have to stretch the imagination to believe it.  I think it more likely he's getting under mine and it isn't comfortable.  We'll see if he misses me this time.  If not, I'm off to find another base.  Or just off.  After all, when I took off, all I got was annoyance at my taking some of his men away.  Damn him.

Then there's Troy.  I'm not sure what to do with him.  He's really got no idea about ordinary men or real life.  Somehow when you become a Jedi, you have to give up emotions, or at least deny you have them.  You're not allowed to have fun at all.  And he doesn't understand that people might not like their life story or feelings spread to everyone in sight.  He spouts free will, but doesn't believe in self determination.  It's not his fault, poor thing.  I'm sure he could get over all this, given enough time and effort.  I'm working on him.  Funny thing is, I think he's more emotional about it than I am.

Which brings me to Grandfather.  Troy thinks there's extenuating circumstances.  If there are, he can tell me himself.  I think he owes me an explanation, not the reverse!  Let him make the first move.

And lastly you.  Damn it!  Why didn't the Rebellion tell me?  And if they didn't know, why not?  They've had two messages to deliver lately.  And what twit organised the drop with Yerkys?  They must surely have made some queries first.  Surely?  Can't these people do anything right?

Must go.  They want me early.  See you soon.

Jess

## **Tim Howe's Comic Adaptation**

![[sw_stormfront.pdf]]

## Written by [[Jenny-H|Jenny H.]] (Jess)

Jess was in one of her rages.  There hadn't been one particular thing that had set her off; there had been several, all men.  It was as if there was a universal conspiracy amongst all the men in her life to annoy the hell out of her.

Troy was currently of the opinion that she was being unreasonable about her grandfather.  Unreasonable!  The man supposedly gets himself slaughtered by the Imperials, leaving his daughter to flee their home, suddenly and unassisted, in fear of her life.  It was just lucky for her that the man she hired to get her away was Taran Storm, who took more than just a passing fancy to her.  Jess had to admit, under all that fluffy socialite front, Annalise Storm had a core of guts and strength that few people knew was there.

And now her grandfather was alive.  He'd decided to reel her into his Rebellion, wanting to use her for his own purposes, but still didn't want to actually acknowledge his disreputable granddaughter.  No, she got to meet him for the first time in her life when he was giving out awards from a stage in front of a thousand people, of which she was just one, damn his eyes!  Well, if that was how much he thought of her, he could just go kick a Wookie.  And Troy thought she was being unreasonable!  He hadn't seen anything yet!

As for Varth, heck, she could get good sex in any bar in the galaxy.  The base commander wasn't the only star on the horizon.  She'd come back from a month away on one of their dangerous exploits and he hadn't even said welcome back.  Probably didn't even notice she'd been gone.  Well, he could go stick his head in a Sarlacc with the rest of them!

And the Crew.  Well, that was just more problems.  Between Deak complaining about her not handing all her hard-earned profits over to the Rebellion, Asher trying to reconcile her grandfather and her, Nogradus doing what a Columi only knew around the place, Lou crying tramp when she hadn't been near any man but Varth for months, Roger and his cursed bugs everywhere.  The whole pack of them drove her crazier than a pack of womp rats!

It was her father, though, who had her worried.  Sure, she travelled around the skies fairly erratically, but the last couple of messages she'd sent him were through Rebellion channels and she was surprised he hadn't replied at all.  Surely it shouldn't have taken this long for something from him?  Jess made up her mind.  She strode off to Varth's office.

"Is Yerkys off my back?" she asked, without preamble.

Varth had years of command behind him.  He didn't turn a hair.  "The gold was left according to his instructions."

"Are you sure he got it?"

"It was collected."

It was the best she could do, under the circumstances.  "Good. I'm going trading."

"Who are you taking with you?"

Damn him, didn't even try and talk her out of going.  Fine then.  "I don't know, I haven't asked them.  Whoever wants to go.  I'll let you know."  She turned on her heels and left.

To her surprise, the entire crew wanted to come.  Probably afraid to let her out of her sight, in case she found another pesky Jedi.  She decided to take Troy as well.  He needed to see a bit more of the world and finish his education.  For a Jedi, he was remarkably naive about people.  A trip to Corellia would finish that.

They loaded the ship and were gone the next morning.  A quick trip to Fest and they had a hold full of salt.  It was cheap there, but even so, it look four of them to come up with the money, most of it Asher's.  He offered to loan her enough to fill the hold with her own goods, but that went against the grain.  Captains who borrowed, even from their navigators, were soon deep in with the sharks, a most unhealthy place to be.  Yet again, she turned him down.  But one day, one day, she'd be rich enough, to fill her own hold.

They jumped to Arcona, the closest she could reasonably expect to find information on her father.  Even so, it was a long trip and for once Jess felt every day of it.  She hadn't told the crew her real reason for heading that way.  They took her story about trading at face value.  It was what freighter captains did.

The paperwork was soon completed and Jess strolled contentedly out to look at the ships in port.  It felt goad to be back in her home runs.  She knew the ships, the captains, the rules, and the runs.  Far more comfortable than dodging Imperials.

_Storm Clouds_, her father's ship was set down at the end of the port, easily identifiable.  With all the modifications the average 'stock' light freighter underwent, any individual ship could be marked long before the actual colours were seen.  Jess smiled in anticipation and headed off in that direction.

Then she stopped stock-still.  The colours were wrong.  The small picture of the storm cloud on the horizon, the "colours' that gave her father's trademark were gone, replaced by something totally different.  No, she wasn't mistaken.  There were the joins where the hull had been damaged in a meteor storm.

Jess' blood ran cold.  There was no way her father would have sold _Storm Clouds_.  It was too reliable a ship.  Tyler had tinkered with the engines, so she was deceptively fast.  Besides, as her own crew pointed out so often, a ship was tied with an invisible umbilical cord to its captain.  It was home, fortune, fortress and lover, all rolled into one.  He wouldn't have sold it, not without telling her.

Jess ignored the crew milling doubtfully behind her and strode off to where the dockhands were unloading _Storm Clouds_.

"Who owns the ship?" she asked, as non-committal as she could manage, given the circumstances.

"Captain Anton de Vere."

"How long has he had it?"

"About five months."  Five months.  Not long after she'd last seen her Father in Minos Sector then.

"Where can I find this de Vere?"

"At the port bar."

By now, her crew was thoroughly confused.  "What's she doing?" asked one of them.  Deak?  "I don't know," came the reply from Lou.  "But it has to be tied up with money or men somehow."  Jess barely registered them.  She was too worried about where Taran was, if the Imperials had him and if it were her fault.  She hadn't exactly kept a low profile in Minos Sector and someone enthusiastic could certainly follow her trail from Generis Base.

Within a dizzying short time, she'd agreed to an irresponsibly outrageous price for _Storm Clouds_.  If she'd been thinking straight, the amount would have frightened her.  The salt would fetch that amount, she hoped, but it would just about clean out all the hard work she'd put in for so long.  Then Jess realised the problem.  She hadn't stopped to sell the salt first and de Vere was looking expectantly at her.

"Ash?"

The crew was close by, still trying to figure out what was going on.  "Yes?"

"I need to borrow some money."

"Sure."

A son of the Tagge family never wanted for money, even if they were hunting him.  _Storm Clouds_ was hers as soon as her mark was registered at the port authority.

"I can't figure out what's going on," Roger muttered.  "That bucket of bolts is in worse shape than her ship, which is saying something.  Where's the profit going to come from?"

Tor twigged.  She'd seen that ship before, in her pirate days.  "That's her father's ship."  It didn't tell them much, but at least they knew why Jess wanted it.

Time to do some planning.  "Deak, I want the name of a Rebellion contact near Carris or Andron Star. I might need to get my mother to a safe planet.  And that salt's got to be sold.  Take Lou with you."  Lou was the most likely person to find a good buyer.  "Tor, I need you to help me check out the ship."  Jess had few illusions about her ability to pick up mechanical clues.

She sent a coded message to her mother and then chaffed until the interlopers were finally gone.  She checked what they were taking with them, but there was nothing of her father's left on board.  Even his tools were gone.  She had help, but it still took time to go over _Storm Clouds_ thoroughly.  The hidden places were cleaned out.  The only clue was a brief message at the beginning of the log.  "The interest has gone up."  There was nothing else from before de Vere had bought the ship.

Jess cringed inwardly.  Yerkys.  It had to be Yerkys.  She'd escaped his clutches, so he'd gone after the next best thing - her father.  Damn him!

Tor pointed out gently the signs of massive repaired damage.  Not enough to guarantee that those on board had been killed, but not a good sign, all the same.  "It wouldn't have even got the repair bill in sale.  It wasn't worth what you paid, Jess."  The other captain simply rounded on her.  "Yes, it was," she said, between gritted teeth.

Jess felt angry.  Damn, couldn't the Rebellion get a simple pay-off done right.  Hadn't they done any checks?  They had some explaining to do when she got back!

The crew got back with the salt money and she paid Ash back.  There went the majority of her cargo money.  Later she wondered just how big a slice Deak had taken for the Rebellion, but: it wasn't worth arguing about.

The next step was to follow up the vendor of the ship.  Jess had his name from de Vere.  Garindam was a slimy character.  More slippery than a Sedrian.  Fortunately, with a mind-reading Jedi in tow, they didn't need him to answer their questions truthfully.  Troy came up with the information that Yerkys was indeed involved.  The crime lord had given Garindam the ship.  It had already been repaired and his instructions were to sell it to someone who would use it locally.  Yerkys had wanted her to know just what was going on.

Jess resisted the temptation to tear the lackey limb from limb and left.  It was Yerkys' hide she really wanted.  
The problem was what to do next.  They headed back to the bar.  De Vere was still there, talking quietly to a Kubaz.  The two left in separate directions.  Lacking anything else, the crew split up, Nogradus and Roger following de Vere and Deak and Tor following the alien.  Jess took the rest of the group to make enquiries around her local contacts.

Deak and Tor followed their contact to a warehouse of the Shesharile Trading Company and called for back up when they noticed how many gun-wielding guards there seemed to be.  The rest of the crew had come up with zip, so were quite happy to try something different.

Later descriptions claimed that Deak and his enlarged team had decided to flush out the enemy, so that Jess would be able to enjoy a fire fight.  After all, in such situations she normally got stuck on the ship, keeping it steady while the crew did the more exciting work.  More enlightened souls wouldn't have a bar of it.  Besides, there were the reports of Nogradus setting off one of his beloved proton grenades and the matter of Tor tripping over some storage bins.

In any case, Jess' team did manage to make mince meat out of the mercs who raced out of the front doors to escape the burning wreckage.

Frankly, there wasn't much worth defending in the warehouse, though a few light-fingered members did manage to lift a few of the more interesting items from the storage area.  The find Jess was interested in though, was a crate containing a small, green Twi'lek slave girl.

She seemed to trust them, particularly Roger.  He was such an interesting brown colour (as opposed to all the other pale-skinned people).  Questioning of her brought the information that she was Sharlie, that she had until recently been a favourite of Yerkys, until she had displeased him in some way and been shipped out with who knew what fate in store.

Jess asked about her father.  Yes, Sharlie remembered him.  He'd been in Yerkys' prison on Farstey.

"Will you show us where it is?"

"Oh, yes," replied Sharlie, looking coyly at Roger.

They made their escape, with a gravlift full of stolen goodies.  The Imperial soldiers heading towards them gave the guilty parties something to worry about, until they realised that the soldiers were investigating an explosion and hadn't yet connected the looters with it.

The ships were underway quickly, Jess unwilling to delay and no one else game to gainsay her.  They divided up the crew, half going in the _Red Storm Rising_ and half in _Storm Clouds_.  Nogradus was itching to pilot one of the ships, but in a saner moment, Jess gave it into Tor's care.

The plan was simple.  One group was to hire themselves as mercenaries to Yerkys, the other to wait a signal to attack and act as a diversion while the ground team searched for Taran.

Of course, like most simple plans, there were problems.  First of all, without two competent navigators between two ships, it was difficult getting them in the same place at the same time.  Lou wasn't bad, but he was a scout, not a fully-fledged navigator, and still got the _Red Storm Rising_ in later than expected.  Fortunately, they'd thought of that problem.

Deak, Tor, Nogradus, Troy and Ash made a disreputable crew when they put their minds to it, but it was probably Tor's presence that tipped the balance.  Yerkys decided he liked the look of her, a fact that didn't require Troy to point out.  They were hired fairly quickly.

The plan was that Jess would make her run at dawn, but she was signalled early when in the middle of the night Yerkys sent a droid to fetch Tor to him.  The droid was sent back in pieces and the race was on.

The mercenary group carefully scouted around, making short work of anyone who decided they couldn't.  At this stage, the hard part was in the hands of the remaining _Red Storm Rising_ crew.

Yerkys' base was at the end of a narrow canyon.  It couldn't be attacked from the top - the angle was wrong.  It had to be approached from along the canyon, but that required expert piloting skills.

"Can you do it?" asked Roger.

"Sure," replied Jess, with false confidence.  "Piece of cake."

It wasn't.

"I'm losing the shields," cried Roger, as they scraped the sides of the mountain.

"Just hold on, I'm nearly through this last turn."

"I've lost the shields."

"Let's just hope no one fires at us then."

There were loud explosions, as the laser cannons struck the base.

"That was fun, let's do it again," exclaimed Jess.

Back at the base, the away team had found the prison cells.  The few people in it didn't look safe to release, but they did find Taran Storm and his navigator, Tyler Jensen, an added bonus, as Sharlie hadn't mentioned him.  The only problem now was to get past the guards at the front door.

Jess landed the ship and left Ash in charge.  This was one fight she didn't want to miss.  Unfortunately, as usual, by the time they got there, it was all over bar the shouting.  The guards, already unnerved by the explosions, were no match for a Jedi master and the trained fighters of the crew.

Her father looked as well as could be expected, under the circumstances.  "Hi, Dad!" she said, casually.

Fortunately, the storage area for transport vehicles was undamaged, and they made a hasty get-away.  It was too much to hope that Yerkys was dead.  He'd have to wait till later.

As they approached the ships, Taran said, "Oh good, you've got: my ship."

"My ship," replied Jess, smugly.

Taran raised an eyebrow.  "My ship."

Jess corrected cheerfully, "My name's on the ownership papers."  Taran shook his head.  They knew it didn't matter whose name was on the papers, it was Taran's ship, but this could be worth a bit of point scoring for a while.  Jess grinned to herself.

They stopped off at Corellia to repair the _Red Storm Rising_, and then it was back to Generis Base.  
To the request to identify themselves, Jess replied, "The Storm Tag Team Fighters are back in town.  Limited season only.  Get your tickets now or miss out!"

Half the base groaned.  Two of them?  The other half wailed in anticipation.  Varth met them at the space dock.  Just as well.  If he had but known it, Jess was giving him one last chance.  Even if he had just come to see what the hell was going on!
