# Reeling in the God-King of Masterhome

_Red Storm Rising_ is sent on a mission to form diplomatic and trading ties with the planet of Masterhome in a nearby system.  After attempting to come to terms with the God-King and his Anointed People, the negotiations were interrupted by the crash landing of an Imperial shuttle.

_Red Storm Rising's_ crew organised an attack on the shuttle, defeating the Imperials.  On board the shuttle was Lord Dixton, ex-Moff of Minos Sector, who fled the uprising orchestrated in part by _Red Storm Rising_.  Also, with him was his personal slave, "Diamond", who was in fact Tor Skylow, captured after a traitor in her crew gave her ship's location to the Imperials.

After _Red Storm Rising_ returned to Generis Base with Lord Dixton as a prisoner and with a Jedi subdued Tor, Troy reversed Tor's mind wipe and personality rebuild.
