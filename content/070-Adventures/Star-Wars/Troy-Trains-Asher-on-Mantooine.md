# Troy Trains Asher on Mantooine

## Written by _[[Ben-G|Ben G.]]_ (Asher)

>**GM's Note:**  As the Force is far stronger on a living planet than on a barren space station, Troy decides to step up Asher's training by organising a trip to the Rebel base on Mantooine.  The Rebels occupy the mountainous region near several cities on Mantooine's southern continent.  The main base has been cut out of the mountains and supports several thousand Rebels.  The lush forests around the mountain stronghold are strong in the Force.

Asher is ecstatic at Troy's suggestion of a trip to Mantooine.  After a minute or so, the fact that this involves training sinks in.  But a shrug of the shoulders and Asher accepts that and makes plans to enjoy the trip anyway.  Then another minute passes.  What about the Triplets?

Asher has become (to put it mildly} quite attached to the Triplets.  The way they talk to each other, react with each other; everything about them makes Asher happy to be there.  Asher's isolation when young did create emotional baggage that he is still overcoming.  He has become used to the fact of his feelings for them, to a large extent.  However, he can't think of an easy way (at any time for any situation.) to say goodbye to them.  But Asher has got a personality trait from Trevelyan that prevents him from just avoiding the sticky situation intentionally.

Asher asks Troy to organize the trip for the next day; Asher has a few things to do before he can leave.  Then (I assume that the Triplets have a roster known to them, so Asher will have it memorized) Asher will chase them up individually and communally, and explain that he has to go to Mantooine for his Jedi training.  (At this point, I want to bring up that Asher would use his force powers to make others happy.  By that, I'm not talking about superficially happy "vunderful partay trik, ma boi".

Asher has always idolized Jedi Knights from his childhood.  Added to the fact that his father seemed to be the living embodiment of a Jedi (as far as Asher was concerned).  He knows that the Force is a major power for good.  Yes, Asher still believes in the opposing values of good and evil, and he believes that being a Jedi means doing good on the small scale as well as the large.

The trip won't be a dangerous mission, just some training, so there's no need to worry.  It won't be for long.  How long?  Um, a quick mental call to Troy who would probably give a vague answer as well as a mental chuckle.  Probably a few months.  Why?  Well, Troy believes that the Force is stronger on a living planet than on a man-made space station, and my powers have to be trained.  This will probably degenerate from the verbal state about now.  Asher really hopes they don't cry.  He just falls to pieces when that happens.

>**GM's Note:**  After a difficult and protracted farewell from the Triplets (yes, they cried), Asher meets up with Troy in the docking bay.  After some gentle persuasion from Troy, they leave Generis Base on a troop ship, one of many that frequently make the journey between the Rebel base on Mantooine and Generis Base.  Asher easily "convinces" the captain to allow him to watch proceedings from a vantage point in the cockpit.  Troy takes his place in the main hold and is treated to some rousing renditions of current Alliance ground troop favourites.

For the trip on the troopship, Asher would have to hang around the cockpit and watch the crew, mostly the navigator.  If he can't talk his way into it, Asher would not be averse to using some of his power to try and make his suggestions be seen in a favourable light.  If it works, Asher is happy just to sit and watch.

>**GM's Note:**  The troop ship makes the short hyperspace jump to Mantooine.  A more hazardous journey at sublight speed through twisting mountain passes ends when the ship slips into a large docking bay drilled out of solid rock.  Soon after arriving at the Rebel base Asher's holocam is confiscated by an irate Alliance security officer, much to Asher's surprise and Troy's quiet amusement.  Troy ensures that the incident goes no further.

The arrival at a new base has Asher acting out one of his many roles, one that his family would recognize oh too well - the fascinated tourist.  Asher will holo-photo everything from the scenery to the base.  I have no doubt that most if not all would be confiscated as too dangerous from a security point of view.  Asher will be amazed and disappointed.  Although it is a short visit, Asher is happy to go out camping.

Asher is even happier than normal.  He hasn't gone camping since Trevelyan took him camping when Asher was 10.  This is really reinforcing the image of Troy as a new father figure.  Troy has many of the same mannerisms as Trevelyan.  Even at short notice, Asher will pick up as many of the bare necessities: temporary modular shelter, glow-rods, luxury food processors, etcetera, etcetera, and etcetera.  All this needs just a hover-sled so … what do you mean, Troy?  I thought we were going camping.  What?  Carry all that?  You're serious, aren't you?  Laden physically and psychically, Asher again knuckles down to enjoy this trip.  "This is fun!  This is fun!  This is fun!  This is fun!  After a few minutes, the self-fulfilling prophecy is ... err ... fulfilled, and Asher does enjoy it.

>**GM's Note:**  Troy and Asher sort out their backpacks ("you only take what you can carry yourself, Asher") and leave the base, hiking down slope for the flatter forest area.  Asher spends the next several weeks under extreme physical duress as Troy pushes him far beyond anything he was previously capable of.  Hiking, running, jumping, swimming, lifting and carrying heavy objects and lightsaber duelling all become daily activities.

The exhausting trip is tiring but being a member of the Star Wars universe, it has no lasting debilitating effect.  Asher is cheerful and uses the holo-camera to good effect.  The training probably seems to speed by at some parts and crawl by at others, but Asher perseveres.

Asher will at first attempt to help in getting food by setting snares, like he had when he last went camping.  But the first time he finds something caught in it, he suddenly realizes that the pleasure of the catch is gone.  This is as much from the fact that the animal is dead (and looks so undignified) as the fact that what seemed big enough for a child to eat is not all that large, big, sizable for an adult.  Asher has a depressed night as he ponders the values of what he's done.  At some stage he realizes that everything has its place, and he leaves the event behind but with the lessons remembered.

>**GM's Note:**  Troy also teaches Asher to expand his consciousness, to "see" and "hear" what others cannot but most of all to feel the Force around him, its ebb and flow, its presence in all living things.  Asher's control abilities receive most of Troy's attention and Asher finds he is capable of quite extraordinary physical feats, but it is the results of Asher's sharpening sense abilities that make him feel he is truly becoming a Jedi.
>
>One incident in particular convinces Asher of the power of the Force.  Troy is once again explaining time as the Jedi see it, as a network of Force lines spread out across the temporal plane.  Asher suddenly sees in his mind a bright line of Light traversing the Darkness.  He reaches towards it and is caught up in a rush of images of which only one stays in his mind.

When Asher "sees" the force line, he is stupefied at the beauty of it.  It's so beautiful.  He's just got to reach and wow!  What was that?  No, don't answer.  I know what it was, but it was so beautiful.  Who was that?

>**GM's Note:**  A woman lies on her back on rocky, barren ground.  A large triple pointed barbed spear is buried through her chest and pins her to the ground.  Blood seeps slowly from her wounds and pools on the ground beneath her, trickling in little rivulets between the rocks.
>
>Her face, even in death, is etched with suffering; her clawed and bony fingers dig into the dirt.  She is dressed in black robes, tattered and torn and marked with dirt.  Through her torn clothes it is obvious that her right leg is a cybernetic replacement for her original limb.  Her other leg lies strangely twisted on the ground beneath her artificial limb.
>
>Standing around her watching her body are the tattered and starving remnants of a once proud people.  The warriors are close by her, many holding triple pointed barbed tipped spears.  They are fearful and disbelieving, none daring to approach her.  One stands apart from the others, empty handed, a vacant look on his face, past pain, grief, past any more emotion.

Asher starts suddenly and finds himself looking into the concerned grey eyes of his teacher.

"Asher, what you see is the past and it is the truth I can no longer conceal from you.  First you must understand the true history of the Jedi, then I will tell you of Boddi".

When Troy mentions Boddi, Asher, although he had been listening, perks up his ears.  He'd always wondered about her ... a Jedi who seemed so unlike what Asher thought a Jedi must be.  Asher's old notion of Jedi had been people like his father and well Troy has proved that that notion couldn't be all that wrong ... could it?  Why did she do some of the things she did?  Asher's attention is slightly divided by the mention of Boddi's name.

Troy tells Asher the story of the Ashla and its enemy and opposite, the Bogan, and of the Jedi Bendu of the Ashla.  Then the topic of conversation changes.

"Over 3,000 years ago I was asked to serve as a Circle member after my mother, a Jedi Knight and a member of the Circle, undertook her journey from this life to what lies beyond.  I vowed to protect and serve and to solemnly undertake the responsibilities of a Circle member.  One of these responsibilities is to teach those individuals with Force potential.  That is why I was teaching the Gesaril natives.  They thought it was in return for rescuing me from my crashed ship and looking after me while I healed my wounds.  They were the reason I had travelled to Gesaril in the first place."

"In all the eons between the Skywalker's discovery of the Force and my joining the Circle, the Jedi Knights were the protectors of the Republic.  Guided by the Circle, the Jedi Knights brought order and justice to the Galaxy and sustained the Republic.  That situation continued until the coming of the darkest, most evil Jedi the Circle had ever dealt with, until the Dark Jedi Boddi".

Asher picked up the information of where little Jedi came from, but the second mention of Boddi froze his thoughts solid.

A minute or two of silence passes.  "I must have been drifting there for a second.  I thought you said something absurd about Boddi being a Dark Jedi?  Ha ha ha … oh, it's not absurd … its what you said.  At this point, Asher gets to practice some of his Control skill.  "Oh, please go on."  For the rest of Troy's lecture Asher's attention is absolutely and totally undivided: he is focused only on Troy and his words.

Troy tells Asher what he knows of Boddi, her past and her present.  It is a stronger and wiser Asher who returns to Generis Base with Troy.
