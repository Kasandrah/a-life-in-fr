# A Tree in the Forest

>**GM's Note:**  Boddi tests the apprentice Jedi by sending each one into a Dark Side tree located deep in the forest.  Ash, Ross, Adam Jr., and Deak conquered their fears and passed Boddi's test.  Leith and Hela didn't pass, but they didn't fail either.  Hadra, Roger, Neena, and Daye failed, and each in their own way paid the price.  Jayme, Lou, Sari, and Vernan have yet to take the test.

## Written by [[Jenny-H|Jenny H.]] (Hadra)

Boddi summoned all the apprentice Jedi to the planet's surface.  There, she escorted them to a huge tree and told them that they were to be tested.  Everyone could feel the presence of the Dark Side in the tree.

Each apprentice went alone to the tree, out of sight of the others.  Some came back changed, some devastated, and some took longer than others.  Deak returned cocky, informing Boddi that he had already passed that test.  Roger returned sober and thoughtful.  Adam Jr. looked like he had instantly become a man rather than a boy.  Ash lost the childishness that had still hung around him.  Daye was catatonic, Leith upset, Hela burnt and embarrassed.  Hadra informed Boddi angrily that she had known Hadra wasn't ready for it.

Sari had been so busy trying to calm and comfort people, with Lou undertaking physical healing, that they hadn't had their turns when Ross came back.  He rather shamefacedly announced to Boddi that he thought he'd broken the tree.  She looked very annoyed when she realised that the Dark Side presence was gone.  Several people cheered.  Hadra laughed.

There was some fallout from the test.  Roger resigned from the Alliance.  Hadra stopped all Jedi training.  When Roger challenged her commitment, Hadra informed him that as she'd proven she had no talent whatsoever for the Force, she refused to be a danger and a liability to the rest of the group.

>**GM's Note:**  When Jess returned as Hadra, that was the last straw as far as Boddi was concerned.  Although Jess doesn't believe she has to confront her problems, now she's Hadra, Boddi knows that problems have a habit of cropping up again despite all the effort spent avoiding them.  Face them and grow towards the Light, or avoid them and fall to the Dark.  As long as Jess hides from her problems, Boddi will consider her unfit to be a Jedi.
>
>Boddi put Jess through the Dark Side test, hoping Jess would recognise her shortcomings regarding becoming a Jedi.  Perhaps Jess will stay away from dangerous knowledge, knowledge that Boddi believes could get Jess and others close to her into serious trouble.  As Jess has decided not to continue training, Boddi considers her job done.
