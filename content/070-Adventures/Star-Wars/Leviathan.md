# Leviathan

>**GM's Note:** Corobus is an experimental mining colony, a pre-fabricated industrial city of nearly 800 people erected in the bowl of a crater.  Miners work for ore and crystal deposits.  During the "black rain" season, storms and bad weather keep miners and their families indoors for several weeks.
>
>A fully automated trading station run by droids is in stationary orbit over the colony.  Smaller automated ships carrying ore and crystal dock with the station and are unloaded.  Large ore carriers visit the station from time to time, taking the ore and crystal from bulk storage and carrying it to the inner worlds.
>
>In the race for profits, the miners have been over enthusiastic and set off a huge blast that uncovers "a fossil of some kind".  Some time later the ORCC intercepts a comms transmission.  The miners on Corobus are under attack by a creature that is destroying the colony.  General Leith sends the team to Corobus in response to the miners' SOS.
>
>Information available from the Jedi Holocron - every colony on Corobus has been destroyed.  Centuries between each colony.  Corobus is a very out of the way system - isolated.  No survivors, no explanations.  Jedi Bendu Miri's advice - mining operations may have disturbed something, possibly a Leviathan, a very ancient Dark Side creature that sucks the Force out of beings when it consumes them.  If so, it could be defeated and destroyed by large amounts of firepower or some of the mining equipment.
>
>The team arrives in a black rain downpour - low light and poor visibility.  They find the wreckage of an Imperial prisoner transport ship that crashed three days prior to their arrival.  Came in at high speed, low angle, out of control.  Only the front cockpit and crew cabin remain intact.  Nearly all of the cryogenic chambers are shattered and blood stained - only one opened normally, the one marked "lockdown, no release".  Remnants of the ship's log show it was hit by the hyper-spatial mass shadow of a meteor shower, and forced into real space.  Once in real space it was severely damaged by the meteor shower and crashed on the planet.
>
>Just beyond the wreckage of the ship, the team finds the remains of the mining colony.  It has been completely destroyed.  Dried blood stains the walls, blaster burns all around, equipment smashed and destroyed, blast doors torn apart.  Huge three-clawed footprints in the mud the size of a tank, mining tools splintered and bent (used as weapons by the miners).  Family units destroyed, dead pets and exotic house creatures, children's toys smashed and bloody.  Attacker just destroyed everything - not searching for anything.
>
>The Force sensitive can "hear" screaming and cries for help when near the destroyed colony - despair, terror, relentless.  Screaming cannot be drowned out.  Sense many presences all around, all in pain, all afraid.  Team feels like they are being watched.
>
>In the ruins of the colony the team find an escaped prisoner from the crashed Imperial transport ship.  He agrees to help them if they take him off planet.  Even though his silver eyes give him enhanced night vision, he knows he won't survive the Leviathan on his own.  This prisoner reminds the team of Boddi, and "the Force is strong" in him.  They agree to his offer.
>
>The creature killed the first miner that came upon it after the blast, and absorbed the Force within him.  It learned the miner's knowledge, and used that knowledge to hunt down and kill all the others in the colony, absorbing their Force and learning their knowledge.
>
>Night is dark and brooding.  The whole place has a nightmare aspect - "something" is out there.  Voices in the heads of the Force sensitive are screaming and wailing.  The team tracks the "voices" to a localised area - a cavern high up on the crater wall, site of the blast that uncovered the creature.  A very difficult climb hampered by black rain, falling rocks and debris.  Team uses the miners' tunnels to enter the cavern from below.
>
>The tunnels lead to the main generator complex, where the heat exchanger is still active, converting heat from open lava pools into energy.  The lava pools provide enormous power reserves.  Glowing green bugs, the usual inhabitants of the cavern, have been attacked and partially eaten by larval stage creatures, offspring of the creature uncovered by the miners.
>
>Team can destroy the larval creatures by calling down the lightening, or by carving them up a bit at a time.  This will free the colonists' Force energy trapped in the unusual cells on the creatures backs.  By following the footprints of the adult creature, the team finds the creature on the top of the crater wall.  The wall collapses into the underground tunnels, and the creature falls into the lava pools, but survives.  The team must use the cables from the heat exchanger to kill the creature and free the Force energy of the other colonists.

### What Really Happened

One huge fight between the team and the Leviathan that included large pieces of ruined buildings being thrown backwards and forwards, use of ship-based ion cannons on the ground, and concussion missiles.  The Leviathan was eventually cut to ribbons by lightsaber-wielding apprentices mad enough to climb up onto its back and cut open its Force energy storage sacks.

The team takes their new silver-eyed friend back to meet Boddi - yes, he's her descendant and now he's the newest Jedi Bendu apprentice.
