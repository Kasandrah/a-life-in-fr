# Pirates and Parents

Generis Base receives word that a supply ship is on its way from the Core Worlds with much-needed parts and equipment.  The Captain of the ship is an "honest trader" who does not wish to be connected with the Rebellion, so the crew of _Red Storm Rising_ go undercover as pirates and "attack" the supply ship, "stealing" the cargo and "allowing" the crew of the ship to escape with their lives.

"Kidnapped" from the ship for ransom were Deak's parents, Senator Montross Freehold and his wife.  They were escaping from ISB spies and assassins set on them by the Emperor after he dissolved the Senate.
