# The Finale

After obtaining the Imperial food contract for the Gesaril prison asteroid, the _Red Storm Rising_ and her crew participate in a full-scale Alliance attack on the prison.  Drun Cairnwick is freed along with hundreds of other political prisoners.  The  freed prisoners are escorted to Alliance transport ships.

While making a detour to Gesaril before returning Drun Cairnwick to Adarlon, the crew recover a cloaking device and a 3000-year-old Jedi Master named Troy.  Troy stays, but the cloaking device is delivered to Alliance ships still near the prison asteroid.

![[sw_boddi_hela_and_troy.jpg]]

**Boddi, Hela, and Troy by Tim Howe**

Drun Cairnwick is returned to Adarlon to lead the Rebels in Minos Sector again, but the crew learns that Tor Skylow's ship has been destroyed and their pirate ally taken to Travnin as a prisoner of the Imperial Moff.  After attempting to enter Travnin space lanes and being threatened by a vastly superior Imperial force, the crew reluctantly head for the co-ordinates given to them by Wedge Antilles.

## Third Message

**Written by [[Jenny-H|Jenny H.]] (Jess)**

Transmission from _Red Storm Rising_ to _Storm Clouds_.

Hi Dad!

Long distance space travel is a good time to record another log report.  The business is looking good.  I might even be able to come out of the cold, if all goes well.  I'll need to lay low for a while, but given luck Yerkys ne Dago will be off my back.

Let's see - I forgot to tell you last time.  I have a new navigator.  Asher Tagge.  Yes, that's TAGGE.  He's on the run from his family.  I can understand why they are trying to kill him - he has the most atrocious dress sense!  Well, that and the fact he's nice.  The Tagge family is more noted for lunatic psychopaths than naive kids.

I bet the Rebellion is regretting not putting any limits on the deal to break out their local leader.  Why didn't you warn them what I'm like?  I called in a few favours, got Tor to call in a few and requested heaps of back-up.  Did they seriously imagine I was going to attack a prison asteroid without it?

So when we won the food supply contract and took the first load to the asteroid, the rebel fleet attacked.  To escape the devastation, an officer was going to lock us in a cell - for our protection, of course.  You can't have contractors running loose around a prison.  Well, with a little bump on the head and the security code, we started letting out political prisoners by the score.  And getting shot at.

The mayhem was helped by the shooting outside and an orchestrated mutiny on the cruiser.  Piece of cake!  We got in, got 'em out and got out.  The transport ship I ordered got the rest of them out.

Of course there was the usual slight problem.  We got into a dogfight with a couple of Ties and I discovered that I no longer have anyone who can work shields or guns effectively.  Not the time to find out!  Surprisingly, Lou turned out to be a reasonable shot and we finally managed to down one and outrun the other.

We found the transport ship and off-loaded most of our released prisoners , then promised to take the Rebellion leader back to Adarlon.  With a side trip to Gesaril first.  You see, from various sources we'd heard rumours that a ship with a cloaking device had crashed on Gesaril.  It was too good an opportunity to pass up, especially as Boddi could speak with the natives and persuade them not to down our ship, as they had numerous others.

We landed and found a graveyard of ships.  We sorted the likely ones and finally, after testing a few stray buttons, found the cloaking device.  Then the fun really started.

The natives turned up - little furry critters with a stone age society, but "strong in the Force".  They were all wearing little wooden replicas of my jade pendant - you know - the one Mother gave me years ago that has two intertwined snakes - her family crest.

Well, I took it out to see what would happen and they all started acting like I was a god or something.  The crew thought it was a great lark.  The critters kept grabbing onto my ankles when I tried to get away, so the crew were just detouring around me, fixing the ship with the cloaking device for take-off.

When the ship was ready, we went with the critters to their settlement, where I was given several of the wooden symbols to hold.  They contain individual memories of the wearer.  Then the Shaman came out.  He gave me another wooden pendant.  This one zonked me out for some time, as it was a race memory.  The relevant bits were that centuries ago an injured Jedi crash-landed on the planet.  They looked after him and when he was well, he taught them use of the Force and other cultural delights.  Then Boddi came and talked to him.  She took him away and they've been waiting for him ever since.

Which begged the question - what am I doing wearing this thing around my neck?  I never told you because I thought it was silly, but I've always felt it was important.  Boddi intimated later that the Jedi is an ancestor of mine.  I must discuss it with Mother some time.  No, better still, this supposedly live Grandfather of mine.

Anyway, Boddi was sitting there smirking away and being most annoying.  When pressed, she took us to a cave with a little shaped hollow on the back wall, into which my pendant fitted perfectly.  The cave wall dissolved and we found the Jedi in deep freeze behind it.  I retrieved my pendant and found another red one as well.  The key to the next frosted Jedi, I suppose.

Boddi tells us that long, long ago the Jedi were more powerful than now and foresaw a terrible event that would threaten the entire universe.  They deep-froze 10 members of their Circle and left 3 to guard the universe.  One died protecting Boddi, and the other is the Emperor.

I'm not sure how much of this I believe, but Troy's presence is a powerful argument.  It's apparently getting closer to the time of this event, although Boddi is in no hurry to find the other Jedi.  I can hardly wait for that one!

The fun and games over, we took off.  The problem now was that we didn't have the expertise to either extract the cloaking device or fix the ship's hyper-drive.  With the lack of alternatives, we delivered it to the Rebellion who were still mopping up around the prison asteroid.  Nogradus is still in shock.

Next stop was to Tor.  She hadn't showed up at the prison break, which was a bit of a surprise.  We found out why, when we arrived at the meet point and discovered her ship scattered over a wide area.  No-one at Yelsain had heard anything and were too busy conducting a mass Rebellion to do anything.  The Rebellion wanted to stir things up in this area and it's certainly happening!

We went to Travnin to try and get news of Tor.  Dropped into sub-light, got hailed by some alert and irate Imperials and popped out again.  Not worth getting disintegrated ourselves.  Besides, you can't have two good-looking Captains in the same space.

Well, the crew foolishly wanted to follow me still.  Can't imagine why.  We dropped off our Rebellion leader and are skipping to the rebel base co-ordinates Wedge gave us.  This sectors getting a little too hot to handle!

Signing out!

Jess
