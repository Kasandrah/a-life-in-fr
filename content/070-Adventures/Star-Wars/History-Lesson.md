# History Lesson

Karan invited the apprentices to a history lesson on the planet.  They sat around a campfire and gradually fell into jovial marshmallow toasting (without sticks) and popcorn fights.  The navigators in the group pointed out significant stars and explained the mythology behind the naming.

Jess commented that the depressing thing was that the males outnumbered the females three to one and "my chances of getting laid tonight are still remote!"

The Jedi withdrew quietly, and Karan entered the campfire circle.  The fire disappeared.  An image filled the space where the fire was - the lesson began.

## The First Circle

The first scene was of 12 young humans ranging from small children to young adults being taught the ways of the Jedi by their father, The Skywalker.  The subject was the difference between the Ashla and the Bogan, between Light and Dark.  Three of the children were more interested in hearing about the Bogan.

## The Second Circle

Time moves forward to a new Circle.  The Skywalker is teaching his grandchildren the Jedi Code.  The apprentices watching this scene notice a young Boddi, Palpatine, and Karan among the children.  Some setback was discussed, and Boddi argued for quicker, more decisive action - the end justifies the means.  Palpatine and Karan watch Boddi closely.

Another scene of Boddi, this time defeating other Circle members in mock lightsaber combat.  No-one came close to her level of skill with a lightsaber.

## The Dark Jedi

A vision of a forming sun and planetary system - accelerated by the Jedi into a stable system with inhabitable planets covered in water, land, plants, trees and animals.  Time passes, and one of these planets becomes inhabited.  The view changes to inside a grand hall filled with people.  People suffering under the rule of the Dark Jedi Boddi and her reluctant Battle Master, Kezz.  This time the apprentices are part of the scene instead of watching it.

Boddi's reptilian gladiators drag some apprentices out of the crowd and take them as prisoners up to her dais.  Here they witness Boddi slay a young boy who refused to kneel in front of her, and Kezz's abortive attempt to stop her.  It is this single act that touches Boddi's last remaining shred of humanity, and she vanishes from the hall in obvious shock and horror at what she has done.  Kezz and the Royal Guard leave the hall while the rest of the people mill about in confusion.

The next view is of a desert landscape.  Boddi lies on her back on the rocky, barren ground.  A large triple pointed barbed spear is buried through her chest, pinning her to the ground.  Blood seeps slowly from her wounds and pools on the ground beneath her, trickling in little rivulets between the rocks.  Her face, even in death, is etched with suffering; her clawed and bony fingers dig into the dirt.  Her black robes are tattered and marked with dirt.  Seen through her torn clothes, her right leg is a cybernetic replacement for her original limb.  Her other leg lies strangely twisted on the ground beneath her artificial limb.

Kezz stands close by Boddi's body, empty-handed, a vacant look on his face, past pain, past grief, past any more emotion.  The tattered and starving remnants of the Royal Guard stand further away, many holding triple pointed barbed tipped spears.  They are fearful and disbelieving, none daring to approach the body.

Karan informs the stunned apprentices that the Ashla drew Boddi back from the depths of the Bogan because her last thought before she succumbed to the Dark was profound regret for murdering the boy slave.

## The Third Circle

A new Jedi teacher, another new Circle.  Palpatine, Koran, Nichelle, and Kezz are in the midst of an animated lesson.  Palpatine is still withdrawn, but observant.  Darkness laps around him, and he's paying attention to it surreptitiously.

A sudden flash of Light and the Darkness rushes away.  Boddi returns to the Circle.  The others react with shock, horror.  She claims to have had a vision from the Ashla of future disaster, but none believe her - only the Skywalker himself has ever received visions from the Light.  They won't follow Boddi.

Palpatine demands Boddi prove her link with the Ashla.  She changes into a tiny sun, and Palpatine withdraws his objections.

The next view is of a round room, marble floor, lined with pillars of stone, all with a strange gem-like symbol on top.  Palpatine, Boddi, and Karan, the three Guardians who remain awake to protect and serve, are standing in the middle of the room discussing how to deal with the coming galactic cataclysm.  Karan is still a living human Jedi, not the Force ghost he becomes.

## The Bogan

Somewhere black and dark and horrible beyond belief.  Palpatine encounters the Bogan, the Dark Side of the Force.  He wants this encounter, but at the last moment he realises what's happening.  The watching apprentices see his final hopelessness and despair.  Then he turns to look out at them - no-one is home but the Dark.

There is an intense discussion among the apprentices about the true nature of the Bogan, the Dark.  Dermod believes the Bogan is a sentient being of immense, indescribable power, but most of the other Circle members disagree.  Boddi doesn't express an opinion on the matter.

## To the Present

A rapid montage of events leading up to recent times.

## The Clone Wars (Again!)

Neena elbows Jess.  "How did Karan die?"  Jess asks Karan.  "Palpatine."  The brevity and tone of Karan's answer surprises the apprentices, who insist on more details.  "It appears that a history of the Clone Wars is in order."

The apprentices find themselves at ground zero in a massed land battle between two technologically very advanced armies.  Nothing like a little hover-tank combat to liven up a history lesson.  The apprentices eventually work together to destroy a nearby command centre.

Karan is asked if that was a real historical event.  "It will be."
