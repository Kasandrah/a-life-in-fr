# Down and Out on Gelgelar

After successfully completing their mission and proving their mettle as Intel agents, Leith and Sari return to Hoth and meet up again with the crew of the _Star Wind_.

There is a general discussion on the Caroth / Vernan problem.  Is one / both an Imperial turncoat, working for na Dago or is it personal against the Storm family.  Caroth versus the crew could just be Caroth trying to complete perceived orders from Vernan.  Ne Dago also has it in for the crew.  Would it really matter to the Alliance if Vernan was working with na Dago?  They do work with smugglers, pirates, drug dealers, etc.  The group takes their suspicions to Madine.

There is a need to prove a link between Vernan and Ne Dago.  Jaidyn suggests using Jess as bait.  The trouble is, no one knows where she is - Ne Dago might already have her under surveillance.  It is better to use the crew themselves as bait.  They decide to go to Gelgelar, a place far enough out of Ne Dago's territory as to make a chance meeting with any of his bounty hunters unlikely.

They also leave the holo of the under-construction super destroyer with a doctored 'signature' of the crew on it to alert Vernan to their presence.  Max also makes sure a couple of key people are subtly informed in a bar.  They pick up a cargo of repulsorlift parts and head to Gelgelar, a swamp planet.  Sari and Leith pretend to be slumming tourists.  The rest are shady characters, registered as bounty hunters.

The highest point in the town is a shrine, the only building made of natural stone.  Blue robed pilgrims of every species approach the building, chanting 'the way to Kooru is the way to peace.'  In the centre of the building, on a pile of tattered animal skins, is a small human wearing tattered clothes.  He is smoking, mouthing platitudes in response to the pilgrims' questions, and being fed food.  He registers with some Force ability.  Nichelle takes one look and wanders off in disgust.

Sari is too kind-hearted to leave it at that and attempts to cure his mind.  The attempt fails and he falls over, much to the consternation of the pilgrims.  Sari decides that a mob of angry pilgrims is not a good idea and beats a hasty retreat, leaving Spar to establish that the being did this on purpose to tease Sari. Spar's teasing on his return to the ship confirms this.

The crew set out to investigate the settlement, tracked by Sari's Jedi senses.  Spar (failing yet another bargain roll) pays an enormous amount for an upgrade on the ship's shields.  It is a good excuse to stay in the area for a couple of weeks.  They head to a bar, but a street urchin steals Jaidyn's COM link.  A chase ensues and Jaidyn catches the thief.  After interrogation and a sob story, Jaidyn decides to buy the starving urchin a meal.  They then decide to hire the thief, whose name is Dermod, as a guide.

He then informs the crew that someone has been asking after them and their ship for the last few days, from before they arrived on the planet.  Noval Garaint is a freelance bounty hunter who has worked for Yerkys Ne Dago before.  Dermod is taken back to the ship, where he is forced into a bath, new clothes, and a hair cut.  He scrubs up to a handsome human, older than first appearances.  He is not from the planet originally, but barely remembers his parents and the circumstances of his arrival.

Jaidyn offers him the option of staying on as part of the crew (especially as he'd let slip Spar's real name) or being dropped off on another planet.  Dermod decides to stay on and is later inducted into the Alliance.  In return, he gives them the location of Garaint's ship and the information that he is very good.  They discuss the possibility of luring Garaint into a trap.  Sari and Spar converse telepathically about the recent events.  Dermod is bright enough to suspect from their body language that they are talking.  Spar confirms this by winking at him, much to Sari's disgust.  Sari offers to teach Spar how to keep his mouth shut.

As Sari's farseeing shows that ambushing Garaint on the ground would be a very bad idea, they decide on a space battle.  They leave the system, followed by Garaint's ship.  The battle takes place at close range, with both sides trying to ionise the other's ship to take the crew alive.  Even with Spar's Force skills, it is an even fight with a stalemate certain.  The crew are unhappy.  They've never had trouble with a foe like this before.  Breaking off the attack, they head out of system and hide out for a week before returning to Hoth.
