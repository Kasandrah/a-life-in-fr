# Command Performance on Yavin

The first recruits from Masterhome arrive at Generis Base; three of the Anointed People destined for training on Mantooine.

Boddi and Hela leave with the troop ship as Boddi wants to continue Hela's training in a more natural environment, i.e. Mantooine's extensive forests.  Boddi has left orders with Troy to take _Red Storm Rising_ to a particular set of co-ordinates.

Following Troy's instructions, _Red Storm Rising_ arrives in Yavin System just as the Death Star explodes near the Yavin moon.  After battling shock waves and observing a fleeing TIE fighter, _Red Storm Rising_ is escorted in to the fourth moon of Yavin by Wedge Antilles and another X-Wing pilot.

_Red Storm Rising_ is asked to search two nearby moons for missing Rebel ships and possible survivors.  There is very little time, as Imperial re-enforcements will be arriving soon.  The Rebels are packing to leave Yavin IV.

_Red Storm Rising_ recovers one Rebel pilot and a clue to another "sleeping" Jedi Master.  They return to Yavin IV, recover the Jedi Master Nichelle from the ruins of an ancient temple and leave just before the Rebels evacuate the base.  The Rebels are leaving a detonite surprise for any Imperials, a surprise which will totally destroy Yavin Base, the surrounding temple ruins and a large part of the jungle.

After _Red Storm Rising_ left the area the bulk of the Imperial Navy arrived to finish off the Rebels.  The Imperials found the fourth moon abandoned.  Many Imperials were killed by the detonite explosions laid by the now well away Rebels.

After abandoning the base on Yavin IV, the Rebel HQ moved to Thila.
