# A Run-in with a Tagge

_The Maker_ and the _Star Wind_ were to join forces on a mission to infiltrate an Imperial technology post on the planet Lowtide, in the Mortex Sector.  They were given a droid, QT7, that looked like a suitcase that could be used to tap into security systems.  The isolated tech post had information on Imperial supply systems that would be extremely useful.  There was also research going on at the base that might be useful as well.

Hadra's group went off to steal an Imperial shuttle while suitable ID was being prepared.  Both ships were taken - one with the most appropriate-looking members to act as Imperial auditors and the other as backup.

They arrived at the Imperial base.  With Spar acting as the Imperial Auditor and Jaidyn, acting as military commander, ordered away the storm troopers that met them.  They next met the base commander, and Jaidyn imperiously took over his office and quarters.  They promptly removed the bug and hooked up QT7 to download transport rosters and codes to translate.  Two personnel were unaccounted for.  They found separate code for several files and assumed the two missing personnel were associated with it.

Spar demanded a tour of the base.  There were five locked doors that the base commander himself couldn't open.  They were associated with a special project that he didn't have authority over.  Back at their office, Nadoc informed them that the special project had an energy converter he had made, and they were trying to make it work. If the Imperials got it wrong, they could blow up half of the sector.  The periodic power vibrations the base was experiencing were part of this.  They decided to destroy the base to prevent any further problems.

Just then, the group found out who the other two personnel were - Silas Tagge and an assistant were headed to their office.  Spar ran out the other way - Silas would have recognised him.  Max, much to Silas' disgust, faced off against the duo.  Max demanded to know who they were and refused them entry.  Silas threatened retribution, but there was little else he could do.

The group planned the attack for that night as soon as the scientists had left.  Hadra and Max disabled the comms system.  The first ISB plant on the base then attacked them.  Hadra fired at him with little effect, then hit him over the head with her blaster when it jammed.  During the rest of the firefight, Nadoc retrieved his toy.  The Imperials had been playing with it, so he needed to stabilize it.

In the meantime, Spar was pretending to inspect the security guards when the alarm sounded.  After some confusion, they realised he was an infiltrator, and he surprised them by parrying their blaster bolts back at them with a suddenly produced lightsaber.  QT7 was busily locking doors when he was taken to the weapons testing area to download information.  Running to meet them, Spar ran into Tagge and his assistant.  Luckily, Jaidyn was able to take them out with a couple of stun grenades.  The duo was taken prisoner.

Thermal detonators strategically placed (i.e., thrown everywhere) took out the important areas of the base.  Alvak in the _Star Wind_ landed to collect them, while _The Maker_ was holding off two frigates.  They managed to escape with minimal damage, and two hyperspace jumps took them out of reach.  Tagge's data pad showed the device had been collected from a ruined city that was being excavated with ISO support and military backup.  Nadoc informed them that Miri, another of the Jedi, was probably there.
