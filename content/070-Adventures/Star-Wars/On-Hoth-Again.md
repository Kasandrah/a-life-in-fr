# On Hoth Again

There they have a report of Jess' death, defending a space liner against attack.  They are now at a loss as to what to do next.  There is still no proof of a direct link between Vernan and Ne Dago, although a leak from the base is confirmed.  The team had no particular role on Hoth, so after asking Nichelle to pass on a message to Deak to come to Hoth to help investigate Vernan, they pursued their own interests.

Spar decided to follow up earlier reports on his family's fleet and discovered that a noble faction linked with the Imperials had attacked a section of the Tagge fleet.  Why had yet to be determined

Nichelle asked Sari to teach Dermod Force skills.  Sari didn't feel ready for the task, but reluctantly agreed.  Dermod needed convincing himself, but the mere mention that his hero Jaidyn was also learning was enough to persuade him.  He asked some difficult questions about Jedi morals and rationales that Sari did her best to answer.

In her spare time, Sari collected an entourage of pilots to flirt, gossip and amuse herself with.  Leith didn't seem to mind - she never went beyond flirting and was busy collecting useful information.  Leith enjoyed being back on a base and was busy trying to wrangle himself a base command - what he was actually trained at doing, rather than an undercover operative that he'd been so far stuck with.

Spar, Dermod and Jaidyn went Tonton riding.  Tontons were smelly beasts and Hoth wasn't exactly paradise, but it was still fun.  They arrived back at base at the start of a storm and the news that Commander Skywalker was missing.  Much against the advice of the base personnel, Captain Solo went out to look for him.  They discussed the possibility of accompanying Solo, but the Jedi had been specifically told not to use Force skills around Skywalker, so they thought they might be more hindrance than help.  Nichelle seemed confident all would be well and reiterated that Skywalker had a separate destiny than them.

The storm lifted in the morning.  Search teams found Solo and Skywalker relatively unharmed.  Solo also found an Imperial probe droid that promptly self-destructed.  Preparations were made to abandon the base.  The team persuaded General Madine that he was more use to the Alliance as a live general than dying defending an abandoned base and left on his transport.

Somehow, Spar had determined where Vernan was.  Nichelle wasn't happy with the method, though neither was prepared to discuss how he had found out, let alone why it wasn't approved.  They did persuade General Madine to plot a course to where he was.
