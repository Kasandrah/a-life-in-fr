# A Visit from Rogue Squadron

**Written by _[[Jenny-H|Jenny H.]]_**

Leith announced that Rogue Squadron was to pay them a visit.  He also asked the Circle members to keep a low profile during the visit.  Wazza, Royd, and Bursurra decided to organise a party.  The more they got into it, the more complicated the party became, going from just a party to a chance to increase funds to a full-scale training exercise.

Much to her crew's disappointment, Hadra chose this time to go ship-jacking.  Wazza wanted Morgan to help with the entertainment, but loyalty to the crew won out.

>**GM's Note:**  Jess bailed because she was afraid Wedge Antilles, Commander of Rogue Squadron, would recognise her.  This was odd, as the people who knew Jess best had been around her since she'd returned to the base as Hadra, i.e., the crew of the _Star Wind_, base personnel (Varth, Kryll, Bursurra, Royd, the "Triplets"), the other Jedi apprentices (particularly Deak, Hela, and Ross), Tor Skylow, and the Circle of the Ashla.
>
>Leith won't order Jess to remain on the base if she wants to leave.  Leith has to negotiate use of _The Maker_ with General Crix Madine - Commander, Alliance Special Forces.  Leith doesn't just order Special Forces personnel around to suit himself.   Also, Jess accepted a Special Forces command because of the freedom of action Madine delegated to his personnel.  Madine will not order Jess to remain on the base, either.
>
>As long as _The Maker_ is bringing back hijacked ships, both Madine and Leith will be happy.  Neither of them is going to tell Jess how, when, or what she can and can't do.

Sari went to Bursurra to organise a dress and ended up helping with the organisation.  They planned to have the Twi'lek dancing girls, Sari singing, Sabbac games and more.

When the squadron arrived, Kryll invited them to an official reception, which was as boring as they could make it.  By the time they were ready to agree to anything for escape, he took them on an official tour to the planet.  Halfway there, an attack was announced, though Kryll wouldn't let them return to the base to scramble their fighters.  Instead, the entire base was evacuated to the planet.

The squadron members were highly annoyed when they realised the whole thing had been an excuse to have a party, but most were mollified by the copious amounts of alcohol forced on them and the fancy dress costumes they were offered.

The theme was 'come as you aren't'.  There were pirates, Imperials and other undesirable types portrayed, plus the groups of safeworlders and Alliance personnel who simply traded costumes.

## After the Party

**Written by the GM for _[[Ben-G|Ben G.]]_ (Ash)**

Anna and Zoe would love to chase after Kezz, it's just that on some level he scares them half to death.  The ladies have decided that discretion is the better part of valour and won't be pursuing Kezz. They don't want to give Boddi any reason to be annoyed at them.  So, sighs and admiration from afar will have to do.  They do actually behave like smitten schoolgirls when Kezz is around, and this has not gone unnoticed among other base personnel.

As far as Ash can tell, Kezz is very charming with the girls and genuinely interested in their wellbeing, but no more so than he is with any other woman on the base, or down on Asher's Pride.  He has the ease of a man who knows his way around women, but there isn't so much as a rumour that he might have gone further with any particular woman.  If Ash is watching Kezz very closely, he will catch one instance otherwise.

In a more secluded corridor, Kezz takes Boddi's hand and kisses her palm, while she reaches up with her other hand and gently strokes the side of his face.  The hood of her cloak falls back from her face, and in the subdued light of the corridor, she is really quite beautiful.  Almost a match for Cass.  It's a very private moment.  If Ash can't drag himself away, then he will see them slowly vanish as Kezz takes Boddi in his arms and kisses her.  The depth of feeling is unmistakable, and the echoes of a deep love sustained over millennia and tempered by trial and separation wash over Ash as he stands staring down the empty corridor.

This isn't the light, flirtatious indulgence that Ash has had with the girls.  It certainly isn't what he thought Kezz was all about.  "Predator Beast of the Dusk", wasn't he?  Didn't he slay Boddi when she was a Dark Jedi?  Surely they'd harbour some reservations about each other now?  It's not the selfish bickering that Varth and Jess indulged in.  The physical lust is there, but as a note in a symphony, not all that there is.  At its heart is a calm acceptance of all that each is, a steadfast commitment to each other and the simple joy of each other's closeness.

There is Light in the strangest places.

Ash has nothing to worry about from Kezz.

Dermod, however, is unattached and like his father in many ways.  Now, if Anna and Zoe could just get around the thought of Boddi and Kezz as their in-laws, Dermod could be a real worry.

Yes, the girls are pursuing other relationships.  Absence didn't make their hearts grow fonder in Ash's case.  They think of him more now as a younger sibling.  Besides, all three women were hoping that Ash would eventually choose just them alone.  However, Ash didn't make a choice, and now only Carly is still interested in being with Ash.  She has a lot of patience and simply outwaited the other two.

Anna and Zoe had an exceptionally good time at the recent party come concert in the forest.  What with Rogue Squadron's hot shot pilots, Jayme from _The Maker_ (after all, Hadra doesn't seem to know what to do with him!), Dermod being so charming, Ross (a little too serious but still good for dancing with), some of the base's own pilots and the Alliance personnel on Asher's Pride, the ladies had lots of fun.

Anna was a little under the weather and didn't remember making a pass at Varth.  Neena's still a little frosty in Anna's direction, but Varth thought it was pretty funny, when he wasn't deliberately trying to avoid Hadra.  Carly still doesn't know what's up, but knows Varth seems to hold back an intense dislike of Hadra.  Even Neena's commented on it.

>**GM's Note:**  Varth recognised Jess when she returned to the base as Hadra.  He is waiting to see if, after inflicting all this obviously unnecessary pain and grief on her friends, "Hadra" will come clean.  Varth is furious at Jess for carrying out this unnecessary and hurtful charade, and has concluded that she is overwhelmingly selfish.

Neena and Varth are "an item" as they say.  Neena is a lot more committed to the Alliance than Jess ever was, takes the whole thing as life or death serious and shares Varth's intense drive to defeat the Empire.  She would never embarrass him in public and is the model of a dedicated Alliance fighter pilot.  Also, a very talented pilot to boot.

Carly will point out that Varth only returned Neena's affections after _The Maker_ arrived on the base. Since then he has been quite public about his feelings for Neena, even to the extent of kissing her passionately while they were in the flight hanger in front of the other pilots and crew after one particularly hair-raising mission.  Neena's not complaining at all, but Carly can't quite reconcile Varth's "showy" new attitude regarding Neena with his past behaviour.  His timing and his dislike of Hadra have led Carly to conclude that he's trying to make Hadra jealous. Although she has no idea why.

>**GM's Note:**  Carly has identified the symptoms, but not the root of the problem.  Carly hasn't recognised Jess, so she doesn't know that Varth's dislike of Hadra stems from his extreme disapproval of Jess' current disguise.  Also, Varth has resolved things with his ex-wife Selene, and is now ready and willing to move on with a new partner.  Neena's in the right place at the right time with the right stuff to catch Varth on the rebound from Jess.

That Neena now shares Varth's quarters is even more amazing. He's usually a very private man.  Adam Jr. is sharing with Royd and attempting to get Royd on the straight and narrow.  On that subject, Adam Jr. likes Neena a lot and is encouraging her relationship with his father. Not something Adam Jr. ever did with Jess.  Come to think of it, Adam Jr. also seems to have taken a dislike to Hadra.  "Like father, like son?" Carly muses.

>**GM's Note:**  Yep! Adam Jr. also recognised Jess.  Not straight away, but considering his father's dislike, Adam Jr. took a closer look at Hadra.  "Cool, she's turned blue, Dad!"  Varth's response was unprintable.
>
>Varth has determined, correctly, that if Jess is here as Hadra with Hadra's crew, then the real Hadra must have died while trying to save the _Star Rider_.  What about recognition for her supreme sacrifice in defence of Alliance ideals?  She gave her life for the cause, yet the Alliance has not recognised and formally honoured her sacrifice, and her name doesn't appear on the Honour Roll.  As long as Jess is masquerading as Hadra, the real Hadra can't be honoured and remembered for her sacrifice.  Varth has filed this away as yet another example of Jess' selfishness.
>
>Jess' name won't appear on the Honour Roll because she wasn't a member of the Alliance when she "died".
