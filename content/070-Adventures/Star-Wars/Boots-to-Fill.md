# Boots to Fill

## Written by _[[Jenny-H|Jenny H.]]_ (Jess)

SYSTEM REPORT TO SEC COM COMMAND

FROM: COMMANDER T'CHAREK HADRA

RE: IMPERIAL ATTACK ON CASINO LINER _STAR RIDER_

Approached by Jess Storm, trader, but with Alliance contacts.  Her ship stolen, along with at least 5 others in-system.  She suspected Alliance operation, but we were not responsible.

Enquires indicated at least 10 ships missing.  Records show ships heading to different jump points and in different directions.  No correlation.  Imperial action suspected.  Logistically, likely to be preparing for in-system operation.  Contacts indicate no non-Imperial ships at known Imperial bases.  Surmise possible connection with local crime bosses.  Unable to confirm.

Determined possible target as Crying Dawn Singer, a popular entertainer and significant religious figure for the Ho'din race.  Due in-system on the _Star Rider_ for a series of concerts.  Ho'din have potential Alliance sympathies.  Killing this being and blaming it on the Alliance would destroy any potential defection of the race.

Infiltration of the crew and passengers undertaken, along with Storm.  Probable Imperial plants in key positions in the crew, particularly in Security and Comms.  These captured or eliminated when attack by missing ships took place.  Storm and I commandeered two ships landing on liner, apparently designed to retrieve on-board operatives.

Sensors determined _Red Storm Rising_ and _Lucky Lady_ freighters loaded with thermalite.  _Lucky Lady_ destroyed by lasers.  Boarding action of _Red Storm Rising_ attempted unsuccessfully.  Storm's guns jammed.  She deliberately rammed _Red Storm Rising_.  Last transmission ended "Security risk this Caroth."  Meaning unknown.  Resulting explosion destroyed _Red Storm Rising_, Storm's ship and two other attacking ships.  Remainder of attacking fleet either neutralised or escaped.

Interrogation of plants on the liner confirmed this was an Imperial action.  Prisoners passed on to Intel agents on Reegian.  Possible defection of at least one, as retrieval of same was clearly token at best.

Recommend approach Ho'din for potential Alliance sympathies.

ADDENDUM: As per request, debris of _Red Storm Rising_ confirmed.  No escape pods jettisoned from ship.  Resulting explosion makes survival impossible.  Confirm Storm not attacking, as reported by Intel, but defending liner.

End of report.

### A NEW HOPE

Life has it's ironies.  Jess was thrown off Generis Base because Base Commander Caroth saw her as a security risk - openly contemptuous of Alliance procedures, fiercely independent and willing to go a round with a Wookie if she felt it necessary.  Now I am returning as a commander in Special Ops, dedicated to the Alliance and an apprentice Jedi.

But I get ahead of myself.  It all started when Caroth demanded Jess leave the base.  I was informed much later that this was a misreading of his orders.  Personally, I think that General Leith was correct, that her being thrown off base was part of a plot to put her unlamented grandfather at risk through Jess.

On base, she was relatively protected.  Off-base, she was at risk and there were still people who thought she had value to the Alliance Minister of Supply.

The big surprise was that none of Jess' crew decided to leave with her.  They all had friends on base and were more openly committed to the Alliance, but she had thought that after so much time together that they would have understood that she was actually as committed as they were, despite her words.

Then there was Varth.  He was hardest of all to leave.  But Jess' comments to him were accurate.  He wasn't committed to her.  His heart was still in pieces with the liner his wife and son had been on.  Still, she was as cranky as a cornered wamp rat from missing him.  Sad for a woman who was used to a man in every port.  Still, if he had truly loved her, he would never have let her go.  And if she had really loved him, she wouldn't have gone.  The epitaph on her first and only long-term relationship.

Who did leave with her was Nichelle, one of the Jedi masters, her apprentice Sari and General Leith, a recent addition from the Empire and Sari's lover.  Even that hadn't gone well.  Leith had been assigned to Bissillirus to source supplies and recruits, a ludicrous assignment for a military man.  It was most definitely a set-up to get rid of him.  However, pointing this out to him and arranging help from Deak and Tor, her old Intel friends, still didn't stop the pack of them ditching her at the first opportunity.

Well, the second opportunity.  Though ditching her on Omze's when she'd so nearly been bounty meat would have been bad form.  At least Nichelle was the only one who'd known what a ridiculously easy target she'd been in her distracted state.  However, even Nichelle didn't understand that underneath all her bluster, Jess was as committed to the Alliance as any of them.  All they cared about was her words, not her actions.

So when they rendezvoused with Deak and Tor at Draenell's Point, it was thanks for the help, you can go now.  Jess would have helped them further, but they thought her as unreliable as the others did.  She would have thought Tor would stick up for her, especially as it looked like she hadn't been consulted about the decision, but Jess had her pride.  She wasn't going to beg to stay where she clearly wasn't wanted.

Finding a new navigator was an interesting exercise.  Most of the ones Jess found couldn't navigate their way to a homing beacon; they were just looking for a free way off-planet and Jess told them to find some other sucker.  Finally she found Flar, a Torgian who wore two blasters, a male version of her first navigator and long-time friend, Flis.  They were even from the same clan.  He'd lost his last position because the navigator he was supposed to replace had sobered up long enough to keep his job.  His credentials checked out, though his ship warned her about the 'singing'.  Having put up with Flis' caterwauling for long enough, that was the least of Jess' worries.

Then followed a long period of boring, meaningless trading and freight hauling.  After the excitement of the jobs she'd done for the Alliance, even the occasional spot of smuggling was dull in comparison.  And then when her life couldn't get much worse, the _Red Storm Rising_ was stolen.

Jess had only been away a short time, but that was enough for whoever it was to break through her security systems, give the correct clearances to the spaceport and take off.  When she lodged her complaint and made enquiries, Jess learnt that there had been other ships go missing recently, all small freighters or light transports, workable with a 2 man crew.  Jess started to suspect that the Alliance had some sort of action coming up soon and that it was collecting ships for it.

Ready to live up to her name if her suspicions were correct, Jess tracked down two of the affected captains.  One of them voiced her own suspicions that the Alliance was behind it, but the other had nearly been killed while following his own line of investigation.  Which pretty well ruled out the Alliance, but left local crime lords.  Or Imperials who were planning to frame the Alliance for a particular event.

It was time to go direct to the source.  A few careful enquiries led to T'Charek Hadra and her crew.  She denied it was them, but were interested in Jess' theories.  She also promised that if they found them, the Alliance would help get the ships back.

Not prepared to wait, Jess organised access to Starport records on the missing ships.  They weren't particularly helpful, as the ships had headed to different jump points and in different directions, but she did discover that there were more missing ships on other planets in-system.  Logistically, whoever it was doing the stealing was likely to be preparing for something in-system.  When she asked them, the Alliance checked and found no indication of non-Imperial ships at known Imperial bases.

Unable to trace the ships, they looked at possible targets.  Hadra offered the most likely target as Crying Dawn Singer, a popular entertainer and significant religious figure for the Ho'din race.  He was due in-system on the luxury liner _Star Rider_ for a series of concerts. The Ho'din had potential Alliance sympathies, so killing Crying Dawn Singer and blaming it on the Alliance would destroy any potential defection of the race.

Without definite evidence of Imperial action, Jess and Hadra's team decided to simply join the crew of the liner to try and find out more.  If there was action planned, there would be plants on the liner already.  If they were wrong, it would be a pleasant diversion.

Already having established covers in-system, it was relatively easy for Hadra's team to obtain positions in medical, security and comms.  Jess used the remaining time to establish a reputation in the planet casino at the Sabbac table and then transferred to the ship.  The games were a lot more controlled and sedate than she was used to, but it gave her the opportunity to watch Crying Dawn Singer.

Jayme, Hadra's 2IC, quickly determined the probable Imperial plants in key positions in the crew, particularly in Security and Comms.  In the cargo hold, Jess discovered two ships, apparently designed to retrieve the on-board operatives.  The team laid plans to neutralise the Imperials when the now-expected attack occurred.  The element of surprise was very useful.

Once the action started and it became obvious to the liner's crew what was happening, they were happy to back up Jayme and Hadra's actions in capturing the Imperials.  Nord, Hadra's medic, had a wonderful time bossing around the captive Imperials, while Jayme took over the defences.  Not that the crew were told they were Imperials - that would just complicate matters - they somehow gained the impression that the attackers were merely every-day criminals and that Hadra's team had been sent undercover by the authorities.  Which just left the attacking force.

Commandeering the two ships in the hold was all very well, but they weren't exactly brimming with weapons.  Sensors also indicated that the 2 light freighters, the _Red Storm Rising_ and _Lucky Lady_, were loaded with thermalite and headed in the liner's direction.  It was quite clear that stopping their assault on the liner wasn't going to be easy.

Between the weapons the liner had to discourage pirates and the limited weapons Jess and Hadra commanded, they accounted for several of the accompanying vessels, but the two freighters continued their inexorable passage towards the liner.

The _Red Storm Rising_ had modified shields and it was probable the _Lucky Lady_ wasn't the standard stock light freighter either.  That's why they were targeted for the mission.  Jess edged her borrowed ship closer, trying to see if she could get an opening to try and board the _Red Storm Rising_, but a blast that narrowly missed her showed her how hopeless that thought was.  Then she heard Hadra's COM link crackle.  "My guns are jammed.  This isn't going to work.  Any brilliant ideas?"

"I can lay down covering fire for you, but I can't see how we're going to stop them. Can we evacuate Crying Dawn Singer?"

"That still leaves the rest of the passengers. I'm going to try something else."

"Yee ha!" It was Hadra's next transmission that alerted Jess to what she had in mind, but it was too late to stop her.  Hadra had rammed her ship directly into the _Lucky Lady_.  The momentum was enough to set off the load of thermalite, which was already set to detonate on impact.  The resulting explosion took out the freighter and two other ships.

Jess swallowed hard, in shock.  Jayme's urgent, "What happened?" on the COM link alerted her to the still present danger.  Jess knew her ship.  There was a tiny section of the hull that wasn't covered by the shields.  A reminder of a nasty accident with a canyon wall.  She targeted it and fired.  The resulting fireball wasn't as great a sacrifice as Hadra's but it still hurt.  But at least it was her hand that killed her ship.

It took short work between her and the liner's guns to persuade the rest of the attackers that it wasn't worth the effort of a failed mission to continue.

Jess and Flar sat in a bar, morosely nursing a synthale each.  They would have to make some decisions soon, but at the moment neither of them felt up to it. Jess looked up as the surviving crew members of _The Maker_ slid into the booth beside them.  There was a suppressed excitement about them.  Jess wondered what they were hatching now.

Jayme took the role of spokesperson.  "We were wondering if you'd given any thought as to what you were going to do now."

Jess shrugged.  "I have a promising career as a Sabbac supervisor."

"We had wondered if you'd thought about joining up."

Jess had expected that.  "I don't think I'm cut out to be an X-wing pilot."

"We were thinking more about a commander in Special Ops."

They had her attention now.  "What did you have in mind?"

"You look a lot like Hadra and have the same sort of skills.  If you dyed your skin blue and your hair black, only the people who know her well would know.  And you did say you had powerful people after you.  If you were dead, they'd stop looking."

It did make sense in a way.  No, it was a crazy idea, but it could have its advantages.  For her, but what was in it for them?  "Why?  Why wouldn't you want someone you knew better to take over?"

Morgan answered for Jayme.  "Because we've heard who's in-system and would most likely take over.  Lorn is a rules-junkie.  We've crossed lasers before and there's no way we want him leading our team.  If Hadra wasn't dead, there'd be no reason for it."

Jess tried to think of the holes in the plan.  "And this is a unanimous request?"  She scanned the group, but her eyes rested on Nord.  If anyone were opposed, it would be him. Nord realised what she was thinking.  "Lorn opposed my transfer to Special Ops.  It would not be pleasant working for him."

"If I agreed to this, then it would be a permanent change.  You couldn't decide down the track that you'd changed your minds."

Jayme replied quickly, sensing her tacit agreement.  "We've worked with you once.  We can't see any reason why we couldn't work well long-term."

"I can't navigate well enough to cover that side of things.  Flar has to be part of the agreement too."

"We've thought of that.  What would make better sense than us enlisting Storm's old navigator.  If he wants to?"

Flar growled his agreement.  "I think that settles it then.  Let's drink a toast to the memory of Jess Storm.  Long live Hadra T'Charek."  Jess raised her glass and couldn't repress a smug smile.  All those people who had tried to get her to join the Alliance and these people had succeeded in five minutes.

"Long live T'Charek Hadra!"

So here I am, trying to fill Hadra's boots.  And how did I become an apprentice Jedi?  Well, that's another story.
