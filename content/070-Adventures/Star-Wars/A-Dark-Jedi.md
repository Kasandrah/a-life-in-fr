# A Dark Jedi

>**GM's Note:**  This episode starred an old adversary, Aldric the Dark Jedi, who escaped the party in an earlier encounter.  Aldric had arrived on Ngieba in an ancient but still potent Sith Interceptor, a ship constructed from a unique ebony glass-like substance.  This ship is the first real clue of the return of the Sith.
>
>It was also an opportunity for the party to learn that running about with low-level followers in tow, even Alliance Marines, could prove very dangerous for the followers.  The party has advanced to a level where any credible threat to them could easily prove fatal for their non-Jedi followers.

## Written by [[Jenny-H|Jenny H.]] (Hadra)

All the captains were summoned to talk to General Leith.  Elan turned up, as he didn't trust either Lou or Ash to be the captain.  He still invited Ash, deciding that a genuine Alliance presence was probably called for.  They were all (Wedge, Hadra, Elan, Ash and Ross) being sent to scout out a planet proposed as an Alliance safe world.  The initial survey had been promising, but there were inconsistencies, which was why the group was being sent en masse.

Ngieba turned out to be a marshy, tropical planet.  It had an elliptical 18-hour orbit around an asteroid belt.  While still in orbit, Flar informed them that the planet would be subject to regular asteroid activity.  As such, it was a likely haven for smugglers and other undesirables hiding out.  Not a good start.

Hadra and Feylis had an increasingly flashy race down to the planet surface, to be met by torrential rain.  They landed in mud, though were lucky enough to find relatively solid ground.  Ash's routine Force scan detected a village of about 20 souls and a Dark Jedi.  The detection also alerted the Dark Jedi to their presence.

The group, with a small marine escort, headed towards the village to investigate further.  They came under attack by a rancor, which Ash determined was illusionary.  It took a great deal of presence of mind to disbelieve it, but they passed unharmed.

Next, a huge tree fell in the middle of the group, narrowly missing Ash and Elan.  Ash resisted the mind attack that came quickly afterwards, but the last marine in line suddenly shot the marine in front of him in the back.  The sergeant automatically turned and fired at him.

Down two marines to what was obviously mind control, they applied med packs and called for the _Star Wind_ to come and collect them.  Ash levitated the injured and the Sergeant up to avoid a rocky landing.  Hadra was particularly keen to have the non-Jedi's return as well, but Wedge and Feylis refused to leave, despite being warned about their susceptibility to mind control.

When they reached the village, they found it inhabited by green, scaly lizards of the same evolutionary chain as Barabels, but much more primitive.  Hadra was able to follow their language enough to translate.

"Put down your weapons and follow us."

They complied with the obvious weapons and were lead into the village to meet with a heavily painted chief.  He was convinced that they were there to hunt them for their skins.  Hadra protested that they were actually there to make friends and anyone who said otherwise was a liar.

The chief was a little taken aback, but agreed to discuss the matter with the people.  They were led to a stockade within a cave and locked in.  The group agreed to this as a gesture of goodwill and promptly broke out when the lizards had left.  Then they sat around and played cards to pass the time.

This and their reasonable attitude when the group of lizards planning to execute them turned up helped diffuse the situation a little.  The resulting discussions amongst the lizard folk gave the impression that they were worried something had happened to the chief.  The group acted politely and asked when they would be able to present their gifts to the chief, confusing them even further.

On their way to see the chief, Hadra asked one of the young lizard folk what was happening in the village.  The teen started to speak, then started to choke violently - obviously an attack by a Dark Jedi.  The reaction from the folk around was understandably antagonistic.  Hadra tried to look inoffensive and tried to calm the situation.  Ash detected the presence of a Dark Jedi back in the cave, made more obvious by the large boulder he hurled at them.  Ash and Elan headed back to try and find him.

In the meantime, Hadra gave an impassioned speech to the lizard folk, convincing them that they were being used.  ("Let us not go crying into the night...")  This broke the Dark Jedi's control so that they became merely confused and amnesiac, rather than bloodthirsty.

>**GM's Note:**  Even with the little formal Jedi training Jess has had to date, she could break the mind control the Dark Jedi had over the village's populace.  This forced Jess to rethink her belief that she has no Force abilities and to reconsider training with a more suitable Circle member.  Jess asks Dermod to train her.

Back in the cave, Ash was mind-controlled by the Dark Jedi into attacking Elan with his lightsaber.  Elan avoided the attacks and to draw him further into the cave, where he met Aldric, the Dark Jedi.

"You won't succeed, you know," stated Elan calmly.  Aldric sneered back, "Prepare to die."  Elan tried to persuade Aldric to return to the Light Side, but failed.

With the situation outside under control, Jayme and Wedge headed to the cave to provide backup.  They fired at the roof above Aldric as a distraction.  They failed to bring down the roof, but did provide an opportunity for Ash to break free of Aldric's mind control.

Elan took a light sabre to Aldric.  "The Dark Side deserts you."  Aldric threw a huge rock at Ash, who threw it back at him and caused the roof to cave-in.  "May the Ashla forgive you" stated Elan before taking a mighty sweep with his light sabre and cleaving Aldric in half.  The party fled from the Dark Side force storm that erupted from Aldric's remains.

A little searching around the outskirts of the cave revealed a fully functional Sith Interceptor still emanating Dark Side energy.  As blasting it had no effect, the ship was re-programmed to fly into a sun.

The group reported back that the planet was unsuitable as an Alliance safe world.

A few days after the team returned to base, the marines that had accompanied them developed severe morale problems.  The team suspected that this was a consequence of being totally unequipped to deal with Dark Jedi mind tricks.  Elan proposes that the marines be trained to resist mind control, and approaches the Circle members to seek approval and support for this training.  They reluctantly agree, but only if Dermod trains the marines.
