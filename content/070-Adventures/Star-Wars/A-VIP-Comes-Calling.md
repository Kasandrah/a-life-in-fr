# A VIP Comes Calling

Princess Leia arrives on Generis Base in her blockade-runner.  She is the VIP whose visit was delayed by the Imperial spy uncovered after the training exercise and the appearance of the bounty hunter, Crutag.

She formally inspects the base and greets the officers, including Deak, while her crew unload weapons and supplies for the base.  Deak recognises Jess's grandfather Elkior Toiyabe, Alliance Minister for Supply, amongst Princess Leia's party.

A few days after the Princess leaves Generis Base, word is received from Intel that the Princess's ship was destroyed in some kind of "accident".  All aboard were killed.
