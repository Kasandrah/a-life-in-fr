# Conspiracy on Corellia

>**GM's Note:** Jess Storm exchanged a ship cloaking device she found in Minos Sector for Alliance gold and a haven on Generis Base.  In order to persuade the Twi'lek crime lord Yerkys ne Dago to leave her alone, Jess instructed the Alliance to deliver her gold to Yerkys.  Because of the intervention of an enterprising ISB double agent, Yerkys never received the gold.
>
>Still believing Jess owed him for the cargo she dumped (see _[[Calm-Before-the-Storm|Calm Before the Storm]]_) and having heard news on Taran Storm's whereabouts from a Rodian informant, Yerkys arranged an "accident" for _Storm Clouds_ and captured Taran and his navigator, Tyler Jensen.
>
>Taran's friends on Corellia knew he was missing but couldn't contact Jess, as she was on a secret Rebel base.  Then one of them saw the wreck brought into the shipyards.  The story was that Taran and his navigator had been killed and _Storm Clouds_ nearly destroyed in a subspace accident.  Taran's friends sold the wreck for scrap and held the money on Jess' behalf.
>
>Unknown to Taran's friends, an agent of Yerkys ne Dago bought the wreck.  Yerkys funded the repairs, then instructed his Arconian agent Garindam to sell the ship to someone who would use it in Jess' haunts.  Yerkys hoped to bait Jess out from wherever she was hiding.  This led to the events described in _[[Storm-Front|Storm Front]]_.
>
>Yerkys now believes Jess owes him for his original cargo and his hideout on Farstey, destroyed when Jess, Deak and company rescued Taran and Tyler from Yerkys' dungeon.  Supplying slaves, drugs and nerve toxins is a lucrative business, and Yerkys has rebuilt quickly after losing the last round to Jess and Taran.  Once again, Yerkys ne Dago is turning his attention to the Storm Family.
>
>The enterprising ISB double agent who "re-directed" Jess' Alliance gold has since moved on to Corellia.  Once there, the agent intercepted all of Jess' messages to her father that she sent via Gwilliam Hall, Taran's cargo supplier, including a box with the Toiyabe family crest.  The agent gave the box to the Imperial Governor on Corellia, who wants to capture Elkior Toiyabe, Jess' grandfather and the Alliance Minister of Supply, and hand him over to the Emperor.
>
>While Yerkys ne Dago and the Imperial Governor of Corellia are making separate plans against the Storm Family, Jess' mother Annalise Storm brings matters to a head.  Having lost contact with Taran, and prompted by a message from Jess via an Alliance agent on Andron Star (the droid C3RJ), Annalise sees Gwilliam Hall.  Soon after the crew of the _Red Storm Rising_ rescue Taran and Tyler from Yerkys ne Dago's dungeons, Annalise leaves the Snow Flower Festival on Andron Star and travels to Corellia aboard the liner _Star Gate_.

## Written by _[[Jenny-H|Jenny H.]]_ (Jess)

Jess gave _Storm Clouds_ a final going-over.  She really wanted to use the _Red Storm Rising_, but it was a little too conspicuous for ordinary trading.  Too much armoury for even a semi-honest trader.  Much safer to take the less readily identifiable _Storm Clouds_.  But it was still a wrench.  Taran Storm put an arm around his daughter's shoulders.

"Don't worry, Jess, Deak will look after her.  We'll be fine on my ship."

"My ship," replied Jess, automatically.  Her father grunted.

"I just wish I could leave her to someone who would, oh, I don't know.  Still, Tor will keep an eye on Deak and I've warned him that she's only to be used for the direst emergencies and if there's so much as a scratch on her when I get back..."

"You're babbling.  They already know their lives would be misery if that happened.  Don't worry."

Jess grinned ruefully.  "I think I'm going through withdrawal already!"

"Do you good to break the habit then!"

It felt good to be back with her father.  Their bantering didn't have some of the undercurrents the crew's conversations with her had and Jess could relax with him.  It ought to feel funny to have the kind of relationship with her father that most women had with their mothers, but it really didn't.  Annalise and Jess had never understood each other.  Total opposites.  Well, not really, but it often seemed that way.

Time for the farewells.  She wandered off to Varth's office.

"You're all ready then?" Varth asked.

"About to ship out," Jess replied.  She steeled herself for the usual brush-off.

"Be careful, Jess.  The Alliance doesn't want to lose a Captain of your calibre.  When you come back, you will have to show me how your present is worn.  Good luck."

Jess had automatically bridled until she realised that, although Varth had begun in a fairly business-like tone, there was the sound of genuine affection in his voice.  Even a definite twinkle at the mention of the box.  It almost threw her, and she gave him a strange look.  She felt like saying she'd miss him, but didn't.  Instead, she mumbled something trite and left.  No sense in making an utter fool of herself.

Roger was already on board.  He was really looking forward to this.  Anything Jess didn't want him to know, he was vitally interested in.  Lou, in the absence of a better offer, had also come.  Nichelle also wanted to come.  She informed them that life on the base was too restrictive, Boddi and Troy too dour, and most of the men too young.  The latter with a speculative glance in Taran's direction.

Tyler had already calculated the jump to Fest.  They were off to pick up some more gourmet food processors.

"What is it about gourmet food processors that has you hooked?"  Nichelle goaded Jess.  "Isn't that an incongruous cargo for a cynical spacer like you?  What next, pate forks?"

It's going to be one of those trips, thought Jess, and it hasn't even started yet.  "Food processors will sell well on Andron Star," she replied, in her best cynical spacer manner.  "If pate forks sold well, I'd have a cargo of those instead."

They were going to Andron Star for the first try.  Annalise loved the Snow Flower Festival and they had a good chance of finding her there.  The jumps were straightforward, dull and uneventful.  They had time to discuss possibilities.

"I sent Mother a message, saying she might be in danger from a mutual enemy and that she was to contact someone to get her safely out.  Deak gave me names of Rebel contacts on a couple of places, but I didn't say who they were, just that they would help her.  Given that my messages to you didn't get through, I don't know whether she got it or not.  We'd best assume not."

Nichelle asked, "You didn't send those names over an open channel, did you?  Something like that would be sure to come to the notice of Imperial communication scanners."

"I told her that they were friends.  I do have friends, you know" Jess replied with heavy sarcasm.  "I'll grant you that if I had no friends, it would be suspicious, but I doubt anyone listening in is going to decide 'Jess Storm is sending a message about friends, they must be Rebel friends' ".

Jess also got the other half of Taran's story.  His only link to the Alliance was through Gwilliam Hall, one of his suppliers of cargo on Corellia.  Hall was also Tor's link to the Alliance, via Taran.

"Then Tor could easily have been betrayed by him as well.  Did you tell Deak any of this?"

"No, should I have?"

"Well, besides Deak's interest in Tor's welfare, he has a lot of contacts in the Alliance."

"I talked to the Intel officers on Generis Base and they're following all this up."

Nichelle interrupted again.  "They knew Taran's cargo of repeating blasters was lost.  Their agent on Corellia told them the freighter met with an accident, but they didn't know who the Captain was.  The agent was probably following Alliance policy of not mentioning the names of Alliance sympathisers.  As far as they're concerned, everything is fine on Corellia."

Sometimes it's handy to have a mind reader in your midst, thought Jess.  She looked at Nichelle's angelic demeanour.  Sometimes!

"I've got Deak following up my messages that never got to you.  We don't know for sure, but we've some suspicions he's Intel as well."

"Well, well, well.  He doesn't look sneaky enough."

"You'd be surprised!  Well, it looks like Gwilliam Hall was the block for your messages, but it means that mine should have gotten through to Mother.  They weren't sent via the Alliance at all."

"Up until now, I've trusted Gwilliam.  He's a long-time business associate.  Things have run pretty smoothly.  He handles a lot of cargo for the Alliance and supplies many freighter captains out of Corellia.  He has large warehousing facilities at all the major spaceports on Corellia.  Gwilliam has had several run-ins with Yerkys ne Dago over the years and hates him as much as I do."

"What's Hall doing turning you into ne Dago for then?"

"I don't know.  I do know I'm one of the few captains aware of Hall's link with the Alliance.  The others aren't aware they're carrying cargo to and from the Alliance."

"Maybe he wants out."

"Could be."

"Perhaps it was another agent who was the problem," offered Nichelle.  "You're assuming Gwilliam Hall and the Alliance agent are the same.  He was Taran and Tor's only link to the Alliance, but does that mean he was the Alliance's only link to Corellia.  It might have been the next link in the chain who cracked."

"Possible," mused Taran.  "Anyway, when I was taken I was carrying a cargo of repeating blasters from an Alliance-friendly planet named Bestine back to Corellia.  The proximity alert went off, and _Storm Clouds_ dropped into normal space.  Turned out to be an asteroid that had drifted into the space lanes.  I wasn't worried at first, but then three heavily armed freighters came out from behind the asteroid.  I suppose they deliberately towed the asteroid into the space lane.  It was just a little one, big enough to cause trouble if I hadn't dropped out of hyperspace."

"Common ploy of pirates," offered Roger.

"You'd know," commented Jess.

"I thought it was a bit of bad luck at first.  They boarded the ship, took Tyler and me prisoner, and rigged the ship to explode."

"But when we got it back, it wasn't that bad."

"Not bad!  They blew half the side out!"  Taran stopped and looked exasperated at Jess' smirk.  "Well, they were pretty careful with how much and where they put the detonate.  I didn't know why, but they must have wanted to make sure they only damaged her, not destroyed her."

"But why?  Yerkys could have sold her intact.  The only effect was to worry me more than if she'd been whole."  Jess frowned.  "It looks more and more like a personal vendetta, but why?  What have I done to cross him?"

"That's 'what have I done to cross him'.  He's been after me for years."

"OK, same question.  What have you done to cross him?"

"I've become too successful and came to his notice.  He wants a piece of the action and has been pushing me for years.  I'd say he's finally taken more than his considerable ego can stand and opened hostilities.  I refused to freight cargo for him under any circumstances."

"You could have warned me."

"I didn't know it had gotten that bad until Flis was killed.  Then I thought you were safe in Minos Sector.  I was hoping it would all blow over."

"Not when I cost him a cargo of nerve toxins and the fun of seeing me fry for having them.  Yerkys is not known for his forgiving attitude."

"All I know is that when _Storm Clouds_ blew, I felt the pain in my heart, even though we were in hyperspace."

"Can't have," Jess teased.  "The Force skills are on Mother's side of the family."

"And who was it going berserk about leaving her ship?" asked Taran.

"That's different."

"Sure it is."

"Just get on with the story, will you!"

"There's nothing more to tell.  We were taken to Farstey and locked up.  Yerkys got a cargo of blasters for free and pretty well ignored us.  The only thing he said to us was, 'No one gets the better of Yerkys ne Dago.  Not you, not your smart mouthed seed and not those puny Alliance upstarts.  Let this be a lesson to you.'  We never saw him again.  I suppose he couldn't find you and went after me."

"Hang on, didn't the Alliance wonder where their blasters went?"

"Perhaps they weren't a proper cargo," offered Lou.  "He could have been creaming them off in case he needed them for something like this."

"I suppose so," replied Jess, doubtfully.  "Hang on, how did he know where you'd be?"

"Gwilliam had set the route.  I could have followed it up when we were on Corellia, but with Yerkys unlikely to be dead and more than likely on our tail, it didn't seem like the sensible thing to do at the time.  The Intel agents on Generis said they'd deal with him, but I'd like to know when."

"When they get Yerkys off my tail, like we agreed" said Jess, dryly.  "Don't hold your breath!"

Later, away from the rest of the crew, Taran informed Jess that Tyler really didn't like pirates, so he'd appreciate Jess' help in keeping Roger out of Tyler's hair.

"Great!" she replied.  "That's all I need.  Look, the best thing, going on past performance, is for Tyler not to let on.  And if Roger notices I'm trying to keep him out of Tyler's hair he'll be all over him like a Ranwen rash.  All right, I'll see what I can do."

Andron Star was a small but very salubrious colony, famed for its yearly Snow Flower Festival.  It didn't take long to track down Annalise Storm.  Andron Star's finest hotel was famed throughout this and neighbouring sectors as a superb throwback to the style and elegance of the Old Republic.  A scion of Toiyabe House would consider it mandatory to stay there during the Festival.

And she had, up until shortly before.  An insect-like receptionist informed them in pleasant tones that Annalise Storm had been staying at the hotel, but regretted to inform them that she had left about three weeks before.

"Where did she go?"

"Well, she did make some rather hurried travel arrangements through Reception.  I believe she went to Corellia."

"Corellia?" blurted out Jess.  "Oh great!  Do you know where Annalise went and how?  Did she get a message from me?"

"Yes.  Madam then contacted Passenger Services at the spaceport and reserved a stateroom on the next liner to Corellia.  Two days later Madam left in an air car which took her to the spaceport."

That made it fairly conclusive.  "I should have been clearer that she wasn't to go looking for you.  Damn!  How did she expect to find you anyway?  It's not exactly easy to track you down!"

The Rebellion contact was a protocol droid called C3RJ, assigned to the passenger desk at the Andron Star spaceport.  He informed them that he had reserved a stateroom for Annalise on the _Star Gate_ 'as per her request'.  Her destination was Corellia.

"Why Corellia? Why not somewhere safer?"

"I was acting on her instructions."

"Oh, for the moons of Stigod!  Didn't you make any suggestions?"

Her father patted her arm.  "Jess," was all he needed to say.

Later he told Jess it made sense.  Annalise had always felt she could rely on him to help and could always get to him through Gwilliam Hall.  C3RJ would have had no reason to tell her not to go there.

"Are you sure she left?" Taran asked the droid.

"Certainly."

"Can we intercept her?"

Taran checked the stops the liner would make and did some quick mental calculations.  "We should get there about two days before the _Star Gate_ is scheduled to arrive."  They left Andron Star quickly.

Although the long jump through hyperspace felt like forever, they arrived in Corellia's system with two days to spare.  This was home turf and Jess and Taran knew how to avoid detection.  As they were sneaking in, they noticed a huge passenger liner in geosynchronous orbit.  A few subtle enquiries elicited the information that the _Star Gate_ had missed her last port of call before Corellia, due to an outbreak of Bledsoe's disease, and had arrived at Corellia four days early.  Annalise had had the previous two days to get into all sorts of trouble.  Jess' reply was unprintable.

A quick inquiry brought the information that Annalise had two days previously taken an air car to Gwilliam Hall's main warehouse.

A side trip to their favourite haunts elicited an exuberant greeting from Taran's friends.  They had assumed he and Tyler were dead when the wreck of the _Storm Clouds_ was brought into the shipyards.  No one could have survived the damage to it, though they were surprised that someone was prepared to pay for its repairs.  Considering how much de Vere had paid for it, they knew someone had credits to burn!

Over the next period of drinking, which they assured him wasn't a patch on his wake, they learned that Gwilliam Hall had been roughed up by thugs two days previously and was currently in the med centre.  He had not regained consciousness, as the injuries were quite severe. Nichelle offered to help.

At the med centre, the group was denied access to Gwilliam, until Nichelle "suggested" they had a right to see him.  They found Gwilliam attached to more hoses than a disabled spaceship.  Nichelle soon hastened his healing and at the same time was able to elicit information about recent events.

Annalise had arrived at Gwilliam's warehouse and he had the unenviable task of telling her about Taran's supposed death and his reappearance with Jess a few weeks previously.  Her indignation at not being told sooner was very impressive.  Before he could finish discussing the situation with her, the warehouse was attacked.  Only he and Annalise were there and they were soon overcome.  After beating Gwilliam to within an inch of his life, the leader of the gang told him he could have Annalise back in exchange for Elkior Toiyabe.

It took a little while before Jess was able to coherently express her opinion of the matter.  Firstly, it meant that her messages to Taran had definitely been intercepted and the family code broken.  Exchanging Annalise for Elkior had three problems.  One, he hadn't given a brass jogki for them in the past, why should he care now?  Two, the Alliance weren't going to allow him to do an exchange and three, they didn't have the brawn to be able to get him themselves.

It was a lot simpler to just stage another rescue, which was exactly what they did!

>**GM's Note:** The crews of the _Red Storm Rising_ and the _Dark Heart_ rescued Annalise from the clutches of the Governor of Corellia.  While treating Annalise as his honoured guest, the Governor was secretly using her as leverage in capturing her father, Elkior Toiyabe.
>
>While trying to remove Annalise from the Governor's exclusive formal dinner at a first class lakeside hotel, the group had to contend with Deak's near fatal poisoning at the hands of Mara Jade, his former lover and now an Imperial assassin and ISB double agent.  The crew of the _Red Storm Rising_ saved Deak's life and freed Annalise from the Governor's guards.
>
>With the addition of _[[010-Star-Wars-Characters#Alvak, Captain T.|Alvak]]_, an Imperial TIE fighter pilot defecting to the Alliance, the group fled to the spaceport just ahead of the Governor's forces.  The _Red Storm Rising_ and the _Dark Heart_ blasted out of the spaceport and headed for the first hyperspace jump coordinates.
>
>Before either ship could achieve hyperspace, Imperial fighters attacked.  Superior piloting and gunnery won out, and the _Red Storm Rising_ and the _Dark Heart_ made the first jump back to Generis Base.  Annalise rejoined Taran Storm onboard _Storm Clouds_.
