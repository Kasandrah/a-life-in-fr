# Novel Technology

Daye, Tinian, Jayme and Morgan started to help Nadoc put together what might turn out to be a ship.  Daye was impressed with the 'novel' concept and had to be persuaded not to try and patent it.  Morgan was more concerned with how the black hole drive and star matter life support systems were supposed to work.
