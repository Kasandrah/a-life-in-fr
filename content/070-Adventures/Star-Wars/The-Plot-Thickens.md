# The Plot Thickens

Over the following months the _Red Storm Rising_ and her crew are kept very busy.

While on Adarlon the crew accept a three month contract to carry a small travelling circus to all the systems in Minos Sector, starting with Yelsain.  The circus is actually a team of Rebel spies under the leadership of Terno, the director of the Kitonak band.  Their objectives are to find out what happened to Drun Cairnwick, leader of the Rebels in Minos Sector, re-establish some sort of Rebel network and to build people's resistance to the Empire.  The crew also pick up a young woman named Hela, the sole survivor of an Imperial raid on a backwater planet.  Hela's mother was killed in the raid and now Hela is looking for her aunt, Boddi.

While on Yelsain the crew join the Grand Moot just south of Tradetown, where they help the circus set up for performances.  Here the scout Lou Ashton joins the crew, as does Hela's aunt, a mysterious woman by the name of Boddi.  Boddi is in a hurry to leave Yelsain and pays for her passage with a handful of small red gems.  The last two new crewmembers onboard before lift-off are Nogradus, an ex-Imperial storm trooper now looking to join the Rebel Alliance, and Deak Freehold.  Deak's Alliance contact in Minos Sector, Tor Skylow, has told him where to find Jess.  Deak tells Jess that her maternal grandfather, who Jess thought had been dead for years, is alive and an important member of the Rebel Alliance.  He also tells her that her father, Taran Storm, is a member of the Rebel Alliance.  Rebellion runs in Jess's family on both sides!

While on Eliad, the crew attend a young noble's party to add "atmosphere", brushing shoulders with "The Iceman".  Also there is the pirate, Tor Skylow, and her crew.  They are adding to the "atmosphere" for a large fee.

While in the Mestra system, the crew become acquainted with "The Vigil", a pro-Alliance group of rich ex-miners' sons.  Here Zeff leaves to join the Alliance, and [[Asher-Norton-Tagge|Asher Fletcher]] joins the crew as the new navigator.  The crew help a prospector remove a valuable alien artefact, a weapon with enough potential power to destroy a Star Destroyer, before a company processing ship could crush his claim.

After returning to Eliad, Deak Freehold contacts a group of young men, sons of the Eliad nobles, who wish to join the Alliance.  The youths are taken on board and, with the prospector and his find, are delivered to the Alliance via Tor Skylow and, surprise surprise, Taran Storm, Jess' father.  Tor arranges a new name and registration for the _Red Storm Rising_, and Jess and her crew are well paid for their share in the recovered weapon.

### First Message

**Written by [[Jenny-H|Jenny H.]] (Jess)**

Transmission from _Red Storm Rising_ to _Storm Clouds_.

Hi Dad!

I don't know how long this will take to get to you, but you will probably have already heard about what I'm doing anyway.  The chances are I'll be already dead, on the run or stuck in some prison asteroid.  I know you said Minos Sector is the back of beyond and you were right there, it's even more of a hole than you said - but you know me.  If there's a sniff of trouble anywhere, I'll find it!

Currently, I have a hold full of illegal passengers we had to shoot up some Imperials to get, and alien weapon we had to shoot up a company ship to get, and a passenger list that reads like a "Who's Who" of the Rebellion!  I'd better start at the beginning.

As soon as I arrived, I got to work trying to find some freight to haul.  Figured I'd start on something basic until I found my feet.  Got a contract and got back to the ship to find my navigator was breathing through his stomach.  Calling card said it was the Iceman - a local bounty hunter.  Dumb bastard, beats me how he got himself in that deep in a matter of hours.  Scared the willies out of me - thought the Iceman was after me.

Hired myself Zeff, a short green Chi Chi Psi with the unpleasant habit of being totally honest.  I don't know how these races survive.  Didn't find that out before he tried to make a deal for me - fire control systems for turbo lasers for a ridiculous price just because that was the original fee for carrying the stuff!  Can you believe it!

That was before I found out transporting the stuff involved taking it to an Imperial asteroid prison planet and getting jumped by pirates on route.  I got a better price for the two I saved from them.  Now don't worry, they got to who I was to deliver them to in a roundabout way, and I got what fee I should have got in the first place.

I'm carrying a bit of freight, a few passengers and have a contract to ferry a circus troupe around the planets.    Trouble is they're the local Rebellion organisers.  Found that out too late too!  My passengers are an amazing list.  Hela and her aunt Boddi are a couple of Jedi.  I took Hela on as cook, bottle washer and navvy.  She cooks a mean salad and her headache preparations are something else.  Haven't yet got her to do me a good cure for a hangover.  She disapproves of my wild style.  A real health fanatic.

I didn't think much of this Force stuff, but a couple of things happened that have me wondering.  Detected a fault in one of the ship's systems that could have been messy.  No way she could have done that herself - I checked with Zeff.  And a couple of times we talked our way out of tricky situations.  I'm trying to keep a more open mind.

Nogradus turned out to be an AWOL Imperial spy.  So he says anyway.  Definitely an AWOL Imperial, but his methods of trying to find the local Rebellion are so open, they're laughable, so I don't think much of his spying techniques!  Wants to join up he says!  I'd reckon something else, but Hela and Boddi say otherwise and I trust them in this.

Nah, if anyone was an Imperial spy, it would be Lou.  He's the quiet type - a scout who's paying his way until he finds an interesting planet to get off at.  I've stopped charging him, as his local knowledge is worth heaps more to me.  Knows a lot of places where I can get better deals.

The last one is interesting.  Deak Freehold.  I don't know if that's his real name, but he's a big shot from the Core Worlds and certainly knows a lot about us.  Says Grandfather is still alive and is big in the Rebellion.  Feasible I suppose.  He knows a few details that sound right.  Tricky to be sure.  I never met him.  Could have sent me a few birthday presents if what Deak says is right and he was interested in me.

Deak then went on to say you were involved as well.  I laughed myself silly.  Not when there's no money in it and a short lifespan.  I reckon he's just trying to get my services for free.  Reckon he might anyway if any more things happen.  I'll have to keep my nose clean for a while to avoid the Imperials getting wind of me and there's fat chance of that!  Still, at least, if I'm going to get involved with the Rebellion, they sent me a cute guy as a contact!  A bit straight for my tastes, but a nice bum!

The bank balance is healthy and now I'm getting the hang of the place, should get healthier.  Nogradus is bankrolling cargo in part.  The handling fee is pretty good.  Trouble is, with the pirates, Imperials and Company men around the place, I need some shields and firepower first.

I finished the model.  That should be with this.  I'm working on one of a Bantha now.  Can't get the fur quite right though.  Will take a bit of practice I guess.

Take Care!

Jess

___

Now that she can afford them, Jess goes to Adarlon to have improvements made to her ship by Alliance-recommended mechanics.  Jess' ship is heavily modified by little furry experts, Chortose natives led by Porgo Goo, in a secret underground shipyard in the Adarlon mountains.  While on Adarlon, Deak and Nogradus meet with a vampiric noble in a fantasy dungeon at one of the theme parks.  Here they are offered "No Holds Barred" holos for sale to black market contacts in other systems.  "The Iceman", acting under contract from the Imperial Sector Moff who suspects the crew of being rebels, attempts to hunt down Deak, Lou and Nogradus after they joined the rest of the crew at the shipyard.  They defeat him, and Boddi puts him in hibernation deep sleep after revealing to Jess that he is a clone.

Later, while still at the underground shipyard, the crew meet Wing Commander Varth and Wedge Antilles, forced to dock at the Goo brothers' underground shipyard for repairs.  Their Y-Wing was damaged after becoming involved in a dust-up between a Tagge House ship and an Imperial battle cruiser.  Before anyone can leave, Imperial forces attack the underground shipyard.  Someone inside sabotaged the grav-lift, making departure even more hazardous.  Some crews narrowly escape; others are not so lucky.

## Second Message

**Written by [[Jenny-H|Jenny H.]] (Jess)**

Transmission from _Silver Griffin_ to _Storm Clouds_.

Hi Dad!

Yes, I have a new name.  Actually my second new name.  Tor was really pissed, but things got hot before the _New Dawn's_ paint was dry.

Let's see - after I came back from my meeting with you, I went to get the ship's alterations done.  By the way, laughed myself silly.  So should you when you get my vid-com.  There I was spouting off about you not being the type to be Rebellion material and there you were as my contact!

You have no idea what it feels like to be free of those spoilt brats.  I wish the Rebellion well with them!  And with the miner and that weapon gone, my ship is my own! Free!  Well, except for the rest of my crew that is.

Anyway, we headed off to Adarlon to get my modifications done, about 30,000 credits worth.  I had a wonderful time working out what to get.  Yes, I know I should really play it safe and pay back the money I owe Yerkys ne Dago, but who wants to play safe?  Besides, I still contend I don't owe him anything.  He can't know I jettisoned his cursed drugs deliberately.  And anyone can get boarded!

Anyway, those little furries drove me nuts!  Took the ship apart in no time flat.  I would have taken myself off to the capital, but I was one of the few crew left, so I just took myself off to the bar to drown my sorrows.  There I found the Iceman waiting for a kill.  Gave me the shivers.  He didn't want me, that's for sure.  When Deak and Nogradus got back they had a wild story about being followed.  They then got very paranoid about the Iceman.

I took Boddi and went to talk to him.  I was furious with Deak and Nogradus for their selfish attitudes.  Whatever Deak claimed later, I really don't believe they were concerned about anyone's skin but their own.  So when we got back, I exaggerated the Iceman's comments into suggesting he was after them.

Well, when Lou, Deak and Nogradus went off into the sunset, Boddi, Zeff and I rigged up a few surprises in the Iceman's ship.  Bit superfluous, as they came back with the Iceman's unconscious body.  Boddi put him into a deep trance and then told me privately he was a clone.  A clone!  Charming, eh?

Things calmed down, the ship was resurrected and a damaged Y-wing turned up.  Zeff and Nogradus helped with the repairs and I entertained Commander Varth.  He plays a devastating game of holo-chess.  Much easier to beat him at Sabbac.

Things hotted up again when we discovered that someone had sabotaged the grav-lift and the Y-wing couldn't get out.  Then the Imperials attacked and we had to fight them off.  We got the overhead hatch open and came out shooting.  Fun eh?  Blew my new papers, of course!

We saw the Commander and Wedge off.  I trust they got back OK.  Would be a pity to waste such talent.  Say hello to the Commander for me!  Well, we went off to get Tor to get some new papers and organise what we need for our jailbreak.

Zeff left me on Mestra.  Reckons I'm not doing enough for the Rebellion.  1) I am not part of the Rebellion, 2) what does he think I've promised to do!  Breaking the local Rebellion leader out of a prison asteroid isn't exactly nothing.  I don't understand how that alien mind works at all.

Well, I've done my survey run of the asteroid.  Took in a sample of the next food contract and expect to get it.  With all the other plans under control, I expect to be moving on it soon.

Wish me luck!

Jess
