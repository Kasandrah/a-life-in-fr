# Reckeene's Roughnecks

The crew of the _Star Wind_, with the assistance of a marine detachment from Generis Base, capture an Imperial customs frigate in the back blocks of Atrivis Sector.  Prisoners onboard the customs frigate include Major Bren Derlin, Zev Senesca, Dak Ralter and Salo Reckeene, members of another Alliance group called Reckeene's Roughnecks.

After depositing the Imperial prisoners at Generis Base, the crew of the Starwind returns the Roughnecks to their home base in a converted water hauler.
