# Oplovis Fleet Comes Visiting

Intel warns Generis Base that the Oplovis Sector Imperial Fleet is coming to destroy the Rebel ground forces on Mantooine.  The Rebel fleet is expected to arrive in the sector just before the Imperials and to "surprise" them.

At the same time a force of Alliance troops, with the crew of _Red Storm Rising_ for support, leaves from Generis Base to acquire an Imperial scandoc decoding computer from the Imperial's base orbiting Mantooine, then destroy the base to cover their tracks.

The Alliance Fleet surprises and destroys the Oplovis Sector Imperial Fleet.  The Rebels and _Red Storm Rising's_ crew steal the decoder and destroy the Imperial orbital base over Mantooine.  _Red Storm Rising's_ crew distinguish themselves by saving a unit of Rebel troops trapped on the base before the base is destroyed.
