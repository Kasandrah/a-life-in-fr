# Strange Frontier

>**GM's Note:**  Disguised as Imperial officers and aboard the _Dark Heart_, a ship conveniently found drifting near Generis Base under suspicious circumstances, Deak and Tor journey to Nord Sector to destroy an Imperial cloning centre.  They are assisted by Boddi, Hela, and the Iceman.  Boddi reveals her true nature to Deak and saves Deak and Tor from the Dark Jedi Prince Espaa Valorum.

### Scene 1 - Generis Base Docking Bay

In the background is the _Dark Heart_.  Techs with scanning equipment are climbing all over the ship.  In the foreground Kryll and Varth (still in his flight suit) are discussing the incident.

**Kryll:**  "This is an awful risk, Varth.  I hope its another one of your legendary hunches."

**Varth:** "Don't worry, Kryll.  We may be able to use Valorum's ship against the Imperials."

They are joined by a technician from the crew investigating the ship, who salutes them.

**Tech:** "Sirs, we've recovered a strange device from Captain Skylow's Y-Wing.  None of us has ever seen anything like it before.  We've also removed the bodies of the _Dark Heart_ crew for ID and disposal.  There's some very unusual circuitry in the ship attached to the life support and hyperdrive coolant systems.  A thorough investigation will take some time."

The technician hands the device to Kryll, who takes it cautiously.

**Varth:** "Are there any identifiable tracking devices on board?"

**Tech:** "Not that we can detect, Sir."

**Varth:** "Very well, continue."

Change to Deak and Tor standing in a quiet part of the docking bay watching the action from some distance away.

**Deak:** "I don't believe it.  How could Varth have brought Prince Espaa Valorum's personal ship ..."

Tor cuts him off, staggering slightly and holding one hand against her chest.

**Deak:** "Tor!"

**Tor:** (recovering quickly) "I'll be OK.  Just ... a premonition.  Like someone walked over my grave.  Deak, please don't go in that ship.  It doesn't ... feel right."

**Boddi:** (appearing unexpectedly behind Deak and Tor) "Premonition Tor?  I didn't think cynical space pirates had premonitions."

**Tor:** "I suppose you're going to tell me it's the Force!" (turning to Deak) "Let's get out of here before Kryll asks us to do something dangerous." (turns and walks away)

**Deak:** (to Boddi) "Boddi."

**Boddi:** "Commander Freehold."

Deak follows the departing Tor.  Boddi walks over to where Kryll and Varth are standing.

**Boddi:**  "General Kryll, you have my beckon call.  Its ancient and irreplaceable.  I'd like it back, thankyou."

Kryll hands Boddi the device.

**Kryll:** "Of course.  What about the ship?"

**Boddi:**  "I'm sure Intel will find a good use for it."

### Scene 2 - Generis Base Briefing Room

Present are CIC Travia Chan, 2IC Loom Carplin, Major Edriss Sark (Intel), General Kryll (ORCC), Wing Commander Varth, Bursurra, Deak, Tor, Boddi, Hela and Ice.

**Sark:** (addressing the room) "As you are all no doubt aware, yesterday Prince Espaa Valorum's personal ship appeared in the sector in response to a summons from a device later recovered from one of our Y-Wings."

Sark glances in Deak and Tor's direction.

**Sark:** "The owner of the device has since reclaimed it."

Sark glances in Boddi's direction.

**Sark:** "The Imperials aboard were killed by hyperdrive coolant gas vented into the crew compartments just prior to the ship's return to normal space.  We think this happened when the ship was called by the device.  The nav computer and ship's log show they were going to Nord System.  Valorum wasn't onboard, so we suspect his crew were going there to pick him up."

Sark glances around the table.  He has an attentive audience but so far no questions, so he continues.

**Sark:** "Intel has learned that Nord System is the location of the Emperor's cloning banks.  Its top secret and off-limits to all but the Emperor's most trusted aides and officials.  We can use this opportunity to strike a blow against the Empire.  A small elite strike force undercover as the crew of the _Dark Heart_ could enter Nord System on Valorum's authority and destroy the cloning banks."

**Chan:** "Are these his only cloning banks?"

**Sark:** "There's no way to be sure of that, Commander Chan.  This technology was universally outlawed after the Clone Wars, and remains so even within the Empire.  But there are those who will make exceptions for the Emperor himself."

**Boddi:** "Palpatine has ways to persuade them."

**Sark:** "Even if the Emperor has other cloning banks, we have the co-ordinates and recognition codes for those in Nord System.  In Valorum's personal ship, we have the best chance of getting in and destroying them.  One less option for the Emperor's continued existence."

**Kryll:** "What about getting out after the cloning banks are destroyed?"

**Sark:** "That will be up to the team, Sir.  They will have to rely on their own resources to get out of Nord Sector once the job's done."

**Deak:** "We're well prepared, General.  Intel has given us everything they have on Nord System."

**Kryll:** "And if you run into Valorum himself?"

**Boddi:** "That's why I'm going.  Leave Valorum to me."

### Scene 3 - Generis Base Docking Bay

Deak, Tor, Boddi, Hela, Ice, Kryll and Varth are standing by the entry ramp to the _Dark Heart_.  Ice is dressed in elite stormtrooper armour, Boddi is dressed in basic black, and Deak, Tor and Hela are dressed as Imperial Navy officers.

**Kryll:** (looking at Boddi) "May the Force be with you all."

**Deak:** "We'll see you again, General.  I know we will."

### Scene 4 - First Jump

Boddi is training Hela in lightsaber techniques.

**Boddi:** "Hela, sense my lightsaber, sense me, know where I'm going to strike.  You're my own flesh and blood.  I don't want to lose you too."

**Hela:** "I can't sense the Force in you."

**Boddi:** "No-one can Hela.  Sense my movement instead, my mass moving through space, my speed, my dance around you.  Sense the rhythm of my strikes."

Hela stops and lowers her lightsaber.

**Hela:** "Can't you show me, Boddi?  Show me just once?  I want to see the Ashla in you."

**Boddi:** "Hela, if I do that every Dark Side entity in the sector will sense the Light, and head here for a meal.  I didn't survive 5,000 years by showing off.  Now, lightsaber up and let's continue."

**Hela:** "You're no fun at all."

### Scene 5 - Between Jumps

Tor and Deak are seated in the cockpit of the _Dark Heart_.

**Tor:** "We're receiving a subspace SOS from a nearby system.  A colony requires immediate medical assistance.  Deak, it's Bledsoe's Disease."

**Deak:** "We don't have a cure Tor, or even a vaccine.  Only the Imperials do.  It's one of their weapons against us."

**Boddi:** (suddenly behind them) "Bledsoe's leaves a genetic marker in its victims.  Victims that survive the initial infection pass it on to their children, who die after reaching adulthood.  Once in the gene pool, Bledsoe's kills for generations.  We have to stop this.  Take us there, Tor."

**Tor:** "Can you cure Bledsoe's, Boddi?"

**Boddi:** "Yes, but it means altering each victim's genetic code."

**Deak:** "To what?"

**Boddi:** "To something better."

**Deak:** "Better how?"

**Boddi:** "Better than dead."

### Scene 6 - The Colony

The _Dark Heart_ lands close by some dilapidated pre-fab buildings.  A number of tattered, starving and dying colonists gathered near the ship's entry ramp, barely able to stand.  Boddi, Deak and Tor leave the ship and stand among them.

**Boddi:** "We don't have time to stay.  I will choose one only, and he can heal the rest.  Tor, bring that young boy over here."

Tor retrieves the young boy showed by Boddi from the silent crowd around them and leads him to her.  Boddi gathers him up in her arms and holds him briefly, then puts him back down on the ground.

**Young Boy:** (to Boddi) "Why me?"

**Boddi:** "Because you remind me of someone I knew a long time ago.  Now, show me what I've taught you."

The boy walks back to his mother, puts his arms around her and holds her.  She's cured.

**Deak:** "They look healed, but not different."

**Boddi:** "No, not on the outside.  The difference is on the inside.  Increased strength, speed, co-ordination, intelligence, a stronger immune system, and no inheritable genetic defects.  You call it evolution.  I call it responsible guardianship."

**Deak:** "And the boy?"

**Boddi:** "Their new Guardian."
