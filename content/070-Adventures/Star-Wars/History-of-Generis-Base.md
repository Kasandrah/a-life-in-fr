# History of Generis Base

Atrivis Sector is situated in the Outer Rim Territories.  Here the Alliance presence consists of the Atrivis Resistance Group (ARG) which includes the Fest and Mantooine Ground Forces, and a hidden Rebel base aboard a long-abandoned space station in the barren Generis system.

Fest Ground Forces operate on Fest and in the nearby systems.  Fest is a densely populated planet so the Fest Ground Forces are street fighters and urban guerrillas.  Mantooine Ground Forces consist of several thousand Rebels organised into companies, regiments and divisions.  They occupy the mountainous region near several cities in the southern continent, the most populous area of Mantooine.  The Imperial orbital base over Mantooine was recently shot down.  Communications and supplies of hardware are the biggest problems for the Mantooine Ground Forces.  On the space station are Intelligence (Intel), Ordnance and Support (SecOrd), the Outer Rim Communications Centre (ORCC) and Starfighter Command (STARCOM).

Intel is responsible for keeping tabs on Imperial activities within Atrivis Sector and keeping ISB from infiltrating Atrivis Sector Forces.  The base has two trained interrogation officers and Intel analysis capabilities (heavily assisted by droids and computers).  Also an equipment lab for useful items of Intel field support.

SecOrd keeps the Atrivis forces supplied with weaponry, food and equipment, provides transport, maintenance, medical, droids and virtually all other material and services.  Atrivis sector is a prime supplier of food for the Alliance, so periodically Alliance transport ships call to pick up food supplies.

The ORCC answers directly to Atrivis Sector Command for everything except security, manpower and supplies, which are controlled by Chan and Carplin.  Atrivis Sector's complement of starfighters offer protection to the base and the ORCC.  There is a full wing of 36 starfighters but rarely more than 18 are put into an offensive operation at one time.  This is because six starfighters must always stand ready to defend the base against attack, one or more flights are often called on by STARCOM for operations outside the sector and the base is woefully under equipped in mechanics and spare parts.

On top of all that there are only twenty or so competent Alliance starfighter pilots in the entire sector.  Of these pilots, two are fighter squadron commanders, one is a recon squadron commander (all reporting to Wing Commander Varth), and nine are flight commanders (reporting to their squadron commanders).

Earlier in the history of the ARG, Generis Base was paid a visit by Mon Mothma and the Minister of Supply, Elkior Toiyabe.  Mon Mothma proposed to link Atrivis' forces with countless others throughout the Galaxy, integrating them into the Rebel Alliance.  Mon Mothma illustrated the benefits of a Galaxy wide communications network and the usefulness of a single galactic Resistance command.  In exchange for the loss of control on the part of Chan and Carplin, Mon Mothma proposed making Generis Base a key communications station linking many Outer Rim resistance units through satellite beam transmissions.  Mon Mothma also proposed that the Alliance would establish a fighter outpost on Generis to protect the Base.  Up until then, the ARG had no starfighters at all.

The Treaty of Generis was signed at the conclusion of this visit, binding the ARG to the Alliance for the Duration.
