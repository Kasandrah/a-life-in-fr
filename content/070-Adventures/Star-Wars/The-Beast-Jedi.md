# The Beast Jedi

Posing as beast hunters and holovid documenters, they went to the site.

Descriptions of the creatures were inconsistent, but they correlated the beginnings of the attacks with the device being found.  Nadoc got them into the site under cover of darkness and another Jedi master was found.  Like the other members of the circle, Miri wasn't keen on her descendants.

To cover their exit, the group ran screaming and firing blasters back to their ship, followed by a huge, black beast chasing them (courtesy of Miri) and various searchlights.  Exit stage right.
