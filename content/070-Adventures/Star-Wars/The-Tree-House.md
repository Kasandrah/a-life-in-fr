# The Tree House

They took a shuttle to Asher's Pride and discovered an impressive Jedi tree house.  Troy met them in the foyer and talked to Jaidyn and Spar while Dermod explored further in, accompanied by Sari to 'keep an eye on him'.

Dermod asked more of his difficult questions on the Jedi, with Sari answering as best she could and resorting to 'why don't you ask Nichelle' on many occasions.  Shortly afterwards, there was a steady stream of arrivals of the remaining Jedi and descendants.  The final two Circle members were Cass and Rana.

Sari was not impressed to discover that Dermod was actually part of the Circle and in fact her ancestor.  No one was impressed when they were informed that now the Circle and their descendants were complete, that the real work would begin.
