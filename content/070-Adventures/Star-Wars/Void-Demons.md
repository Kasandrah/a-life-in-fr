# Void Demons

The crews of _The Maker_ and the _Star Wind_ are sent to destroy the Void Demons, ruthless pirates led by the infamous Abav Ghart operating out of an asteroid belt.  The Void Demons have been responsible for numerous ship-jackings.  More than 50 ships have come to grief in the system.  The Void Demon's most recent target was an Alliance blockade runner carrying Mon Mothma and Elkior Toiyabe.

The pirate's favourite asteroid belt is located in a system that is exceptionally difficult and dangerous to navigate due to gas clouds, forming planets and large rocks.  The pirates know their way into and out of their base, but anyone pursuing them is usually pounded to pieces.  Speed is of the essence though, as Intel has learned that Abav Ghart will be auctioning his prisoners off to the highest bidder very soon.

The party determines that the best way into the auction is to impersonate Prince Espaa Valorum and his staff and travel aboard the _Dark Heart_.  Its still not widely known that Valorum is dead, so the disguise should hold until the party is close enough to rescue the prisoners and inflict large amounts of damage on the Void Demons.  Nadoc, Daye and Tinian construct a personal shield device and hide it in a decorative belt made for the purpose.  This will be the bait for Abav Ghart to part with his prisoners.

Things go pretty much according to plan, much to everyone's surprise.  The party even finds a Sith star map in one of the ships that crashed on the Void Demons asteroid base.  Its only on the way home that things start to unravel.
