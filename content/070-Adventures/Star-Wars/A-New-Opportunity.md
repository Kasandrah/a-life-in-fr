# A New Opportunity

>**GM's Note:**  Jess Storm applies for a job at a planet-side casino, her first step in recovering her beloved _Red Storm Rising_.  Jess hopes that this will bring her closer to those who stole her ship.

## Written by [[Jenny-H|Jenny H.]] (Jess)

Jess was coming home in triumph.  The _Red Storm Rising_ was hers again and the traces of the ship's captivity had been swept away.  In the front view screen, points of light coalesced into the familiar star system of the base.  Her heart surged.  She was nearly there.

Her now standard signal rang out on the com-link.  "Honey, I'm home!"

On the base, General Caroth spluttered.  "What?  She can't be...  Check that signal immediately."

Jess grinned as she anticipated Caroth's reaction and pressed a button to transmit the latest Alliance recognition code.  Let him argue with that.

Caroth ground his teeth in annoyance.  "That signal is obviously faked.  Blow that ship from the skies!"

Jess heard the order on the com-link, which had been mistakenly held open on the other end.  "I don't think you want to do that Caroth.  I am carrying important visitors who would taken exception to being blown out of the skies."  She looked back towards the lounge where Princess Leia, Admiral Ackbar and a host of other dignitaries were chatting.  "The Alliance High Command would like to have a little chat to you about recent events."

The communications officer on duty confirmed that the signal indicated just that.  Caroth fumed impotently as the _Red Storm Rising_ was escorted to the landing bay.  Jess executed a perfect three-point turn and landed with a flourish.

Her passengers departed to deal with Caroth and she looked round the familiar, welcoming faces.  Her old crew raced over to thump her on the back.  "We've missed you.  It just hasn't been the same without you."

Jess greeted them warmly and looked around for a missing face.  Varth broke into the landing bay in a run, threw himself at Jess' feet and begged her to make love to him, right there as the base alarm rang.

Jess sat bolt upright, then turned off her chrono-alarm.  Nice dream, pity about the reality.

"I told you no good would come of this."  Her mother's voice was so real that Jess actually turned around to see where she was.  It has to get better than this, she thought wearily.  Not much else can go wrong; though the way my luck is going I expect it to!

She prepared carefully for her interview at the casino.  Professional, conservative, polished.  She'd checked out the supervisor's style, just to make sure.  At least her mother had taught her something useful.  Her credentials were spotless and her cover story prepared.  She'd left her last position on a whim - the only one in her life - for a man.  Never again!

"You realise that this is only a temporary position?  One of my staff is ill, which is the only reason I have a vacancy at all."

"I understand.  If there isn't a suitable position for me when your staff member returns, I was planning to apply for a position on one of the liners that come through here."

"We'll see.  In the meantime, I want you to take a walk around the Sabbac tables and tell me what you think."

Jess had been expecting this.  One of the tables would be set up with casino people cheating and she would have to determine who and how.  Easy.  Most casino cheats weren't up to the standard of some of the spaceport dives she'd been in.

She wandered around the casino, being as unobtrusive as she could about examining the patrons.  They knew there were people watching them, but didn't like it being obvious.  Gamblers were too superstitious as a rule.  She kept her face impassive as she reported back, but was grinning inside.  It had been fairly straight forward, the casino people anyway.

"And the Twi'lek on the third table is palming cards.  I must say, that was clever of you, planting the cheats on different tables. I almost missed him."

The interviewer looked surprised and shocked.  "Are you sure?"

Jess feigned surprise.  "He wasn't a plant?"

The interviewer didn't reply, merely stating, "Vila is one of my best operators."

Jess played her ace.  "Twi'lek men emit powerful pheromones.  Perhaps she is distracted by them."

No experience is wasted unless you die from it, she thought, smugly.
