# Deak and Tor - Fun in Space

Deak and Tor attempt to retrieve a Senator from Opuchi.  They run into an Imperial star destroyer commanded by Mendel Stromm, an old enemy of Tor's.  In fleeing from the destroyer they crash on Opuchi and are eventually captured by the Imperials.  After narrowly avoiding a suicide pill, they escape and are rescued by the crew of the _Red Storm Rising_.
