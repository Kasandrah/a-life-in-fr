# The Elusive General Vernan

When the _Star Wind_ came out of hyper drive so did the _Dark Heart_, apparently given the coordinates by Nichelle.  Vernan was meeting with the Praxian pirates.  There was a brief fire fight before the pirates decided that discretion was the better part of valour.  They left Vernan's ship behind, which was 'captured' without any trouble.

Vernan was furious.  He had been at the culmination of a three-year plan to persuade the Praxians to act against the Imperials when he had been interrupted.  Spar and Sari detected him as Force sensitive.  He was also showing potential as a Dark Jedi.  This was confirmed when he tried to read the some of the marines' minds and to influence them to help him.

The ship left for the jump point, preferring not to stay around in case the Praxians came back.  After much discussion on options, the team decided to confront Vernan.  Their crew would keep an eye on his crew while they did so.  They got Vernan alone by offering to share information on their mission.  Then they confronted him about the Intel leaks.

Reading Vernan's mind gave the impression of the need for revenge.  Then he wondered why he couldn't read them.  It became clear that Vernan wasn't fully aware of what he was doing.  He had no idea of Dark / Light side issues, but was just going with what worked.  He didn't even know himself why he'd made the choices he had, and was now aware of the gaps in logic.  Nichelle showed him that the Dark Side was clouding his thoughts.  The group persuaded him to tell them everything.

He had made a deal with the Praxians for a cache of weapons to use against the Imperials, in exchange for the team.  It hadn't occurred to him that the team were being more effective in gaining weapons than what he could trade them for.

When Leith confronted Vernan about the situation on Generis Base, he confessed that Caroth might have misinterpreted his instructions and compromised the base.  He was confused by the information that it had relocated and so was safe - there were far too many people and not enough ships for such a task.  The information that the base was full of Jedi had never reached him - not that it had it reached anyone outside the base.

That left how to clean up the mess.  Leith was offered as a replacement for Caroth, much to everyone's relief.  Vernan resigned as head of Intel and Nichelle offered to take him to Karan, who would help him complete his journey back to the Light.  Nichelle informed them that there was a disagreement between the Circle at present, so she would not be available.  They guessed it was on the subject of Jess, as Troy was being the most intractable.

>**GM's Note:**  The Circle is in disagreement over Jess.  Boddi doesn't want Jess to train as a Jedi while she's running away from her problems.  Boddi believes that Jess will fall to the Dark Side, the easy path to power, to control, to getting what you want when you want - or so the Dark Side makes it seem. 
>
>Those who support Jess outnumber Boddi, Troy and others who voted against Jess.  The Circle of the Ashla is a democracy - for better or worse - and one that believes in free will.
>
>Boddi's response to the outcome of the Circle's vote is "I have a test for Jess.  If she passes and she asks me, then I'll train her."  Boddi knows full well that Jess would never ask her for training, even if Jess passes the test.

They met up with Deak.  Jess had asked him to look into the situation with Caroth, but he had been tied up with minor crises, obviously as misdirection.  They found a ship to send off Vernan's crew, and made their way to Generis Base - now renamed Home Base.

They were met by three X-Wings through the nebula, then an escort of heavy troops when they landed.  Caroth was waiting for an immediate report.  The team grinned.  They had been looking forward to this.  Caroth deflated like a pricked balloon when he was told he had been replaced by Leith.

Leith's first meeting with the base commanders went well.  He was an old-style general, quite different from Caroth.  The rest of the team scattered for various sources of scuttlebutt.

Spar's Triplets had had a falling out.  With Spar off-base so much, two of them had found new boyfriends.  Only Carly was left loyal to him.

The base's ground forces had doubled.  The Jedi were spending most of their time off base on Asher's Pride.  Taran and Annalise Storm had visited to follow up reports of Jess' dismissal.  Annalise had apparently torn Caroth into strips.  He hadn't been aware of Jess' connections.

And at night there was a huge party to celebrate the new regime.  Sari, Max, Vernan and Jaidyn went to Asher's Pride.  While the other two waited, Roger went into the forest and called Karan to him.  As a Force ghost, Karan didn't need to travel.  He just appeared in the clearing.  When Vernan's situation was explained to him, he agreed to help but warned there were no guarantees.  Vernan would have to make his own journey back to the light.  Jaidyn brought Vernan to meet Karan.  Vernan knelt to the Force ghost and spoke to him privately for a while.  Then he thanked the rest of the group for their help and left with Karan.

Back at the transport, they met Nichelle.  The argument between the Jedi had been resolved for the moment.

Max decided to stay with Boddi for more training.

Back at the base, a stir occurred when the famous Alliance ship, _The Maker_, arrived with Commander T'Charek Hadra and her crew.  On board was also a scruffy-looking Jedi.  After reporting to Leith, who asked for more details of Jess' death, they went out to explore.

>**GM's Note:**  Remember - Hadra is actually Jess in disguise.
>
>Varth knew Hadra was really Jess as soon as she stepped off _The Maker_.  Must have been all those intimate nights, and a critical perception role from the GM.  Varth took the matter up with Leith, then both of them took the matter up with Kezz.  Kezz's advice was to let the situation play itself out and not to inform anyone else of Hadra's true identity, not even her close friend Tor, who was grieving for Jess.
>
>Ross also recognised Jess, but for reasons of his own is not telling anyone else.

Nadoc, the new Jedi, presented Boddi with a new beckon call and asked to meet Tinian, who had dismantled the last one.  He then decided he'd like to play with the base.  Spar raced off to warn Leith, but it was too late.  A general alarm sounded and then stopped mid-whistle.  There was a feeling the base had started up, then stopped dead.  Gradually, first the coms and then other systems came back on-line.  After a while, they realized that the base had just acquired 2 more levels.
