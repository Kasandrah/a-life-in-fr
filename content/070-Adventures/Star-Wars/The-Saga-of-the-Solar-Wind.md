# The Saga of the Solar Wind

Alvak's guilt over his involvement in the destruction of the Solar Wind and the death of 2000 innocent civilians is eating away at him.  One night in the bar he attempts to drown his sorrows and ends up telling the story to the crew of the _Star Wind_.  Knowing that Varth's wife and son were killed when the liner was destroyed, they remove Alvak to a quieter place and attempt to find out the whole story.

Alvak knows a life pod survived the attack.  He refused to shoot it down and didn't report it to his superiors.  He suspects that, if the occupants of the life pod survived, they would be on Kuras III, part of the unexplored Kuras system on the edge of Elrood Sector.  Not wishing to engender false hope, the crew of the _Star Wind_ leave Generis Base without telling Varth where and why they are going.

The planet and survivors are found, including Varth's wife Seleene and son Adam, now 12 years old.  His wife is now involved with another of the survivors.

On the return to Generis Base, Ash picks up images from one of the survivors of another of Boddi's missing Jedi, a man entombed in rock beneath the surface of the planet.  Troy confirms the find as Kezz Sreik Kuras, Coynite for "Predator Beast of the Dusk".  Kezz was Boddi's General and Battle Master, a warrior Jedi with battle meditation abilities.  Ash is determined to return to the planet and recover Kezz.

A difficult reunion between Varth and his family is complicated by Selene's new partner, with whom she wishes to stay.  However, Adam now wants to spend time with Varth, who he refers to as his "other" father.
