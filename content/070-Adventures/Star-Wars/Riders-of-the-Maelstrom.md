# Riders of the Maelstrom

>**GM's Note:** This episode introduced a new PC - _[[010-Star-Wars-Characters#Sari-Blackwood Blackwood, Sari|Sari Blackwood]]_.  Sari's backstory is written up in [[Parry-Riposte-Checkmate|Parry, Riposte ... Checkmate]].

The crew of the _Red Storm Rising_ are sent to recover a defecting Imperial General (Leith) from Aris, a sprawling jungle planet located in the Albarrio system.  Leith has been provided with an Anomid disguise and a cruise ticket by Rebel agent Tiree, who has instructed Leith to board the _Kauri Princess_, a luxury space liner soon departing from Aris, and await contact by other Rebels.

The _Red Storm Rising_ crew run into trouble on the planet, quickly discovering that Aris is the capital of the Imperial Albarrio sector and home for stormtroopers and Imperial bureaucrats of every governmental level.  The crew escape pursuing stormtroopers by fleeing across residential platforms high in the trees and finally boarding an automated ferry carrying passengers to the _Kauri Princess_.

Once onboard the _Kauri Princess_ they discover two Moffs (Venko and Torpin) planning to deceive the Emperor and the Rebel Alliance and feather their own nests.  The crew also contact Leith and flush out Sari, his former lover and a compromised Rebel agent listed by Intel as untrustworthy.  After interrogating Sari (gently) they decide to bring her back to Generis Base for debriefing by Intel.

The journey is interrupted by pirates who attack and board the _Kauri Princess_ while the liner is passing at sublight speed through an unusual area of space known as the Maelstrom.  The pirates lock the liner on a collision course with a nearby moon named Oasis, home of a competing band of pirates.  The crew of the _Red Storm Rising_ learns that Oasis is also the site of a Rebel safeworld.

The crew of the _Red Storm Rising_ manage to warn the Alliance colony on Oasis and, with the assistance of Alliance starfighters from Oasis, overcome the pirates.  The crew then leave Oasis aboard a stolen Imperial shuttle and return to Generis Base with Leith, Sari and the two Moffs as prisoners.

## Afterwards

**Written by [[Jenny-H|Jenny H.]] (Sari)**

Neither Sari or Leith can be sure of the other's affections.

From Sari's point of view, she was using Leith for information.  She left him in the lurch in a terribly compromised position, even if it wasn't her fault.  She was never really sure how much Leith cared for her before, and he has less reason to care for her now.  She's decided to play things cool until she knows where she stands.  It's that, or risk losing everything.

From Leith's point of view, he was using Sari to get information to the Alliance.  Whatever she may have said to him, she was an Intel agent and so her affections might have been feigned.  He's decided to wait and see.
