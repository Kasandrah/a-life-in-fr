# Domain of Evil

Captain (now that Jess has gone) Roger, Ash and Lou team up with Alvak to "obtain" some medical supplies from Enigra Sector.  The suspicious "pile of junk" loaned to them by the Alliance gives out on route from Mantooine.

They find themselves stranded in the Yartiv system on the swamp planet Trinta, fleeing from the Taloron Hunter, Crutag and a Dark Jedi, Halagad Ventor.  The crew survive and return to Generis Base in Crutag's ship, renamed the _Star Wind_.
