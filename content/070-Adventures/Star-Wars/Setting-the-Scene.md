# Setting the Scene

The _Red Storm Rising_ is docked at the starport on Travnin collecting docking fees.  Jess and her new navigator, a medium sized green lizard of the Chi Chi Psi race whose name is Zeff, are attempting to prove the validity of their licences to the Imperial bureaucrats.  Zeff is a replacement for Jess' previous navigator, Tag, who was shot dead by "The Iceman", a contract killer.

Having finally been told the licences are valid, Jess goes to the Grand Design Bar to negotiate a trading opportunity.  Here Jess meets a young singer named Neena (actually an Intel agent) who introduces her to Tecknel Shnick, the owner of fourteen large crates bound for Gesaril.

While in the midst of negotiations Jess and Shnick are interrupted by a very drunk freighter captain, Axtor Bridgeman, who seems to think that the cargo under discussion is his.  Before he can finish protesting he is hauled off by two of Shnick's men, who sit him in one of the spinning booths and buy him a drink.  Shnick prefers Jess as she has not lost her last three cargoes to pirates, so negotiations are concluded.

He warns her not to open the crates, otherwise he will send "The Iceman" after her.  Later the same evening the crates are loaded into the cargo bay.  Two local inspectors come on board to check for proper licences (i.e. hunting for bribes) then the _Red Storm Rising_ is cleared to leave the Travnin system.

While in hyperspace an examination of the contents of one of the crates is conducted.  It is found to be full of fire control systems for ship's turbo lasers, highly illegal cargo.  When the ship exits hyperspace on the outskirts of the Gesaril system, Jess is waylaid by the pirate Tor Skylow (also an Intel agent).  Tor and her crew remove all the crates they can find, Jess already having hidden two in her secret smuggler's hold.

Trading in Minos Sector is off to an interesting beginning.
