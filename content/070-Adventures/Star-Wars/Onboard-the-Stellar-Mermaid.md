# Onboard the Stellar Mermaid

After intercepting a distress signal the crew take an invitation meant for the dead occupant of the life pod, one Morgotou of the Kessel Spice Corporation, and crash an auction on the _Stellar Mermaid_, a Corellian luxury liner.

The bidders include Yerkys Ne Dago, Natja the Hutt, Commander Saz Tyrson, an Imperial officer, and Pari' Notgoth.  The item being offered for auction by Sard Nightbringer is a prototype personal defensive shield, the first example of such technology outside of Imperial hands.

Despite Ash nearly being stabbed to death by a competing bidder and spending time in one of the _Stellar Mermaid's_ bacta tanks, the crew manage to obtain the personal shield and prevent the liner being blown up by Commander Tyrson.

They return to Generis Base, give the prototype shield to General Caroth and inform Intel and General Kryll of events on the _Stellar Mermaid_.
