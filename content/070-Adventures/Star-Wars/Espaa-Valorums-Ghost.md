# Espaa Valorum's Ghost

The _Dark Heart_ is returning home after a successful mission to retrieve Mon Mothma and Elkior Toiyabe and destroy the Void Demons.  The crew notices a growing Dark Side aura within the ship, that eventually takes Riddick over despite the efforts of the crew.

He attacks Roger before Jess can break the Dark Side aura's hold over him.  Once the aura's hold over Riddick is broken, he collapses on the deck unconscious.  After consulting the Jedi Holocron, the crew use the Sith star map to locate the closest Dark Side planet, with a view to giving the Dark Side aura something that might attract it from the ship.

All non-combatants and non-Force sensitive beings are dropped off at the nearest suitable planet.  Jayme stays with them so he can contact the Circle and arrange a pick-up by the Alliance.  As the _Dark Heart_ proceeds to the Dark Side planet, the ship suffers a series of dangerous internal malfunctions that nearly prove fatal for the crew.  The crew consult Boddi, who tells them the Ashla is stronger than the Dark and encourages them to continue.  Valorum's Dark Side aura must be removed from the ship.

As the _Dark Heart_ continues towards the planet, the Dark Side aura causes the ship to vent all air in the crew cabin.  The crew survive long enough to don space suits and subdue the now conscious Riddick (again).  A Light Side anomaly appears at the rear of the _Dark Heart_ and begins to consume the ship from stern to bow.  There is an intense Force storm inside the ship as the Light Side anomaly absorbs the Dark Side aura, then all is restored to normal, or what passes for normal in Star Wars.
