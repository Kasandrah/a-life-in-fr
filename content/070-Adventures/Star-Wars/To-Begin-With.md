# To Begin With

The Outer Rim Territories are relatively poor and of little strategic importance, thus receiving little Imperial attention.  These Territories provide the bulk of Rebel Alliance food and materiel and are fertile recruiting grounds for Rebel soldiers.  For these reasons Intel puts a lot of resources into setting up and maintaining intelligence networks in the Outer Rim Territories.

Due to there being thousands of sectors within the Outer Rim Territories, Intel's resources are stretched to the limit.  Intel usually organises a group of agents, gives them new cover stories, some money and equipment, ships them into a Territory and tells them to do their best.  Intel doesn't want to hear from agents until they have an intelligence network up and running, after which time Intel will give agents new orders.

One of these agents is Commander Deak Freehold, who receives orders to travel to Minos Sector and unite the various pro-Alliance groups into one effective network.  However, he also has a more personal objective.  The Alliance has found that it can supplement its meagre supply capacity through the use of tramp freighters.  A large portion of the Alliance's material is produced in small factories hidden on thousands of worlds scattered across the Galaxy, far from where the fighting is and far from where it is needed.

Tramp freighters haul this cargo from its point of origin to Alliance owned warehouses, where the cargo is collected over time.  When these warehouses are full Alliance bulk freighters stop at the world and collect the entire contents.  Intel has orders from Alliance High Command to recruit tramp freighter captains to take over these cargo runs, thus ensuring that the carrier has some idea of what he or she is risking.

Commander Freehold's more personal objective is to recruit Jess Storm, captain of the tramp freighter _Red Storm Rising_.  Why this particular "honest trader"?  Unknown to Jess, her maternal grandfather is Senator Elkior Toiyabe, Alliance Minister of Supply.  He has taken a particular interest in her and has asked Deak to recruit her.
