# Up to No Good

Alliance commando raid to recover a fleet of ULAVs from a lightly guarded warehouse on Hiffis.  The ULAVs were loaded onto a freighter and _[[Taron-Roa|Major Taron Roa]]_ and Alvak kept the ULAVs company during the six-month trip to the Outer Rim Territories.

Nichelle, Neena, and Ash's girls persuade the _Star Wind_ crew to take a side trip to Omze's Incredible Travelling Starport.  Asher bought a Jedi holocron from a little leathery being who "obtained" it from some Sith Pirates.  Nichelle "bought" Hal'noan, a Twi'lek gladiator who was Omze's bodyguard.  One marine identifies Hal'noan as a famous slave/gladiator.  Yerkys Ne Dago and Bobba Fett arrive on board the cruiser.  On leaving Omze's, the _Star Wind_ is chased by Sith Pirates.

Recovery of Kezz from Sith ruins on Kuras III and his reunion with Boddi.  An amazing lightsaber duel ensues, after which both seem quite smug.  PCs note that Kezz, Troy and Nichelle were all hidden in Sith ruins.

Three "Anointed People" arrive from Masterhome for transfer to Mantooine.

Generis Base pilots are complaining about Varth.  He has reverted to his earlier self now that Jess has gone.  Max discusses matters with Varth, including Varth's suspicions about Vernan.  Varth relents on his pilots somewhat after his conversation with Max.

Recovery of the survivors from Flitter, the former Alliance safe world discovered and bombed by the Imperials.  Kezz destroys two Imperial corvettes blockading the planet.  The crew of the _Star Wind_ now has a grateful Derfel serving as the ship's cook.

Alliance transport ship arrives at Generis Base to pick up food supplies.  It has been attacked by two frigates leading the Imperial Fleet, which will soon pass through Atrivis Sector in search of Alliance bases.

Exploratory trip to Joralla, co-ordinates provided by the Derfel, with Nichelle and Hal'noan - not a suitable location for Generis Base personnel as it has already been scouted by the Imperials.

X-wing bombing mission to Mantooine.  Generis Base personnel and Mantooine Ground Forces leave the sector on several transports, headed for Asher's Pride.  The journey will take six months.  During the evacuation, Porgo Goo's fiddling with little understood Generis Base features causes the Base to alter, trapping the final transport crew and passengers, as well as Varth and a couple of X-wing crews.

How to annoy Yerkys Ne Dago in one easy lesson.  The _Star Wind_ crew go to Minos Sector, specifically Shesharil 5 and 6, for supplies and information.  Here they meet the mysterious Aldaric Brandl while pretending to be bounty hunters.  They attack shipping leaving the sector, including that belonging to Trynric, a Devaronian smuggler, and a ship crewed by tough, lizard-like beings carrying Twi'lek slaves, drugs, 1.5 million credits, and lots of illegal heavy blasters.  Both ships were carrying Yerkys Ne Dago's cargo, which was on its way to Jabba the Hutt.  The crew spaced the drugs before taking the ship and its remaining contents.  The ex-slaves are now working in the bar on (what was) Generis Base.  The crew kept ten percent of the credits, part of which they used to purchase a bacta tank; the rest they gave to the Alliance.

Boddi moves Generis Base to Asher's Pride and reconfigures it into "active mode".  It arrives in orbit just prior to the return of the _Star Wind_, and is now called Home Base.

Recover Tinnian I'att and Daye from an Imperial Base.  Jaidyn "accidentally" dismantled Tinnian's beckon call, a type of device the crew had only ever seen before in Boddi's hands.

Mon Calamari and the brewing civil war between the Mon Cals and the Quarren.  The crew bought another bacta tank for Home Base.

Tour of the Galaxy with Nichelle aboard the _Outward Bound,_ courtesy of Aeysha and Grania.  View of the entire galactic spiral from above, the pulsing, heart-like energy of the Galaxy visible only to the Force-sensitive.  A view of the Kuat shipyards, a view of Kessel, and a very close view of Coruscant, the Emperor's planet.
