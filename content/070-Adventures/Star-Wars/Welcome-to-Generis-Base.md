# Welcome to Generis Base

After arriving at Generis Base the crew of the _Red Storm Rising_ settle in and make themselves comfortable.

Captain Roger, a highly experienced shields operator, is assigned to the crew by Wing Commander Varth.

Jess arranges with Varth to get the crime lord, Yerkys Ne Dago, off her back via payment in gold.  A fair exchange for her effort in obtaining a cloaking device for the Alliance.  Varth agrees to arrange payment of Jess's debt through Alliance channels.

The crew participates in a X-Wing and Y-Wing training exercise.  Deak and Jess with Lou and Roger manage to gang up and defeat Wedge Antilles, the top gun of Generis Base, after all others had withdrawn "destroyed".  One of the other pilots turns out to be an Imperial spy.  He tries to attack Deak and Jess for real, but fails.

Boddi's diagnosis - the spy is suffering delusions and has been at large for too long.  He believes the Imperials are going to do something big in Atrivis Sector and his role is to provide distraction by taking out Generis Base's best pilots.  Boddi says he is suffering increasing mental instability brought on by the stress of undercover work and his belief that the Rebels will kill him if they find him out.  The propaganda view of Rebels is that they are terrorists and pirates.

While on a routine sector scouting mission the _Red Storm Rising_ comes across the notorious bounty hunter, Crutag, aboard his ship, the _Scent of Fear_.  He doesn't appear to be doing anything.  _Red Storm Rising_ avoids a confrontation (underpowered in comparison with Crutag's ship) but informs Generis Base.  As he is a very high priced bounty hunter Generis postpones their VIP visitor, just in case.  This and the spy is making them nervous!  Later, long-range sensors no longer detect Crutag in the area.

## Fourth Message

**Written by [[Jenny-H|Jenny H.]] (Jess)**

Transmission from _Red Storm Rising_ to _Storm Clouds_.

Hi Dad!

Your daughter's gotten herself heavily mixed up with the Rebellion now.  Hope you don't mind!  Seems to be paying OK, but I miss the trading.

Let's see.  The co-ordinates Wedge gave us ended us up in the middle of nowhere.  Nothing in sight and no instructions as to what to do next.  I could hardly signal "_Red Storm Rising_ reporting to join the rebel base", so I tried "Hi Honey, I'm home!"

Unorthodox, but it did get us a couple of Y-Wings wanting to know what we we're doing in the sector.  I told them we'd been invited and they escorted us in.  No sense of humour these Rebellion people.

The base is full of mostly raw recruits under the age of 20.  There's some pretty good talent around.  I put an arm around Hela and commented "I'll take the ones on the left, you take the ones on the right", which earned me a dirty look from Boddi.  She reckons its going to be hard enough keeping that lass' mind on training without me stirring the pot.  I don't often get to score points off Boddi and love it when I do.

Wing Commander Varth and Wedge were happy to see us again.  I arranged free board, lodging and equipment in exchange for doing some jobs around the place.  I also got them to organise getting Yerkys ne Dago off my back in exchange for the cloaking device we delivered.  I hope it works.  I've more than enough trouble on my hands.

After dinner, I discovered how Generis Base gets its work priorities sorted out.  General Kryll, Commander of the Outer Rim Communications Centre, and Wing Commander Varth invited me to play cards.  They don't play for money, but for favours.  Got to be quite interesting.  I was playing like a farm boy in the big smoke at first, but pretty well cleaned up in the end.  One reason why this message is being sent on via Rebellion channels.

I'm going to be mostly transporting ground troops to Mestra or doing food runs.  I prefer the latter.  Whatever the cause, I hate looking at all those fresh young faces and knowing some of them won't be coming back.

There's a competition here to see who can climb to the top of the list of best pilots, pinned on a wall in the pilot's ready room.  Varth bet me I couldn't beat a quarter of his pilots.  Quarter!  I reckoned I could beat at least half of them.  I had to borrow a Y-Wing, as the _Red Storm Rising_ hasn't the manoeuvrability.  One thing I intend to change, although I'm frequently carrying more than my legal limit of passengers, something that should be fixed first.

Anyway, I took Deak as my gunner and the two of us cleared the skies.  We had to gang up on Wedge's X-Wing with Roger and Lou, but did it.  Lou then turned on me and we circled around for a while before calling it a draw.  The base rang with the strains of "We Are the Champions" for a while, and I made sure my new quad guns got installed pretty quick.

Roger is the latest addition to the crew.  He's an ex pirate and a brilliant shields operator.  I won him in the card game.  He's a real party animal.  Should be fun.  I think I got him because he has the same regard for rules and regulations as I have.

So wish me luck!

Jess
