# Annalise and Taran

>**GM's Note:**  Annalise and Taran Storm pay a visit to Generis Base.  Annalise and Caroth have a discussion regarding Jess' whereabouts.

## Written by _[[Jenny-H|Jenny H.]]_ (Jess)

Taran brought _Storm Clouds_ out of hyperspace.  "The Storm Tag Team's back in town," he transmitted over the coms, quickly followed by the Alliance code.  If his sources were correct about the nature of the man now in charge of Generis Base, he didn't want Caroth to have any excuse to blow him out of the skies.  But that didn't mean he couldn't start stirring the pot before they arrived.  He hadn't intended to come back here, but the rumours of trouble on the Base were too much to ignore and the Storms were never good at avoiding trouble.

Taran landed the ship in the assigned bay.  By custom, his next stop should have been to report in, but he wasn't ready for that yet.  There was too much he needed to find out about events on the Base and what trouble Jess' big mouth had gotten her into this time.  Besides, he had an independent brief and, in theory at least he could ignore certain amounts of protocol.

What he found out from Jess' old crew disturbed him.  What he found out from a couple of old friends disturbed him even more.  Annalise was dutifully following him around, listening carefully, but he could tell from the subtle tension in her manner that she was becoming increasingly angry.  And the dutiful attention was a very bad sign in itself.  She might not approve of Jess' career choice, but no one was going to malign her daughter.

When he thought he had the situation clear, Taran suggested that they visit Caroth.  Annalise replied that she thought it best if she were the one to talk to the base commander.  Having seen Annalise in action on a number of occasions, Taran decided he wouldn't stand in her way.  At least, for once, Annalise's devastating manner was not to be directed at him.  This might even be amusing!

Annalise swept into the ante-chamber of Caroth's office.  "Is the General in?  I see he is.  I'll just pop in to chat to him."  She was gone into Caroth's office before the poor clerk had a chance to fully register her presence.  Taran smiled in sympathy and followed her in.

"Ah, General Caroth.  So nice to meet you."

Caroth had been reading reports and didn't take kindly to the disturbance.  "What the..?" he started to say, but Annalise interrupted him.

"I hope you aren't about to say anything uncouth.  And don't yell at your clerk."  Caroth had been about to do just that.  "I felt sure that you'd be happy to talk to me."

"And just who are you?" Caroth asked, rudely.  He wasn't sure how to take this strange woman.  She was confident, unfailingly polite and of obvious breeding, but he wasn't used to being spoken to like an underling.

"Oh," replied Annalise, pretending to be non-plussed.  "I thought all base commanders were required to keep up to date about important figures."  She shrugged.  "I am Annalise Storm and this is my husband, Taran.  I came to talk to you about my daughter, Jessamine."

At last Caroth had a name he could understand.  "Jess Storm is no longer on this base."

"Yes, I'd heard that.  I also heard an astonishing rumour that you'd had her thrown off, but that couldn't possibly be true."

"She refused to join the Alliance and non-Alliance personnel are not permitted on this base.  She was a danger to security."

He got no chance to question their own credentials.  Annalise's steam-rolling tactics were on the move again.  "Great stars, that was a courageous decision to take."

Caroth became uncertain.  "What do you mean?"

"You do know who she is?"  Annalise's manner had become condescending.

Taran couldn't resist a comment in a loud whisper to his wife.  "Perhaps his security clearance isn't high enough."  He knew Jess wasn't going to thank Annalise for what she was about to reveal, but there was little that Annalise did on Jess' behalf that her daughter thanked her for.

"It can't be," she commented back.  She cut off Caroth's attempt at bluster.  "Her grandfather is Elkior Toiyabe."  She added, kindly, "Minister of Supply.  Still, I'm sure he will be surprised to learn that his granddaughter is a traitor.  He will want to consider the proof, of course."  She pretended to notice Caroth's white face for the first time.  "You do have proof, don't you?  Oh dear.  Still, I did say is was a very courageous decision."

Caroth tried to justify himself.  "But she refused to join the Alliance."

"My poor man, is that all the proof you had?"  Annalise was gently scornful now.  "Do you have children?"  Caroth shook his head, wondering what that had to do with the situation.  "If you did, you'd realize that sometimes they like to go their own way.  Father has had Intel quietly working on Jessamine for years.  " She added sotto voce to her husband, who was trying to keep a straight face, "I didn't realize a base commander's security clearance could be that low."  To Caroth she added, "Still I'm sure you know what you're doing."

She started to turn away, then as if in afterthought added, "I did hear another disturbing rumour that Jessamine had been sent away with an officer and a woman of doubtful character who were on a dangerous mission."  She asked her husband, "Or was it the cover story that was dangerous?"  Taran knew better than to interrupt the flow for a rhetorical question.  "However, I'm sure any base commander worth his colours would be able to correct that error."

Caroth could hear the subtle threat in Annalise's voice and muttered something unintelligible.  As she swept from his office, he could see the clerk's face.  The story would be all over the base by nightfall.  Just wait until he got his hands on the Intel officer!
