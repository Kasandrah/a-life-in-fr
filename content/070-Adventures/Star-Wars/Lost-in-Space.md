# Lost in Space

While returning from Yavin IV, Ash makes a navigational error and _Red Storm Rising_ discovers a new planet system.

Deak convinces the crew that the planet should be used as a new Alliance safe-world.  They name it "Asher's Pride" in honour of their hapless navigator.

Nichelle shows her ability by intuitively navigating back from unknown space to Generis Base.

## Fifth Message

**Written by [[Jenny-H|Jenny H.]] (Jess)**

Transmission from _Red Storm Rising_ to _Storm Clouds_.

Dear Dad,

I'm enjoying my time on the base, despite myself.  I've done some exploration work for the Rebellion, plus training of their new pilots.  Some of it, like an attack on a local Imperial base, is horrifyingly dangerous, but we lead charmed lives it seems.

Sometimes I ask myself why I'm doing this.  To tell the truth, I'm really not sure.  I almost feel like I have no choice.  I'm here through circumstances beyond my control, the dependence of others on my actions and what I could call fate.  It's not even that I don't have a choice, but that, somehow, there isn't a choice to make.  Does that sound crazy?

Anyway, I'm coasting along seeing how it works out.  There are advantages.  Varth's got pretty good legs and there are a few other good bits of him too.  He just drives me crazy with his attitude.  Apart from in bed, he doesn't want to know me.  Tor says that's because he lost his wife and son to an Imperial attack on a merchant liner years ago.  That's why he joined the Rebels, and why he is reluctant to get involved again.  I don't want him involved, just relaxed a bit!

Tor.  Yes, we rescued her on a mission to form diplomatic and trading ties with a nearby planet.  She'd been taken prisoner and brainwashed by the Moff of Minos Sector.  Things were getting too hot for the Imperials there, so he took off, Tor in tow.  They jumped blind and ended up near us by accident.  The Moff and his security chief were a nice addition to the Rebellion interrogation sector.  We just took Tor away.  I hated doing it, but Troy recommended we just leave Tor as she was until we got back to base.

Tor was essentially not there.  Her mind had been buried so that she obeyed commands, but did not initiate action on her own.  Troy wasn't sure he could help.  It was horrible.  If the raid on the asteroid had failed, it could so easily have been me, and my imagination was working overtime.  It was a more subdued return to base.  I sent the crew off so Tor had some peace to recover in.  Troy was able to reach into her mind and bring her out.  She immediately fell asleep, so I had to worry through the night that she'd not wakeup herself.

I eventually fell asleep and was awoken by a summons to an 8.00 am briefing.  I told them to piss off, but ended up getting there an hour later, well after they had finished, and then discovered they had woken me up to tell me to wash my ship!  Can you believe it!  What stupidity!  I did what any red-blooded Corellian would do - I broke out the deckchairs and cocktails!

The bigwig arrived when the party was well underway.  Some princess or other.  She arrived, did an inspection tour, met the officers (including Deak) and left.  She totally ignored the rabble in the corner.

The one low point came when I was half tanked and had word that they were going to be taking the Moff with them.  I panicked about what Tor might do if she saw him.  I asked Tor inside the ship and then my mind went blank.  I ended up asking her about Varth, something I wouldn't normally dream of doing.

She tells me he's scared of getting close to anyone since the Imperials killed his wife and son.  The more I learn about what kind of things they do, the less I mind the circumstances that mean I'm on the other side.  Then Tor dropped a bombshell.  She wanted to know about Deak.  Seems she was just keen to get back and check him out.  I felt like a right royal twit.  By then the Moff was safely out of sight and we could get on with the serious partying.

The princess, with much pomp and ceremony and a wave goodbye from us, departed down the farewell line and left.  Later on we found out her ship was destroyed.  Kind of takes the edge off the joke, doesn't it.

The next few weeks were general jobs, you know, an attack on a local Imperial fortress, the occasional bit of piracy, the usual.  One of the piracy bits ended up being a rescue of Deak's parents.  Seems life as a Senator was getting a little more hairy than was safe.  The two of them took off in a hurry with an aide.  Our report of more interesting equipment to be found on the ship was accurate, so we swept in like pirates.  No, Roger would deny that vehemently.  He finds it offensive to pirates that various people keep implying that I'm one too.  He reckons I'm not nearly good enough at it and should keep to "honest trading".

Anyway, it must have been somewhat surprising to the Senator and his wife to see their nice upright son dressed disreputably with a three-day growth and a sexy pirate on one arm.  Hey, gives him a bit of character, suits him.

The other interesting trip was to Yavin Base.  Not that we knew that's where we were heading.  Boddi just disappeared off to Mantooine to do some more training with Hela, and left a set of co-ordinates for us to jump to.  No, it was sneakier than that.  She left them with Troy, who refused to tell us what they were until we were halfway there.  I have to teach that man about family loyalty.

Anyway, we ended up in the middle of exploding debris from a battle.  I was not impressed.  Just as well we jumped with our shields on.  I would have ripped Boddi limb from limb if the _Red Storm Rising_ had been damaged.

Troy thinks Boddi isn't as bad as she seems.  She's just that annoying to relieve the tension of having to keep things going while the other Jedi slept.  Fine, but that doesn't mean her wisecracks about the Empire being "necessary to wield us all together to face a greater threat" endear her to anyone.  And if I thought she'd actually engineered it, she'd be looking at deep space from the wrong side of the hatch.  Fortunately, our motives for what we do have nothing to do with Boddi.  At least, I think so.  I have trouble sorting out my own motives, let alone those of the crew.

Yes, this battle.  Apparently the Empire had a massive space vessel as big as a moon that could destroy planets.  Hard to credit, even having seen the amount of debris and talked to some of the pilots.  Wedge Antilles was there and it was good to see him and swap news.

Our timing was impeccable.  We arrived in time for the celebrations.  Well, they would have been celebrations except for another visitor - Elkior Toiyabe, head of Rebellion supply and transport.  Dad, I hate to ask, but I have to.  Did you know my Grandfather was in that role?  I prefer to think he's been using us, but I'd like to know the truth, even if you've been working for him and hadn't told me.

I don't think I've ever hated anyone as much as I hate that man.  He must know now that I know he's alive and what he's doing, so Troy's claim he's been protecting me doesn't hold any water.  I got to see him for the first time in my life as he was making a speech.  It was an awful shock.  How dare he!  It wasn't as though he'd even used an assumed name.  He must think I'm stupid or something.  Even then he didn't try to approach me.  So later on when I got the chance, I let him know just how little I think of him.  I very pointedly turned my back on him.  I would have liked to stick a knife in him!

After the party, we got on with what we'd come for - finding another of those pesky Jedi.  We didn't have much to go on.  Boddi had been as vague as usual.  Just as well she was off with Hela out of our hands!

We broke into groups to search the ancient temple area.  We figured that was the best place to search for an ancient Jedi.  Well, Deak and Tor went off to canoodle, but I assume they searched first.  The more intellectual of us followed various symbols and clues of staggering complexity, dug our way under the temple and discovered a sleeping Jedi.  She's got Roger's colouring.  We're beginning to suspect there's one for each of us.  I can't wait to see Nogradus' ancestor!  I assume that's why Boddi said we had to stay together.

On the way back Ash made a mistake in the navigation and we ended up in the middle of nowhere.  The crew expected me to explode.  I'm not sure why.  What's the point of exploding at genuine mistakes?  If he'd done it on purpose, fine, but it was just an error.

We searched around and picked up readings of a planet.  Checking it out, we discovered it wasn't on the charts.  Its exciting.  The mineral rights alone could make us wealthy beyond our wildest dreams.  Which was when Deak burst the bubble.  The Rebellion could do with safe planets to hide people wanted by the Imperials.  I called a democracy.  Deak's arguments were pretty convincing, so we've sewn up a deal whereby the Rebellion gets use of the planet and we get 10% of any profits for the duration of the war and full rights afterwards.  We named it for the discoverer - Asher's Pride.

OK.  A joke's a joke.  When are you replying to all this?  I'd really like to know about the family skeleton.

Jess
