# 1925 Rolls Royce Silver Ghost Tourer

![[cc_rolls_royce_silver_ghost.jpg]]

This legendary car was the ultimate in elegance and design, the culmination of an era.  There was never another one like it.  It was the incomparable masterpiece that forever established Rolls-Royce as the standard of quality and luxury.  And this was the last Silver Ghost.  A pinnacle of achievement.
