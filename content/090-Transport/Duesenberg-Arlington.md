# 1933 Duesenberg Arlington Sedan SJ Twenty Grand

![[cc_duesenberg_sj.jpg]]

A spectacular, one-of-a-kind show car designed by Gordon Buehrig, the chief body designer for Duesenberg. It was the most luxurious Duesenberg ever, and only one was ever built. It has genuine wood instrument panels, one for the driver and one for the passenger, each with an array of switches, gauges, and levers.
