# 1930 Duesenberg J Derham Tourster

![[cc_duesenberg_j.jpg]]

In the 1930s, a custom-built Duesenberg was the American luxury car preferred by the rich and famous. Hollywood film stars, celebrities, and wealthy tycoons. So exclusive was the Derham Tourster's clientele that only eight were ever built.

The distinctive cowl, the innovative tonneau windshield between the front and rear seats, the tufted upholstery providing the right degree of "give", a fully detailed instrument panel, the special foot-shaped accelerator, highly polished overhead camshaft covers, carburettor, and custom-made spark plugs made the car a "legend that will live as long as men worship beauty and power on wheels".

Diana's car is a Model "A" Duesenberg, built in 1921. It carries two shotguns and several boxes of cartridges, a gun cleaning kit, rags, a tool kit, rope, a tyre pump, a can of extra fuel, a puncture kit with matches and a torch in the boot. There is also a full bottle of whiskey and two shot glasses, travel rugs, a first-aid kit, maps, and a set of field glasses in the car.
