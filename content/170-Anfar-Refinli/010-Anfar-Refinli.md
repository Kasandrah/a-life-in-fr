# Anfar Refinli

I played _[[010-Dranffilo-Voklaynn|Dranffilo Voklaynn]]_, a healer, and _[[020-Asrano-Farso-Ala|Asrano Farso Ala]]_, a fighter. Voklaynn’s partner was her husband _[[Anwyn|Tintagel]]_, and Ala’s partner was her second-in-command, _[[030-Asrane-Yalesse-Sen|Asrane Yalesse Sen]]_.

Neil wrote two moves in a play-by-mail featuring _[[010-Voklaynn-and-Tintagel|Voklaynn and Tintagel]]_, including _[[020-Choices-of-the-Unicorn|Choices of the Unicorn]]_, and I ran several episodes based in Anfar Refinli’s _[[030-The-Summer-Country|Summer Country]]_, a Celtic Fae realm where the Powers (minor gods and goddesses) lived.

After my turn as the Summer Country GM, Neil added “Transition”, “Rhinnatania”, and “Interlude”.

- _[[040-Transition|Transition]]_ is a Summer Country story in which Voklaynn and Tintagel visit Mabhan, the Power of Music and Crafted Musical Instruments.
- _[[050-Rhinnatania|Rhinnatania]]_ is also a Summer Country story in which Tintagel serves as the catalyst for a confrontation between the wizardess Rhinnatania and Dian Cecht, the Power of Healing and War.
- _[[060-Interlude|Interlude]]_ is a story of Voklaynn’s alter ego, the White Saurian, in which she learns how the Saurians attempt to correct the destabilising activities of the Ogre Invaders.

I translated Voklaynn and Tintagel over to my Earth-based campaigns after Neil died. They initially appeared in my _[[010-Arthurian-Britain|Arthurian Britain]]_ campaign, then in my _[[010-Dark-Ages|Dark Ages]]_ campaign.
