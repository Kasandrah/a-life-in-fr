## Star Wind

### Alvak, Captain T.

Pilot. Ex-Imperial TIE fighter pilot (Flight Captain, DFM) attached to the Solaest Fleet Long Range Patrol Wing.  Defected to the Alliance after the attack on the civilian liner _Solar Wind_.  Boarded the _Red Storm Rising_ as the crew were trying to leave Corellia with Jess's mother.

Dedicated, proud, competent, model of a proper officer, demanding, thoughtful and remote (toward subordinates), intelligent, good humour, can find something to drink even when light years from the nearest distillery, reliable, occasionally insufficiently respectful of authority.

### Ashton, Lou (Max Doon)

Scout and Medic.  Picked up on Yelsain, Minos Sector.  Left home (Derilyn) at a young age because of constant political disagreements with his parents.  Only child - no remaining family ties.  Travelled ever since but prefers being planet-side.  Dislikes (bordering on hates) the Empire.  Tall and solid.  Regular features with blue eyes and a Roman nose.  Shortish, straight, blonde hair, dyed brown and permed.  Wears a full beard.  Cynical, vaguely mercenary, and a bit of a joker.

Wants to:
- become a better doctor; do a secondment
- make lots of money
- further explore his Jedi powers - won't accept he's Force sensitive
- check if his new force sensitivity could result from a meta-virus in his system

Played by _[[Michael-H|Michael H.]]_

### Pendragon, Captain Nogradus

Ex Imperial Intelligence.  Defected to the Rebel Alliance when he found out from secret Imperial tapes that his family were killed by Imperial troops.  The Imperials suspected rebels in the city, so destroyed the entire city.

Played by Dragon B.

### Roa, Major Taron

Major, Alliance Intelligence, military specialist, spy.  Also an Alliance Intelligence operative code-named _[[Taron-Roa|Harlequin]]_.

Played by _[[Craig-B|Craig B.]]_

### Roger, Captain

Captain onboard the _Star Wind_.  Officer of the Rebel Alliance (Generis Base).  Ex-pirate, once a 6' 8" Negro with a scalp lock, pirate-style dress, named Roger, then Jaidyn.

Roger was drunk when he was "drafted" into the Alliance by Busurra.  When Roger sobered up, he was on Generis Base, Atrivis Sector, and under the command of a very unamused Wing Commander Varth.  Varth assigned Roger to the _Red Storm Rising_ when the ship joined Generis Base.

Has since progressed in the ways and the lore of the Jedi, hence the change to _Elan_. He is becoming more serious about being a Jedi. Elan is a 7-foot-tall ebon being with golden eyes, in priestly robes.

Wants to:
- improve his Force skills - increase control, make a lightsaber, Jedi growth stuff
- find stuff to improve the ship
- see if the person he is impersonating has found out about him and might come looking.

Played by _[[Simon-M|Simon M.]]_

### Tagge, Asher Norton

_[[Asher-Norton-Tagge|Asher]]_ (then *Spar* and now *Syll*) is learning to be a Jedi (Consular) from _[[Jedi-Bendu#Troy|Troy]]_. He is mending his ways from being a superficial playboy to being a man, and a Jedi, that his father would have been proud of.

Navigator onboard the _Red Storm Rising_, then the _Star Wind_.  Short (5' 4"), very short black hair, blue eyes, fair complexion, medium build.

Wants to:
- be a better Jedi - training and resulting skill increases
- follow up on the Tagge family (on the prison planet Kessel).
 
While still growing in maturity and power, Syll keeps what he is at heart.  He likes _[[010-Star-Wars-Characters#Carly - X-Wing Pilot|Carly]]_, luxuries, the senses, and the Force.  He dislikes discomfort, the Dark Side, and _[[010-Star-Wars-Characters#Tagge, Baron Ormon|Baron Ormon Tagge]]_.

Played by _[[Ben-G|Ben G.]]_

## The Maker

### Storm, Commander Jess

_[[Jess-Storm|Commander Jess Storm]]_ is a Corellian, Captain of _The Maker_, and a Jedi Bendu apprentice.  Tall (5' 8''), slender but strongly built, leggy, short slightly curly naturally blond hair, hard steel grey eyes.  Rough around the edges.  Usually dressed in an Alliance uniform or a pilot's outfit.  Can scrub up well if chooses.

Wants to:
- protect the crew of _The Maker_
- make sure the Alliance gets its act together and wins
- become a better Jedi - especially defensively, learning from Nadoc and Dermod
- learn about Jess' uninterested friends, Varth, Yerkys ne Dago, Hadra's friends and family

Played by _[[Jenny-H|Jenny H.]]_

### Hela

### Iceman - Bounty Hunter

Tall and athletic, fair complexion, blonde hair.  Bodyguard for Hela and Boddi (not that Boddi really needs one).  A clone from Nord Sector, one of the Emperor's highly secret cloning centres.  Unlike other clones, the Iceman can feel emotion.  He has learned how to express his emotions appropriately, mostly because of Boddi and Hela's influence, but remains reserved and aloof most of the time.  He and Hela have recently become lovers, and he is exceptionally protective of her.

## The Kierra

A ship "possessed" by a droid intelligence named Kierra.

### Ross, Captain Thaddeus

_[[Thaddeus-Ross|Captain Thaddeus Ross]]_ is a Corellian bounty hunter and smuggler, handsome with soft, spiked blonde hair clipped short, green eyes, a very attractive smile and a mischievous expression.  Easy-going 28-year-old who comes from a long line of smugglers.

Has a ruthless occupational edge and a reputation as a good enforcer derived from his ability to capture the most dangerous criminal personalities quickly and with a minimum of fuss.  Easily provoked by senseless violence.  Cynical and self-righteous, weakness for protecting the underdog.

Often interferes to even the odds, ignoring danger. The Jedi Bendu _[[Jedi-Bendu#Nichelle|Nichelle]]_ raised Ross from the dead after he was ambushed and killed by Rodian bounty hunters onboard Omze’s Incredible Travelling Starport.

## Home Base

### Anna - Y-Wing Engineer

Redhead.  Mechanic and star fighter technician.  Was involved with Ash until she met Kezz - now playing the field but has a fondness for Dermod.

### Azur-Jamin, Daye

Young Intellectual.  Formerly a senior supervisor with I’att Armaments, armaments specialist and lover of Tinian I’att.  Prematurely grey streak marks the centre of his left eyebrow.  Slender and bookish looking, with lively brown eyes.  Daye had been severely injured while blowing up I’att Armaments main factory in order to prevent I’att’s new personal shield device from falling into Imperial hands.  Boddi restored him to full health after the crew of the _Star Wind_ rescued him and Tinian from an Imperial prison.

### Blackwood, Sari

_[[Sari-Blackwood|Sari Blackwood]]_ is a singer. Slender, 4'10", short and straight black hair, beautiful. Clothes are sophisticated and sexy.  Quite capable of "dressing the part".  Was an Alliance Intelligence agent but now training as a Jedi Bendu under Nichelle.

Wants to:
- marry Leith and produce lots of offspring
- make sure the Alliance gets its act together and wins
- become a better Jedi, stay away from Dermod
- find the hole in the Alliance contacts she had.

Played by _[[Jenny-H|Jenny H.]]_

### Busurra - Wookie

Blind in one eye and missing three fingers on his left hand as a result of Imperial torture.  Despite this, he is surprisingly mild-mannered and good-natured.  He is an superb organiser and administrator, and carries out these functions for Chan and Carplin, acting as Chief-of-Staff in Loom Carplin's place when Carplin is off base on a mission.  Busurra is always accompanied by his translator, a protocol droid named C3-L1.

### Carly - X-Wing Pilot

Brunette.  Involved with Ash.

### Caroth, General - Commander, Home Base

Took over from Travia Chan as Commander of Generis Base when the Alliance expanded the Atrivis Sector forces.

### Carplin, Loom - Chief-of-Staff

Highest surviving founding member of  the Mantooine Liberators.  Acts as Chan's eyes and ears in the field.  Big, bluff and immensely strong, looks every inch a warrior.  He travels from outpost to outpost, checking up on soldiers and taking charge of tough missions.

### Chan, Travia - Commander-in-Chief

A frail, aging woman confined to a repulsorchair by a childhood illness.  Her disability keeps her firmly tied to the base and its medical facilities.  One of the original founders of the Fest Resistance Group, and then the principal architect of the union of Fest and the Mantooine Liberators, creating the Atrivis Resistance Group (ARG) and becoming its first military commander.  She maintained that position when ARG joined the Alliance.  Sharp mind and iron determination, fine theoretical strategist able to judge the most complex and dangerous military situation coolly and without emotion.  Her enemies call her the "Icewoman".

### da Silver, Mynos

Light-absorbing Derfel that can only be seen in the ultraviolet spectrum. 1.8 m tall, 120 kg male with red eyes, a dog-like head, long teeth, and a stocky build .  In ultraviolet he is a rusty red colour.  Large silver earring and a silver armband with intricate carvings.  From the Silver Clan - sold into slavery after the Iron Clan did a deal with the Empire.

Escaped to the Alliance, where he has yet to find a niche.  Angry with the Empire and those with no honour, really murderously angry with oath breakers.  Blood feud with the Iron Clan.  Appears nasty but quite mild-mannered.  Likes adventure and a chance to stretch his limits.  Likes to cook in his time off.  Bit reserved with strangers.

Played by _[[Craig-B|Craig B.]]_

### Erris, Royd

Business associate of Busurra.  17-year-old street kid from Fest who was rescued by Busurra after attempting to blow up an Imperial supply depot.  Devastating Sabbac player who is continually coming up with "a great investment opportunity".  Short and wiry with blonde hair and green eyes.

### Goo, Porgo and Brothers

First met in Minos Sector.  Came to Generis Base with Axtor Bridgeman and are now doing starship and vehicle repairs.

### Hal'noan - Twi'lek Gladiator

Originally from Ryloth head-clan.  Driven out into the Ryloth dark side to die when one of the head-clan was fatally poisoned.  Captured by slavers and then became a gladiator.  Found by Nichelle aboard Omze's Incredible Travelling Starport, and will follow her to the Bright Lands and beyond.  Comes from Twi'leks genetically engineered by Boddi.  Fluent in several languages, but hides this skill.

### Henrickson, Markus

Ex-Imperial Guard, defector to the Alliance.

Played by _[[Craig-B|Craig B.]]_

### I'att, Tinian

18-year-old granddaughter and heiress of I'att Armament's founders, Strephan and Augusta I'att (both murdered by Imperials).  Explosives expert.

### Kryll, General - Commander, ORCC

An Imperial officer who defected to the Alliance, he maintains a stern military bearing softened somewhat by his penchant for singing old spacers' songs off-key in the base's bar when not on duty.  He claims he defected because the Imperial officers' corps didn't appreciate his fine tenor voice.

### Leith, General

_[[Leith|General Leith]]_, Commander, Home Base, is formerly of the Imperial Army.  Defected to the Rebel Alliance.  Leith is stable, thoughtful, and dependable, with a leader's charisma, strength, and decisiveness.  His genuine care for the troops under his command has boosted morale on the base to an all-time high.

## Others

### Brandl, Aldric - Dark Jedi, deceased

Originally an actor, Aldric was trained by the Emperor and became one of his servants.  Arrogant prideful "noble".  Highly intelligent self motivated individual whose grim obsession with tragedy stemmed from his career as a tragic actor.

Even before his seduction by the Dark Side he was a callous, moody man preoccupied with the visage of the next star-crossed character he was meant to play.  An apathetic murderer, he killed without remorse and moved on to his next target, which made him an invaluable resource to the Emperor.

### Dago, Yerkys ne - Twi'lek Crime Lord

Physically fit, well-dressed, changes the ornamental painted designs on his head tails daily.  Right head tail gestures and points to add flair to his speech; left head tail reflects his emotions.  Escaped slavery by allying himself with a band of smugglers.  Eventually took control and established a criminal network of black marketeers, smugglers and other undesirables which is practically unrivalled in the Outer Rim Territories. Now moves in the highest circles.

Pursuing Jess Storm for the money the cargo of nerve toxins she dumped would have brought him.  He has already murdered Jess' friend Flis and intends to have Jess suffer the same fate.  Superficially very gracious and courteous, actually very base, corrupt and evil.
