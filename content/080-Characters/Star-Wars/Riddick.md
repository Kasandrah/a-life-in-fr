# Riddick

![[sw_riddick.jpg]]
**Riddick**

Riddick is a very dark character who is struggling, knowing that for the first time in his life he is being treated as someone special and unique, for reasons he doesn't yet accept and expects to evaporate real soon.

He began life in a dumpster with an umbilical cord tied around his neck and went downhill from there.  He suffered appalling abuse from all sides until he became big enough and strong enough to return like with like.

His life has been exceptionally brutal and violent, and now he discovers his ancestor and teacher, Boddi, has the power to have stepped in at any time and taken away his pain. Another reminder of his own insignificance in the scheme of things.

He is not dealing at all well with Boddi's past inactivity in the face of suffering, not just his but all those who suffered under the boot of the Imperials.  His motivation to become a Jedi is more about obtaining the power for prodigious amounts of revenge than it is about "becoming one with the Light".

Riddick will need a lot of help from Ross and Karan, whether he welcomes it, to become the powerful and compassionate Jedi warrior that Boddi has foreseen.
