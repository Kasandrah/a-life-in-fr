# Captain Thaddeus Ross

![[sw_ross_kirby_morrow.jpg]]

**Captain Thaddeus Ross**

>**GM's Note:** Sourced from emails between myself and a guest player, Robin E., who played Ross for a few sessions in February 2001.

The mission was complete to the point of all scrambling aboard the Dark Heart and beginning the journey back to Home Base.  The players recovered the Sith artifact that Boddi required.  The group now has a map to Sith sites, as well as most of unknown space.  Known space in the Star Wars galaxy is only 10% of what's out there. Navigating in unknown space is very dangerous, but now they have a map!

>**From Robin E.:** I've been thinking about the incident with "the Tree"! I'm not sure that Ross would have been that apologetic. I suspect that his response would more likely have been - "Oh! That's interesting. Fine - I wouldn't have it any other way."
>
>As I see the character, he's been through an amazing, awesome and unique experience - and trying to come to terms with it would be immensely difficult, if it weren't for the fact that the experience itself has changed him, fundamentally. He's a person in touch with himself, and even though there are occasional waverings, and probably lapses into old patterns (he really was tickled by the idea of having the Empire **pay** him for handing over Abav Gart!), Ross has found a demonstrable (!) connection with a greater Reality which grounds him and gives him a stability and acceptance which most of us strive for all our lives!

Sorry, Robin, my error.  In your absence, I supply occasional commentary and derring-do from Ross (and Wazza when _[[Michael-B|Michael B.]]_ isn't there).  I try to keep this to an absolute minimum, as I more often than not get the character wrong.  That's why I prefer to use my own NPCs as plot-forwarding (or correcting) devices rather than other PCs.

However, some players perversely need to speak to or interact with Ross and or Wazza when the players of said characters are absent.  I'm sure they are only doing it because they think they might get away with something that Ross and or Wazza would jump on them for if their players were actually there.

Gamers!

>**From Robin E.:** Actually, he's probably somewhat more playable as an NPC than as a PC - particularly if he is going the road of healing and diplomacy, the "passive" skills. There's a tension between the "Star Wars gung-ho" which we were talking about last night and the reflective and mystical quality which now informs his life.
>
>Although, come to think of it, it will be fun to role-play some of that development and that tension. His past, his experiences and skills, will still be useful - and he's not sure that Cass is right when she puts aside all weapons. After all, what he did to the Tree was using a weapon - not just a material one!

I think Ross fits and fits perfectly.  I have the extreme good fortune to have someone playing him who understands the search for higher meaning and spiritual truth.

>**From Robin E.:** Yes, I think Ross will be a fun character to play. I just wish I knew more about the mechanics and the options of the system! Can you let me have some idea of the range of skills available to him once you've worked it out? I think he might still have a few surprises (eh, Boddi?) up his sleeve.

Ross is the Ashla's wild card.  Boddi didn't see him coming and still can't see where he's going.  Ross is a disturbing blind spot in Boddi's vision of the future. Boddi may have come a long way since her Fall, but the Ashla is still holding back a few trump cards in case Boddi runs off the rails again.

Nichelle asked the Ashla to return Ross.  Ross was returned because the Ashla has plans for him.

I'm still working on the Force skill mechanics.  I'll let you know when something materialises.
