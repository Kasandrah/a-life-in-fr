# Sari Blackwood

**Written by _[[Jenny-H|Jenny H.]]_ (Sari)**

Sari didn't know she had Force abilities, only that if she spoke in a certain way, she had a better chance of persuading people she was telling the truth, and that she could tell whether or not people should be trusted.  Sari has a secret spot within her mind that she retreats to when she doesn't want to be aware of her outside condition (cold, hungry, being tortured, etc.).  Meeting someone who could follow her there would scare her half to death.

Sari has had little direct contact with the Rebellion and has been isolated from the Imperials' worst excesses for some time now.  She's gotten to know a number of Imperials and, in her opinion, they're really not that bad.  She hasn't changed sides, by any stretch of the imagination, just forgotten how urgent the situation is.  She feels it's her turn for something nice to happen and Leith is awfully nice.  She really isn't doing any harm by being with him.

Sari is in her early twenties, bright, bubbly, emotional, impetuous, almost irrational as far as some people go.  After all, you'd have to be irrational to join the Rebellion on the spur of the moment.  If she really thought about it, Sari would realise she put Leith in a dangerous situation simply by becoming involved with him.

### TOP SECRET - AGENT'S EYES ONLY - AGENT REPORT

Agent: Sari Blackwood (normally uses only first name as a stage name)

Status: Suspect.

Current Location: Unknown. Last known location: Reci 5.

Danger Level: Low combat ability. Has previously killed by trickery.

Background: Blackwood was recruited by the Alliance three years ago.  She approached a person she suspected to be an Alliance operative and offered her services as an agent.  This action was prompted by the murder of her family in an Imperial raid.  The raid is believed to be a case of mistaken identity and was covered up as a Rebellion action.  Blackwood has always maintained that she knew better, but has never explained why.

Despite some misgivings - she is regarded as emotional and impressionable - Sari was recruited and has provided valuable information to the Alliance, mainly on ship movements and officer attitudes.  She is a professional singer and ensured her singing engagements were often directed towards Imperial officers and soldiers.

Eighteen months ago Blackwood stopped sending information.  An agent was sent to investigate the change six months later.  Blackwood claimed he was an Imperial spy and that she killed him.  Neither status has ever been confirmed.  Blackwood provided the agent with valuable information.  In the process both were compromised.

>**GM's Note:**  Unknown to Intel or ISB, Sari used her growing Force skills to "read" Brormal, and then naively "persuaded" him to take a fatal walk into a garbage disposal unit, gaining two Dark Side points.  Sari won't explain her actions to Intel, as she is afraid this will expose her as Force-sensitive and endanger her.  Sari's mother was a Jedi who was killed in an attack by Imperials disguised as Rebels.  Now Sari hides her Force sensitivity from both sides.

Blackwood was forcibly withdrawn from operations, but sufficient information did not exist to confirm her status.  She was posted to the Core Worlds and watched.

>**GM's Note:**  Sari's forcible removal by Intel Commander Deak Freehold is described in _[[Parry-Riposte-Checkmate|Parry, Riposte ... Checkmate]]_.  Since those events, Sari has been doing "little jobs" for Intel while making a living as a nightclub singer.  Intel is loath to use her for anything critical, as they have doubts about her loyalty.  She's known to have played a role in the accidental death of Brormal, an Intel agent (and ISB double agent undetected by Intel), and was involved with Imperial General Leith.

Recently Blackwood has dropped out of sight.  There is some reason to believe she may try to prevent the defection of an Imperial officer.

Summary: Blackwood can appear to be a harmless, naïve young woman.  In reality, she is clever, resourceful and very persuasive.  Treat with caution.

### CURRENT STATE OF PLAY

It's about 12 months on.  The previous events have left their mark on Sari - she is more mature, but more introspective and certainly more subdued.  She has accepted her loss and, to her surprise, can view events calmly.  Her inner "secret hiding place" helped.  Sari has, rather ironically, learnt to love Leith more now she is away from him than when she was with him and wildly in love.

Sari resisted the temptation to keep tabs on Leith, as this would endanger him further.  She made sure the Rebellion sent a message offering to swap her for a Rebel agent, and then let matters be.  She felt sure that if Leith were in serious danger, she would somehow know.

What do you do with an agent whose only real talent is her voice?  You get her to sing.  Unfortunately, given Deak's report, they are reluctant to initiate her into too many Rebellion secrets, so Sari is at a bit of a loose end.  She has spent a lot of time communing with nature, contemplating her navel, and whatever other things potential Jedi who don't know they are do.  The Rebels wanted her to do propaganda holos but Sari refused for obvious reasons.

Just recently, Sari has had a sense of foreboding and has decided its time to check on Leith, just in case.  She would give anything to have him safe and with her again.
