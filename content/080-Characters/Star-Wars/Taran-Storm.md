# Captain Taran Storm

**Written by [[Jenny-H|Jenny H.]] (Jess)**

Jess' father, a 56 year old Corellian who owns a small fleet of light freighters.  He leads a fairly healthy lifestyle (apart from being shot at occasionally), so is still fit and well preserved.  He looks typically Corellian - dark hair etc.  Ruggedly good looking.

Taran is a superb pilot and a mean trader.  He can talk a Chura-Fan into selling its fur and then sell it clothes to keep warm.  He has a wicked sense of humour.  He won't play tricks on people, but is quite happy to laugh if someone else does.  He dislikes being fooled as much as Jess does - he's just a lot subtler about it.

Taran and Jess are very close.  Jess in nature is much like Taran as a younger man.  He's settled a bit, but still could (if really provoked) rip someone's ears off in anger.  There's a hidden temper there, normally kept under control for tax purposes.

Jess has the relationship with Taran that she should have with her mother - she talks to Taran about her problems - men included - and seeks advice from him as needed, generally in the form of explaining the situation and waiting for the advice to come.  She doesn't need to ask.  Despite the time they spend away from each other, when they do get together it's like they were never apart.

Taran genuinely loves his wife, Annalise, and rarely strays.  Taran and Annalise reunite frequently.  He'll track her down and make sure she's not brought into the feud with Yerkys ne Dago.  He wants to keep her safe.

Taran is just as angry with Elkior Toiyabe as Jess, but doesn't think it healthy to encourage her.  Taran will keep an ear out for trouble (and rescue Jess if there is any) and check up on Elkior himself.  He wants to know what Elkior's game is, and to protect Jess from him as necessary.  And Elkior better have a damn good reason for abandoning Annalise.

### History

When Taran first met Annalise, she was on the run from Imperial assassins and travelling under an assumed name.  After their relationship became established, Annalise told Taran that her father Elkior had been murdered by Imperials, and that she was fearful for her own life.  The subject of Elkior has only come up again as a result of Jess' communications to Taran.

Taran's involvement with the Rebel Alliance began ten years ago when, sympathetic to the Alliance cause, he began using his freighters to transport Alliance personnel and supplies.  His contact with the Alliance has been through an Intel agent on Corellia.  Unknown to Taran, Elkior has been using Intel to keep track of Taran and Annalise.

Four years ago Taran was at Gordon spaceport on the planet of Aquilae, an Imperial training post.  While waiting for clearance to leave Aquilae, Taran noticed a small band of badly wounded Rebel troops hiding near the spaceport boundary.  Taran made himself known to their Captain, one Deak Freehold, and offered the Rebels passage off the planet on his freighter.  Captain Freehold, an Intel agent, knew of Taran's work for the Alliance and accepted his offer of safe passage.  Although Taran's ship, crew and extra passengers successfully escaped from Aquilae, Taran almost earned himself a trip to the spice mines of Kessel from Imperial pursuit ships.

More recently Taran has been Tor Skylow's contact and has passed information between her and his Intel agent while Tor was active in Minos Sector.  Taran told Jess to go to Minos Sector because he knew Tor was there.  He gave Tor instructions to watch over Jess.
