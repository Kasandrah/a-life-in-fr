# Deak Freehold

**Written by Tim H. (Deak)**

Born on the core world Ondos shortly before the fall of the Republic.  Montross Freehold, Senator for Ondos and Deak’s father, is a very moral man and passed his values on to his son, Deak.  Montross was grooming Deak to take his place in the Senate and to carry on the fight for freedom and civil rights by secretly supporting the Rebellion from within the Senate.

It was his father’s values that made it impossible for Deak to dedicate his life to the slow and impossible diplomatic fight against the Empire.  Deak wanted a more active role in opposing the Empire, so he joined the Rebel Alliance.  Leaving his family and friends on Ondos at seventeen, Deak joined the Rebel Navy.  The night before he stowed away on Captain Oxus’ pirate ship with the aid of the Corellian cabin boy, Solo, Deak and his father staged a skyhopper accident in which all believed Deak was killed.

Deak’s diplomatic and leadership skills saw him rise quickly through the ranks of the Rebellion, while his skill with a blaster saw him survive when many of his companions were killed.  Luck, or the Force, was with him.  Already a captain by the age of 23, Deak was serving in a Rebel blockade-runner sent to the planet Utapav in search of the Kaiburr Crystal, a Force-amplifying gem.  The ship was boarded by Prince Espaa Valorum, one of Darth Vader’s Black Knights of the Sith.  The boarders were repelled, but the ship was damaged and plummeted to the blue-green planet Aquilae, an Imperial training post.

Deak and a few others made it alive to Gordon, the spaceport.  It was from here that Jess Storm’s father rescued the group and in the process nearly earned himself a trip to the spice mines of Kessel.  It was through Deak’s knowledge as an Alliance Intelligence agent that he knew of Jess’s father and his sympathies for the Rebellion.

After his promotion to Commander at 26, Alliance Intelligence sent Deak to Minos Sector.  General Vernan, Chief of Alliance Intelligence, ordered him to establish a network between the various underground resistance groups in Minos Sector.

Deak had additional orders from Elkior Toiyabe, Alliance Minister for Supply, to recruit Jess Storm, a tramp freighter captain and Toiyabe's granddaughter.  Deak's instructions from Senator Elkior Toiyabe regarding Jess were to "monitor her, recruit her if you can, don't mention me unless you have to".  As Elkior is on the Imperials' "most wanted" list, he could not have personal contact with either Jess or Annalise, Jess' mother, for fear of reprisals.

Many high-ranking members of the Alliance use Intel to keep track of their nearest and dearest, one of the few advantages of their position.  Elkior was making use of this advantage by telling Deak to contact Jess.  Elkior's desire to get to know Jess and for her to become a part of the Alliance prompted him to see if she could be recruited and he chose Deak as his emissary.

As frequently happens in first contact situations between a member of the Alliance and a non-committed civilian, Deak was acting without sufficient knowledge of his contact, i.e. Jess.  Elkior could not give Deak any guidance on this matter as Elkior didn't know Jess and couldn't guess how she would react.  Under the circumstances, Deak could not give Jess any detailed information about the Alliance, specific members of the Alliance Cabinet or his own involvement with the Alliance, only enough to sound her out and see if she would be interested in helping the Alliance.

On meeting Jess in person, Deak realised that recruiting her was impossible and anything he could get her to do for the Alliance would be a bonus.  Deak was caught in a difficult situation.  He couldn't betray the Alliance to a stranger, but he had to follow his orders, so Jess thought he was "manipulative".  Deak was under orders from General Vernan not to discuss Elkior with Jess because she had not committed herself to the Alliance.  General Vernan didn't want to risk Elkior's safety by exposing him to someone whose loyalties, in Vernan's opinion, lay so obviously with herself.

Despite an initial confrontation with the ex-stormtrooper Nogradus, Deak relies on him as a dependable (and predictable) member of the Alliance.  Jess Storm was too prone to follow her whims and a fast buck; Lou Ashton was an unknown quantity, and Roger was too interested in a good time to be 100% reliable.  Asher Tagge, although being a member of one of the most powerful houses in the Empire, has proven trustworthy but has yet to commit all of his seemingly endless wealth and spirit to the Alliance.  Deak trusts Boddi and will fight and even die for her crusade, but sometimes the ways of the 5,000-year-old Jedi Master elude him, creating friction.

After being seduced by the dark-haired pirate Tor Skylow, Deak has fallen in love with the Corellian who, like Nogradus, had defected from Imperial forces.  Her skills as a pilot and leader were such that the Empire dropped its normally sexist employment policy.  Although many women serve the Empire, they are usually assassins and spies.

Totally committed to the Rebellion, Deak lives for the day when the new order is defeated and the killings and murders that continue on a planetary scale cease.
