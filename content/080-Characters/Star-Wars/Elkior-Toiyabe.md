# Elkior Toiyabe

Jess' maternal grandfather.

Elkior is a senior member of the Alliance Civil Government.  He is a close friend of Mon Mothma and was a close friend of Bail Organa, Viceroy and First Chairman of Alderaan until that planet's destruction and Bail's death.  Elkior's whereabouts and doings are a closely guarded secret, but the Imperials know him by name.  Many senior officers would know him on sight through newsholos made 30-plus years ago when he was a senator, and through more recent holos made by ISB infiltrators.

Elkior was nearly killed by agents of the Emperor before Jess was born.  He was a powerful Senator and had voiced strong objections to Senator Palpatine and to Palpatine's ambitions.  Elkior and others did not trust Palpatine.  Then one day Elkior's airspeeder crashed.  He barely survived; the others on board were not so lucky, including one of his closest allies and a lifetime friend, Senator Halloran of Ghorman.  In the resulting chaos surrounding the crash, Elkior's identity was confused with that of Halloran, and the news was released that Elkior had been killed.

On hearing this news, Elkior's daughter Annalise immediately and correctly suspected Senator Palpatine and so fled under an assumed name, thus completely confusing Elkior's allies in the Senate who were trying to find her and protect her.  These allies included a young firebrand, Senator Mon Mothma of Chandrila, and a veteran of many years in Republic politics, Senator Bail Organa of Alderaan.

Elkior spent months in bacta recovering from his injuries.  During the same time, Mon Mothma found out what had become of Elkior's daughter.  Annalise had made it all the way to Corellia on her own and was now safe in the arms of a Corellian pirate (sorry, make that "honest trader") by the name of Taran Storm.  Mon Mothma left well enough alone.  Any attempt to contact the girl may have attracted undesirable attention.  When Elkior was out of bacta she convinced him it was for the best.  If her contacts had such a difficult time finding Annalise, then so would agents of Palpatine.

Elkior agreed for two reasons.  One, he believed Mon Mothma's conclusions as to Annalise's safety were correct.  Two, Annalise was a member of the nobility, a daughter of Toiyabe House and of a Senator.  Elkior had been expecting a match between his daughter and the young son of his friend, Senator Halloran.  He had not discussed this with Annalise but felt that if the two were suitably encouraged they would fulfil their parents' expectations.  Instead, Annalise had married a Corellian.  Elkior knew only too well the difficulties Corellians have with distinguishing who owns what cargo.  He was sorely disappointed in his daughter.

When agents of the former Senator, now President Palpatine, discovered that reports of Elkior's death were exaggerated, Elkior left for a secret destination.  Some months later, when President Palpatine declared himself Emperor, Mon Mothma barely escaped from the planet just two minutes ahead of ISB agents.  She joined him at that destination and began forging the Rebel Alliance.

Over the intervening years Elkior was fully occupied with the formation of the Rebel Alliance and with advising and supporting Mon Mothma.  He used the growing Intel network to keep tabs on Taran Storm and Annalise, then Jess. Over that time, except for an occasional unidentified birthday present for Jess, he did not contact any of them personally.

Elkior feared what might happen to them had their relationship to him became known to the Imperials.  No relative of an Alliance warrior or government official is safe from Imperial reprisal.  The Empire's agents and informers are everywhere.  They routinely kidnap, torture, and kill family members of known Alliance personnel.

When Elkior visited Generis Base for the second time, he was surprised to recognise Deak among the officers who were introduced to Princess Leia.

Elkior then recognised the markings on the _Red Storm Rising_ and knew that somewhere among the rabble beneath the ship was his granddaughter.  He didn't recognise Jess because he had never met her and Intel had only ever provided him with verbal information, no holos of Jess.  He was disappointed by her behaviour, so poor that the Princess herself later remarked on it.  He considered Jess her mother's daughter, and that combined with her obvious situation as yet another "honest" Corellian freighter captain decided him not to attempt contact.  As Jess did not appear to recognise him, he returned to the Princess' ship.

Elkior was pleased that Deak had brought Jess into contact with the Alliance but concerned for her safety all the same.  Even though Elkior had waited until reports of Jess' actions proved she could handle dangerous situations, he felt now that he did not want to know just how similar Jess was to his idea of her father.

On the Yavin moon, Elkior was once again disappointed.  At very short notice he had been invited to the victory celebrations at the main Rebel Base.  The last information he had was that Jess was still on Generis Base, so he was not expecting to see her there.  After arriving on Yavin, he had no chance to look around as he was involved in an important meeting with his military counterpart; the Supreme Allied Commander of Ordnance and Supply.

Immediately before the presentations, Elkior spoke with Deak, who told him Jess was on Yavin.  Elkior expressed concern for Jess to Deak, but there was no time for Elkior to find Jess and speak with her before the presentations began.  After the presentations he realised Jess was expressing a strong hatred toward him.  It certainly did not require a Jedi's skills to read Jess' feelings; it was obvious in her venomous glances.

As Elkior has never met, or ever wanted to meet Taran Storm and as Elkior has every reason to believe Taran's contact with the Alliance is only informal, i.e. Taran would not know that there was an Alliance Cabinet, let alone who the Minister of Supply was, Elkior has concluded that Annalise poisoned Jess against him.  Elkior left the Yavin moon shortly after the post-presentation party started.

Elkior's intentions regarding Jess came from his genuine concern for her welfare, which extended beyond her physical well-being to include her moral and ethical outlook.  Elkior was confused and more than a little hurt by the outcome of his attempt to expose Jess to the high ideals and purpose of the Alliance, and made no further attempt to contact her prior to her "death".
