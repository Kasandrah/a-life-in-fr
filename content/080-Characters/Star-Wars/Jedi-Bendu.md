# Jedi Bendu of the Ashla

The Jedi Bendu had many great and mysterious powers far beyond the ken of modern life. They understood the nature of the Force, and this gave them mastery over **_life, thought, and matter_**. They manipulated the very structure of the universe, summoned awesome strength from the frame of reality itself, lived beyond death, and guided the path of intelligent beings toward righteousness.

The plans the Jedi Bendu laid are coming to fruition through the revival of the Circle of the Ashla.

# Troy

![[sw_troy_jurgen_prochnow.jpg]]

Three thousand years ago Troy was injured in a battle and crash-landed on Gesaril, in Minos Sector.  He was found by the natives of Gesaril and nursed back to health.

When he had recovered fully, he taught the natives about the Force and how to drag unwanted ships from the heavens and crash them on Gesaril.

Boddi came and talked to Troy about the threat she had foreseen and the Circle’s plan for dealing with it.  She assisted Troy into hibernation and then “set him in stone” at the back of a cave.  Boddi released him from his tomb three thousand years later by using the Orin reluctantly provided by Jess.

Boddi recovered a red Orin (key to another “sleeping” Jedi) from the stone surrounding Troy.
