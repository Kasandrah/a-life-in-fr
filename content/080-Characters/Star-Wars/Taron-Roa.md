# Major Taron Roa

**Written by [[Craig-B|Craig B.]] (Taron)**

### Confidential Top Secret

From Commander Generis Base to General Falis Hawk, Eliad, Minos Sector.  Sending requested information on operative code named Harlequin use code Tango Charlie.

Name:  Taron Roa
Birthplace:  Corellia
Rank:  Major
Security level:  Delta

### Background

As far as we can tell, he was falsely convicted for a murder by underworld elements.  During the arrest by Imperials, his family was killed, and he was badly injured.  Convicted, he was sentenced to life on Kessel.  On Kessel he came in contact with Rebel elements and two years later made a successful escape, bringing with him information on an Imperial unit training on Kessel.  Recruited by the Rebel Alliance, he has made himself invaluable as a deep-cover operative, performing various covert infiltration and recovery missions against enemies of the Alliance.

An expert at infiltration, security and the location of various items, this man has developed an impressive network of contacts spanning many sectors, all of which even we don't or rather don't want to know.  Also, he is a man of many faces with many established identities, many again we don't know.  He works under the direct command of Intel and reports only on completion of the mission.

### Personality Profile

Driven, aloof, meticulous, he pursues his aims with a dogged, single-minded determination which borders on the obsessive.  An incredible hatred of the Empire fuels this warped crusade.  Many are of the opinion that he is on the verge of losing it and going rogue.  I don't share this view.  If he were going over, it would have happened long ago.  A consummate professional, I fully recommend him, though his methods are highly irregular.
