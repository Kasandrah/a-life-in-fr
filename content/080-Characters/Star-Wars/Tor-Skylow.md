# Captain Tor Skylow

Tall, physically fit, long dark hair, quite beautiful.

Was a member of the Imperial Navy, but convinced the crew of her patrol ship to rebel against the Empire.  Those unwilling to join were dropped in an escape pod and left to float in space.  Imperial propaganda reports declared the men were left to die, but Tor called the Imperial base on Travnin and informed the authorities of the pod's location.  Tor and her crew then took her ship to a remote part of the Yelsain system where they modified the customs craft so it was not instantly recognisable as an Imperial vessel.

After defecting to the Alliance Tor was led to Taran Storm by her contacts on Yelsain.  Taran provided Tor with information on Imperial shipping movements within Minos Sector, and Tor provided Taran with supplies and personnel for the Alliance.

Tor was previously involved with Wing Commander Varth not long after his wife and son were killed, but only for a short time.  Varth was undercover in Minos Sector recruiting potential Rebel pilots for Generis Base when he met Tor Skylow.  Tor was "pirating" Imperial shipping and passing the proceeds on to Taran Storm.  Varth and Tor were very discrete about who they were and what they were doing, neither telling the other the full story.

After receiving instructions from Taran Storm to monitor Jess, Tor arranged to "pirate" Jess' ship of cargo destined for the Alliance.  This allowed her to meet Jess and gain some first-hand impressions of her.

Tor was imprisoned by the Imperials after a traitor in her crew gave her meeting place co-ordinates to them.  She and her crew were captured and her ship was destroyed.  Tor was taken to the main Imperial city on Travnin where she was tortured for information on the Alliance.  Too strong-willed to crack under the Imperial scanners and torture droids, Tor was subjected to a total mind wipe.  Lord Dixon, the Moff of Minos Sector, took a fancy to her and had her personality changed to one more submissive to his whims.  Tor then became his personal slave, renamed "Diamond".

After rescuing Tor from the Imperials captured on Masterhome, Troy rebuilt Tor's personality and memory from her subconscious mind and sequenced her memory correctly through an intense dreaming period.  The only outward sign of this process was her deeper than usual sleep.

Tor is a born leader, honest and with contagious confidence.
