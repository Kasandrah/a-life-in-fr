# Asher Norton Tagge (Syll)

## Background

Asher is a member of the Rebel Alliance and a scion of Tagge House - a merchant family, quasi nobility.  He is the son of [[010-Star-Wars-Characters#_Tagge, Trevelyan Lewis - deceased_|Trevelyan Tagge]] and [[010-Star-Wars-Characters#_Tagge, Evayne - deceased_|Evayne]], a mysterious and beautiful woman with no known past.  Evayne’s death when Asher was 5 was the accepted reason for Trevelyan’s withdrawal from the family - i.e. to be with his son.  Accepted as truth everywhere but within Tagge House, as Ormon Tagge had his father the Baron and his father's younger brother Trevelyan slain to become Baron Tagge.

Fearing for years that Ormon was going to do something drastic, Trevelyan had set up a secret account with enormous sums of money and a refuge within the Tagge Fleet amongst some personal friends and ex-followers.  For a couple of years after Trevelyan's death, things remained as they were, with a new Baron at the helm and a young cadet in the Fleet.  But as Asher's popularity amongst the Fleet grew, he suffered four near-fatal accidents in the space of two days.  As friends died in these failed assassination attempts, Asher fled to the distant and unimportant Minos Sector and joined _Red Storm Rising_ as Asher Fletcher when the opportunity arose.

When Asher originally joined the Tagge Fleet, he assumed the behaviour of a young and very rich playboy to allay Orman’s concerns about him.  Until Asher encountered Troy, he had forgotten that being the playboy was an act.  Although he still lapses into the act occasionally, he is trying to be like the man his father was, with the emotions of his mother.

## Written by [[Ben-G|Ben G.]] (Asher)

Entered the Tagge Fleet when 17.  Place for Asher to be out of the way from Orman's plans.  Originally, just to be a cadet and general officer but in time found an amazing aptitude for astrogation.  Everyone amazed at almost instinctual grasp of physics and maths.  Although extremely popular with the general crews, officers avoided Asher when not on duty (it was politically and professionally 'bad news' to be around him.

After 2 years service, Asher gained his skills from the senior crew members (none of the officers) with handling the bureaucracy and working with starship engines.  His command skills were gained from before he joined the Fleet, and it seemed that he didn't need to be taught astrogation, just pointed to what was possible and a sketchy reasoning for it.

The news reached Asher of the "accidental" deaths of his father and uncle very quickly.  His father had organized some contingency plans for just such an event.  An extremely large, untraced bank account had been set up a long while ago, while a computer program had kept continual track of Ash's location in the Fleet, so the information of his father's demise and instructions for a quick escape reached Asher almost within the hour.

Asher's father was a lot like a Jedi.  He had practically all the same (popularly known) ideals as a Jedi, and a few of the same mannerisms as Troy (in particular, the same emotional control).  Asher's obedience of Troy is the exact same obedience that Asher gave to his father.  Asher's father, Trevelyan Lewis Tagge, was a proud man who embodied the ideal Tagge.  Unfortunately, because he was so, well, wonderful, he was required to give practically all his attention to accomplishments for the family and Fleet.  As a result, he saw little of Asher.

Asher's mother died when he was 5.

Asher saw his father very little over the years, but when Trevelyan did visit, it was with a lot of presents and he lavished a lot of attention on Asher.  However, his emotional control was too powerful for him to show honest emotions.  He knew he loved his son, and he knew the actions to show his love, but when he did those actions, they just weren't convincing.  The lavish gifts from his father and the instant gratification of any material need created someone who tended to be emotionally and mentally eclectic.  Totally spontaneous.

Asher loved his father wholeheartedly, but recognition of everybody else's respect / esteem / regard / love for his father added a tinge of hero-worship.

Over the months on the run after his fathers death, Asher's mild case of hero-worship altered into the worshipping of an idealized father.  All memories of his father's failings and disappointments were totally forgotten, and Asher has dedicated his life to be as much like his father as possible.

Asher was brought up in a household of female servants.  He had a nanny / foster-mother until the age of 12 when Trevelyan retired her and changed the staff so Asher could learn to be a leader (of the house at least).  This leads to Asher's relationship with the Triplets.  He is buoyant that someone cares for him.  Of course, the problem is that Asher is slightly childlike and exuberant in his relationship with them.  I wouldn't be too surprised to find he brings out the mother instinct in them.  He has little clue in how to handle a relationship with a female and Asher has to be constantly trying to act in a mature and comfortable manner to avoid being painfully embarrassing.

Asher is trying to mature beyond a lot of his spontaneous childish actions, but he doesn't want to lose all of the innocence.

Trevelyan's famed control was actually part of his self-developed skill in latent Force.  This development was through meditation, an attempt to live according to his ideals, and practice at ignoring hunger, thirst, pain or exhaustion, while he was needed (which seemed all the time).  It was when Asher was 16 that Trevelyan had an inkling of what he possessed, and tried to teach to Asher what he knew.

Asher tried to learn, but Trevelyan just couldn't put what he felt into the right words and couldn't spend enough time with Asher to really get Asher to know anything more than recognize the Force within himself.  Asher had been told of all the Jedi beliefs and ideals, and there were actually a few books given to Asher to study.  It seems quite a coincidence that Asher has found himself in the company of some Jedi Knights and a ship or two in search of more.

Asher has always tried to do the right thing to make everyone happy in his search for affection.  He finds he has to step in to any family quarrel he sees to stop the hurt.  He hates to see anyone sad, and when noticed, Asher's actions often take on a clownish overtone.  However, anger tends to make him sad and conciliatory.  Having a very sunny disposition, Asher has found that he rarely gets angry, and when he does, he can control it.

The major exception to this is the anger that is the driving force behind his aspiration to be similar to his father, or more to the point, a Jedi Knight.  This subconscious anger is a combination of anger at himself for not being there to comfort his father, at himself for not being there to prevent his father from dying, and at his father for not showing more affection to him.  Asher recognizes the anger he feels towards Ormon, but he refuses to allow it to drive him towards revenge that would be unworthy of the Jedi (and his father's) ideals.

ALIASES: FLETCHER, A. N., no other known

PERSONAL DESCRIPTION: Eyes: Blue. Hair: Blonde, curly. Complexion: Fair. Other Distinguishing Facial Features: None. Hand: Left. Height: 5' 4". Weight: 140 lbs. Build: Medium.

MAJOR SKILLS: Astrogation, Blaster Use, Knowledge of Bureaucratic Systems, and Command Abilities.

MINOR SKILLS: Starship Repair, Computer Programming and Computer Repair.

SPECIAL NOTES: It has been noted that the subject has shown signs of a burgeoning use of special powers which have been colloquially been gathered under the title of the "Force".

FAMILY: Most noted fact about the Tagge family is that they are the owners of the largest private merchant shipping line, which also carries a great deal of influence in Imperial circles, mining exploration, spice harvest and transport.  Their fleet is said to exceed that of the Rebellion's forces.  The scientific and commercial resources under Tagge control is staggering.  It is commonly known that they use their Imperial connections to further their own ambitions.

The main line of the family has 3 brothers and a sister.  The eldest brother of the Tagge line is Baron Ormon Tagge.  Other than being the head of this powerful Family, he is also a rival for the emperor's throne.  He was blinded by a light sabre used by Darth Vader, and has to use cyber-vision to see.  He has publicly stated he will get revenge on Lord Vader in a duel.  He and the rest of the family do not believe in the powers of the so-called Force.  Independent assessment has concluded that Baron Ormon suffers from megalomania.

The next sibling is [[010-Star-Wars-Characters#_Tagge, Silas_|Silas Tagge]].  He is an emotionless scientist who has become a noted designer of ingenious Imperial weaponry.

The third sibling is [[010-Star-Wars-Characters#_Tagge, Domina_|Domina Tagge]].  She has been cloistered in a monastery (the Order of the Sacred Circle) on the wishes of her brother Ormon.  It is believed that Ormon views her as "the only pure and innocent thing in this (the Tagge) family" and he doesn't want to see her corrupted.

The youngest sibling is [[010-Star-Wars-Characters#_Tagge, General Ulric_|General Ulric Tagge]], who is an Imperial Fleet Commander.  All other details have been withheld for security reasons.

The current subject is a first cousin of the leading Tagge siblings.  His father was the younger brother of the siblings' father.  Although not proven, suspicious circumstances surround the deaths of both the subject's father and the previous Baron.  (It is widely believed that Baron Ormon killed both of them to become Baron.)  There have been no notable events in the subject's life, except for being immersed in the family's fleet as an astrogator, until recently.  There have been four near-fatal accidents near the subject.  The subject left the Imperial planet and has headed off to one of the minor sectors.  The subject lost the operative in the spaceport.  Current whereabouts are unknown.

### Addendum

It's nice to know that people are looking out for me.  Unfortunately, I don't think that it is for my own benefit that they are doing so.  At least they don't know some of the finer details about me.

After my father and uncle's deaths, I thought that the safest place for me was to place myself in the middle of the Family business.  My father had discussed with me the fears he felt for our Family.  I always knew that Ormon was extremely ruthless, but I couldn't accept my father's fears on the extent that Ormon was willing to go to achieve his ambitions.  After his death, I found my father had set up an account, with an astronomical amount, for me.  He had also set up a refuge for me within the fleet among some of his friends.

I was growing used to being a member of the trading fleet when the 'accidents' began.  I guess Ormon must have thought I was getting too powerful a group of friends amongst the fleet.  And I guess that was what I was trying to do, even if it wasn't intentional.  It was the third 'accident' before it finally got through to me that they all were under very suspicious circumstances.  They were all the results of happenstance, freak happenings of nature, or so the reports said; but all examinations of the 'accidents' were confiscated.

My friends covered for me as I went through the spaceport.  I bought a couple of tickets to different sectors, but I was smuggled out as one of the crew of another ship.  After going from sector to sector I ended up in Minos Sector.  I was at an acquaintance's formal dinner when some interesting guests arrived and I saw an opportunity to vanish from the public eye.  The rest is yet to be done.

### Later Addendum

**What Is Asher's Allegiance - the Alliance or the Force?**

Although we haven't played this for a while before last Friday, it's starting to come back to me.  I know that I had reached a kind of plateau with my "training" as I went out adventuring on Alliance missions.  It had come on over a while, such that when we continued with our usual missions (which happened to find a new Jedi almost half the time) I hadn't really noticed.

I remember when we had that last session before putting it away, I felt that we had reached a closure, an end of the chapter so to speak.  We had gathered for the feast / picnic / public exhibition.  All Jedi's were seen in a habitat they preferred and we got to know them a bit more.  I knew then that we were in for some changes, but as I said ... it's been a while.

Yes, Asher still has the belief that the Alliance must win for the good of all.  Yes, Asher still has the familial attachment to the Alliance (let alone the Tagge fleet).  But he believes, too, that he has become a Jedi.  With the quasi-aura that it still has based on the way Darth Vader is referred to in the films.  It's a priesthood-like thing to outsiders, but he knows it's more than that but it takes the same kind of dedication (as that of priests with faith).  Remember, celibacy isn't a rule in the Jedi!

With the Circle gathered, surely it is time for us apprentices to take more of a role in the "Jedi" work.  I am currently a Lieutenant in the Alliance.  I don't know how the Alliance is going to take the fact that some of its officers want to join the priesthood, essentially.  I know that Luke stayed within the Alliance, but he has a more public face, so to speak, than us.

I get the feeling that this "mass defection" of successful operatives is something that's going to land in the lap of Leith.  He might accept the value of it, but would he filter the happening from passing to the Alliance Command and what would happen if they issued demands for us still?  I don't know, maybe you have had the ideas for a long time now and have been waiting for us to reach the same point.

Thinking back on it, with the proviso that there is no trace of Force use on them when they have dealt with him, I can't see Asher reacting any differently.  Yes, he was (is) in love with all of them.  They all excited him physically and mentally, and he loved that they had so much love for each other.  But I pictured he always felt guilty about leaving so often.  That's why he was buying presents all the times he was away.  (When he was buying them presents with them, that was something else, that was the pleasure of shopping with them!)

Wow, almost lost track of what I was trying to say.  Because he had always felt guilty, when the Triplets had fallen out (as far as Asher knew) and Anna and Zoe had started with others, he couldn't fault them for doing what he had always feared they would do anyway.  If there is a trace of Force use on them, which my Sense might pick up on, then I think Asher would have acted quite differently.  To find out just what had been done to them, and why, and why they thought that I would accept that.  And to reverse / stop / fix it.

**What's Up With Being A Tagge?**

Pretty much the same tack as I took with the Alliance and the Force.  I want to find out more about the Tagge and noble family conflict.  I think that there had been a possible start with my uncle Silas being captured; a possible start in the ending of the estrangement from the family.  Well, I seem to recall that it was my aunt who I was closest too, and then it was the Tagge people, then it was my uncles.  And the thought the Tagge Fleet might join the Alliance like my father had was also a dream.

But the responsibilities of the Alliance seemed to overflow my desires for the family.  Now, of course, the responsibilities of the Jedi seem to overflow my desires for the Alliance.

**The Circle?**

As I ran through with you at the end of Friday ... well Saturday morning, I think that with the completion of the Circle it is time to alter the focus of my training.  I have progressed as a Jedi trying to maintain my balance and progress evenly.  With all the due respects and honours, for me to "advance" further with Troy, I think I need to learn other Jedi's views and lessons so I can "appreciate his teachings fully".  Of the other Circle members, as I had mentioned to you, I desired to expand my abilities in certain areas of expertise (which I knew of) and also to experience the other Circle member's company.  Hey, I'm more curious than the next guy.

So I wanted to learn more of Nichelle's spatial abilities, more fighting skills from Miri (Kezz still rubs me up the wrong way, and I don't know anything about Boddi's abilities) and I was interested in Aeysha and Grania, both for what they had shown and also for the fact that I was Aeysha's descendant and I wanted to get to really know my family.

Asher has never had any problems with Boddi.  Where everyone else (Lou and Roger) always had qualms or concerns about her, I've always tried to have Asher have complete trust in Boddi.  She has remained "active" awaiting the reawakening of the Circle, and has a goal.  You could say it's an article of Ash's faith.  So while Jess continued a little diatribe against the schemings of Boddi, I felt that Asher would continue to take what Boddi says on face value, as she has tried to keep the galaxy on track to its destiny that she has foreseen.
