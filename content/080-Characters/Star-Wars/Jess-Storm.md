# Commander Jess (Jessamine) Storm

**Written by _[[Jenny-H|Jenny H.]]_ (Jess)**

### Appearance

![[sw_jess.jpg]]

In her early thirties.  Avoids makeup and feminine artifices.  Inside is a ravishing woman longing to get out, but Jess keeps her hidden - its not safe!  Hidden in the ship are a couple of daring and very expensive evening clothes, never worn.  Her underwear tends to the risqué and impractical as well.  Normal dress is purely practical - loose trousers, calf high boots worn over the top, loose open shirt, short flying jacket, dirty white scarf with multiple uses weighted at one end, blaster, wicked-looking knife down her back.

Jess wears an "Orin" under her shirt, a jade pendant and good luck charm of two intertwined snakes given to her by her mother, Annalise.  The design is the crest of Toiyabe House (Annalise's side of the family).  The pendant is also a Jedi Orin, the key to the release of Jedi Master Troy.
### Attitudes

>**GM's Note:**  This description was provided by the player at the start of play.  There's been a lot of water under the bridge since then, and some of Jess' attitudes have changed over time.

Moody and emotional.  Cultivates a hard and rough exterior for business purposes that hides a moral streak and a deep thinker.  Adventurous, fun-loving risk taker.  Fiercely loyal to family and friends.  Too independent to take orders easily.

**Smartness** - A tidy ship is easier to manage.

**Uniforms** - If you look the part, you help to establish credibility.  Show a bit of cleavage and they're butter in your hands.

**Parades** - You've got to be joking.

**Discipline** - Jess welcomes open discussion, but its her ship and when it becomes a democracy, she'll let you know.

**Alcohol** - Do what you want, when you want, with who you want, provided its not too illegal.  Affect the credibility or operations of the ship, and you're in deep shit!

**Training** - We spend a lot of time in hyperspace.  You'd be wise to use it productively.

**Imperials** - Why get steamed up over something that's inevitable?  Just make sure the log shows what they want to see and try not to get in too deep.

**Alliance** - Jess might do a job here and there, but she's not one of them and they'd better remember that.

**Locals** - Jess can't help it if they get in the way.  They wanted the Rebellion!  On the other hand, She'll do what she can to mitigate the damage.

**Missions** - Teamwork is all important.  Jess can't work with someone she can't rely on.

**Crew / Captain Relationships** - If Jess tells you to do something, she expects it done, but you can handle the details.  If you can't do it, tell Jess and she'll work it out with you.  Don't do it at all and she'll want to know why.

**Rewards** - Jess is deep in hock, but if she can pass something your way, she will.

**Technology** - Jess' ship is her wings, her love and her life, but she can see circumstances under which her people would come first.

**Politics** - Don't get messed up with it.  Ignorance is dangerous, getting involved doubly so.

**The Crew** - Jess' responsibility; be loyal to her and she'll move heaven and hell to help you.

### Interests

Weapons, money and flying.  Jess would love the chance to be hedonistic, sensual and exotic.  Maybe later, when she's made her pile.  She loves adventure, travel, likes reading and has an interest in other cultures.  Jess couldn't bear being purely a wife and mother, and doesn't want to be a mother at all.

### History

Originally from the Core Worlds, Jess is the daughter of a Taran and Annalise Storm, a Corellian trader and owner of a small fleet of light freighters, and a socialite.  Jess' mother was the daughter of an outspoken Senator of the Old Republic, Elkior Toiyabe.  Elkior was believed to have died in mysterious circumstances shortly after Senator Palpatine came to power.  Annalise decided to move to safer ground, fell in love with a disreputable but very handsome smuggler, and settled down with him.

Jess has been flying ships of some description for as long as she can remember.  She was born on a ship - her mother persuaded her father to travel on a luxury liner.  Taran hated every second of it, but was willing to indulge a very pregnant Annalise's strange whims.  Jess arrived unexpectedly early.  "Impatient as ever" was her father's comment years afterwards.

Jess loved ships and spent as much time on them as she could.  She trained with her father and was working on his ships as early as possible.  When she was 21, she became a captain of one of her father's ships.  Her co-pilot and navigator was a Torgian named Flis, the daughter of one of her father's navigators.  The two became extremely good friends.  After all, they could paint the town red and not have to compete with each other.

Then Jess dumped a cargo of "medical supplies" after she discovered it was actually nerve toxins used by Imperial troops against resisting worlds.  When her supplier asked about the missing cargo, she claimed she was boarded by customs.  Jess didn't know that the real supplier of her dumped cargo was the infamous Twi'lek, Yerkys ne Dago, enemy of her father and a staunch Imperial supporter.  Yerkys retaliated for his financial loss by arranging a fatal shipyard "accident" for Jess, that killed Flis instead.  After this setback, Jess ran and hid in Minos Sector (pointed in that direction by her father).

Jess' next navigator was Tag, an all-round bad guy who was subsequently "iced" by the Iceman, on a contract from a disappointed gambling mark.
