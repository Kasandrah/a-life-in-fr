# Boddi

![[sw_boddi1.jpg]]

**Boddi**

Boddi is over five thousand years old, a Jedi Master and a member of the [[Jedi-Bendu|Circle of the Ashla]], an ancient brotherhood dedicated to protecting the Galaxy.

She was one of three Guardians assigned to "protect and serve" while the other Circle members were "sleeping".  This is completely unknown outside of her current associates.

As a foreseer of unimaginable skill and power, Boddi can see the past, present, and possible futures over any distances, spatial and temporal.  She can see any place, any time.

The possible futures spread out like a network of Force lines ahead of her on the temporal plane.  Every time a choice is made and an action carried out, no matter how small, the immediate lines are "set" and the possible future becomes more probable.  Each time a change is made or an alternative selected, the repercussions ripple through the Force net, ultimately bringing into being an alternative future.

Any being with prior knowledge of the future has the potential to alter that future by choosing another alternative.  As a Jedi Master, a Guardian of Justice, Boddi must attempt to bring into being the best possible future for the galaxy as a whole, not for any particular individual or group of individuals, and not for herself.

For three thousand years she has been driven by her precognition of the total destruction of all life in this galaxy by invaders from another galaxy.  Boddi is the only being who can set in motion the events needed to bring about an alternative to this end, as she knows what those events are and can watch them unfolding outward through the Force net.

Boddi cannot share her knowledge of future events with any other beings, as those beings may then use their knowledge to alter the future, either deliberately or accidentally, so that the worst outcome is more probable.  In avoiding this, she appears secretive.  What she does to set up the actions that will result in a positive outcome may appear as manipulative, in that actions taken by others are determined by what she says or doesn't say.  However, given the Jedi creed of allowing beings freedom of choice, Boddi has no alternative but to be "secretive and manipulative".

Now Boddi has partly explained her purpose, she does not want to be relied on as the font of all knowledge.  As a Jedi, she is also responsible for the advancement of other beings less able than herself.  Claiming "things are unforeseen" is her method of withdrawing her skills and knowledge and forcing beings to tackle a situation with their own resources.  A learning experience always advances a being.

Boddi possesses great skills in genetics, a talent she abused in a pursuit of power and darkness over three thousand years ago.  She eventually went insane and was slain by her lover, Kezz, a Jedi Battle Master on the Path of Light.  The Ashla recovered Boddi's spirit from the depths of the Dark Side and Boddi now exists as an embodiment of the Force, her physical form created from star matter, stabilised into flesh and bone by her control skills and imbued with her spirit and the Ashla itself.

Through ritual, fasting, and deep meditation, Boddi cleansed herself of the stain of Darkness and was admitted to the Circle of the Ashla.  Since then Boddi has kept herself hidden from all others powerful in the Force, particularly the Emperor and Lord Darth Vader in more recent times.  Her shielding abilities are total and undetectable.

Although there are trillions of beings in the galaxy, the number of beings with even a little ability in the Force is perhaps in the hundreds.  The number of potential or latent users of the Force is perhaps ten times as large.  Thanks to the depredations of the Emperor and Vader, beings proficient in the Force are quite rare.  Boddi has every reason to be very careful whom she trusts and how much she trusts them.  It is a mark of her regard that chosen beings know as much about her as they do.

Boddi was teaching military tactics to Alliance troops when she disappeared without a trace while on a mission with Alliance ground forces.  Some time later Hela found Boddi on Yelsain (Minos Sector) after Boddi had returned from the mountains carrying pockets full of small red gems.

Boddi wears the red Orin, a force-amplifying gem and the key that unlocked Nichelle from her "sleep".  Boddi is Dermod's mother.
