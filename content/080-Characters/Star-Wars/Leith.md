# General Leith

Commander, Home Base. Formerly of the Imperial Army, defected to the Rebel Alliance.  Leith is stable, thoughtful, and dependable, with a leader's charisma, strength, and decisiveness.  His genuine care for the troops under his command has boosted morale on the base to an all-time high.

## Background

Leith is a career soldier.  He came up through the ranks by skill and merit when those things mattered.  He's a veteran of the Clone Wars and other respectable battles.

Leith doesn't approve of the Emperor or his tactics, and was set on reforming the Imperial Army from the inside. Although he was smart and knew to tread carefully, he soon realised that this approach just would not work.

Leith has been firm friends with Carlist Rieekan ever since they attended the Imperial Army Officer Candidate School together.  It was Rieekan’s defection that started Leith on his own road from the Imperial Army to the Rebel Alliance.  Given a choice between a dangerous role within Imperial forces committing atrocities beyond belief, and a short-lived role within the Alliance fighting a losing battle against overwhelming odds, Leith strongly believes that beings of honour, compassion and conscience will choose the latter.  Many of the Empire’s finest have already made that choice and are now serving with the Alliance.

Imperial Security Bureau (ISB) agents were watching Leith closely, as they suspected Sari had something to do with Brormal's death.  ISB also suspected Leith was passing information on to Sari who then disappeared, severely compromising Leith with ISB.  Leith distracted ISB by alerting them to Sari's actions and then contacted an Intel agent named Tiree.  He helped Leith escape before ISB could arrest him.  Just as well, because when Leith evaded ISB at the Aris spaceport, they were planning an "accident" for him.

## Attitudes

Leith was angry when Jess used the words “Imperial turncoats” to describe defectors to the Alliance, but said nothing.  He knows that this is what many in the Alliance perceive him to be.  Without examples like Dodonna, Madine, and Rieekan, the Alliance would never have accepted him into its ranks.  There have been several instances of “rescued” Alliance prisoners and Imperial defectors turning out to be ISB plants.  Leith's hard and dangerous work in Bissillirus won him the respect and trust of his superiors, and command of Home Base.

Unless Leith becomes a lot more hard-nosed, Sari will continue to have influence over him because he loves her.  But as base commander, Leith has to remain as impartial as humanly possible in his approach to personnel issues, especially any that come up involving Sari.  Besides which, it's not good for effective command of the base for the personnel to have any reason to think that Leith is Sari's plaything.  It's a hard balancing act - but that's one of Leith's challenges.  He is developing a "public" and "private" relationship with Sari - the "public" one more distant, the "private" one more passionate and unrestrained.  A separation of Leith's personal desires regarding Sari from his public role as base commander.

Leith wants Sari to reach her full potential as a Jedi.  He will encourage her to advance her skills, as the other apprentices are doing.  Kezz may even demand that, for Leith to concentrate on learning battle master force skills and reach his full potential, Sari be out of the picture for a while.  Cass may demand that Leith be out of Sari's picture for a while for the same reason.
