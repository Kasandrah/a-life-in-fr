# Heije Gamou

Jess' maternal grandmother.

## Written by _[[Jenny-H|Jenny H.]]_ (Jess)

Heije met and fell in love with a Corellian CorSec agent, Elkior Toiyabe.  He thought that marriage and a child, Annalise, would slow her down.  (Even then he had conservative leanings.)  He thought they would build a quiet, respectable life together.  He was wrong.

Despite several arguments on the subject, Heije refused to step down as a Jedi Knight.  She insisted that Elkior did not understand the Force and her abilities (and was right).  She simply couldn't turn her back on it.  Despite their differences, they were surprisingly devoted to each other.  The Force was simply a bone of contention that spoiled their happiness together.

Heije recognised that the infant Annalise was also strong in the Force.  Despite Elkior's opposition, she arranged for her to be tested as a potential candidate.  She also gave her the family heirloom of the Orin pendant.

Unfortunately, shortly afterwards, Heije was killed.  Elkior was distraught and refused to allow Annalise to be tested.  He planned to bring her up in a safe, appropriate environment, far away from any Force influences.  He resigned form CorSec to be able to better protect her and went into politics.

Annalise (and Jess) have a strong resemblance to Heije, physically and in Force abilities.  Annalise's remain untested and largely unused, though the same 'hiding' abilities that Jess displays have protected her from suspicion and harm.  Few ever discover that the ditzy, harmless socialite hides a keen mind and a strong will.

Annalise's resemblance to Heije distanced her more and more from Elkior.  He protected her, introduced her to the best circles, met every need, but found it too painful to be too close to her.

Despite this, Elkior could not stop the Emperor's interest.  He was gradually hunting down all Jedi.  Then he turned his attention to Jedi families, knowing that the Force often followed family lines.  Elkior's 'death' gave him the excuse to disappear.

Annalise forestalled any attempts to safeguard her by escaping herself.  She had limited foretelling skills and knew it was time to leave.

Elkior's reactions to Jess stem in part from a) her resemblance to Heije b) her choice of lifestyle c) Elkior's commitment to the Alliance (and Jess's lack of it) and his position in it d) her association with Jedi and e) her Force skills.  Unfortunately, any change of heart on his part will not be met with any welcome on Jess' part.  Annalise herself long since stopped caring about it (or so she tells herself).

Neither Jess nor Annalise have any knowledge of the Jedi side of Heije.  Annalise always thought that Heije was a CorSec agent.  She was too young to have any clear memories of anything else.
