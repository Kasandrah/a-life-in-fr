# Angelica

3rd Gen Vampire, Clan Bahari.  Beautiful red-haired daughter of the Emir of Seville and Rollon’s lover.

In 778 AD, Angelica was Oliver, Roland’s lover.  Roland slew Oliver near the end of the battle at Roncevalles.  Roland, weak from his own wounds, was overcome by the jealous Dark Fae spirit of his sword Durandel, and made to slay Oliver.

In Celtic Britain, 200 years earlier, Angelica had been Arthur’s wife, Maeve.

In **1199 AD,** Rollon made Angelica a vampire, breaking one covenant of Lillane’s Clan, i.e., progeny are forbidden to dilute the strength of the Clan by making their own progeny.  By slaying the renegade 3rd Gen and earlier progeny of Lillane’s, Dion of Kush, and drinking his blood, Angelica became a 3rd Gen herself.

Thus Angelica became worthy of Lillane’s regard and saved Rollon’s neck from Lillane’s sword.
