# Abdiel

The angel Abdiel, a Seraph and the Servitor of Judgment, is an important and valiant angel who played a leading role in Milton’s “Paradise Lost”.  He stood firm against the call of Satan to the angels to revolt against God.  He is a member of the angelic choir of the Seraphim and is described by Milton as the “flaming seraph”.

During the first day of fighting in the war in Heaven, serving under the Archangel Michael, Abdiel routed the rebellious fallen angels Rameil, Arioch, and Ariel.  Even Satan himself was driven back by “the mighty sword stroke” of Abdiel.

Abdiel was also mentioned during the Middle Ages in the mystical Jewish work “The Book of the Angel Raziel”, traditionally attributed to Rabbi Eleazar of Worms.  Abdiel’s name means “servant of God”.  Milton described him as “unshak’n, unseduced”.
