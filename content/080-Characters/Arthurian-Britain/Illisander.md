# Illisander

Illisander is _[[Lady-Voklaynn|Lady Voklaynn]]_ and _[[Lord-Anwyn|Lord Anwyn's]]_ youngest son.  He looks much like his elder brother _[[Karwin|Karwin]]_, but appears to be about three years old.  A garrulous and brave little individual, he is unafraid to speak his mind and stands up to any adult.  Lord Anwyn is of the opinion that Illisander takes after his mother.  His father was never that obvious.

Illisander is a vegetarian, as are all unicorns.
