# Tyler

![[da_christopher_lee_4.jpg]]

Knight of the Praetorian Guard, Equestrian Order, now Ghoul, Clan Bahari.  The Roman "equites" were horsemen, the cavalry wing of the Roman Army, and the source of the Army's officers.

In 83 AD in Rome, Tatianus was wrongly accused of conspiracy against Emperor Domitian and sentenced to die in the arena.  Lillane recognised Tatianus as the reincarnation of Theshen, her Egyptian lover, and made him her ghoul so he could survive.  Also known as Tyler, Tomas, or Thomas.
