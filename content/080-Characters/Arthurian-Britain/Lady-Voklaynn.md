Lady Voklaynn appears to be in her mid-20s, 5' 10'' tall, 10 1/2 stone with hip-length white hair and very fair skin.  Her most striking feature is her red eyes, in the depths of which a glow like embers can be faintly discerned, particularly in low light.  This alone ensures people don’t mistake her for a Fae.

Lady Voklaynn would be very beautiful, but for her unusual colouring.  She has high cheekbones with a well-shaped brow and jawbone, a long, slender neck and a slender body, lithe and muscled from the disciplines of dance.  Her hands are delicate, with long and nimble fingers, ideal for the sensitive work of healing or music.  On the middle finger of her right hand she wears a fine silver ring, a gift from the man who took her in and cared for her when she was very young.

Lady Voklaynn prefers to wear gowns of vibrant blue with silver embroidery, or deep green with gold embroidery.  Whenever she is outside, Lady Voklaynn wears a long, hooded cloak with a knotted and beaded fringe that covers her face but does not obscure her vision, and soft leather gloves to protect her hands.

Years of singing have strengthened her soprano voice, which she usually accompanies by playing her lute.  The lute is remarkable for its screws, tiny animals carved from mountain hardwood by a master craftsman.  Lady Voklaynn can read and write, speaks a number of local languages well and can make herself understood in a number of lesser-known tongues.

Only Lord Anwyn, Lady Voklaynn’s husband, surpasses her as a healer and herbalist.  However, it was she who taught him the secret of the rare wild orchids that grow here.  From the essence of a single orchid, she can distil a potion that increases the strength and stamina of anyone who drinks it.  Her medical bag also contains several rare and miraculous herbs that produce amazing results when correctly applied to wounds, injuries, or illness.

Even though she is a healer, Lady Voklaynn has learnt the value of a short sword, a dagger, and a crossbow and is skilled with all three.  The wilds beyond her realm are not to be traversed lightly, so she travels with her short sword at her left hip, her dagger in her belt, and her crossbow within reach.  Her fae armour of gold and silver chain mail, padded with silk, has protected her from harm on a number of occasions.  She has superb vision and always serves her travelling companions as a scout as well as a healer.

A large, wolf-like dog often accompanies Lady Voklaynn about the castle. Asho is very protective of Lady Voklaynn and her two sons, _[[Karwin|Karwin]]_ and _[[Illisander|Illisander]]_.

Lady Voklaynn is also very fond of two donkeys stabled in equine splendour in the castle grounds, and often visits them with her children.  A great fuss is made of the donkeys, which adore having their long ears scratched and their noses rubbed.  The donkeys are so alike that only close inspection will distinguish them from one another.  Lady Voklaynn calls them Number One and Number Two.

# The Red Dragon of Cymru

This vast, red dragon with eyes wholly crimson and a huge ruby inset into one of its front fangs is a fearsome sight.  Its awesome appearance and the sibilant hiss of its deep voice strike fear into the hearts of all who behold it.  However, brave Celts of true heart can approach it without fear of immediate roasting.

The Red Dragon shows a marked preference for the company of children.  Children in their turn seem able to conquer their fear quickly under its benevolent gaze.  Many will climb up into its proffered hand, ignoring the great claws, and converse with the Dragon while it holds them up level with its glowing red eyes.

The only other to be favoured with contact, conversation, and the exhilaration of flight upon the Red Dragon’s back is Lord Anwyn.  He acts as a mediator between the Dragon and its infrequent adult visitors, ensuring that exchanges between the Dragon and any fools don’t become fatal for the fools.

The Red Dragon can often be seen in the sky over the castle, swimming in the clear mountain lake below the castle, or sunning itself on a granite ledge beside the lake.  Rumours abound about the size and nature of the Red Dragon’s hoard, supposedly hidden in the mountain on which the castle was built.  No one is foolish enough to ask the Dragon directly, but all speculate on the items that may be found among the gold.

The Red Dragon of Cymru is the standard of Arthur Pendragon, King of the Britons.
