Sen (pronounced "Sean") is a soldier who was born in Sellenn, the fourth largest Toleki city after Ulleran, Gelfinlorn and Yelnas.  His family has had soldiers on both sides for more than four generations.  Sen rose to be Commander of the Left Wing of the Sellenni Army (the 'wing' concept he partly evolved) in its interminable wars with Barannas and/or Donakal-lorn and/or Padrannas.  Sen was sponsored to some extent by Count Envere Pallas, who was intent on martial glory at any cost and inveigled the command of the prestigious Right Wing of the Sellenni Army.

In a series of vicious fights with Donakal-lorn in lands to the north (theoretically Ullerani territory), the Envere ordered Sen to expend his men in a number of attacks designed solely to give great advantage to the Right Wing and undying glory to the Envere himself.  As the pointless death of men in battle is not the Toleki way, Sen became angry and refused to carry out the Envere's orders.  The Envere relieved Sen of his command and threatened further disgrace for Sen's family.  One of the Envere's lackeys was given command of the Left Wing and undertook the ploy.

The Sellenni lost the battle, the war and over half of the Left Wing.  Sen killed the Envere in something that would have been a duel, but for the lack of formal niceties on Sen's part.  He was too angry, upset and distraught to pay attention to social details.  Later Sen realized he should have pretended to obey the Envere's orders, but somehow subverted those orders during the battle.  If a similar situation arises he is determined to do, or advise, just that.

Unfortunately the Enveri are a wide-spread family with powerful connections (some of them even non-human), and a price of 6,000 pennies was put on Sen's head.  This represents a year's living for a knight and an astronomical fee for a bounty hunter, and as such shows the strength of the Enveri desire for Sen's death.

In bitterness Sen abandoned his career, swearing to never again allow himself to be put into a position where his men could be murdered by stupidity.  He intended to find a suitable person to teach and instruct, and to guide them into the position Sen desired but decided he "didn't want" any more.  He believes his career will live on through his student.  He refuses to command any more, but refuses to be a common soldier again.  He wants the advantages with none of the disadvantages.  Sen understands, appreciates and nurtures Ala's desires, but regards himself ultimately as a commander - a chief of staff rather than the flashing, superlative hero.  Besides, the hero needs her chief of staff!

As a commander Sen needs to know his own men, his allies and above all, his enemies, while at the same time remaining unknown to them.  He doesn't like his potential enemies knowing too much about him.  Sen is not the sort to talk about himself but he will not conceal information if directly asked.  He is possessed of a quiet pride but his most over-riding characteristic is his desire to know everything about everything military.  Sen worships the War Goddess, Starrion.
