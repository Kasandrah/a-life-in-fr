# Appearance

Ala has waist length straight blonde hair, very tanned skin and grey eyes.  She has scars on the top of her right leg and on her left hip, the result of injuries suffered in battle.

Ala wears noble armour of burnished silver consisting of a padded light mail hauberk with padded lamellar sleeves, leggings and a plate helm.  Her favoured weapon was a two handed, single edged battleaxe named "Night-watch".  The blade was black with gunmetal grey edges and both faces were engraved with gold.  The axe was magical and Ala could not be surprised when she was touching the blade.  Ala lost the axe during an encounter with a guardian of the realm of the dead while searching for clues on who was murdering her troop.

# Manner

Hot-blooded, passionate, emotional, instinctive and impulsive.  Very dangerous when confronted.

# Interests

Ala's skill with a battleaxe, her fast reflexes and her quickness on her feet has won her the respect of other soldiers from Ulleran to Dennil.  She is a highly skilled field healer and an excellent scout, thereby filling a number of roles in her unit.  She is also skilled with a crossbow and a dagger, which she has used on numerous occasions.

Ala is now the Captain of a Dennil light infantry unit consisting of 120 troops.  She is ably supported by her lieutenant and second-in-command, Sen (who is also her lover) and six sergeants.  As a result of the success of her most recent venture to gather information about the invading Iryash, her troops are solidly behind her.

Ala worships the War Goddess, Starrion as do most Toleki soldiers.  She also worships the Cow (Healing).  Military interests.

# Background

Ala's parents were farmers and she grew up on their farm near Ulleran.  After first serving in the Ulleran light infantry, Ala moved south to Dennil.  During Dennil's war with Atrenol Yern Ala defeated the brother of the King of Atrenol Yern in single combat.  As the prince was on horseback at the time, this was considered a great show of skill and courage on Ala's part.  Ala's unit also captured the King's cousin.

Towards the end of the war the King of Atrenol Yern was killed in combat and so his brother, Ala's prisoner, inherited the position.  Ala ransomed the new King back to the survivors of Atrenol Yern for two stallions and four mares, a very large prize indeed.  She kept one stallion of very good blood, even though he was old, one good mare and one average mare.  The rest were sold for 12,700p.  In recognition of her achievements Ala was knighted by the Queen of Dennil, hence the "Asrano" title.
