# Cendrian

Cendrian is a small, winged, bipedal lizard, only 5" 7' tall and weighing about 80 kilos.  He wears a hooded ring-mail hauberk, carries a heavy mace and a hoplite shield, and has three war boomerangs strapped to his back.  He has played a two-holed flute occasionally.

Cendrian is Lady Voklaynn’s official bodyguard, named Smudge after his unusual colouring, which looks like smudges of paint.  He stands erect on his hind legs and projects a silent but formidable hurdle for anyone intending harm to Lady Voklaynn.

He appeared on the trail one day and declared himself Lady Voklaynn’s bodyguard.  Lady Voklaynn was heard to mutter something darkly about “Saurians” in general and this one in particular, but then sighed and continued on.  Smudge followed along behind and has been with her ever since, much to her continued annoyance.

After an encounter with a demon in a witch’s temple, Smudge grew wings.  Lady Voklaynn surmises that the shock and stress of the encounter forced a change on Smudge that took him one more rung up the evolutionary ladder.
