# Appearance

But for her colouring Voklaynn would be considered very beautiful.  She is an albino and has pink eyes, hip-length straight white hair and white skin.  She has high cheekbones with a well-shaped brow and jaw, a long, slender neck and a slender body lithe and muscled from the disciplines of dance.  Her hands are delicate with long and nimble fingers, ideal for the sensitive work of healing or music.

On the middle finger of her right hand she wears a fine silver ring, a gift from the man who took her in and cared for her when she was young.  She wears Elven armour of gold and silver chain mail padded with silk.  She also wears royal blue, a very unusual choice of colour for any Toleki, male or female.  It makes her appearance even more striking.  As she suffers from sunburn very easily, whenever Voklaynn is outside she wears a long, hooded cloak with a knotted and beaded fringe that covers her face but does not obscure her vision.  She wears leather gloves to protect her hands from sunburn or other injuries.

# Manner

Cool and reserved with strangers.  Warm with friends.  Very loving to her husband and sons.  Has an “otherworldly” manner made more so by her appearance.

# Interests

Loves her unicorn husband, [[Lord-Anwyn|Tintagel]] and her sons [[Karwin|Karwin]] and [[Illisander|Illisander]].

As a healer and herbalist, Voklaynn has no equal in Anfar.  Her skills are known and respected throughout nearly all the Toleki lands, and her expertise in plant lore is much sought after.  She is the only herbalist on the island who knows the secret of the wild orchids that grow in the footprints of the goddess Princess Mindmage.  From their essence she distils a potion that can increase the strength and stamina of anyone who drinks it.  Her medical bag also contains other rare and miraculous herbs that produce amazing results when correctly applied to wounds, injuries, or illnesses.

Years of singing have strengthened her soprano voice, which she usually accompanies by playing her lute.  The lute is remarkable for its Gelfling screws, tiny animals carved from mountain hardwood by a Gelfling craftsman.

Voklaynn's travelling companions include a large, wolf-like dog named Asho, two donkeys and a small Saurian bodyguard named [[Cendrian|Cendrian]], whom she calls "Smudge".  Asho's left hind leg was crippled during an attack by thieves, so he normally rides along in the back of Voklaynn's cart, keeping an eye on her sons, her belongings and her hidden pouch of gold and silver pieces.  The donkeys provide their own brand of entertainment for the rest of the party and are so alike that only close inspection will allow them to be distinguished from one another.  Voklaynn calls them Number One and Number Two.

All things Saurian, including the Celestial Dragon.

Voklaynn has learnt the value of a short sword, a dagger and a crossbow and is skilled with all three.

# Background

Toleki healer and entertainer from Gelfinlorn.  Raised by an entertainer, but not his daughter.  Origins unknown.

# Education

Although she is mostly self-educated, Voklaynn can read and write, speaks Thellessan (Elvish) well and can make herself understood in Gelfling.  She is the only non-Saurian in the whole of Anfar who can partially communicate with Saurians.  When she has demonstrated this ability, she has brought on herself the suspicion and fear of those witnesses who do not know her well.

# The White Saurian

This vast, white Saurian with eyes wholly crimson is Voklaynn's alter-ego.  A bipedal, lizard-like creature 8 and a half feet tall and weighing nearly 300 kilos, covered with pure white, pearlescent scales.  She wears a cream coloured robe and trousers of tanned leather under pale grey full ring-mail armour.  There are large claws on her hands and feet, a huge ruby inset into one of her front fangs and a grey metal bracelet on her right arm.  This awesome appearance and the sibilant hiss of her deep voice makes it impossible for any non-Saurian to distinguish her gender.

When fully armed the White Saurian is a fearsome sight, as she carries an arbalest, a quiver full of bolts over her shoulder and a one-handed bastard sword.  As her senses extend into the infrared spectrum she is a superb scout.

In this form Voklaynn retains all of her original intellectual skills, has greatly improved perception and manipulative abilities (her hands may be clawed but they are extremely nimble) and a natural ability to communicate fully with other Saurians.  However, her sheer bulk has severely compromised her stealth abilities.  Her Saurian lute is a big one with two necks and two sets of strings.  Amongst Voklaynn's belongings is a small statuette of a white Saurian bearing the inscription "This is the White One, Child of the Cosmic Dragon".  It exactly resembles her in her Saurian form.

Voklaynn first became the White Saurian after a wizardess tried to change her into a dryad.  Voklaynn changed, but into a form that was more natural for her, into her true self.  On the Prophesy Wall of the Gelflings is a painting that shows the White Saurian standing before a host of Saurians bowed down in worship.  A unicorn stands behind the White Saurian, while the gods above make gestures of welcome.  The Saurians believe Voklaynn is their goddess and treat her as such.  Voklaynn tries not to think of this and lives one day at a time with her husband and her son.

"You feel massive, titanic, monumental, like a mountain, an oak in its prime, like the very land or the ocean.  Strange knowledge courses inside your head, strange concepts inexpressible in primitive Tolekol.  Everything seems so small, everything so light.  All has grown small and tender around you and the living has the curious seen-unseen metallic purple overlay/glow.

For a moment in your mind you see ahead, beyond yourself, and also behind, a different beyond.  Like pages stacked in a row with you the writing on each page, you see impressions of "that-which-is-you" stretching to infinite distances "behind-beyond" and "ahead-beyond".  Before you can quite grasp the meaning of this it vanishes from your sight like mist on a warm morning.

There is a "something" lurking in "there".  If you let it, it would swoop out and make you happy and content.  No, that concept was wrong, but ... it masqueraded as "Correctness".  Before you could think where you remembered this from, you have forgotten.  It is "you-not-you".
