# Zatarn

![[dd_zatarn.jpg]]

**Zatarn**

Zatarn is the evil wizard who is Lissa’s nemesis. Zatarn is 6’ 6” tall, lean, has long pale blond hair and is clean-shaven. Chemical burns have scarred badly on the back of his right hand. His eyes are black-green; his complexion is medium.

Zatarn’s heritage is unknown. He has lived in Karameikos for over thirty years, though he looks only thirty years old. He has hinted that he came from farther south. Zatarn dresses richly in furs, brocades and embroidery and loves luxury and soft living, even though he remains in shape. “Unseen servants” and some domestics serve in his tower. He lives away from any major villages.
