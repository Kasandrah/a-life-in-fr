# Yakov

>**GM's Note:** The story begins just after Baron Sherlane Halaran resurrected _[[010-DnD-Adventurers#Kubinski, Yakov|Yakov Kubinski]]_ after [[DnD-Mystara-Timeline#Moldain 11|a fatal battle]].

You become aware of struggling up to the light.  A man is calling your name.  Your memory of a conversation with a handsome huntsman wearing forest green clothes and carrying a bow is rapidly fading.  This other man calls your name again and you awake.  The first thing you notice is his nose, a prominent, jutting nose that must have earned him many interesting nicknames.  His eyes are blue, his hair snow white, his face clean-shaven.  He appears to be in his early sixties and wears unpretentious clerical robes of the Church of Karameikos.  In a cheery but soft-spoken voice he introduces himself as Baron Sherlane Halaran, Patriarch of Threshold.  He seems very pleased and relieved when you remember meeting him before at the home of Lord Kaerin Penhaligon.

You are lying in a very comfortable bed.  The Patriarch is sitting next to your bed on a straight backed wooden chair.  Over his shoulder you see Ludmilla and Erystelle standing behind him, looking very relieved.  You are very weak and can barely talk, let alone move.  Your memory of recent events is jumbled and confused.  Something about a bell, but what you are not really sure.  You are too tired for conversation and sink into a deep and dreamless sleep.

In the days that follow you very slowly regain some of your strength.  Ludmilla and Erystelle tell you that Marcus and Vlad are also recovering as guests of the Patriarch, who raised all three of you from the dead.  When they are assured of your continued safety and eventual recovery, they leave Tarnskeep.  Ludmilla is going east to Guido's fort while Erystelle is going north to Alfheim and his Elven homelands (some pressing business about a tree).  Ludmilla regrets having to go but does seem to be in an awful rush.  She explains that their trips are courtesy of Teldon and his magical teleportation device, so you are not to worry about her.  She will catch up with you in Specularum at a later date, about four to six weeks from now.
