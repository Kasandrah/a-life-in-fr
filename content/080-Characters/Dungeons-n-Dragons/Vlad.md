# Vlad

>**GM's Note:** This is the introduction of a love interest for _[[010-DnD-Adventurers#Dromirecki, Vlad|Vlad Dromirecki]]_, a hero of my D&D campaign.
>
>I wasn't sure whether Vlad would "take the bait", but he did.  The mighty Traldaran warrior fell head over heels in love, much to the amusement of his companions, and now looks for the time when he can be reunited with his beautiful Aleena.
>
>The story begins just after Baron Sherlane Halaran has resurrected Vlad after _[[DnD-Mystara-Timeline#Moldain 11|a fatal battle]]_.

You become aware of struggling up to the light.  A man is calling your name.  Your memory of a conversation with a red-headed Warrior King is rapidly fading.  This other man calls your name again, and you awake.  The first thing you notice is his nose, a prominent, jutting nose that must have earned him many interesting nicknames.  His eyes are blue, his hair snow-white, his face clean-shaven.  He appears to be in his early sixties and wears unpretentious clerical robes of the Church of Karameikos.  In a cheery but soft-spoken voice, he introduces himself as Baron Sherlane Halaran, Patriarch of Threshold.  He seems very pleased and relieved when you remember meeting him before at the home of Lord Kaerin Penhaligon.

You are lying in a very comfortable bed.  The Patriarch is sitting next to your bed on a straight-backed wooden chair.  Over his shoulder, you see Ludmilla and Erystelle standing behind him, looking very relieved.  You are very weak and can barely talk, let alone move.  Your memory of recent events is jumbled and confused.  Something about a bell, but what you are not really sure.  You are too tired for conversation and sink into a deep and dreamless sleep.

You awake to the cool and gentle touch of a damp cloth on your face.  It is withdrawn and you gaze into large, expressive blue eyes set in a face of classic Thyatian beauty.  Her flowing blond hair falls halfway down her back, and she is wearing a brightly coloured Traldaran gown.  When she notices you are awake, she blushes and drops her gaze.  She says her name is Aleena and that she will be caring for you until you are well enough to leave Tarnskeep.  She leaves to fetch Ludmilla and Erystelle.

In the days that follow, you very slowly regain some of your strength.  Ludmilla and Erystelle tell you that Marcus and Yakov are also recovering as guests of the Patriarch, who raised all three of you from the dead.  When they are assured of your continued safety and eventual recovery, they leave Tarnskeep.  Ludmilla is going east to Guido's fort while Erystelle is going north to Alfheim and his Elven homelands (some pressing business about a tree).  Ludmilla regrets having to go but seems to be in an awful rush.  She explains that their trips are courtesy of Teldon and his magical teleportation device, so you are not to worry about her.  She will catch up with you in Specularum at a later date, about four to six weeks from now.

Over these few days, you notice that Aleena is paying you a great deal of attention.  She is taking great care to ensure your comfort and continued improvement.  When you are more up to talking, she asks you many questions about yourself and your life, particularly your more recent adventures.  She tells you her mother and father died of disease a few years ago, and she came to Threshold to live with her uncle, the Patriarch.  Aleena is quiet, soft of voice and temperament, but seems to be very attracted to you.  This attraction grows stronger as the days pass.

Two weeks have gone by.  You are strong enough to walk about and have been to Threshold proper with Aleena, but you were very tired when you returned to Tarnskeep.  Although you are much improved, you know you cannot fight yet.  A short workout with Sergeant Arthol of the guards and you were exhausted.  It is now night and you have been asleep for some time when a noise awakens you.  Your eyes adjust to the dark and barely visible in the pale light of the stars through your window is Aleena.  She has closed the door behind her, walked across the room and now stands beside your bed.  She slips gracefully out of her gown and throws it over the back of the wooden chair.  You catch a brief glimpse of breast and hip glowing in the pale starlight as she climbs under the covers with you.

"My love, I have waited this long in the hope that you would be well enough.  If you do not have the strength, say so and I will leave, but I promise I will be very gentle with you."

**Written by _[[Michael-H|Michael H.]]_**

My dearest Aleena,

It is only three days since we parted, but it seems much longer.  I particularly miss our afternoon rides, which were a great joy to me, as well as the other times we spent in each other's company.  Ludmilla and Yakov are good enough travelling companions, but they seem to find our relationship extremely amusing.

The first evening saw us in Verge where we met Retameron and Halia in fine health and fair spirits.  Yakov delighted in telling them all about us, and I'm sure Halia will be full of questions when you are next together.  Verge and its residents seem recovered from the trials caused by Skarda, with Retameron's fighter school and Halia's apprentices progressing well.  We stayed in Verge, as both Yakov and myself are not as strong as we should be.

The little mage has discovered that he can see the missing lad in his crystal ball, and has been looking in nightly.  The lad is currently safe, and Ludmilla believes he will remain so for the near future.  We spoke with the owner of the inn, a Halfling with the original name of Stubbs, who let the boy sleep in his stables.  We believe he has gone to see the sage Arkayz, who I've told you about, to find work.  This is a piece of luck since we are planning to do the same.  We will be leaving early tomorrow with an Elven guide.

The journey has provided me with ample time to consider the matter that took us to Meyric.  Ludmilla tells me that the dragon and the wizard learnt more from our book than they are telling us.  I have come to the belief that the urgency of the matter is less than originally thought, since this pair do not seem duly worried.  Maybe Ludmilla's judgement was affected by her relationship with Meyric, and her desire to impress him.  The poor lass is quite put out by the whole affair.

Aleena, you have awoken something within me that I did not know existed, the power to love.  I love you.  I eagerly await the moment when we will be together again and I will be able to hold you in my arms, where you belong.  I await the moment when I will hear your sweet voice and behold your beautiful face.  I await the gaze of your lovely eyes that hold such power over me, and the touch of your loving hands.  May the Immortals keep you safe, my love, until we are reunited.

Your love, Vlad.
