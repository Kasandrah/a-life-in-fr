# Shandell

**Shandell (inspired by [Travis Fimmel's](https://www.imdb.com/name/nm1379938/?ref_=nmbio_ov_bk) Calvin Klein days)**

_[[Devis|Devis]]_ has described Shandell as:

- a beautiful Elven lover, exquisitely beautiful face, every bit as beautiful in the flesh as his absence had made him in Darius' mind, hauntingly beautiful
- breathtaking smile, fragile smile, shy smile
- finely arched brows, large expressive magnificent green eyes, eyes that are rich expanses of emerald green shining with passion and life
- long pointed ears elegantly framing his head
- a heart-shaped face and soft, full lips
- pale flawless skin, smooth and flawless skin
- long white hair, usually worn braided before the Fomori butchered it, now it's just over an inch long all over his head, short and ragged, short white hair that glitters like snow
- perfectly formed buttocks
- walks with lithe grace, his sinuous movements completely captivating.

Shandell's height and build are 5' 6" and 135 lbs.

---

## Talents and Abilities

confident and able in the water, an excellent swimmer, totally fearless even in deep water

led a much-feared Sidhe war band that decimated the Fomori for three years before he was ambushed

deadly with a skinning knife - skinned the Fomori that he killed

---

## Background

legitimate firstborn son of the Sidhe High King, and a Sidhe war god’s paladin

---

## Mannerisms

the little round “Oh” shape his mouth made whenever he was shocked or surprised, a tiny crease that appeared between his brows when he was worried about something

## Personality

optimistic nature
