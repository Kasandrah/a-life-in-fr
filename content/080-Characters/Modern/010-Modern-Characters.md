### Brasilia

_Cabral, Detective Inspector Pedro_ - a Brazilian Federal Police officer, first met when he was working on the disappearance of Rosa Montoyez.  DI Cabral is now Ambassador Bennett’s Brazilian Federal Police liaison officer. ^57ea6a

_Dunbain, Anna_ - (15), Leonardo’s daughter and attends the same exclusive girls' school as Rebecca Wallace.  The school is within walking distance of the ambassador’s residence. ^793f63

_Dunbain, Leonardo_ - is Managing Director of Dunbain Engineering, a civil construction company, and a business associate of Ambassador Wallace.  Dunbain used Wallace’s influence to win contracts for the construction of a US petroleum company’s Brasilia HQ and for the construction of a dam in the southwest of Brazil.  The dam contract was won from Butantan Construction, builders of the Plaza.  Another Dunbain Engineering director and his wife were recently killed in a road accident.  Five Dunbain Engineering directors (including Dunbain himself) remain. ^48931a

### Sao Paulo

_Amado, Inspector Jorge_ - a Brazilian Federal Police officer who assists Antonio and Angela from time to time.  He looks like a Latin "Columbo". ^6388f4

_[[Abdiel|Andrada da Silva, Antonio]]_

_Andrada da Silva, Milton_ - Antonio’s father and a Director of Butantan Construction, a very successful Brazilian construction and engineering company.  Milton keeps his son informed of the General’s doings in relation to Butantan Construction. ^b52f62

_The General_ - a Brazilian Army officer and managing director of Butantan Construction.  He is also associated with the Butantan Institute of Sao Paulo, which produces snakebite serum, the Social Democratic Party, and Brazil’s secret police. ^a00b29

The General arranged a road accident that killed a Dunbain Engineering director and his wife, and the subsequent kidnapping of the two young sons of two other directors.  The general was last seen in the tunnels at the dam site.  A bomb blast, actually a Sidewinder missile given to Antonio by Colonel Snow and launched from a ship off the coast, later destroyed his residence in Sao Paulo.
