Aletta is an Egyptian adventuress and thief with a liking for gemstones, hard currency, and jewellery.  She began her career as a circus trapeze artist and knife thrower, but graduated to “upper story work”.  Aletta has recently retired to Casablanca on the proceeds of her illegal activities, where she is living the high life in the French Quarter.

## Appearance

![[cc_aletta_and_viktor.jpg]]

**Aletta and Viktor**

Aletta is a small, exceptionally beautiful Egyptian woman with hip-length black hair, dark eastern eyes, and dark brown skin.  She wears Egyptian-style clothing in keeping with her professed Muslim beliefs, although the veils are sheer silks and the jewellery is exquisite and expensive.

## Manner

When Aletta moves, it is with the lithe grace of a dancer, accompanied by the tinkling of tiny golden bells around her ankles.  She is very agile and quick with excellent reflexes and uncanny hearing.

## Background

Aletta is the only female flier in her circus trapeze troupe and often performs a knife-throwing act which graphically illustrates her expertise with these weapons.  She is a consummate thief specialising in high-storey work with a taste for gemstones and jewellery.  Her calling card is the Queen of Diamonds playing card left at the scene of a crime and providing police with the only clue they will ever get.

The jewellery is always stolen from those who can afford it.  Most of the proceeds are given to missions for the sick and the poor, as well as to several orphanages.  The orphanages gain Aletta’s sympathy and support because she was orphaned at a young age and had to fend for herself on the streets of Cairo and Alexandria.

Aletta has been charged with grand larceny only once, when missing diamonds were found in her footlocker at the circus.  The evidence against her was too circumstantial, and she had an exceptionally good alibi, but the charge stood.  Aletta was subsequently approached by the British Secret Service who told her that the charge would be dropped if she agreed to do a job for them, a job they considered child’s play for the Queen of Diamonds.

Although Aletta did not admit to being that Queen, she did agree to do the job.  This involved stealing some documents from a safe on the third floor of the German Embassy.  Dangerous but not impossible, and as Aletta achieved this, she now has contacts in the British Secret Service.

## Education

Aletta has no formal education, but is very streetwise.
