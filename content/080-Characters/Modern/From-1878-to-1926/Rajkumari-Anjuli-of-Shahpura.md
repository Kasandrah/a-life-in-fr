Anjuli is an Anglicised Indian princess who is the oldest daughter of the Rajah of Shahpura and his British wife, the only child of an English Earl.  Anjuli is engaged to marry Sam Houston Krescke.

>**GM's Note:** Anjuli is one of my original player characters. I rolled a critical on my class roll; hence she is a member of the [Kshatriya](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kshatriya) (warrior) caste, as is her father Bharmal, Rajah of Shahpura.

## Appearance

Anjuli’s arresting looks throw back to her Russian grandmother.  Anjuli is tall, willowy and graceful and shows signs of her mixed blood in her bone structure, dusky skin colour, long gracious body lines, breadth of her slim square shoulders and curving hips, her narrow feet and slender ankles.  Her hands are firm, capable and square, not the traditionally delicate and slender hands of an Indian woman.

She has hip length, long silky black hair with a touch of copper, particularly noticeable in direct sunlight.  Her skin is the colour of warm ivory.  She has a small, square-jawed face with high-cheek bones and a firm chin, level broad brows and magnificent wide-spaced green eyes the colour of peat-water with little gold flecks in them and heavy lashes.

The tip of her nose is slightly tilted upward and she has a lovely generous mouth (too large for accepted standards of Indian beauty).  She always wears a caste mark between her brows and a small mother-of-pearl fish on a strand of silk around her neck as a good luck charm.  Just above the golden bangles on her left arm is a crescent shaped scar made by the teeth of a monkey when she was five years old.

## Interests

Anjuli plays chaupur and pachisi, and loves roses.  She and Sam also like hiking and climbing.  She learned to climb like a squirrel at an early age in pursuit of a troublesome pet monkey.

## Personality

Anjuli has been described by those who know her best and love her dearly as loving, faithful, passionate, courageous, forgiving, stubborn, grave and solemn at times, possessed of quiet fortitude, perceptive, quick witted, a good listener and wise beyond her years.

She is accustomed to work, dealing with servants, listening to complaints and settling disputes, supervising waiting women, ordering meals, cooking and sewing.  She is experienced in the plotting and intrigues of an Indian court and has learned to control her emotions and hold her tongue, as well as shouldering burdens without complaint.  One of the few traces of nerves or upset is her habit of biting her lower lip, particularly when she is lost for words.

## Education

Primary education at home (1907 to 1912).

Secondary school in England (1913 to 1918).

University in England (1919 to 1921) - degrees in Indian Languages (including literature) and Indian History (including society and culture, mythology, religion, art and architecture).

## Background

Anjuli is an Anglicised Indian princess who is the oldest daughter of Bharmal, Rajah of Shahpura, and his British wife, the lovely Catherine, Rani of Shahpura.

Bharmal is a Rajput autocrat of the Kshatriya caste who is very popular with his subjects.  In the reign of Sikander Lodi, a Rajput chieftain and adventurer came north from Delhi to defeat a robber chieftain and win a fortress.  The son of this 15th-century adventurer became the first Rajah of Shahpura.

Catherine is the only child of Baron Ormond, the 10th Earl of Carmarthen (Irish “East Munster”).  Baron Ormond’s Earldom was established in 1660 after the English Civil War (his ancestors were loyal to the King).

Catherine’s mother was the daughter of a Russian Cossack officer, a nobleman and adventurer named Sergi Vodvichenko.  Catherine is referred to as the Feringhi-Rani or “Foreign Queen”.  She met Bharmal while touring India.

Anjuli’s oldest brother Lalji (“the beloved”), eight years her senior and the son of the Rajah’s first wife who died in childbirth, was killed by a tiger while out hunting when Anjuli was 10 years old.  Lalji was married at the time of his death, but he had no children.

Her younger brother by one year is Nandu, who is now Yuveraj and will succeed his father to the throne.  Nandu is married with children.

Anjuli’s youngest surviving sibling is another brother, the 10-year-old Prince Jhoti.  Anjuli’s sister, Shushila, who was married but had no children, died recently under very mysterious circumstances.  It is forbidden to mention Shushila or refer to her in any way.
