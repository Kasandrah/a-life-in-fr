Sarah is an English architect with a passion for Egyptology and travel.  She is married to Charles van Buren, an adventurer and bootlegger from New England who owns the [[The-Silk-Scorpion|Silk Scorpion]], a 120’ long ocean-going ship with a crew of 25.

## Appearance

Sarah is a tall, slender woman with long legs, short straight blond hair in a bob, dark green eyes and a low, husky contralto voice that men find irresistible.  She is always beautifully dressed and has a flair for wearing clothes that suit her colouring, her flawless, lightly tanned skin and her great legs.

## Personality

Sarah has a gregarious nature and likes to laugh and have fun.  She is possessed of a wicked sense of humour and delights in joking, laughing and playing up to her husband Charles in public.  She is very much in love with and devoted to Charles.  Sarah exhibits a streak of high-spirited independence and has an ability to think for herself and make her own decisions.  Her relationship with Charles is based on a meeting of equals, and though sparks often fly, their love runs very deep.

Sarah is robust, has initiative and enterprise, exudes tremendous vitality, and craves excitement.  She is eager for new experiences and can face up to any challenge, believing it is up to her to create opportunities to get ahead.  She is impetuous and unrestrained and so enthusiastic about the current focus of attention that she can be quite single-minded.

Sarah possesses driving force, self-assurance and ambition, is courageous to the point of being foolhardy and always ready to take risks to obtain the objective.  A trailblazer, she will be the first to go where no one has dared go before.

Mentally, Sarah is imaginative and often brilliantly creative, but not deeply reflective (too impatient).  She is firm in her convictions and will argue fiercely to defend them; however; she is no match for people skilled in the art of debate.  Sarah is apt to jump to conclusions far too quickly and can display a lack of sound judgement.  However, she will not take too kindly to anyone who points this out.

Sarah has no guile and will speak her mind frankly and make her motives very clear.  She will not employ underhand methods to gain her ends.  Tact is not one of her strong points and she will probably be considered outspoken.

Her quick temper will cause Sarah to say more than she means, and she finds it difficult to apologise for this.  On the other hand, she will never bear a grudge or be vindictive.  Emotionally Sarah is very ardent, passionate in love and extremely loyal to people she loves and respects.  She has a very romantic streak and can fall in love on first sight, idealising the object of her affections while being possessive and a little domineering.

Sarah is somewhat accident prone, usually as a result of carelessness, haste or wilful disregard of safety precautions.  Travel has great appeal for her, as she loves the outdoors, especially warmth and fresh air.

Sarah can make her way ahead in a man’s world, as men like the masculine streak in her personality.  She can think like a man, though her feelings are those of a romantic, ardent woman.  When in love she will sacrifice all for romance, but she is a careerist by temperament and not very interested in domesticity.

She can be very jealous, but is very loyal, and even if a marriage turns out badly, she will not become involved with anyone else.  If the partnership is not working, she will make a clean break.

Sarah can be a good friend with other women.  As a parent, she will maintain strict discipline over her children, but give them plenty of encouragement to become self-reliant.  She will be especially proud of her sons but may find it difficult to get on with her daughters when they pass beyond childhood.

## Interests

Although Sarah is a qualified architect, she has an interest in Egyptology sparked by her father, a retired merchant banker with a lifelong interest in archaeology.  Sarah has developed skills in reading and writing Demotic, Hieroglyphics, Coptic and Hieratic, and is learning to speak, read and write Japanese from Charles.

She and Charles own the [[The-Silk-Scorpion|Silk Scorpion]], a 120-foot long luxury sloop with oak and brass fittings registered in London.  Charles made his money as a bootlegger and still uses the yacht for it’s original purpose.  They also own a red Mercedes-Benz SSKL two-seater roadster that they love to drive at breakneck pace along tight, winding roads.

They divide their time between homes in London, Boston, and Long Island, New York.  When not at home, they travel aboard the [[The-Silk-Scorpion|Silk Scorpion]], taking care of Charles’ many and varied business interests.

## Background

Sarah is English.  Her mother’s family are descendants of Cornish shipbuilders from Falmouth (surname of “Penrose”), and of landowners from eastern Cornwall (surname of “Heale”) who made their money in tin mining.  Sarah’s Cornish grandmother, Grania Heale, is a witch, a healer with prophetic powers.

“Penrose” - Cornish name meaning “top/end of the heath” local to places in Cornwall and Devon (mid and west Cornwall).

Sarah’s father’s family has a long history of association with the Royal Navy.  They come from Plymouth (surname of “Bateman”), and from Southampton and Portsmouth (surname of “Templar”).  The men of the family joined the Royal Navy, while the women married officers and sailors.

“Bateman” - occupational name meaning servant of Bate (“boatman” in Old English), in northern dialect form.  Also could be a nickname meaning “profit, gain and good husbandry”.  From Old English and Old Norse.

Arthur Bateman was one of the very few men of the family who did not join the Royal Navy, deciding instead that the world of banking was more attractive than a life at sea.  His father never forgave him.

In early February 1858 Thomas Bateman, an older brother of Sarah’s paternal grandfather Franklin Lloyd Bateman, dredged a Celtic shield up from the Thames at Battersea.  This shield - known as the Battersea Shield - now resides in the British Museum.  It was this incident that sparked Arthur’s interest in archaeology, which is now an interest of Sarah’s as well.
