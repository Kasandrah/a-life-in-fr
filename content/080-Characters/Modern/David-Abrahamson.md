# David Abrahamson

**Written by _[[Jenny-H|Jenny H.]]_**

David has been missing for some time.  In 1979 he stepped back in time as the result of an unusual explosion to 1925.  Here he lived until 1927 when he forward stepped, circumstances unknown, to 1938.  Here he lived until 1945 when he forward stepped through a gate to 1996.

In January 1945, while on a train to Buchenwald as a prisoner of the Nazis, David met Karl Ernst Krafft.  Krafft was the author of “A Treatise on Astro - Biology” which he wrote in 1938 and predicted the bomb attempt on Hitler.  Krafft had drawn up detailed horoscopes for Hitler, Hesse and Bormann.

After Hesse’s unexpected defection to England, Hitler embarked on a purge of self-proclaimed astrologers, Krafft included.  Now Krafft was dying, so he passed on to David his predictions for the future.  Krafft believed Bormann would set up a Fourth Reich in the future.  David tried to find out more, but Krafft died before he could finish telling David what would happen to the Fourth Reich.

On arrival at Buchenwald, David was assigned to assist Bormann, a magician, and his alchemist.  During this time David witnessed Bormann and his alchemist conducting Mythos experiments on prisoners, distilling elixirs from their bodies and turning them into horrible, deformed, dwarf-like creatures.  David also overheard Bormann’s plans to move a 20,000-man base into the future and observed experiments with magical gates through time and space.

_David also saw, unbeknownst to him, the vampire Gwyn while she was in a torpor from Bormann’s magic and experiments.  David thought she was just another dead body._

Later in 1945, news of the advancing American Army caused Bormann to flee through a specially constructed magical gate.  David jumped through the gate in pursuit after having stolen papers from Bormann’s briefcase.  The papers include plans for a new base and a copy of Hitler’s “Mein Kamph”, annotated with strange symbols.

In October 1996, David was discovered floating in the Gulf of Mexico by a US Navy corvette and rescued.  He was escorted to Washington and held in a military hospital because of the papers he was carrying (documents from Nazi Germany detailing Bormann’s plans for the future).  He has no memory of his time in the 1920s.
