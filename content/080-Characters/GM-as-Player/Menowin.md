# Menowin

![[gm_menowin.jpg]]

**Menowin**

A [Vistani](https://ravenloft.fandom.com/wiki/Vistani) fighter, grandson of [Madame Eva](https://ravenloft.fandom.com/wiki/Madam_Eva). Wielded the [Sun Sword](https://dnd-5e.fandom.com/wiki/Sunsword) against [Strahd von Zarovich](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Strahd_von_Zarovich).

>_"Being the son of a famous adventurer and the grandson of the most famous seer in Barovia are hard acts to follow. As I value family above all else, I will not disappoint my father, and especially not my grandmother, Madam Eva._
>
>_My family raised me to honour our bloodline, talents, lore, and traditions. I love my grandmother dearly and will do anything she asks with commitment and tenacity. This would be easier if she didn't speak in riddles, but that's part of her charm._
>
>_I know I am young in the eyes of my clan, but I have seen enough already to choose my path in this life. If we are to live without fear in Barovia, then the vampire Strahd von Zarovich must suffer true death. It is my solemn vow to make that happen."_
