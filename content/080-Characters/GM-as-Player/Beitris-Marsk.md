# Lady Beitris Marsk

![[mr_lady_beitris_marsk.jpeg]]

[_Lady Beitris Marsk_](https://www.dndbeyond.com/characters/75746890) _- inspiration from_ [_Arno_](https://www.zbrushcentral.com/u/arno/activity/portfolio) _on_ [_ZBrushCentral.com_](https://www.zbrushcentral.com/t/cleric-part-ii-standing-ready/434744)

A female variant human cleric from House Marsk with the Noble Scion Feat. Worships Lathander with passion (read as "eros"), is a card-carrying member of the Church of Sun and Storm because "someone needs to keep an eye on these sheep before they mobilise as religious zealots and start a holy war with our neighbours".

She won't say that out loud unless she knows she's with someone who shares her concern. She will be working to assess and evaluate the leaders of the Church and find out whether or not the main threat to Moonsea might be the Church of Sun and Storm. Who's in charge and how close are they with the Militarists and High Stormreaver Maxillar Bruel? She's a firm believer in the separation of church, state, and military as the pillar of a fair and free community.

Has friends in House Norster and works with them on their charity efforts because she's at heart an empathetic reformist. Note - small "r" reformist as she thinks the movers and shakers behind the Church of Sun and Storm are most probably Imperialists. She'll pretend she's one too in their circles.

## Moonsea Backgrounds - Young Reformer

Feature: Can You Hear the People Sing?

Once per day, you can call upon your co-conspirators to have "made arrangements" on your behalf, allowing you to attempt a History check in place of a single Deception, Intimidate, Sleight of Hand, or Intelligence/Wisdom (disarm trap) check. You may add double your proficiency bonus to this check. The DC of this check is equal to the DC of the skill check it replaces.

Whatever arrangements you make — for doors left unlocked, guards strong-armed out of your way, stolen keys left for you to find — must be reasonable to have anticipated and achieved beforehand; you could arrange to have a noble convinced you're a very important diplomat travelling in disguise, for example, but if a fight breaks out you could not rely on your network of contacts to feint for you in combat. You don't have to use this ability in advance; you can announce its use as you encounter a challenge, implying you foresaw this challenge and made arrangements prior to your arrival.

## Character Notes

Has a +2 when making reputation-based rolls, e.g., her charitable works.

Beitris has a collection of animal and human remains; one horse ear (from Mystra), one little finger each from five children (turned into spiders), one little finger from a woman murdered in the old keep, one ear each from two other slaughtered horses, finger bones from Harken Betany, and finger bones from Max.

Copies of "War for the Crown" game books

Average damage per dice roll:

*   Infuse Vitality - +1d4 = +2.5 (weapon strikes)
*   Divine Lance - 5d4 = 12.5
*   Disrupt Undead - 5d6 = 17.5
*   Holy Light - 5d6 = 17.5
*   Divine Wrath - 4d10 = 22    
*   Heal - 5d8 = 22.5
*   Holy Cascade - 3d6+6d6 = 10.5 + 21 = 31.5.
