# Alejandro Guerrero

![[gm_alejandro_guerrero.jpg]]

**Alejandro Guerrero**

A [Tabaxi](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Tabaxi) (feline humanoid); a Spanish conquistador in an Age of Lightning. He could easily go native if the colonisers get too heavy-handed with the original inhabitants.

## Character Sheet

**_[[gm_alejandro_guerrero.pdf|Alejandro Guerrero]]_, D&D 5E, [D&D Beyond](https://www.dndbeyond.com/characters/110803387/pMjCcO)**

### Special Equipment

Xiucoatl aka 'Flame' - Sentient Obsidian Itzopilli (Axe) - 1d8 Slashing

At Will: As you hit the target, it takes an extra 1d6 fire damage from the attack and begins burning. At the start of each of its turns, the target takes 1d6 fire damage and then makes a Constitution saving throw. On a failed save, the burning continues. On a successful save, the burning ends.

Proficiency Bonus times per day: Striking the ground, as an action, a line of molten earth 30 feet long and 5 feet wide emanates from you in a direction you choose. Each creature in the line must make a Dexterity saving throw (DC 15). A creature takes 3d8 fire damage on a failed save, or half as much damage on a successful one.

Once per Long Rest: As a bonus action, and lasting 1 minute, you channel billowing flames of Xiucoatl from your feet, granting you explosive speed. For the duration, your speed increases by 20 feet, and moving doesn't provoke opportunity attacks. When you move within 5 feet of a creature or an object that isn't being worn or carried, it takes 1d6 fire damage from your trail of heat. A creature or object can take this damage only once during a turn.

Xiucoatl is directing Alejandro to find the matching crystal axe, also sentient!
