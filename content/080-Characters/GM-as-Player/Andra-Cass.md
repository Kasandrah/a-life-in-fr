# Andra Cass

25-year-old human pirate with a cybernetic left arm, 5'10", short red hair, green eyes, pale skin with freckles.  Dresses in green, orange, and black. A tough, sceptical facade covers a superstitious nature. Doesn't like primitive cultures as she associates them with magic. Hates unpleasant smells and being dirty. Enjoys the notoriety of being a pirate. Hardy, doesn't take danger seriously, party animal. Looking for wealth and a good time.
