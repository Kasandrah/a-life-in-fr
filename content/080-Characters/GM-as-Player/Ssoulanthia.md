# Ssoulanthia

![[gm_ssoulanthia.jpeg]]

**Soulanthia and Green**

Ssoulanthia is a [Lizardfolk](https://2e.aonprd.com/Ancestries.aspx?ID=81) [Witch](https://2e.aonprd.com/Classes.aspx?ID=38) with a [Familiar](https://2e.aonprd.com/Rules.aspx?ID=2121) named Green who looks like a [Velociraptor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Velociraptor).

## Green

Green is Ssoulanthia's familiar; a magical creature provided to her by her mysterious and otherworldly patron, the [Whisper of Wings](https://2e.aonprd.com/Patrons.aspx?ID=21).

## GM's Background Questions

(1) Why is your PC coming to The Magaambya?

Her bloodline has a disproportionate number of magically talented individuals. Coming to the Magaambya is a family tradition.

(2) Why would your PC stay at the Magaambya after graduation and become a teacher?

“[Those who can do, do. Those who can’t do, teach.](https://medium.com/@strontiumz38/the-those-who-cant-do-teach-fallacy-8116b0e12de5) Those who can’t teach, consult.” A line from one of my previous consulting clients who wasn’t happy I was being paid 10 times what she was.

Ssoulanthia wants to stay at the school because she feels safe and supported there. The outside world is a scary place.

(3) What is the most difficult part of coming to the Magaambya? Or what do you fear most about this huge commitment?

Disappointing her parents.

(4) What is your PC’s view of man? Are people innately good? Are they broken but able to be guided? Are people innately selfish and not to be trusted?

Humans? Upstarts without wisdom. Like adolescents. No frontal lobe development.

(5) What excites your PC most about magical instruction? Power? Knowledge? Understanding? Emotional connection? Utilitarian goals? This might overlap with “why Magaambya.”

The opportunity to do her bloodline proud.

(6) What is your PC’s biggest flaw?

Lizardfolksplaining to non-lizardfolk.

(7) What kind of student will your PC be? Feel free to borrow from your own life experiences.

Working so hard, she has a massive breakdown. She’s terrified of letting her parents and extended family down. She’ll spend hours and hours studying to achieve near-perfect results.

(8) Use one word to describe the student/teacher your PC would be.

A grinder as a student, but kind to others and offers personal support once she’s a teacher.

(9) What secret does your PC hope none of her classmates will discover?

The reason her bloodline is atypically magically talented is that they have from time to time interbred with powerful human sorcerers. Her bloodline has developed some physical characteristics from its unions with humans. Ssoulanthia has hair growing on her head and four fingers on her left hand. Physical abnormalities among normal lizardfolk.

> **Player's Note:** After a string of near-lethal encounters and a sexist insult from Dean Ot, Ssoulanthia subbed out in favour of her brother. He, in her opinion, was better able to protect her companions as long as they remained at the Magaambya.
