# Ciannia

One of my favourite PCs. Priestess of Mund. Part of a D&D campaign run by [[Craig-B|Craig B.]] in which the party became vampires while wandering about the Continent far from home.

Her party included:
 - _Drost_ - halfling ([[Michael-H|Michael H.]]) ^46e8c5
 - _Trennis_ - wizard ([[Simon-M|Simon M.]]) ^863903
 - _Flan_ - ranger ([[Ben-G|Ben G.]]). ^bbaf47

## Appearance

![[gm_ciannia_full.jpg]]

**Ciannia, Pictish Priestess of Mund**

A female halfling in early adulthood. Waist-length thin braids with beads and feathers. Close-fitting light leather shirt and pants. Studded leather armour.
