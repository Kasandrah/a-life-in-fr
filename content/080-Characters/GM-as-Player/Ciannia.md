# Ciannia

One of my favourite PCs. Priestess of Mund. Part of a D&D campaign run by _[[Craig-B|Craig B.]]_ in which the party became vampires while wandering about the Continent far from home.

Her party included:
 - _Drost_ - halfling (_[[Michael-H|Michael H.]]_) ^46e8c5
 - _Trennis_ - wizard (_[[Simon-M|Simon M.]]_) ^863903
 - _Flan_ - ranger (_[[Ben-G|Ben G.]]_). ^bbaf47

## Appearance

![[gm_ciannia_full.jpg]]

**Ciannia, Pictish Priestess of Mund**

A female halfling in early adulthood. Waist-length thin braids with beads and feathers. Close-fitting light leather shirt and pants. Studded leather armour.
