# Hrithik Olafsson

![[gm_hrithik.jpeg]]

**Hrithik Olafsson**

Hrithik is a male Aasimar with shiny feathers instead of hair, shimmering glossy black wings, taloned fingers and toes, and amber eyes. His father is the angel Shahrukh, a noble and impetuous bird-like member of Alteria's heavenly host, making Hrithik a half-celestial.

Shahrukh's many offspring are recognisable for their black wings and body feathers, hence they are known as "[Plumekith](https://2e.aonprd.com/Feats.aspx?ID=2285)". They are all beautiful medium height lightly built half-celestials with smooth dark skin and an exotic, androgynous appearance. Although every male Plumekith has a burst of brightly coloured feathers around his face, clueless humans still mistake Hrithik for a female. He enjoys the shocked look on their faces when he speaks. Hrithik has a melodic voice, but his tone is firmly in the tenor range.

Hrithik wears black studded leather armour, a deep red quilted gambeson, and a white linen shirt. These are all tailored with enough space around his wings so that he can fly unimpaired. He also wears matching black studded leather pants with a covered groin guard big enough to give observant onlookers another hint he is a male Plumekith. He wears a pair of black leather knee-high boots with embossing around the top.

He carries a light crossbow, a morningstar, darts (small javelins the size of large arrows, with weighted heads), a sling with a bag of slingstones, and a dagger. He is right-handed. His overall appearance is more that of a winged ranger than an armoured cleric, as he can only fly if he is lightly armoured and unencumbered.

Hrithik always has a small, fresh-picked flower tucked between his armour and gambeson on the left side of his chest. He refuses to say why. Rumours abound that these flowers are gifts from his many lovers, as Plumekith are renowned for obeying Shahrukh's "go forth and multiply" edict with great enthusiasm. Hrithik never confirms or denies these rumours. Close acquaintances know he is not irresponsibly promiscuous, unlike many of his Plumekith half-siblings. There must be another reason for the flowers.

One final notable aspect of Hrithik's appearance is his rare smile - like a burst of sunshine.

## Family

### Blossom and Olaf

Hrithik is 20 years old. He is the eldest child of his mother, Blossom, and his stepfather, Olaf, an innkeeper in the village where Hrithik grew up. Blossom and Olaf, both human, have five other children together, all boys, ranging from 10 to 18 years old. Hrithik's two eldest half-brothers are independent of their parents but live and work in the village.

After a wild and rebellious youth during which Blossom had a short but passionate relationship with Shahrukh and conceived Hrithik, she settled down with her solid and reliable husband Olaf to become a pillar of her village community. Hrithik loves his mother dearly but still chafes under her frequent attempts to "clip his wings" and turn him into a responsible adult. Blossom adores her "beautiful boy" but has accepted Hrithik's need to strike out on his own and his subsequent close relationship with his father Shahrukh.

Hrithik's stepfather Olaf is a tolerant soul who took on the responsibility of raising his stepson with compassionate discipline and admirable consistency. Olaf instilled in Hrithik self-discipline, tolerance, and a solid work ethic. Hrithik is far less wayward than many of his Plumekith half-siblings. Olaf also taught Hrithik how to brew alcohol, specifically beer and single malt whiskey, so Hrithik would be able to make his way in life without relying on handouts.

Despite Hrithik's agreeable home situation, his relations with the rest of the village were marred by the status conferred on him as a half-celestial. Hrithik was held responsible for successful harvests and crop failures, for fair weather and foul, for feast and famine, in short, all manner of events outside of his control. This led Hrithik to leave his village at 15 and seek out his father.

### Shahrukh

![[gm_hrithik_shahrukh.jpg]]

**Shahrukh**

Hrithik came across a small community of Plumekith in an aerie in the mountains not too far from his village. Here he met his father, Shahrukh, for the first time. Won over by Shahrukh's genuine charm and attention, Hrithik dedicated his life in service to his father and became one of his clerics. Hrithik has a great deal of love for Shahrukh and serves him with total commitment and sincerity. Shahrukh is the source of Hrithik's divine power and his hatred of snakes and snake-like creatures, e.g., nagas.

Shahrukh has increased his influence on Alteria by fathering many half-celestial Plumekith among his beloved mortals; hence Hrithik has many half-siblings. He expects his Plumekith sons and daughters to procreate with mortals as well, including Hrithik. If Hrithik chose not to do so, he would be in breach of one of his father's commandments and would lose all divine power as a punishment.

While Hrithik has strong reservations about this commandment, he has met his responsibilities to Shahrukh. Hrithik has two young children, a boy and a girl, by two different mothers. Both children are Aasimar with Plumekith characteristics, but neither has wings. The boy is two-years-old, and the girl is one-year-old.

Hrithik vetted both sets of potential parents extensively before agreeing to make each woman pregnant. He wants all his children to be raised in good and loving homes by equally attentive parents - i.e., to have the love and support he has from his parents, Blossom and Olaf. Even with that level of love and support, mortal expectations, good and bad, can buffet severely the life of an Aasimar in Alteria. Hrithik wants his children raised with love so they can be confident, compassionate, and resilient when they venture out into the world.

Currently, Hrithik is keeping an eye out for his third mother-to-be. He must do this to maintain his standing as a Cleric of Shahrukh, but Hrithik is meeting this commandment on his own terms. He is looking for a principled, well-off couple who want children, could not conceive a child yet and might like an Aasimar force for good in their family.

## Evelyn

Hrithik’s third conquest

Evelyn is the middle-aged, successful aunt who never found the right man to start a family with, especially since she has been preoccupied with her career for most of her life. She came from the mainland shortly after Whitehall was established and while the war was winding down to assist in high-level administration, such as the distribution of resources, workers, and the acquisition and transportation of goods and soldiers from the mainland.

Later she assisted in the expansion of Whitehall and helped design some buildings as an architect, including the temple to DaVinci (angel of knowledge) and the magnificent library of Whitehall. This work ensured that she earned a significant amount of money, and she has effectively retired early. She likes to spend her time reading or relaxing in the gardens, and she often visits high-class taverns to mingle and hear wonderful stories and rumours of this wonderful, unexplored land. She also goes to every public hearing and keeps up to date with all the local politics. She is a follower of DaVinci and believes that knowledge and understanding of our world and all that's in it is the most important thing for a society to develop.

Evelyn is the only one of her family to come to Whitehall so far, although one of her favourite nephews has been considering joining her to see the new land. Evelyn’s parents are both alive and well and keep in regular contact, mostly to tell her to visit home and make sure she’s doing okay, but rarely ask about her love life as they don’t expect to hear much in that regard. She has an older brother who fought in the war with the savage orcs on the mainland and has a beautiful wife and a son who is training to join the army himself. She also has a younger sister who has a husband, two daughters and a son.

### Garuda Blooded (Plumekith) - Pathfinder 1

Garudas are noble but impetuous birdlike celestials, and most Garuda-blooded Aasimars grow graceful feathers during puberty. The majority of these Aasimars have shimmering wings; the wings can be of virtually any shade, ranging from metallic colours to muted hues to pure white, or rarely, glossy black. Plumekith are sometimes born with taloned fingers or toes, and occasionally amber eyes like those of an eagle. Their avian features make Plumekith instantly recognizable.

Like their celestial ancestors, Plumekith tend to act on the spur of the moment when their abilities seem needed. Plumekith rush into the heat of battle to face off against evildoers, only stopping to ask questions afterward. Though they can act rashly, Plumekith never shirk their duty after making a commitment.

Plumekith often have an extreme hatred for nagas and other snakelike creatures, a racial disdain no doubt stemming back to their Garuda forebears.

### Half-Celestial - D&D 3.5e

“Half-celestial” is an inherited or acquired template that can be added to any living, corporeal creature with an Intelligence score of 4 or more and non-evil alignment. A half-celestial creature retains the base creature’s statistics and special abilities except as noted here.

#### Challenge Rating

Hit Dice 5 or less, as base creature +1.

Hit Dice 6 to 10, as base creature +2.

Hit Dice 11 or more, as base creature +3.

#### Alignment

Any Good.

#### Type

The creature’s type changes to outsider (native). Hit Dice, BAB, and saves are not recalculated.

#### Armor Class

Natural armour improves by **+1**.

#### Defences/Qualities

Half-celestials gain:

 - dark vision 60 ft
 - immunity to disease
 - +4 racial bonus on fortitude saves vs. poison
 - acid, cold, and electricity resistance of 10
 - Damage Reduction (DR) 5/magic (if Hit Dice 11 or less) or 10/magic (if Hit Dice 12 or more)
 - Spell Resistance (SR) equal to Hit Dice + 10 (maximum 35).

#### Base Speed

Unless the base creature flies better, the half-celestial flies at twice the base creature’s land speed (with good manoeuvrability).

#### Special Abilities

##### Daylight

Half-celestials can use a Daylight (Sp) effect (as the spell) at will.

##### Smite Evil

### Religion

Religion is a pretty big thing in the world of Alteria; angels and demons (also called deities) regularly interfere with mortals and are the closest thing to gods that are accepted in the world. There are also mysterious elder beings that exist in the beyond (the void or soup that all other planes exist within) that have a connection to warlocks; however, they tend to be forgotten unless one is a warlock themselves.

There are far more angels than demons because angels are good and demons are not, and they cannot enter the material plane without help from someone who already exists there. Not to say that demons aren't around, but they tend to be defeated quickly if they cause a ruckus, so the only ones who stick around are the sneaky demons who make deals and manipulate behind the scenes (generally).

There are many, many, many deities and many, many religions worshipping them, so finding a religion that suits you is very easy. Typically, deities see themselves as superior, and while many like being worshipped, they won't do anything for people unless said person does something for them. This means that resurrection in Alteria can be difficult, and without a connection to one of these deities or a reason for them to revive you, there is no way to come back. There are rumours of alternative ways to revive oneself without help or to become immortal, of course, but typically if you die, you are dead.

Warlocks get their power from mysterious being in the beyond, and nobody knows why. Deities grant power to people (clerics or evil clerics respectively) either so that person can work for them, because they just felt like it, or possibly because of a genetic relation. People do breed with these creatures (Aasimar for instance are directly related to angels) however, the offspring do not gain superpowers, although they may become clerics who are granted power by the deity that is attempting to aid its offspring.

NOTE: Interactions between characters related to religion is treated basically the same in the real world, but instead of a few major ones, you'll get many religions, usually with a primary one for each town. The deities do little for people in the same way gods in the real world don't.

### Alteria and the Planes

Alteria is set in magical fantasy world and treated like a medieval era but with magic, which means less of the real world medieval problems like plague. Alteria is the planet you live on and while you inhabit a single continent you'll be able to spread out later into the campaign and explore the rest of the world, other worlds and even other planes.

Interplanetary travel is not known in the general civilised world but you never know what you might find on your adventures. The three primary planes are the material plane (the one you live in), heaven and hell (where the angels and demons come from). There are many other minor planes that are connected to the material plane and can be travelled to easily such as the elemental planes of fire, water, air and earth. 

### Races

In terms of race relations, some are monster races and may not be well liked in civilised society but nothing is inherently evil. Goblins and orcs which are usually monsters can live with humans (some happily and some not so much). Which races like which depends on history between those races and general racism and a clash of beliefs or personalities. Races a typical personality traits which you can find in the handbooks and some races will tend to be more evil then others simply because of there circumstances (eg dark dwarves usually live in the underdark and are generally evil because some an environment does not breed happiness and friendliness).

NOTE: Information on the races and how they will typically behave can be found in the handbooks and other materials i will post in group chat but in the end, they are guidelines and you can make your character react to people and have any personality you like. There's nothing wrong with a goliath that loves to dish out crushing bears hugs to everyone he meets.

### The Town You Live In (Kherkrub)

What started as a small orc settlement has evolved into a bustling city around the mercenery guild, run by a famous group of adventurers who set up the guild after the loss of there leader. The mercenary guild brought many merchants who provide weaponry and supplies to the adventurers that work in the guild and that in turn has brought in demand for new homes and luxuries to accomodate, not only the resident merchants, but also the adventurers when they return from their quests. These days there is a much larger population in Khergrub with a mix of orc, goblin, human, gnome and many others.

### The Mercenary Guild

The mercenary guild provides the majority of income for Khergrub. They loot and pillage dungeons for artifacts, do escort missions for people in dangerous territory and basically anything else you can pay them for. As members of the guild you and your team have worked together for the past three years in this guild doing whatever jobs you can get. The deal is you are given contacts and jobs by the owners of the guild and they send you all over the continent to do whatever jobs need doing.

You are paid for completing a mission (depending on how dangerous it is and how long it will take), in exchange the owners also get a cut of whatever loot you may find (if there is loot to be found). There are severe penalties for not handing in all loot that is due to the admissions board including the subsequent beating to death by the guild head, Korg the barbarian goliath.

Mercenaries who cannot afford lodgings due to lack of jobs are also provided lodgings on site, however they are very low quality.

### The Gallant Band

The greatest adventuring party ever known.

Mendel began his career as a soldier and quickly became a commander in the elven empires armies. He was hired by Eldwyn Whitehall to assist in the colonisation of a new continent. His best friend Edwin Cohen and he led his regiment for the purposes of opposing the "wrongful culling and subjugation" of the native species whom they had learned were not monsters like in their home continent but good people. After a brutal counter attack on an outlier village Mendel came to rescue a small girl named Leliana. He left Leliana with a trusted friend to raise but came back often to visit her and see how she was doing.

Many years later their relationship developed and they fell in love. Later Still Mendel mended hostilities between a goliath tribe in the mountains by defeating their leader in one on one combat. Gaal (the tribe leader son) saw this as proof of his great strength and decided to follow Mendel. After years of fighting for equal rights and to mend hosilities between the natives and the invading elven nation, Mendel and Edwin succeeded and were both knighted as a result.

Afterward Gaal, Leliana and Edwin; led by Mendel, began life as an adventuring group. They helped people move further afield in Eldtyssan, defeat demons and monsters and explored ancient ruins. Eventually Mendel was killed in a fight with a particularly powerful demon and could not be revived, so the group disbanded until 2 years later when they opened the mercenary guild. Today they are the most successful and famous adventuring group in history and are known all trhoughout Eldtyssan.

#### Sir Mendel Gallant

Lawful good elf paladin (leader)

Sir Mendel Gallant was a natural leader, always doing what he could to help as many people as possible, with a natural charm and intelligence that drew people to him. Years of experience quelling evil monsters and leading armies into battle on his home continent made him a formidable warrior as well but he always made sure that when he fought someone he would first try to subdue, rather than kill. Mendel was a Paladin who worshipped the angel "Solemn", the angel of sanctuary and protection of the helpless. He led his life doing good deeds and helping the helpess, first by protecting people in war, then against the subjugation of the natives of Eldtyssan, and finally as a life long adventurer. He died at a relatively young age for a half elf, slain by a demon when investigating rumors of an orcish cult. He was not revivable and so that was his final adventure.

#### Leliana

Chaotic neutral human sorceror

Leliana was found by Mendel after a brutal orc counter attack and for the majority of her childhood was quiet and withdrawn. After witnessing the death of everyone she knew at the age of five she suffered trauma and many thought she would not recover. Mendel was able to break her out of her stupor with his regular visits and charm and by the time she was ten she was almost a normal human girl. The only problem then was she had developed a dependence on Mendel. People often said that she was a different person around Mendel, opening up more and generally becoming happier, and she grew to love him.

Leliana is a sorcerer who is intelligent and charming, if a bit odd. She is a very nice person to be around and her need to care for others is reflected in Mendel himself. When Mendel died she attempted to get him resurrected but could not find anyone who could or would do so. When she could not revive Mendel, Leliana fell into a deep depression and retreated out of the public spotlight until two years later when she announced the construction of the mercenary guild. She got the remaining members together and they agreed to run the guild in Mendel's name. She has never been the same however and her demeanour is dulled and she is more reserved then she used to be. It is known that she still doesn't respond well when someone mentions Mendel around her.

#### Sir Edwin Cohen

 Chaotic good half elf rogue
 
Sir Edwin Cohen is a dashing rogue in every sense of the word. He is charming, clever and good with his hands. He is good natured and, like his best friend, is happy to help all those who need it. He will often use his stealthy rogue skills to steal from the corrupt and give to the poor and thinks himself a hero of the poeple. He is also a very promiscuous gay man and was known to try to get Mendel into bed before he met Leliana, although it never worked.

These days he likes to do occasional adventuring with Gaal and act as a shadow administrator/mentor to the members of the guild. He has gotten into trouble with Leliana a few times for "welcoming" near members of the guild somewhat inappropriately.

#### Gaal

Chaotic neutral Goliath Barbarian

Gaal chose to join Mendel when Mendel defeated his father in combat. Until that point Gaal had seen his father to be the strongest creature alive and so followed him. When Gaal's father was defeated he decided that the whole tribe was actually very weak and decided to follow Mendel hoping that he could learn some of his power or at least be powerful by being associated with Mendel.

Gaal is not very smart. He doesn't care much about others so long as they don't get in his way and he respects strength above all else. He followed Mendel because of his great strength and was happy to take some of the treasure they found while adventuring with him. Mendel would not accept bad behaviour while he was leader and so Gaal has learned to not rage unless necessary. He is now the official head of the mercenary guild, mostly he intimidates members into paying their share of treasure found but he also goes adventuring on occasion with Edwin.

### Angels and Demons

#### Aduun

Angel of Innovation and Invention.

Aduun used to be a master smith in the angelic army, making powerful artifacts to be used in the never-ending conflict with the demons. Long ago when humanity first developed and was said to use tools Aduun become intrigued, previously thinking that creation was impossible from the inferior creatures who lived there. He began keeping in contact with the scholars as they continued to study life in the material plane. As they began to develop, the angels established contact and he used his high position among the angels to give him leave from the fight and go to the material plane using a dwarf (Durin Bola) as a link.

Aduun believes that creation is not only the most important aspect of any beings existence but also the most beautiful. While he still considers them inferior as a whole Aduun has learned to respect certain individuals with a talent for craft. Aduun monitors and encourages innovation and will even train some individuals personally. It is well known that Aduun see's potential in mortals and feels they can be used against the demons themselves, to him they are another tool to be crafted and harnessed. His superior mindset does not deter mortals from worshipping him however, as he actively assists and and bestows gifts upon those he favours, he is a very popular angel. He is particularly liked by Gnomes and Dwarves.

#### Pizzik

Deals and Pacts OR Selflessness and Giving

Pizzik is a well known but not very well-liked, cherub like angel. He is very weak as far as angels go but craves the attention and worship that his stronger kin receive from mortals. Because of his lower power and status as an angel very few people choose to worship him, so he has made a business in using his angelic powers to resurrect anybody who will worship and spread his good name through good deeds.

His unique method for doing this means that Pizzik can retract this gift at any time, instantly killing the recipient in the process. He has done this a number of times to people who have tried to get resurrected and run, or to those he feels are not upholding their end of the deal to generate worshippers for him. This is why he is known as the angel of pacts and deals by most people.

Pizzik wont do anything unless he thinks he can get something from it and those he resurrects must make a warlock pact with him to serve. Most followers of Pizzik work as mercenaries or priests in small temples to Pizzik, they are often seen preaching the greatness of Pizzik in taverns or in market squares to try and get more followers but this doesn’t usually generate more followers because most people are aware of Pizzik and consider his followers a nuisance.

Pizzik is especially hated by the angel Solemn due to their conflicting views of the divinity of immortality and followers of Solemn will never assist followers of Pizzik and vice versa.

#### Shahrukh

God of Agriculture and Propagation

Shahrukh is an angel who's bird like features are more prominent than most other angels. He is a farmer in the realm of heaven and as such is worshipped by farming communities who believe he can grant them a greater crop each year. He lives almost permanently in the realm of heaven, only visiting the material plane when he deems it necessary. The times he deems necessary being when he wants to "tend his crop" and "sow the seeds".

Shahrukh is a charismatic angel who enjoys the company of mortals and wishes to protect and help them as any angel would. Shahrukh's best method for protecting those living on the material plane is to spread his angelic influence and gain a foothold in the material plane, which he believes is best done by leaving behind his children. When Shahrukh first visited the material plane, he approached some mountain communities, partially because they were most willing to accept help with growing crops and partially because he favours those kinds of environments. His time was spent teaching and "plowing the fields," and when he left, he left many women pregnant.

The offspring of Shahrukh are known as Garuda. They resemble large bird-like humanoids who are incredibly powerful and agile, as they retain some god-like power of Shahrukh by being direct descendants. The Garuda continue to propagate and reproduce as their father did and spread his word and influence as their religion. The offspring of the garuda are encouraged to go out into the world.

Shahrukh's farm is often attacked in heaven by a stealthy species of snake-like demons, whose job it is to cripple the production of heaven in an attempt to weaken their soldiers in the never-ending war. These snake demons have many abilities suited to guerrilla warfare such as chameleon like invisibility which makes it very difficult to stop them from burning down his fields. Shahrukh hates these demons and anything that resembles them, including snakes of the material plane and this hatred is shared by all his progeny.

#### Solemn

Angel of Sanctuary and Protecting the Helpless

Solemn is an old angel, one of tradition. He believes that all angels are superior to mortals and should be treated as such; however, this doesn't mean he dislikes mortals. Solemn thinks of them as helpless puppies that need protection from the evil influences of demons, the world they live in and themselves. He lives permanently in heaven; however, solemn gains knight followers whenever he can who can protect the weak in his name.

Solemn values mortal lives higher than that of angels in the sense that they only get one. Angels are immortal and will always return to heaven eventually, but mortals are fragile, and it is more important that they be protected as a result. Solemn hates resurrection magic, however, seeing it as an encroachment upon the divine right of immortality which should belong to angels, as such he will deny resurrection of any mortal, even his knights.

#### Souloth

Beast Angel of Doors and Energy

Souloth is an incredibly powerful angel with the approximate intelligence of an animal. It has a particularly frightening visage; it is a floating lump of flesh, eyes, wings, and rings that all rotate and pulsate. It has the ability to create mass warp fields to any dimension (including beyond the angelic and hellish realms) without invitation. Its motives are unknown, but the angel seems to have taken a liking to a group of changelings who lived in the shifting planes, and it protects them and provides them with safe passage to other dimensions, including the material plane where they now reside.

Souloth appears to be a benevolent creature that responds positively to displays of change; such as a pool of water forming, changing weather, plants and animals growing, or shapeshifters changing their shapes. It responds negatively to displays of aggression and will instantly banish anybody it sees displaying an aggression toward others. As a result, all remaining members of the village are good-aligned and vegetarian.

When Souloth first appeared to the shapeshifter village, it appeared above the square and caused a great deal of distress before vanishing again shortly after. Some members of the community believed it a previously unknown inhabitant of the shifting planes that might be stalking them and began preparing for a fight. When Souloth returned, however, it would always just float in the distance and disappear again when people approached. Eventually Souloth began to be a regular sight and people began to ignore it.

Some of the younger shapeshifters, curious and bold, tried getting close by changing their forms into regular animals that it had been known to allow within close proximity in order to talk with it. While it didn’t speak it did pulsate and move in ways that seemed excited when the young ones changed back into their original forms to talk and it didn’t teleport away.

Eventually Souloth moved over the village again, quietly watching until one day a large creature moved too close to the village and attempted to attack, only to suddenly disappear just before it could reach any of the shapeshifters. Now it is a guardian over the village but Souloth also appears to banish members of the village if they attempt to harm one another as well.

#### Sylantra

Angel of Prosperity

Sylantra is an angel with a grand ego and her love of herself means she feels she should be worshipped by lesser beings as well. Although she believes she is entitled to worship she also understands that mortals may disagree or have fragile egos that prevent them from worshipping anyone. As a result, she doesn’t fret over those who don’t worship her and chooses instead to focus on the positive attention she gets from her followers.

She loves and appreciates her followers and rewards them with power and insight as thanks for their faithful service. While she doesn’t play favourites she does have a disproportionately large following among the elves (especially the nobility) and her influence has helped the elf nation achieve its current greatness.

#### Tenebris

The Demon King

Tenebris is the original and most powerful of the demons and used to rule over them with the casual disinterest one might take to ants going about their day. Occasionally a particularly foolish demon would challenge him and learn not to do so again. Tenebris was so powerful that he probably could have been the driving force in a demonic victory over the angels if he cared enough to fight in the war, however, Tenebris felt it would be too easy and would just drain his life of the little drama it had.

Instead the only thing that could spark some interest from Tenebris was the question of how they had all come to exist and what the elder beings had to do with it. Tenebris was aware that the elder beings that live in the void were around before he was and probably before any other living creature and suspected they created everything. The question of why and how is what drove him to try to reach out to them and maybe try to understand how to create universes under their tutelage. He was already the most powerful demon but wanted to see if he could become as powerful as the elder beings.

Tenebris fathered a child (Asmodeus) to rule hell for him and disappeared one day and hasn’t been seen since. Some believe he died trying to get to the elder beings or by their hand. Others think he managed to gain tutelage with them after all and await his glorious return. Whatever the case nobody really knows what happened to him and its been so long since it happened that most have very little to say on the matter.


#### Asmodeus

Prince of Hell

Asmodeus is the prince of Hell and one of the most famous demons in the material plane because he was the first demon to make it there and could spread his influence without angels stopping him for a short while. Asmodeus is one of the most powerful demons in hell and has his eyes on ruling everything: hell, the planes, even heaven one day.

He managed to gain dominion over the material plane and establish a temporary kingdom before the angels showed up, breeding with various mortals to make master races to serve as his enforcers. Tieflings made from humans and kobolds made from goblins are the most common ancestors in the material plane today. These creatures retain the demonic look from their ancient lord but they do not retain the demonic personality of Asmodeus. This doesn’t stop others from being suspicious of these two races and the unfair treatment of members of these races does lead them to be evil aligned more so than other races, perpetuating the stereotype.

Asmodeus may have lost control of the material plane once but he is patient and always scheming and spreading his influence. His main goal is to find a way to kill off angels permanently so he can have free reign of all the known dimensions and has powerful demons researching ways of doing this for him. In the mean time Asmodeus seems content to live in luxury and rule over all demons, at least until his father Tenebris returns.

#### Lloth

Queen of Spiders

Lloth is Queen of the Demonweb Pits within the Abyss (hell), and a high-level demon who has many followers within drow communities. She is known as the dark mother, lady of chaos and mother of lusts. She and her followers rule over drow communites with promises of power for those who follow and endless misery for those who refuse.

A long time ago, she infiltrated the material plane and sowed seeds of distrust and hatred between different elf communities, causing a civil war which splintered the elven societies and forced the drow to escape and take refuge in the underdark. Such an act did not go unnoticed by the angels, and she was chased out of the material plane, and the drow were "liberated".

Lloths influence is not gone; however, by creating links with her greatest followers, she can control the drow people from the abyss. Scheming to slowly conquer and convert more to worshipping her and one day push the angels out of the material plane, which she will claim for herself.

### Timeline of Alteria

NOTE: AC in this case simply means "after colonisation". Civilised people and societies have existed for a long time, including on Eldtyssan; however, the campaign will not take place anywhere except this continent for a long time. I have focused on events after this continents colonisation to keep it simple.

BC-0AC - Dwarves, gnomes, and various monster races live on home continent in relative peace.

0AC - The elf/human nation landed on the shores of the home continent for the purposes of exploration and expansion of their empire. Led by Eldwyn Whitehall, the forerunners established encampments along the shore before expanding inland to find a permanent settlement area. They named the continent Eldtyssan.

0AC-4AC - Colonists come into contact with local monster races and begin fighting/culling them. The local races are less advanced then the invaders and do not put up much of a fight as a whole, leading to civil unrest regarding the "massacre" of indigenous (and mostly friendly) races. 
Some tribes strike back leading to the deaths of some civilian colonists (including Leliana's entire family), fueling both sides of the conflict. Eldwyn arguing it shows the natives cruelty and barbarianism while others argue it is a response from the real barbarians.

4AC-6AC - Dissenters, lead by Mendel Gallant and Edwin Cohen fight against the "slaughter" of the natives and begin negotiations with both sides, attempting to show that war is not nessecary. Using his charisma and levelheadedness Mendel convinces the two sides to parley which goes a long way to halting agressions between sides.

6AC-10AC - Negotiations are finally successful and the native races have been accepted into the elf nation as allies, providing information and skill unknown to the elves and in return being granted land and protecion under law.

8AC-13AC - Mendel and Edwin begin trying to quell hostile tribes and races with diplomacy or intimidation, however they do have a large regiment of soldiers at their disposal if need be. 
A particularly troublesome group of goliaths, whom are holed up in the mountains, are subjugated when Mendel defeats their leader in one on one combat. The tribe leaders son (Gaal) sees this as a sign of Mendels strength and the tribes weakness and leaves to follow Mendel.

14AC - Mendel and Edwin are knighted for their good works and efforts in uniting the natives and the elf nation (also as a show of good faith for the monster race citizens).

15AC-40AC - After a short rest a party of 4 adventurers (Mendel, Edwin, Gaal and Leliana) spend a long career helping the helpless and doing good deeds.

40AC -  The group investigates rumors of a orc village named Kherkrub fratenizing with demons for power and combat with said demon results in the death of Mendel. The Orc village is destroyed and the land is granted to the party as payment for dealing with the threat.

40AC-42AC - Unable to ressurect Mendel, Leliana falls into a depression and becomes a shut in for the next two years, rarely seen by anybody. Edwin and Gaal continue adventuring but are eventually called back when Leliana proposes the contruction of a mercenary guild. The intention of the guild is to give normal poeple the connections and opportunities to help others as adventuring groups to honor Mendel and his lifes goals.

42AC- 52AC - The Mercenary Guild acts as the base for the town of Kherkrub to build up around it. Drawing in adventurers, then merchants and craftmen, builders and taverns and other services, all in support of the growing adventuring community. Kherkrub is now a significant city, drawing in wealth from anyone who can pay for the services of the mercenary guild and also from the riches found in various tombs and dungeons.

### Mog'Durin

Mog'Durin is the dwarf capital, named after Durin Bolas, the first king of the dwarves, who was appointed such by Aduun for his skill in invention. Since that time, the powerful have been made by their ability to invent fantastical contraptions and were called makers. An annual exhibition of all the newest inventions is held within the primary temple to Aduun, with Aduun himself judging and distributing titles and rewards for those that are particularly impressive. Mog' Durin is a high-tech city full of mostly dwarves, many halflings and a smattering of many other races (particularly those who are used to low-light environments).

The city is broken up into terraced districts. The crafts district is where new makers are trained and where craftsmen work. The trade district is where the fantastic inventions of the dwarves are sold for substantial profit and trade with the outside world takes place. The wealthy district is where the successful business dwarves and makers reside, living above all others in Mog'Durin. Finally the slums are where the poor reside, those who weren't skilled craftsmen or were uninterested in making. The slums are where those who do the menial labour reside, such as security guards, miners, maintenance, etc and are often looked down upon by the wealthy class.

The current King of the dwarves is Umafiel Bardator, appointed by Aduun very recently for a yet unknown invention. Since his appointment, he has not left his palace, nor has he let anyone, except Aduun himself, in. This antisocial behaviour has resulted in some of the wealthy class speaking out against him and questioning Aduun, led by Dorlan Muffast, the previous King.

Mog'Durin is a centre of technological wonder and a mercantile powerhouse on the continent of Aedatan. While Mog'Durin does have many wonderous contraptions and artifacts, many of them are not magical in nature, as dwarves usually aren't magical in nature.
