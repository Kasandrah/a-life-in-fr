# Mireta

**Written by _[[Jenny-H|Jenny H.]]_**

Mireta is loyal, gentle, considerate, hard working and dedicated. Because she has spent her life trying to fit in, she is often flexible and conciliatory. She can appear to be easily manipulated because she doesn’t let trivial things bother her. She only fights the fights worth fighting. Those that think this means she is malleable are in for a shock when they try to cross her in an area where she will not bend. When it is an issue she feels strongly about, Mireta is quietly as firm and immovable as a mountain. If she cannot persuade someone of the validity of her position, she will simply do what she wants or arrange things so that they go her way.

 Because she was a foundling and so needs to establish her own roots, Mireta is possessive and jealous of the things she considers ‘hers’. In order of importance, these are currently Linameth, N’ton, Lioth, Master Oldive and Igen Weyr. Because these are all people, not things, she realises that they all have their own interests and needs which must be taken into consideration. However, she is very partisan and will not tolerate anyone trying to usurp her position with them (unless the person concerned allows it and then she will be devastated.)

**Linameth** - The most beautiful, wonderful, clever, understanding dragon in Pern. Mireta is so lucky that Linameth chose her. If anyone ever lays a hand on her, their lives will not be worth living. She expects that having had a great first mating flight and having chosen an excellent bronze, that Linameth will produce a fabulous first clutch and everyone will realise how special she is.

**N’ton** - Mireta’s clever dragon found her a wonderful bronze rider. He is kind and considerate and seems to understand her crafter nature better than the Weyr does. She suspects that he would understand the adventurous streak she has as well. Although she understands that normally after an open flight, the bronze rider doesn’t stay around, Mireta welcomes N’ton’s visits and encourages him to come as often as he can. (She rather likes the sex too, with or without the dragons.) She keeps herself aloof from any other possible romantic entanglements. If the Weyr starts proving difficult, she’s sure that there are plenty of likely herb-growing places to meet. She is going to make sure that Linameth is only flown by Lioth, unless she discovers that N’ton has another lover somewhere. Then she’ll make sure that he doesn’t. If the relationship lasts, Mireta will realise one day that she loves N’ton without ever being _in_ love with him.

**Lioth** - The best of the bronze dragons (how else would he catch Linameth?)

**Master Oldive** - Mireta still wants to train from him as much as she can. Physicians are rare, useful and in demand. (Just her cup of klah!) It is also one thing that she is really good at.

**Igen Weyr** - When they realise just how great a team Mireta and Linameth are, they will come around. Until then, Mireta will be diplomatic, useful, hardworking, apparently obedient and docile. Anything she decides to do outside of their wishes will be done quietly.

___

Mireta daydreamed as she waited for the proceedings to begin. It was one of her favourite pass-times - making up outlandish suggestions for who her parents might be. For years she had dreamt of variations on the theme of a Hold-Lord’s daughter smuggled out to avoid the ravages of some insurrection or other. There were plenty of possibilities of about the right time - Lord Fax alone had conquered, married into or inherited 7 holds and disposed of any who might challenge his right to them. Wasn’t Weyrwoman Lessa of Bendon Weyr revealed as the daughter of Ruatha Hold after 10 Turns in hiding?

Less appealing, but still romantic, was the notion that she was the illegitimate offspring of an irresistible passion between 2 socially-separated lovers. And that still left the possibility that the father of one of the tragic pair would come to claim their only offspring, recognised by the cloth their child had been wrapped in when she had been left on the doorstep of the Tanners’ Craft Hall.

Of course, that Mireta had been probably 2 Turns old when she was found made this scenario unlikely, but her fevered imagination could come up with tortuous explanations for such trivial facts. Loyal retainers, mistaken identity, a host of other ideas were much more interesting than the more likely story that her parents had simply found an extra mouth to feed too demanding in a particularly dismal harvest season.

The Crafters had discouraged Mireta’s flights of fancy and had never come up with a noble crest from a blanket or a cherished heirloom left with her for identification. But that could simply be their way of protecting her until the time was right. And now that the dragons were once again visible in Pern’s skies, a whole new range of possibilities had occurred to Mireta.

The life of an apprentice Physician wasn’t bad. It was better than being a tanner, especially as it had been clearly demonstrated that Mireta had no talent for the craft. Mireta was good at healing and was gentle with her charges. It was just that she lacked a sense of real belonging that most people took for granted. A void that could never be filled.

She knew her teacher, Oldive, sensed that lack in Mireta. He probably also wasn’t fooled by her demure demeanour, but recognised the sense of adventure she possessed, the quiet rebellion that had never been completely sublimated.

Which was possibly why he had brought her to the Weyr to witness the hatching. The rare invitation had been extended in thanks for their help in the last Threadfall. Mireta had held her breath until Oldive had accepted, barely able to contain her excitement.

Now that she was here, it seemed much less exciting. She had a good view of the hatching sand, the candidates ringing the clutch of huge mottled eggs. But the fledgling dragons were more like huge gangly baby chickens, wet and awkward, than the magnificent beasts she’d seen in the skies. A little way in front of her, a newly hatched dragon bowled over a waiting boy, inflicting a huge gash with a poorly placed clawed foot. It wasn’t the first such injury and no one seemed to care that pools of blood were forming beside her. Another dragon was bearing down on her, making further injury likely.

Suddenly Mireta couldn’t stand to sit idly by and watch. Ignoring Oldive’s startled cry she raced onto the warm sand. She tugged at the huge head to redirect its path and found herself looking into the most incredible eyes. Opalescent, multi-faceted, rainbow eyes. Eyes that seemed to reach out and encompass her. Eyes that belonged to the most beautiful, warm, loving, wonderful creature in the world. She knew that they would never be lonely again.

Mireta became aware of the outside voices. Startled, amused, complaining, dismayed, outraged voices. Oldive demanding to know what she thought she was doing. Mireta offered helpfully, “Linameth is hungry.” The voices stilled momentarily and she suddenly realised that she had committed a dreadful faux pas. What had she been thinking of?

Oldive was saying something about undoing what had been done. Mireta’s eyes misted at the thought. It would be like cutting out her heart, but she would say the right thing anyway. “Someone else was meant to do this. I could go away and let them take over.” Her voice broke on the last word. _I don’t want anyone else,_ the dragon protested.

The man in the leather jacket who had been arguing with Oldive turned to her. “It’s a little late for that.” Linameth hissed at him and flapped still-wet wings in what was supposed to be a threatening manner. The dragon protested that stray thought. _Never mind, dearest, you’ll grow into a magnificent dragon and will be able to scare anyone you chose_, thought Mireta back. She rubbed Linameth’s eye ridges to ease the dragon’s distress.

The man seemed a little taken aback at Linameth’s reaction. “You’d better feed her then,” he said, reluctantly. He motioned for another man to show her the way.

Mireta smiled gratefully back at him. This was clearly going to take all her practiced diplomacy. The voices disappeared again as she and Linameth followed directions to the feeding grounds. It didn’t matter what they did, as long as she and her dragon could stay together. This was going to be wonderful!
